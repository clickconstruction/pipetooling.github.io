# v2.4913 — GC Review: the checks sheet opens as a PDF (2026-10-08)

Client only. Punch list #55, piece 3, the PDF half (`to-dos/where-the-checks-went-residuals.md`). The checks sheet (v2.4050) printed from a blank window, so the browser wrote "about:blank" at the foot of each page unless *Headers and footers* was unticked. It is a PDF now, the way the unpaid-invoices print is (`gcUnpaidInvoicePrintIo.ts`).

## The change

- **Model** (`gcChecksAppliedReport.ts`): `gcChecksSheetModel(gcName, report, { asOfYmd })` holds every cell of the sheet: the title and period, the summary phrases with their figures bold, the payments table, and the Open and Paid in full job tables with their subtotals. It replaces `buildGcChecksAppliedReportHtml`, the only other drawing of the sheet. The paper rules carry over: a column with nothing in it is not drawn, a check's lines group under the job named once, and the job column gets a third of the width.
- **Draw** (`gcChecksAppliedPdf.ts`, new): `gcChecksSheetPdfBlob` draws the model with jsPDF on letter paper.
  - Each table's header row repeats on every page it runs onto.
  - A row is kept whole on one page. A row taller than a page (one check over dozens of bills) runs on under its header row.
  - Every page carries the sheet's name and *Page n of N*.
- **Print and file** (`sentCopiesIo.ts`): `printPdfAndFile(build, filing, building)` opens the tab inside the click, shows what it is building, puts the PDF in it and files the PDF as the sent copy (`how: 'print'`). It answers `'blocked'` or `'failed'` and files nothing then. The sheet's filing is unchanged: under the GC, or a development's jobs.
- **Window** (`GcFindCheckModal`): Print the sheet reads *Building the sheet…* while the PDF builds. A refused tab says how to allow pop-ups.
- `gcChecksPdfFileName` names the copy `checks-applied_<GC>_<day>.pdf`, beside the CSV's name.

Print all, every GC's sheet at once, stays on the card. Per GC was the ask.
