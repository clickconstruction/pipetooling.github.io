# v2.3877 — Lien timeline: the demand letter on the strip, whose move it is (2026-09-27)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Punch list **#32** PR 1 of 3 (`to-dos/job-deadline-timeline.md`; the mock-up `to-dos/job-deadline-timeline.html`, drawn 2026-09-27 and taken as drawn).

## What shipped

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienTimeline.ts`** — a `demand` step kind: the newest sent, unvoided letter (`LienTimelineDemandLetter`: sent, reply-by, amount, open remaining, debtor), placed before the first live step dated after its reply-by day (a sent notice is dated on its deadline, so finished steps never push it back), else right after the finished ones. Four readings: *reply by Sep 28 · 5 days · sent Sep 14 · $8,940* (due), *Sep 28 · overdue 3 days · the fee clock runs* (missed — the move comes back to us), *paid Oct 3 · $8,940 · in full* (done), *sent Sep 14 · no reply date · $8,940* (undated, never nags — the Needs You card's rule). Every step gets `move` (`LienTimelineMove`: ours · gc · owner · county · counsel; null once done, missed or blocked): the notices, the retainage notice, the affidavit and the serve are ours; the letter is its debtor's until its day passes; the hold is the owner's; the suit is counsel's; the release is the payer's (the GC on a sub job, the owner otherwise) until paid, then ours. `waitingOn` (`{ who, words }`) beside `next`: a due letter first, else what Next on the path names — *the owner’s name for the notice*, *the affidavit to be filed*, *payment · counsel on the suit by Apr 16, 2027*; null once released. `lienMoveWords` gives the line its word (*us*, *the GC*, …).
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienTimelineDesk.ts`** — both adapters take `demandLetters` (the raw rows) and fold them through `lienDemandLettersFromRows`: live and sent, each owing up to the job's open balance (the callers know the balance, not each letter's covered lines). The Lien window passes the letters it already loads for its history; the desk pane and the Timeline tab pass none yet (their strips carry the moves and the Waiting-on line, no letter node) — the card's follow-up.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienTimelineStrip.tsx`** — the demand node is square (our paper, not a Chapter 53 step); a move pill under each live node on the row (anchored to the node's bottom, so the pills line up), a chip in front of the step on the list; the *Waiting on* line under Next on the path on the row and the list; the Windows view lists the letter as a line of words. The mini row carries neither pill nor line.
- **The guide** *send lien notices from the Lien desk* → *Where a job stands* gains the two bullets; the Lien desk map's table row; the card says PR 1 shipped and names PRs 2 and 3.
- **One truth fix against the drawing:** § 53.055 serve reads *ours* — we mail the copies to the owner and the GC; the county records the affidavit, it serves nothing.
