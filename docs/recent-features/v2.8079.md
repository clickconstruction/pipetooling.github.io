# v2.8079 — Painted maps: Draw it by hand instead brings the map back to the job (2026-11-20)

Punch list #402, found by the audit of the card. The pull-back to the whole circle is v2.7994. Client only: no migration and no function.

## The scenario

A new map opens on the job at zoom 18. The walk's readings stay rough for 4 seconds, so the canvas fits the map to the dashed circle, about four miles across. The painter presses the blue **Draw it by hand instead**.

The window went to draw mode and moved nothing. The painter stood on a view four miles wide under *Press the map where the line starts.*, with the job a speck six zoom levels down.

## What changed

`PaintedMapWindow`, in the walk panel's `onDrawInstead`. When the map is on the whole circle (`walkMe.area`: the reading is rough and has stayed rough), the press also sends the map back to its own ground.

- **Something is on the map**: a saved line or place, a line already looked over, or what this walk painted before the readings turned rough. The `refit` count moves, and the canvas frames every line and place again, as it does when a walk ends.
- **Nothing is on it**: the map goes to the place it opened on (`center`, through `flyTo`), at the zoom it opened at.
- **Nothing is on it and it has nowhere to open**: nothing moves. That map opened on the whole country, and the draw panel already says *The map opened wide* with **Start the map where I am**.

Both canvases read `refit` and `flyTo` alike, and a walk and a line drawn by hand are both on the Leaflet canvas, so no map is swapped under the press.

The guide `paint-a-map` gains one sentence after *You can press Draw it by hand instead at any time*: *The map then goes back to the job.*

## Not changed

- **Pause** from the same panel keeps the wide view. The person is on the way to the phone's settings, and the first sure reading after **Keep painting** brings the map in.
- A press made when the reading was never rough, or was rough and still waited on, moves nothing. The map had not pulled back, so the view is the painter's own.
- A painter who zoomed in by hand after the pull-back is moved too. The window is not told how far in the map is, and the job is where they were heading.

## Checked

- `PaintedMapWindow.walk.render.test.tsx`, five new tests. The canvas stand-in now records each point it is handed to fly to and each move of the `refit` count, read as the real canvas reads them.
  - A new map with a held rough reading goes to the place it opened on (failed before: `expected [] to deeply equal [ 'fly 29.5,-98.2' ]`).
  - A saved map with a line is framed again (failed before: `expected [] to deeply equal [ 'refit 1' ]`).
  - A walk that painted a line before its readings turned rough keeps the line to look over and frames it (failed before, the same way).
  - A refusal with no reading, and a refusal 3,999 ms into the wait on a rough reading, move nothing (passed before and after).
  - A map with nothing on it and nowhere to open moves nothing (passed before and after).
- Not walked in a browser. The move itself is the canvas's `Refit` and `FlyTo`, which this change does not touch.
