# v2.2331 — Portal "Print all": one job per page (2026-08-26)

Owner ask: a Print all button on the portal statement producing each job as
its own sheet, for customers who review job by job on paper. Approved mockup:
claude.ai/code/artifact/bf37d526-b789-4272-89dc-cb9c798ee0da.

- `CustomerPortal.tsx` only — pure print-CSS re-layout, no PDF library in the
  customer bundle:
  - Screen-only **Print all · one job per page** button (bordered ink, right
    of the statement head) calls `window.print()`.
  - Data-attribute print rules: `[data-screen-only]` never prints (button,
    ledger column header, PAY ONLINE anchors, request forms, footer line),
    `[data-print-only]` only prints (cover note, per-page headers),
    `[data-print-page]` breaks each job section — and the closing total — to
    its own sheet; the ledger's overflow/min-width relax in print; html/body
    pin the PAPER background.
  - `PortalPrintPageHeader`: every printed page self-identifies (wordmark +
    "customer · date · Job N of M" / "· Closing") because handed-out pages
    get shuffled. Cover = letterhead + balance + "N jobs — each on its own
    page…"; closing = double-rule Total due + the QR short-address card.
  - `PortalStatement` computes the job groups once (v2.2318 kernel) and
    threads index/count into the sections.
- Verified by rendering the real print output (Playwright `page.pdf()`)
  against live data: 13 pages for Knight Contracting — cover, 11 jobs,
  closing — with buttons absent and check-ref chips present.
