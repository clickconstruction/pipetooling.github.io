# v2.5091 — Edit Job: a returned check's $30 fee stays in the job's total (2026-10-09)

Client, plus migration `20261010023000_revenue_keeps_check_fee.sql`, which is pushed after merge. No edge function. A follow-up to v2.5033 (the returned check fee), found by reading the code.

## Why

`add_ar_return_case_fee` (v2.5033) puts the $30 on the bill the check paid, in its `amount` and as a `fee_lines` entry naming the case. It raises `jobs_ledger.revenue` by the same $30, as `create_hazmat_fee_incident` does for a hazmat fee. Edit Job's billing autosave rewrites the revenue on every money edit (`writeBillingSlice`): `jobFormRevenueDollars(fixtures, riderFeesDollars)`, the line items plus the riders. The riders were the hazmat incidents alone (`sumHazmatRiderFees`), so the first edit after the fee wrote its $30 away while the bill kept it. The job's total no longer covered its bills, and the next Ready to Bill remainder came out $30 short. The fee was a wash.

The same rewrite lived in three functions. `apply_job_discount` (Bill Customer, Add discount) and `record_job_tip_from_deposit` (a deposit's leftover as a Tip) summed the lines and the un-voided hazmat fees. `add_collect_payment_fixture_from_job_book` (the field's Collect Payment) summed the lines alone. It is the same class of bug v2.1029 fixed for the hazmat fee and v2.1033 backfilled.

Shown red first: `JobFormModal.returnedCheckFee.render.test.tsx` opened Southern Post's shape in Edit Job, set the unbilled Final line to 2 and closed. The billing save wrote revenue **17,680** where the lines and the fee make **17,710**. The Job Total read *$15,680.00* where the lines and the fee come to $15,710, and Left to bill read $30 short.

## What changed

- **`returnedCheckFeeCents(bills)`** (`src/lib/jobs/arReturnCaseFee.ts`): the returned check fees on a job's bills, in cents. Each counted line is a `fee_lines` entry that names its case with an amount above zero. A line that names no case is not counted.
- **`jobFormRiderFeesDollars(hazmatFeesDollars, bills)`** (`src/lib/jobs/jobFormMoneyTotals.ts`): the riders, the hazmat fees plus the returned check fees, summed in cents.
- **Edit Job** (`JobFormModal.tsx`): `riderFeesDollars` is assembled from it with `editing.invoices`, so the Job Total (*$15,710.00 ($15,680.00 work + $30.00 riders)*), the billing save's revenue, the Paid → Billed close check, the bill-part slider, the payment and bill figures and "Make the Job Total $X" all count the fee. The ② bar's riders block keeps the hazmat fees only (`hazmatFeesDollars`), because it reads *Riders (hazmat fees)* and the check fee is already on its bill.
- **Migration** (see `docs/migrations/20261010023000_revenue_keeps_check_fee.md`):
  - `job_rider_fees(job)` is the SQL twin of `jobFormRiderFeesDollars`;
  - the three functions are restated byte for byte but for their revenue sums, which now add it;
  - Collect Payment's sum also stops dropping a hazmat fee.
- **The guide**: *match bank deposits to the bills they pay* says the job's total grows by the fee, and Edit Job counts it as a rider.

## Left as it is

- **The GC billing job.** `gc_owner_billing_revenue` resets that job's revenue to the contract plus the interest billed on every pay application, certificate and interest bill. A returned check fee on one of its bills would be dropped there. Its SQL is lifted byte for byte from `spike/gc-mode`, so adding `job_rider_fees(billing_job_id)` is the GC crew's change.
- **Turnaway trip charges.** `create_turnaway_trip_charge` raises the revenue with no line and no rider, so Edit Job's save and these functions still drop a trip charge. That is a separate gap, older than this one.
- **A bill deleted with its fee line.** The fee goes with the row's `fee_lines`, and the next rewrite drops it from the revenue. The case keeps its `fee_*` columns, with `fee_invoice_id` cleared.
- **The ② waterfall.** A fee on a bill whose lines are linked counts as money billed by amount (`dollarCoverageForSegments`). Southern Post's card now reads *Left to bill $2,000*, which is right, but its Final line still reads *$30 of $2,000 covered*. A bill of that line alone comes to $1,970 and leaves $30 to bill, so the money comes out whole, split oddly.
