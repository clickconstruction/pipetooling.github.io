# v2.7190 — New task on a phone: the names show under the search, and Repeats is not cut off (2026-11-13)

Punch list #348, PR 4 of 4, the two of its four rows that are plain fixes. The other two change what a row shows and wait on a pick (below).

## What changed

- **Someone else's names sat under the bar.** With the keyboard up, the window scrolls the focused field back into the form, moving it no further than it must (`scrollTopToReveal`, v2.7155). For the people search that meant the box stopped just above the bar and the names it found were below it. `scrollTopToReveal` takes `align: 'top'`, which puts the field at the top of the form, and `TaskWindowShell` uses it for a field inside an element marked `data-keyboard-reveal="top"`. The Someone else panel (`new-task-everyone` in `TaskFormRows`) carries the mark. All three task windows share the rows, so Edit task and the roadmap's Add task get it too.
- **Repeats was cut off on a narrow screen.** The When bar's four segments did not shrink (`whiteSpace: nowrap`, 13 px, 5 px beside each word) and the bar clips at its rounded edge. At 322 px of screen, which is Safari's text at 125% on a current iPhone, the last word lost its end. The font and the room beside each word now follow the screen (`WHEN_BAR_FONT`, `WHEN_BAR_PAD` in `TaskWhenBar.tsx`: 13 px and 5 px at 361 px and wider, down to 12 px and 2 px at 324 and narrower), and a segment that still cannot fit shortens with … in place of being cut. An iPhone SE (375 px) and wider draw the bar exactly as before.

## Walked

iOS Simulator, iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.4, Safari, the on-screen keyboard up, nothing saved.

- **Someone else**, then its search box: the box is the first thing under the title, with the first three names and the crew row under it, above the bar.
- **The When bar at 125% page zoom** (322 px): *Today*, *Tomorrow*, *Pick a day* and *Repeats* are all whole. At 100% it is unchanged.

## Left for a pick, not built

- **Done when…'s hint is five lines on a phone** and pushes the window past one screen. A shorter hint, or the hint behind a press.
- **Pick a day's four weeks** open under the bar on a phone and push Where and Add below the screen. The iPhone's own calendar already opens from the date, so on a phone the four weeks could wait for a task that repeats.
