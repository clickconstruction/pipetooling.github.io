## Where the course stands

Thirty lessons, thirty drawings, forty-four bank questions, and the content test, the figures test and the render smokes green on all of it. The row `intro-hvac` is seeded **off**, offered and required of nobody.

**What waits on a person, in order:**

1. **A licensed HVAC reader, not yet named.** A Texas air conditioning contractor with the environmental air endorsement who holds the EPA card. The owner or a dev opens the course as a preview while it is off (`/training/intro-hvac`).
2. **The read-through.** Each correction is one commit with the lesson's `from:` line naming the reader. The lines that say *general trade knowledge, not yet checked* are the reader's first stops: lessons 1 to 5, 10, 11, 17, 20, 24 and 28 whole, and parts of 6, 14, 16, 18, 19, 21 and 29.
3. **The code.** Which book and edition the company's cities inspect under, and their amendments. Every number read on 2026-11-21 is the 2024 model code's.
4. **The company's own rules.** Where the line falls between the trades on the condensate drain, the gas line and the flue, and what a plumber does at a gas meter, are the master plumber's and the reader's to write. The lessons say only what the law and the utilities say.
5. **The reader's name and the code edition** on `course.md`'s `line`, then **On** in Settings → Training.

**A gap in the mechanism, not in this course:** a correction made after the course is on reaches the required roles through **Everyone takes it again…** and reaches nobody who passed it as an offered course.
