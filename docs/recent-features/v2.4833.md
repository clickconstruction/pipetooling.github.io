# v2.4833 — GC mode: the record for each trade partner company (2026-10-08)

The Board's B1 in the real build (`to-dos/gc-mode/BOARD_REAL_BUILD.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`): the company record, the gate the other GC lanes read. It is in `supabase/migrations/20261008020000_gc_company_record.sql` (`docs/migrations/20261008020000_gc_company_record.md`).

- Eight tables:
  - `gc_companies`: the main contact, trades, address and miles, language and vetting.
  - `gc_company_people`, with the kinds of email each person gets.
  - `gc_company_vetting_forms`.
  - `gc_invites`: the asks, with the office's own plugs, covers and taken alternates.
  - `gc_quotes`, append only.
  - `gc_company_contacts`, one call log for the company and its asks' stories.
  - `gc_trade_promises` with `gc_trade_promise_moves`.
- Eight dates on `gc_projects`: our bid sent, permit, start date, our contract sent, started, and Start anyway's who, why and what was missing.
- The seven `company_id` columns main's GC tables carried get their foreign keys to `gc_companies`. Each is `RESTRICT`, added not valid and then validated. All were empty or null on prod.
- Seven office functions: `gc_add_company`, `gc_vet_company`, `gc_record_promise`, `gc_keep_promise`, `gc_keep_promises`, `gc_invite_companies` and `gc_office_decline`. `gc_keep_promises(company, kind, project, package, on)` is the seam Building's writes and the trade portal's verbs call.
- Dev only while it is built: one policy a table for `(SELECT public.is_dev())`, `anon` revoked, and the three house sweeps. The call log, quotes and promise moves are append only by privileges. A company's papers come later, as `person_contract_documents` rows (B6). Nothing on screen reads it yet; the types come in the PR after the push.
