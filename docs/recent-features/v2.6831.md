# v2.6831 — A month of in kind credit is one press, and Needs you says when it is due (punch list #301, PR 3) (2026-11-10)

The owner's reason for the card: *I still need to enter each month's credit*, and November 2026 had been missed because nobody was told. Her call the same day: nothing is entered by itself. So the app watches the months of a monthly deal, says when one is due, suggests the amount with its reason, and a person presses.

## What changed

- **Months**, `inKindMonths` (v2.6827) fed by the bank through `monthsWithBank` (`src/lib/inKind/inKindBank.ts`): a month is *entered*, *no credit*, *due* or *waiting*. It is due once it is over, or once its bill has left the bank.
- **The bank**, `bankPaidLines` / `bankPaidByMonth` over `loadInKindBankLines`: the feed's lines out to the deal's payee (`in_kind_arrangements.bank_payee`, matched inside `counterparty_name`), summed by month in the company's calendar; a failed line, a duplicate and money in are left out. A role that cannot read the feed gets no bank reading, and a month is then due only once it is over.
- **The month's window**, `src/components/inKind/InKindMonthWindow.tsx` over `monthChoices` (`inKindMonthChoices.ts`): *What the bank shows*, then the answers with the suggested one first and marked *the bank says this one*: the bill less what left the bank, the usual credit, the whole bill, **No credit this month**, **Another amount**. No amount is offered twice and none is more than is left on the bills. The split is `in_kind_line_add`'s dry run; the memo is written (*Rent credit, the shop, November 2026*); the date is the day the bill left the bank, else the month's last day. The press reads *Record November's credit of $1,500.00* or *Settle October with no credit*.
- **The card and the ledger**: an amber line (`dueStripWords`) with the press for the oldest month due; the ledger's **Month by month** list with each month's state and what left the bank. The card opens a month from `?inKind=<deal>&month=<YYYY-MM-01>`.
- **Needs you**, key `in-kind-credit-due` (billing tier 30, amber, the assistant's): `useInKindCreditsDue` → `inKindCreditsDue` reads every open monthly deal, its scheduled lines, its jobs' balances and the bank, and gives the months due oldest first with the credit each will suggest. The line reads *November's in kind credit is not entered* and its door is the customer's page on that month. Source `inKindCredits`, office roles; Quickfill reads it too.
- **Help**: `keep-track-of-work-paid-in-kind.md` gains *A credit every month*.

## Decisions taken with the build

- **Suggested, never entered by itself** (the owner's call). A payment to the landlord is not always rent, and a month with no rent is only known when it ends.
- **A month with the whole bill paid is settled with a line of zero.** Without it a paid-up month looks the same as a forgotten one, and the line would nag for ever.
- **One month at a time, the oldest first.** Each month's amount depends on what left the bank that month, so a press that entered several at once would be guessing.
- **The suggestion is capped by what is left on the bills**, so the last month of a deal offers the remainder, not the usual credit.
