# v2.7516 — Lien window: the notice tab says when the Lien desk holds the approved notice (2026-11-16)

The fourth fault from the notice the owner scanned on 2026-11-16, agreed the same day. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## Before

The Lien window's § 53.056 tab (`LienFilingTabs.tsx`) builds a notice from the job and prints one unsigned set, behind a help sheet that says to copy it and to sign in ink. That is true of that print. But the tab never said whether the Lien desk already held the job's notice: the draft the leader approved, with his signature. A print from the tab then put a second, different, unsigned notice in the mail beside a desk reading *signed*. Until v2.7512 the desk's own *Just this one* led there.

## What changed

- `src/lib/jobs/lienWindowPrintNote.ts`, `lienWindowPrintNote(state)`: for `ready` (approved, to print) *The Lien desk holds this job's approved notice.* and to print that one from the desk; for `printed` *The Lien desk has printed this job's notice.* and to enter its mail numbers there. Each ends *A print from this tab is a new copy with no signature.* Null for every other state.
- `LienFilingTabs` takes `deskNoticeState` and `onOpenDeskNotice` and draws the note (`data-lien-window-desk-note`) over the tab's presses, with **Open it on the Lien desk ›** when the opener gave a door. The tab's own presses stay: nothing is blocked.
- `LienInstrumentsModal` passes the state it already had for its header (`deskNotice`, v2.7077) and its existing `onOpenLienDesk`. No new read.

## What it does not cover

Only an opener that holds the desk's queue hands the state over: the Lien desk's page and the Pipeline. A Lien window opened from a place with no queue (a job's Documents tab) draws no note. A notice still awaiting approval or held draws none either: the desk has nothing approved to print yet.

## Tests

`lienWindowPrintNote.test.ts` (4): both states' words, null for the rest, plain words. `LienFilingTabs.render.test.tsx` (+1): no note with no state or an unapproved one; the note and its press on `ready` with the tab's Print still there; the words with no press on `printed` when no door was given.
