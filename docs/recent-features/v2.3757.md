# v2.3757 — View bill has a QR code: scan to pay, with our mark in the middle (2026-09-23)

Punch list #36 (`to-dos/lien-notice-pay-codes/`), PR 4 of 4 shipped second — the quick win: the owner's second ask, *"in the payment links there are several options to share, but no option for a QR code"*, and the fun one, *"put our logo in the middle"*. Client only, no migration; builds on the `/pay/<id>` address (v2.3754).

## The change

- **[`payQr.ts`](../../src/lib/billing/payQr.ts)** (7 tests) — one set of props every pay code shares: `payQrProps(url, size, markSrc)` — level **Q**, black on white, two quiet modules, the mark carved out (`excavate`) at 22% of the side. Asked whether level H was the best we could do: no — H is 49 × 49 modules for the pay address, Q is 41 (plain M would be 37) and still survives losing a quarter of the code, with the mark taking a twentieth. `payQrSvgMarkup` (the component rendered to a string through `react-dom/server`, the `PartnerStatement` precedent) for print and PDF; `loadBrandMarkDataUrl` (fetched once, inlined — a print window cannot load an external image); `svgMarkupToPngDataUrl` / `payQrPngDataUrl` (SVG → canvas → PNG) for the clipboard, a download and, later, jsPDF; `payQrSheetHtml`, the half-sheet.
- **[`BillQrModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/BillQrModal.tsx)** (3 render smokes) — *Scan to pay* · the bill's label; the code at 240 px on a white card whatever the theme (a camera reads paper, not tokens); the job, *Still owed $…*, the address in words, one line on why the address never expires; **Copy image** (`ClipboardItem` PNG; a browser that cannot copy an image gets the address as text and says so), **Download PNG**, **Print** (the half-sheet through `openHtmlPrintWindow`), Close. Escape, the scrim and Close all close it.
- **[`StripeInvoiceSharePanel.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/StripeInvoiceSharePanel.tsx)** — three new props: `invoiceId` (the row id — present, the door shows; absent, no code, as a bill with no Stripe invoice has no links), `billLabel`, `remainingLabel`. The QR icon after Email in the icon cluster (View bill), the word **QR** after Email in the labeled cluster (the Bills tab; `JobFormInvoiceList.render.test.tsx` pins the four labels), *QR code* in the text-button variant. `HostedStripeBillPanel` passes the row id, *Invoice #<Stripe's number>* and Stripe's amount remaining (the app's own remainder until Stripe answers); `JobFormInvoiceList` passes the row id and *Bill $9,800*.
- **`public/brand/click-mark.png`** — the hand-and-wrench cropped square from the plumbing logo (shipped with v2.3754 for the `/pay` page; the electrical logo's hand-and-bulb can be cropped the same way if a bill ever carries that brand).
- Guide *bill a customer and get paid*: the cluster reads Text · Copy link · Email · QR; a new **Scan to pay** section with the three doors and why the address outlives Stripe's link.

## Verified
