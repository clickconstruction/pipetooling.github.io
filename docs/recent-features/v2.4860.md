# v2.4860 — Help: the start-here guides match the screens again (2026-10-07)

PR 1 of the help-guides freshness pass after the lien guides (punch list #91). Punchlist assigned it to Helper 10. Three read-only readers checked every app claim in the six start-here guides against main's code. Every fix below was re-read in the code before it was written. Words only, and each guide renders as before. The links in the six guides are now written `/help/<slug>`, the form `CLAUDE.md` names. [v2.4868](v2.4868.md) makes the old `?g=<slug>` form work too.

## The fixes, by guide

- **get started as a leader** (master, dev)
  - Adding a person is **+ Hire** on People → Users, then the **Kind**. Settings → People & teams is a dev-only pointer since [v2.4348](v2.4348.md). A non-dev's hire makes the roster row, and a dev sends the invite (`HirePersonModal.tsx`, `hireWrites.ts`).
  - Limiting a sub to trades is a dev's, on the person's desk (`personDeskGates.ts`). **Link account** sits under the row's ⋯, for a dev or whoever made the row (`PeopleUsersTab.tsx`).
  - The Pipeline runs Waiting through Paid in Full, and Collections shows only while it has rows (`stagesStageBar.ts`).
  - Hours and Payroll show for a leader only once a dev adds them to *Pay Approved Leaders* (`usePeopleAccess.ts`).
  - Approving the walk does not release a sub's money. **Settle → release $X** does, once the step is complete or approved (`StepCommitmentPanel.tsx`).
  - The role table gains Banking (controller, not assistant) and the estimator's Prospects switch. Primaries lose the Pipeline.
  - The Job Mode offer shows to a leader, never to a dev (`jobModeToggle.ts`).
- **get started in the office (assistant or controller)**
  - Banking is controller and above since [v2.3305](v2.3305.md). The paragraph that called a controller's empty Banking queue a bug is gone, and Banking's seven tabs include Visuals.
  - The Job window has six tabs (`jobHistoryTab.ts`).
  - The ☎ on the Lien desk opens the words at once since [v2.4731](v2.4731.md). The desk's find box finds the letter.
  - The morning example shows the Dashboard's Needs you items as they read (`dashboardNeedsYou.ts`).
  - Quickfill is the office's daily round of review sections. The Job Mode tab bar has four tabs. Primaries see bids, not bills. The guide chips list roles below yours (`roleGuideLens.ts`).
  - The new-hire paragraph: assistants and controllers press **+ Hire** themselves. The Kind starts on Helper, and a dev sends the invite and ticks training mode.
  - The clock row reads *Clocked in · 03:12:07* (`ClockInOutButton.tsx`). The purple button's text is *estimating*, and *Ask estimating* is its hint.
- **get started as a superintendent**
  - The Workflow strip reads **Superintendents:** (`WorkflowSuperintendentsStrip.tsx`).
  - The attention pills are *sent back*, *no schedule on current step*, *waiting on … · Nd* and *current step unassigned*. There is no approval pill (`projectAttention.ts`, `Projects.tsx`).
  - **Approve or send back** is rewritten from `stepLifecycle.ts`. This settles the phrase the sweep kept word for word, "the previous step reopens for rework automatically". The **Office:** row shows on a step that is waiting or under way. Send Back marks that step *sent back*. If the step before it was done, it goes back to in progress for its crew. When that step is done again, the sent-back step returns to waiting on its own. The reason is optional.
  - Forecast → Subs shows the subs' work on your projects, offers faded, and a red outline for a double booking or a day off (`ProjectsForecastSubsTab.tsx`). The tab bar has four tabs. Arrived and Leaving wear their colours. Marking someone off is any office person's.
- **get started as a sub or helper**
  - In Job Mode, clocking in is the card's right-hand button, **Clock In** (orange) or **Start First Job**. Clocking out is **Clock out** under the card, or the red **Wrap Up Day** on the last job (`DashboardJobModeCard.tsx`).
  - Steps live on the full Dashboard, in the Projects card under **Assigned Steps**. Set Start is grey and Mark Complete is blue (`DashboardProjectsCard.tsx`, `AssignedStageCard.tsx`).
  - The tally prompt counts any un-jobbed card purchase, not just today's. The team feedback card comes after clock out, and its put-off button reads *Not now · remind me in 7 days*. So clocking out asks up to three things, not four (`ClockInOutButton.tsx`, `CrewReviewDeck.tsx`).
  - *How far along*, *Pick your days* and *Your days* are on the work and pay portal that **My statement ↗** opens, with the portal's button words (`SubPortal.tsx`, `subPortalI18n.ts`). The offer card is *New offer* in Your money, on the full Dashboard.
  - **teammate** sends a coworker a task. **Show full dashboard** lasts until the app reloads. Job Parts Tally opens from Settings.
- **get started as a primary**
  - Jobs shows a primary Reports only, with *Newest*, *By job* and *By person*. **Edit job** opens the job window (`Jobs.tsx`, `JobsReportsListView.tsx`). The example row becomes the Edit tab's stepper.
  - Ready to Bill is the Dashboard card, and billing is the job window's Bill tab (`DashboardTeamReadyToBillSection.tsx`). The duplicate link is gone.
  - Emailing reports is office-only, so the Reports line only covers reading them.
- **get started as an estimator**
  - Counting and the CountTooling import are on **Counts**. Parts per fixture are on **Takeoffs** (`Bids.tsx`, `BidsCountsTab.tsx`).
  - There has been no Submission tab since v2.1387. The proposal is built and sent from **Cover Letter**, and the Bid room button reads *✍ Get the link* (`BidRoomPanel.tsx`).
  - Materials names the Parts Book and the Assembly Book, plus the Supply houses directory. Prospects needs the account's switch. Only a dev widens trades.
