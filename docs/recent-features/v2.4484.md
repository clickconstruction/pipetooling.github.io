## What was wrong

The estimator emails the package and records what comes back with *Their answer*, so the revision stays a draft ([v2.4450](v2.4450.md): "Estimators send the package by email and record the answer on a draft"). `submittalJourney` read answers only on a shared revision. On BP375 — 14 rows, 4 sent back, 10 with no answer, 6 cut sheets short — the strip read *Next: 6 rows still need a cut sheet*, steps 6 and 7 were grey and folded while they held the answers and the resubmit button, and *New revision* was held with *turns on once the package is built*.

## What changed

- **The kernel** ([`submittalJourney.ts`](../../src/lib/submittals/submittalJourney.ts)), on a draft with at least one answer:
  - *Their call* is waiting while any row has no answer, done once every row has one.
  - Rows sent back: *Resubmit* is current and the Next line is the one a shared revision gets (`sentBackNext`, shared by both), with the resubmit button.
  - Every row approved: Resubmit done, *Next is the order log, Step 8.*
  - Some approved, the rest still with the GC: the draft's own next thing stands (cut sheets, the package), with Their call lit as waiting.
  - *Share* is left as it is. Nothing was shared from the app, and its button still waits for the package.
  - `stageGate('resubmit')` is on once Their call is past *later*.
  - With nothing approved the line reads *structura sent 4 rows back*, not *approved 0 and sent 4 back*.
- **The tab** ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx)): a step stays open when any live step reads from it (`liveStageKeys`), not the first live step alone; the Share step's line reads *Not shared from the app · answers typed in* (`describeRoomLine`'s fourth argument).
