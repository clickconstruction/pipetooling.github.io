# v2.4970 — Lien money: the Deadlines, the Dashboard reminder and Put a GC on notice count what is billed (2026-10-08)

Client only. PR 2 of [v2.4969](v2.4969.md): the Lien desk's own readers moved to what the sent bills owe (`lien_billed_open()`), and three readers that take the job from the client still counted the job's whole balance, `revenue − payments_made` — so a job billed in stages read one number on the desk and another on the Deadlines list, the Pipeline's lien chip, the Dashboard's *lien* reminder and the GC run.

## What changed

- **Kernel [`lienBilledOpen.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienBilledOpen.ts)**: `lienBilledOpen(job, invoices, payments)` is `openBillRowsForJob` + `sumRemaining` from bill truth (`_shared/billTruth.ts`) — each `billed` invoice net of the payments tied to it, clamped at zero; a job billed as one shell is price less payments; anything else 0 — the client twin of the SQL. `gcNoticeRowsWithBilledOpen(rows, jobById, invoices, payments)` rewrites a billed job's `open_balance` on the GC run's rows and leaves an unbilled job's row alone.
- **The Pipeline's `lienRunwayFor`** (the Deadlines list, the Billed row's clock, the lien chips) passes `lienBilledOpen(job, job.invoices, job.payments)`; the board already carries both tables.
- **`useLienWatchNudge`** (the Dashboard's and Quickfill's *lien* reminder) reads the billed jobs' bills and payments in id chunks beside the jobs, then counts the same way.
- **`useGcOnNoticeData`** (Put a GC on notice) already loaded every job's bills and payments for the jobs band; its rows go through `gcNoticeRowsWithBilledOpen` before the queue and the run are built. `list_gc_unpaid_months` is untouched: it lists jobs not billed yet (`is_billed = false`) on purpose, and their *what it will bill* figure stays the job's balance.

## Tests

`lienBilledOpen.test.ts` (3: 922's shape with a draft, part and full payments and an untied payment; the shell and the unbilled job; the GC rows). The runway, the watch and the GC run kernels are unchanged and keep their own suites.
