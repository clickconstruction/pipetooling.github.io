# v2.7302 — The affidavit always names the month of the last day on site (2026-11-14)

Client and dev seat: no migration; `dev-mcp` carries the kernel mirror and is redeployed after the merge. Three Dudley Mason tickets at 628 Terrell Rd were drafted on the Lien desk on 2026-11-14 after their § 53.056 notice was recorded by hand. The notice, one paper for the house, named April, June, July and August; the tickets' work was in September, the last day set by hand. Two of the three affidavits printed a blank at *Payment is requested for work done and materials furnished in …*, because since punch list #305 the paper named only the months a recorded notice names inside the job's work span. The owner's reading of 2026-11-14: that line matches the month of the last day on site, and a day set by hand counts.

## What changed

- `lienAffidavitSwornMonths` in `src/lib/jobs/lienAffidavitNotices.ts`: the span's end month (`lienWorkSpan`: the hand day, else the last approved clock day, else the last month the desk knows) is always among the sworn months, beside the noticed months inside the span. Months set by hand (v2.7270) still stand in for the whole. Every paper, pane and sheet reads this one kernel, so the short form, the Lien window, the help sheet, the notice record, the stop-paper pages and the dev seat's `plan_affidavit_draft` all change together. The `empty` case of `lienAffidavitNoticeCheck` (a paper that names no month while a notice is on record) is kept for a job with no day and no month at all.
- `lienAffidavitNoticeDueLine` in `src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeOwed.ts`: the line above *Send for approval* and the seat's `notice_still_due` no longer say the paper will not name the month. They say the paper names it from the last day on site, and to record the notice so the month has one behind it.
- The guide *file a lien and never miss its deadlines*: the two paragraphs on the months the paper names.
- `npm run gen:edge-kernels` refreshed the mirror under `supabase/functions/_shared/gcKernels/`.
