# v2.5048 — GC mode: a trade partner answers a charge and asks for a change from its portal (2026-10-09)

P4b-ii of the trade partner portal (`to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p4.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`; P4b split in three, approved 2026-10-09). No migration, no deploy: it reads the slice P4b-i (v2.5044) carries and posts the two kinds it added.

- **Charges from {gc}** (`GcTradePortalBackCharges.tsx`, from the spike's `GcPortalBackCharges.tsx`): each charge on a trade that is the company's, with its chip, reason and words. A photo is a link to Drive, drawn only when it is `https`, with one new word key (`bcPhotoOpen`: "See the photo" / "Ver la foto"). An open one offers **Agree** and **Dispute it**, and a dispute needs its reason (`answer_back_charge`).
- **Changes to your work** (`GcTradePortalChanges.tsx`, from the spike's `GcPortalChanges.tsx`): while `portalCanAskChange` holds, each request with its chip and words, and of its change order only the company's part. **Ask for a change** opens what changed, the three whys, what it asks and the working days (a whole number), and sends `ask_change`. The photo or ticket goes by email until P5a, and the form says to whom (`changeFileByEmailWords`, two new word keys).
- **The trade's page** (`GcTradePortalProject.tsx`) branches on the award as the prototype's `PackageBlock` does: on its own job the quote asks nothing more (its statement of work's block comes with P2c), and a trade awarded elsewhere reads **This one went to another company** with no questions to ask. The live check on the deployed sample found the job still asking for its quote again.
- Presses go through `usePress`: a refusal shows under the press in the company's words, the page re-reads quietly, and the office's preview posts nothing.
- **Tests**: the page's presses on the sample's job (agree, dispute with its reason, the ask form with whole days and no file, a refusal, the preview), the job and lost-elsewhere pages, and the email words.
- **Docs**: the guides `share-a-trade-partner-its-portal` and `see-what-a-trade-partner-sends-from-its-portal` (text only until a real company has a job), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`'s trade portal paragraph.
