# v2.8487 — GC projects and Document builder: three faults a live walk found (2026-11-24)

The walks of punch list #416 (`to-dos/document-builder-whats-left.md`, walks 1a and 1c, on clicktooling.com on 2026-11-24) wrote down faults they did not fix. The owner asked for the fixes the same day. Three are client words and are here. The fourth, the bench's refusal, is a database function and ships apart.

## 1. The one press names who each email goes to

**Seen:** the sheet's **Send to 2 trades who are ready** sent to each company's first contact and named nobody before it went. One of the two went to an address the mail service refuses, and the walker learned who it was for only from the failure.

**Now:** above the press, a line a ready party reads *‹company›: it goes to ‹name› at ‹address›.* The person is the first on the party's record, which is who the press has always sent to. More than three ready fold under one line, *See who the N emails go to*. Nothing is drawn while nothing is published.

- `src/lib/documentBuilder/send.ts`: `batchGoesTo(ready)` returns the lines, each marked with `val`, and the fold's summary past `HELD_NAMED_AT_MOST`.
- `BuilderSendList.tsx` draws them with `ValueSentence`, for the sheet's trades and the builder's GCs alike. The press, the held list and the GC card's count question are unchanged.
- **Not changed:** who the press sends to. Picking a person is still the row's own box.

## 2. The ask window counts emails only when it sends them

**Seen:** with **Email the invitations now** unticked, the line at the bottom of *Ask for quotes* read *1 email goes out, each with the company's own portal link*, and no email went.

**Now:** `askStanding` takes whether this press emails. With the tick on it reads *1 email goes out, with the company's own portal link.* or *2 emails go out, each with…*. With it off, or for a reader who cannot send, it reads *1 company is asked.* or *2 companies are asked.* The sentence about the two quotes we want follows either way.

- `src/lib/gc/askCompanies.ts`, `GcAskCompanies.tsx` (the window passes `emails && emailNow`, the same value the press hands to the write).

## 3. An award on a number that ran out says so and asks first

**Seen:** a Concrete quote good until Nov 7 was awarded on Nov 24 with no word at the press. The table's *Their number holds* row marks it, some rows above.

**Now:** under **Award and draft the statement of work**, a line reads *Their number ran out Nov 7. Ask them whether it still holds.* The press opens a window, *Award ‹trade› to ‹company› on a number that ran out?*, whose button reads **Award on this number**. Cancel writes nothing. A number that holds, or a quote with no day said, awards as before with no window.

- `src/lib/gc/awardRanOut.ts` (new, pure): `awardRanOutLine` and `awardRanOutConfirmWords`, over the portal's own `bidRanOut` and `bidGoodUntil`.
- `GcCompareQuotes.tsx`: the line shows only when the gate lets the award through, so a company the gate stops reads one reason.
- **The call taken on a default:** the award is asked about, not stopped. The office may have the trade's word that the number holds, and **Take the award back…** undoes a draft. To stop it instead, `awardGate` is the place.
