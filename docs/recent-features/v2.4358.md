# v2.4358 — Submittals: What the GC sees opens in a window (2026-10-02)

Grace, 2026-10-02: "when a user clicks 'See what the GC sees' I would like for it to be a modal instead of a sidebar." Since v2.4189 (#62 Layer 2) the preview was a 400 px pane beside the road on a desktop (the tab's root became a two-column grid while it was open, squeezing the rows table and the procurement log) and a sheet only under 640 px.

## What changed
- `SeeWhatTheGcSees` always renders in `ResponsiveModalShell`: a centred window 720 px wide (about the GC's own page) with the shell's enlarge toggle (`fullScreenKey` `submittals-see-gc`, remembered per device), and the same full-screen sheet on a phone as before. × , Esc and a click on the backdrop close it (the shell's press-began-on-the-backdrop guard applies). The `narrow` prop and the `<aside>` pane are gone.
- The tab's root grid is one column at all times; `onSeeGc` only opens the window.
- The strip's button always reads **See what the GC sees** (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`, no pressed state — it used to toggle to *Hide what the GC sees*).
- The why line reads *This is the page the GC opens from your link, drawn from your rows as they stand* (it said it *follows your rows as you edit*, true of a pane beside the rows, not of a window over them).

## Tests
- `SeeWhatTheGcSees.render.test.tsx`: the page is in the dialog *What the GC sees*; × and Esc close it; a shared revision says the link shows this now.
- `SubmittalJourneyStrip.render.test.tsx`: the button keeps its name and says it opens a dialog.
- `BidsSubmittalsTab.render.test.tsx`: the strip opens the window with the reviewer's page; × closes it.
