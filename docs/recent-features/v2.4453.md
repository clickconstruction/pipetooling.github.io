# v2.4453 — Bids on a phone: five tabs no longer slide sideways (2026-10-02)

The pages [v2.4451](v2.4451.md)'s phone check found scrolling sideways because of content lower down (its *Not fixed here*), plus one more the check found once those were fixed. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong
Measured headless at 375 × 812 against prod on BP398, BP403 and BP483, each page settled 6–9 s. Attribution hid each subtree from `<main>` down and re-read `scrollWidth`; a cause found that way was then fixed and the page measured again, which is how the second Labor cause showed.

| Tab | Page wider by | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Labor | 188 px | The labor book's grid column was `1fr`, which is `minmax(auto, 1fr)`: it grew to the entries table's widest row, 545 px (the *Hours from* chip "robot · picked over Default 1/1/0.5" is `nowrap`). The table's box was `overflow: hidden`, so nothing scrolled. Same book on every bid, and on `/bids?tab=labor` with no bid picked. |
| Labor, under that | 66 px | The *Labor rate ($/hr)* row: the rate and the four print buttons (*Rough In*, *Top Out*, *Trim Set sub sheet*, *Print All*) in one `space-between` row with no wrap, 392 px in a 277 px card. |
| RFI, Change order | 105 px | *Response request date (1 week by default)*: a `nowrap` label (275 px) beside a date field that will not go under 148 px. Under it, 73 px from *Project Lead Contact Phone/Email*, built the same way (219 px label, 172 px field). *Project Lead Contact* ran past the card too, though not past the screen. |
| Pricing | 69 px (three buckets), 198 px (four) | *What the bid is made of*: one fixed column per bucket, each at least 120 px (`repeat(N, minmax(120px, 1fr))`), in a 246 px box. Read with v2.4451's title fix in place; without it the title line added more. |
| Cover Letter | 2 px (BP403) | The `nowrap` chip "3 open RFIs — carry as assumptions/exclusions" (294 px) held the letter's grid column at 328 px in a 277 px card. Only on a bid with open RFIs. |

## What changed
- [`BidsLaborBookPanel`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborBookPanel.tsx): the column is `minmax(0, 1fr)` and the table's box is `overflowX: auto`. On a phone the table scrolls inside its box (545 px in 341).
- [`BidsLaborTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidsLaborTab.tsx): the rate row and its button group wrap, so on a phone the buttons drop below the rate.
- [`BidRfiTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidRfiTab.tsx), [`BidChangeOrderTab`](../../src/components/bids/BidChangeOrderTab.tsx): the three label-and-field rows wrap and their labels lose `nowrap`. On a phone each field drops below its label; the date field keeps its 180 px cap.
- [`PricingCompositionBar`](../../src/components/bids/PricingCompositionBar.tsx): `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(120px, 100%), 1fr))`. Where the tiles fit they share one row with the same widths as before, since empty `auto-fit` tracks collapse; on a phone they stack.
- [`OpenRfiChip`](../../src/components/bids/OpenRfiChip.tsx): no `nowrap`. Its rows already wrap, so it breaks onto two lines only when it is wider than the card.
- [`e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts`](../../e2e/viewport-smoke.spec.ts) pins `/bids?tab=labor`, which needs no bid: the book shows under the bid list. Its marker is `/[1-9]\d* entries/`, because "0 entries" shows before the book loads. The other views need a bid id, so they stay with `npm run walk:bids -- phone` ([E2E_SMOKE.md](../E2E_SMOKE.md)).

## Measured
- **Phone, after:** all ten bid tabs on BP398 and BP403 (`walk:bids -- phone`, v2.4451 applied) and on BP483 (by id, since the walk's search does not find it) do not scroll sideways. Before: Labor 188, RFI 105, Change order 105, Pricing 69 / 69 / 198, BP403's Cover Letter 2.
- **The pin:** the spec's steps, repeated in a script at a plain 375 × 812 viewport, pass three runs of three on the branch and fail on the old Labor files (a 563 px layout in a 375 px viewport).
- **Desktop unchanged:** at 1,024 and 1,400 px the four tabs on the three bids give the same element rects before and after, and 22 of 24 full-page screenshots are pixel-identical. The other two are Pricing at 1,400 px. Against a second baseline taken with v2.4451 applied, one is identical and the other differs only in the title row. The tiles' own screenshots are pixel-identical in all six Pricing cases. BP403's Cover Letter is pixel-identical at both widths.

## Not done
- Seen in headless Chromium only, not on a phone.
- The phone check reads the page's width. A box that runs past its card but not past the screen, as *Project Lead Contact* did, is not reported.
