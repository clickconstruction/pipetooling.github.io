# v2.8563 — Messages on a phone: search rows stay on the screen, and the folded reply box shows one whole line (2026-11-24)

Punch list #348, from the walk in an iPhone simulator on 2026-11-25, in Safari at 402 points wide. Two small things were seen in Messages beside the sheets the card asked about. No migration, no edge function.

## 1. The rows under *Lines that match*

**Seen.** Each row's box and its date were cut at the right edge.

**The cause.** The list of hits was a grid that named no column (`Messages.tsx`). Such a grid has one column as wide as its widest row wants to be. A hit's words may hold one long run with no space in it: a link, an address, a long number. That run could not break, so it set the width of every row, and the date, held to one line, went off the edge with the box. It is the fault v2.8232 mended for the lanes (`src/lib/messages/pageFit.ts`). The search list was not given the mend.

**The mend**, all from `pageFit.ts`:

- The list is `LANE_LIST`, whose one column takes the room it is given and no more.
- A row may wrap (`SEARCH_HIT_ROW_FIT`).
- The name over the words is a column of its own that names its width (`SEARCH_HIT_WORDS_FIT`). It keeps `SEARCH_WORDS_ROOM`, 11 rem, before it gives any up. The name and the words may break where they must (`LONG_NAME_FIT`).
- The date stays one line at the right (`SEARCH_HIT_WHEN_FIT`). When it does not fit beside the words it drops to a line of its own.

**Measured** on the page's own markup with the app's stylesheet, in Chromium and in WebKit, with a link of 97 letters put into one hit and a short hit under it:

| Wide | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 402 | every row 77 px past the edge, the date 63 px past, the page 479 wide | every row 28 px inside the edge, the page 402 wide, the date beside the words |
| 320 | every row 159 px past the edge, the date 145 px past | every row 28 px inside the edge, the page 320 wide, the date on its own line under the words |

## 2. The folded reply box

**Seen.** With the keyboard down in a thread, the reply box's second grey line was cut at its foot.

**The cause, now seen drawn.** Folded, the box is one row high. Its grey words were two sentences, *Write to (name)…* and *Type / for a quick reply.* The row is 40 px tall and holds one line of 21.6 px. At 402 wide the two sentences are one line beside a name of ten letters and two lines beside one of twenty-three. At 320 they are two lines beside any name. The top of the second line showed in the box's lower padding. The brief also asked whether the box's foot wants the phone's safe-area inset. It does not: the cut was the wrapped line, and it draws the same on a page with no inset.

**The mend.**

- `boxGreyWords` in `src/lib/messages/replyFold.ts`: folded, the grey words are the first sentence alone. Whole, they are both, as before. `ReplyBox.tsx` reads it.
- `src/index.css`: in a folded box the grey words keep to one line. A name too long for the row ends in dots in Safari and is cut at the box's edge in Chrome. This is the net under the first mend: at 320 wide a name of forty-four letters still wrapped by itself.

**Measured** the same way, with names of ten, twenty-three and forty-four letters at 402 and 320: one line in all twelve looks, where six of the twelve were two or three lines before.

## What did not change

- The search itself, the words it finds and the order of its rows. At a desk a row is the one line it was.
