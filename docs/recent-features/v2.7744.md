# v2.7744 — Lien desk: a link to a job that is not on the list no longer opens another job (2026-11-18)

## Why

Seen live on 2026-11-18: `/lien-desk?tab=affidavits&job=<a test job's id>` landed on `/lien-desk?tab=affidavits`, with `job` gone from the address and another job's affidavit pane open. Nothing said the job had changed. The panes act on the job they show (the press under LAST WORK sets that job's last day of work, a send goes to that job's leader), so a stale or wrong link could put a press on the wrong job.

A job is off a list for ordinary reasons. The test job's affidavit day was 89 days out with no paper started, and the desk keeps a row only inside its thirty days or on a started paper (`lienDeskKeepRows`, v2.7261), while its reader is asked 180 days ahead. A paid job, a job off the lien path, a hidden ZZ test job and an old link all read the same way.

## Where the fallback was

Each of the three paper lists answered a job it did not hold with its own first row:

- **Monthly notices**: the selection effect in `LienDesk.tsx` fell through to `visible[0]`, and the address effect then wrote that row's job over the one the link named.
- **Lien affidavits**: `useLienDeskAffidavits` picked `affVisible.find(…) ?? affVisible[0]`.
- **Retainage notices**: `lienRetainageListView` picked `visible.find(…) ?? visible[0]`.

On the last two the address carries no job once the desk is open, so `job` was dropped whether or not its list held it.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/lien/lienDeskAim.ts`** (new, pure, with `lienDeskAim.test.ts`): the address is read as an aim, a paper list and a job.
  - `lienDeskAimFrom(kind, jobId)` is the aim, or null on a view with no pane (To do, By date, By job) and with no job. The tests run every address the desk is reached by through it, the old `?liendesk=1&kind=…&liendeskJob=…` links that `lienDeskRedirect` maps among them.
  - `readLienDeskAim({ aim, kind, picked, loaded, lists })` answers `none`, `waiting`, `on_list` or `off_list` with the other lists that hold the job. A row the reader picked since is theirs, so the aim no longer speaks. Nothing is decided before the desk's rows are in.
  - `lienDeskAimSpent` says when the desk forgets the aim: once its list holds the job, the reader picks another row or leaves the list. A job that later leaves its list by an act is the list's own business, as before.
  - `lienDeskAimAddressJob` is the job the address keeps: the aimed job while the note stands.
  - `lienDeskOffListWords` are the note's words as `EmptyState` takes them: *<the job> is not on the Lien affidavits list.*, then **Open its Lien window ›**, or the list that holds the job first and the Lien window after *or*.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienDesk.tsx`**: holds the aim in state, set on open and by the re-aim effect. While the read is `off_list` the Monthly notices selection effect opens no row, `useLienDeskAffidavits` and `lienRetainageListView` take `openNone`, and the note stands in the pane's place (`data-lien-desk-off-list`). On one column it tops the list, since no pane is open there. The address keeps `job` on any of the three lists while the note stands. The re-aim effect now hands the named job to the Retainage and Monthly notices selections too, as it did for Lien affidavits, so a row picked before never stays open under an address that names another job.
- **`src/components/jobs/useLienDeskAffidavits.ts`**, **`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRetainage.ts`**: `openNone` opens no row and leaves the list whole.
- The guide *send lien notices from the Lien desk* has a section on the line and its two presses.

## What each list does now

A job its list holds opens as it always did. A job it does not hold: no pane, the note, the two ways on, and `job` kept in the address until a row is picked or the list is left. The other views have no pane a job opens in, so a job named there is dropped from the address on purpose.

## Tests

`lienDeskAim.test.ts` (13 cases), one more case on `lienRetainageListView`, and four cases in `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`: each of the three lists with a job it does not hold, a job another list holds, a re-aim of an open desk, and one column.

## Deploy

Client only. No migration and no edge function.
