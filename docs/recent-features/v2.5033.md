# v2.5033 — Accounts Receivable: add the $30 returned check fee to the bill the check paid (2026-10-09)

Client, plus migration `20261010003000_ar_returned_check_fee.sql`, which Punchlist pushes after merge. No edge function. The owner's call of **2026-10-09**, sent by Punchlist: *Add the $N fee to bill N* on a Came back case adds a fee line to the bill the check was meant for, once, recorded on the case. Punchlist chose option (A): the line goes on the sent bill, Stripe bills are left out, and the RPC's refusals plus the once-per-case columns are the guards.

## The law, verified

- **Tex. Bus. & Com. Code § 3.506(b)**, as amended by H.B. 2793 (82nd Leg., R.S., ch. 333, eff. Sept. 1, 2011): *"On return of a payment device to the holder following dishonor of the payment device by a payor, the holder, the holder's assignee, agent, or representative, or any other person retained by the holder to seek collection of the face value of the dishonored payment device may charge the drawer or indorser a maximum processing fee of $30."*
  - The 2011 act struck the older *"[reasonable] processing fee of [not more than] $30"* for a flat ceiling.
  - Read from the official enrolled text of H.B. 2793 on capitol.texas.gov, and from the current text on vLex (current through the 2023 sessions). The later amendment, S.B. 346 (2019), left (b) at $30.
  - The official statutes site renders by script, so it could not be quoted directly.
- **§ 3.506(c)**: no fee may be charged if a reimbursement fee was collected under Code of Criminal Procedure art. 102.007(e). A fee already collected is refunded if one later is.

## What changed

- **Migration** (see `docs/migrations/20261010003000_ar_returned_check_fee.md`):
  - The case gets `fee_amount`, `fee_invoice_id`, `fee_added_at` and `fee_added_by`. The bill gets `fee_lines`.
  - `list_ar_return_case_fees` reads each case's fee and the bills its check paid, both live and taken off.
  - `add_ar_return_case_fee` is the one press. It refuses unless the case is open with a check that came back, has no fee yet, the check paid that bill, and the bill is not a Stripe invoice. Then, in one go, it:
    - adds $30 to the bill's amount and `fee_lines`;
    - adds $30 to the job's revenue, as the hazmat fee does;
    - fills the case's `fee_*` columns;
    - writes a line in the job's history.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/jobs/arReturnCaseFee.ts`):
  - It holds the $30, the case's line (*$30 — the most Texas allows, Bus. & Com. Code § 3.506*) and the hover, which quotes (b) and (c).
  - `arCaseFeeOffer` gives the press on the oldest bill the check paid that is not a Stripe invoice, naming the job when the check paid bills on two jobs.
  - Once added, it reads *The $30 fee is on bill 1, added Oct 9 by Taunya.*
  - Otherwise it says why there is no press: *Bill 2 is a Stripe invoice, and a sent Stripe invoice cannot take a line.* or *The check paid no bill by name, so the fee has no bill to go on.*
  - It is null for a rejected or unbanked check or a Stripe case.
- **The case pane** (`ArReturnCasePane`) draws a *Returned check fee* box under Next: the line with the statute on hover, then the press, or what happened.
- **The modal** (`BankPaymentsModal`) reads the fee when a bank or hand case opens (`arReturnCaseFeeIo.ts`). It runs the press through its `runCaseRpc`, and on success reads the fee again and refreshes as after any case write that moves money.
- **The printed bill**: `buildPhysicalInvoiceDocumentForBilledInvoice` passes the bill's `fee_lines` (`billFeeLines`) as folded fee rows. The fee prints as its own $30 line, and the work lines still add up to the rest.
- **The guide**: *match bank deposits to bills* says what the fee is and that it goes on once.

## Left as it is

- **A Stripe bill takes no fee.** If the office ever asks, a Stripe bill could take a separate $30 bill on the job naming the check's bill (option B).
- **A check that paid no bill by name** gets no press. Its payment went on the job with no bill picked, so there is no bill it was meant for.
- **The job load's bill embed** (`JOBS_LEDGER_INVOICES_EMBED`) does not name `fee_lines` yet. An embed that names a column the database lacks fails the whole job load, and the client deploys before the push. A follow-up after the push adds it, with the regenerated types. Until then the reprints that read a bill row with every column carry the line, and the reprints from a job load show the amount without the line.
- **§ 3.506(c)** is on the hover, not checked. The app does not know whether a prosecutor's hot-check program collected its fee.
