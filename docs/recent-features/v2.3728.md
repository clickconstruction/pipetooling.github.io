# v2.3728 — Day book PR 6a: Crew Day's one line per office person (2026-09-22)

PR 6 of [`to-dos/day-book`](../../to-dos/day-book/README.md) (punch list #20), the Dashboard half. Client only.

## What the plan said, and what changed

The plan had `get_crew_day_payload` and its `_for_user` twin grow an `outcomes` key by re-creating both (a migration) and the email dispatcher carry it. The second pass split it: the Dashboard section can read `get_day_book_payload` for its own day **as the viewer** — the same gate the tab uses, no RPC change, and a superintendent (whom the Day book refuses) is never asked. The email half (6b) still needs a service-role wrapper because the dispatcher has no `auth.uid()`; it stays open on the to-do.

## The change

- **[`dayBookOneLiner.ts`](../../src/lib/people/dayBookOneLiner.ts)** (+3 tests) — *billed 3 · 4 deposits · 2 contracts sent · approved 12 sessions · moved 2 jobs to Paid*, five phrases then *+N*; a muted line (deletions) is left out; a quiet day is null; a kind the file predates reads as the Day book's own verb, lowercased (*updated the schedule*).
- **`DashboardCrewDaySection`** — for dev, controller, master and assistant viewers it reads `get_day_book_payload(ymd, ymd)` beside the crew payload, builds the Day book view for that day and keeps one line per person; the card ends with *Today:* (or *That day:* when stepping back) and a *Day book →* door (`dayBookDoorHref` for that person and day). A refused, missing or older RPC leaves the card as it was.
- Guide *see what your crews did today* gains *The office's line*.
