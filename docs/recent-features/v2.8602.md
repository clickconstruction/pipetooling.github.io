# v2.8602 — People → Hours: someone still on the clock has a green box that opens My Time (2026-11-25)

Punch list #446. The owner, 2026-11-25, looking at People → Hours: he likes how the table works, and wants the people who are still on the clock shown in the table of boxes, *"with their box being green, where clicking on it brings up their my time card, rather than immediately being able to edit them"*, so he cannot break someone who is still on the clock by accident, *"yet I can clearly see they're accruing time against everyone else's"*. Client only. No migration, no edge function.

**Before.** The grid's figures are closed sessions only (`max(people_hours, closed pending)`), so a person who was clocked in showed an empty box, and that box was a field. The column's head said *+N on the clock* (v2.2915) and nothing in the table said who.

**What a slip in that field did.** A field commits on blur. Tabbing through the empty box, or typing a 0, upserted a `people_hours` row of 0 hours for a person whose session was still running, stamped with the office person's id. A typed number over an open session was caught one step later: `openManualHoursDraftFromBlur` found the open session, raised a toast and opened My Time. And `approve_clock_sessions` adds a session's length to `people_hours`, it does not replace the row, so any hours that did reach the row before the clock-out would have been counted twice. The fix is to take the field away while the session runs.

**Now.**

- `buildHoursGridLiveByCell` (`src/lib/people/hoursGridLiveByCell.ts`) folds the open sessions the page already holds into one entry per person and day. The page memoises it with no clock in it; `PeopleHoursGrid` reads the clock itself on a 30 second tick (`useIntervalNowMs`), since everything it draws is in whole minutes.
- A cell with an entry draws `PeopleHoursGridLiveBox`: a button, 72 wide like the field it replaces, green tint, green edge, the day so far as H:MM and the words *on the clock* under it. The figure is the saved hours of the day plus the running time, so a closed morning and a running afternoon read as one day. The words, the H:MM form with no seconds and the button's shape say it without colour. Its tooltip and its screen reader name read *ZZ Test Avery, Wed Nov 25: 7:38 so far, on the clock since 7:02 AM. Opens My Time.*
- A press calls `openHoursMyTimeForGridCell`, the same door the teal corner uses. It writes nothing. The corner is not drawn on a green box, since the whole box is the door. The amber pending badge of a closed session on the same day stays, and the box's lines move left of it.
- The box sits in the tab order where the field was. When My Time closes, focus goes back to the box that was pressed (`myTimeOpen`).
- **Totals.** `liveCellAddedHours` is what a cell adds. The row's two totals, each day's two foot totals and the week's count it. A total with running time in it is green and reads H:MM; a row's names the running part under it (*7:38 on the clock*) and its tooltip keeps the two apart (*15:47:41 settled and 7:38 still running…*). A total with nobody on the clock is unchanged, seconds and all.
- **Salaried.** A salaried day counts its set hours whatever the clock reads (`effectiveHoursForDisplay`), so the figure stays the set hours, in grey, and the cell adds nothing to a total. The box is still green, says *on the clock* and opens My Time.
- **Left open.** `auto_clock_out_open_sessions_eod` closes every open session at 11:59 PM of its own day, so a session still open on a day before today (in the app's time zone) was missed by that closer. `isLiveCellLeftOpen` reads it: the box is amber, says *left open* and *no clock-out*, adds no time, and opens My Time, where Force clock out is. When it started is in its tooltip, since the box is 72 wide. The head of that day reads *1 left open* in amber.
- **The head** keeps *+N on the clock*. Its tooltip no longer says the column counts closed sessions only.
- **Phone.** Under 640 px every cell is already one tap target. A green cell fills the cell, reads the same two lines, and a tap opens My Time with no day sheet between, so no hours box is offered. If a sheet is open when the person clocks in, its hours box gives way to the same line.

**What the office can do from My Time.** It is the day card the person sees on their own Dashboard (`DashboardMyTimeDayEditorModal`), which already knows an open session: the last segment stays open on a save and is measured against now. From it the office can put the session on a job, split it, adjust its times or use Force clock out. Each is a named press with its own window and its own save. None of it happens by passing through, which is the guard that was asked for.

**Edges read in the code.**

- *Clocks out while the page is open.* The page's Realtime listener on `clock_sessions` reloads the pending sessions 450 ms after the change. The entry is gone, the cell is an ordinary box holding the session's hours, and the amber pending badge is on it.
- *Crossed midnight.* It cannot stay open: the closer ends it at 11:59 PM, and it lands as a pending session with the *still clocked in at midnight* chip.
- *Clocked in on a day outside the week shown.* The page loads sessions by `work_date` inside the range, so there is no column and no box. The clock strip above the grid and the phone's *Who's in* still show the person.
- *A day marked Correct.* The green box wins over the locked figure. `saveHours` refuses a locked day as before.
- *Read-only (training mode).* The box reads and opens the same. This change adds no write and takes one away.

The phone smoke: a green cell opens My Time and never the sheet.
