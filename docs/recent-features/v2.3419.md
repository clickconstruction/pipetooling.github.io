# v2.3419 — Pipeline: the Progress & payment cell says where the job is (2026-09-14)

Client only; no migration (the RPC shipped in v2.3418), no edge function. Fourth PR of the *Where the Job Is* train (v2.3416 legend · v2.3417 recognition · v2.3418 crew feed); the owner's "I love this, build it all" on the brief of 2026-09-14 (artifact *Where the Job Is*, drawn on five real Working rows).

## Why

The cell read the two numbers the office types (bid, % done) and drew money slices with a yellow dot on top; on 2026-09-14, 22 of 31 Working rows had no percent and 13 had no bid value, so most rows showed a dashed track. Meanwhile the crews' clock-ins, sub sheets and status reports said where every job was, and the stage bar we shipped in v2.3198 waited on an Order flag nobody set.

## What shipped

- **Kernel** [`progressPaymentCell.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentCell.ts) (+12 tests, the five rows of the brief among them): `buildProgressPaymentView` → mode `stages` / `lines` / `nobid`, the segments (`widthPct`, `fillPct`, a label, a per-segment **money channel**), the chips' live number, and the words.
  - **Live stage from evidence.** A stage report (v2.3192) says so; else the **money is a floor** — a paid invoice on a line, or the payments poured in order, mark stages done — and the crew is on the first stage after it. Heron: $24,359 covers Rough In → Top Out live. Palmer at Moses Hughes: paid = Rough In to the dollar, three on site today → Top Out, with no percent ever typed.
  - **Fill.** Done stages full; the live stage takes its own report, else a *fresh* percent poured down the stages by weight (v2.3198's spread); a percent older than the last clock-in (`percentIsStale`, v2.3418) is not drawn — the segment reads *on site Sat* and the words carry the date.
  - **The money channel** (`pourMoney`): a line an invoice names takes that invoice's money first; the rest pours left to right — paid, then billed, then done-not-billed. Every segment's 3 px bottom edge shows its own share.
  - **Lines mode.** No stages → the priced line items in sequence order (one line = one segment, the label *Electrical · 80%*); the job's percent poured down them. **nobid** → no bar.
  - **The words** (`crewClause` · `percentClause` · `moneyClause`, `dayWord`): *Top Out · Behar & Malachi on site Sat · 40% typed · $24,359 paid, nothing billed* · *3 people on site Fri · 60% typed Sep 12 · $32,108 billed, nothing paid* (amber) · *Edgar & Jose on site today · no lines on the job · nothing to bill against* (red) · *All 3 stages done · paid in full* (green).
- **[`progressPaymentForJob`](../../src/lib/jobs/progressPaymentForJob.ts)**: one call per row → the legend's money model and the bar's view, from the fields the row already loads plus the crew feed.
- **[`StagesStageBar`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesStageBar.tsx)** rewritten around the view: chips (the live chip carries *· Behar & Malachi* / *· today*), 14 px segments (fill on top, the money channel underneath, a live outline), the label fitted to the segment's width (full → the percent → ✓ → none), the words line toned plain / amber / green / red. **[`StagesProgressPaymentCell`](../../src/components/jobs/StagesProgressPaymentCell.tsx)**: `view` replaces `stageBar`; the classic bar and the yellow dot render only when no view is passed (older callers and tests); the legend keeps v2.3416's four rows in lines mode and Paid / Billed / Left with stages. The v2.3411 red % box is untouched.
- **Wiring**: [`useJobCrewPositions`](../../src/hooks/useJobCrewPositions.ts) called once in `JobsStagesTab` for the board's job ids (`active && authUser`); `crewByJobId` rides the `StagesRowRenderContext` to both tables and the card list; the four cell sites call `progressPaymentForJob`. Render smokes: `StagesStageBar.render.test.tsx` rewritten (Heron's chips and words, Springtown's single segment and four-row legend, DRF's red words, compact, the classic fallback); the three table smokes gain the map.
