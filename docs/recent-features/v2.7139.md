# v2.7139 — Card and footer looks opens to the owner, and Settings search finds both card-and-footer cards (punch list #313) (2026-11-13)

Found by the docs catch-up pass of 2026-11-13: the Glossary and v2.6951 say the owner offers a look by switching it on, and the screen did not let him. One migration, one small kernel, two search rows.

## What was wrong

**The card was a dev's alone, on both sides.** `CardLooksCard` drew when `role === 'dev'` and returned nothing otherwise. The owner's role (`master_technician`) opens Settings → People & teams, the office's tab, and found no card there. Behind it the two settings, `card_looks_v1` and `card_sizes_v1`, are `app_settings` rows no key-scoped policy named, so the baseline's *Devs can manage app settings* alone admitted a write. An owner whose login is not a dev could neither open the card that approves a look or a size nor save it.

**Neither card could be found.** Settings search had no row for *Your card and footer* or *Card and footer looks*, and their element ids were not anchors the deep links knew, so a link to either landed on the tab's top.

## What changed

- **The database** (`20261113130000_card_looks_keys_owner.sql`): insert and update on the two keys for `is_master_or_dev()`, the shape of `master_or_dev_*_bridge_cash_keys`. Its page is `docs/migrations/20261113130000_card_looks_keys_owner.md`.
- **Who sees each card** is one kernel, `src/lib/brandKit/access.ts`: `approvesCardLooks` (the owner or a dev, `isOwnerOrDev`) and `makesOwnCard` (every role but a subcontractor). `CardLooksCard` and `YourCardCard` read it, and so do their search rows.
- **A refused save says its reason.** The card already put the switch back and printed `formatErrorMessage`. A database refusal reads *You don't have permission to save the looks.* The io's own line for a save that wrote no row now names the owner with the dev.
- **Settings search** (`settingsSearch.ts`): the two rows, each with its anchor. An entry may carry `seenBy`, for a card drawn for fewer roles than its tab, and the search bar searches `settingsSearchIndexFor(role)`. So an assistant, who sees People & teams, is not offered the owner's card. The other rows on that tab are as they were.
- **The deep links** (`settingsDeepLink.ts`): `settings-card-and-footer` on Your account and `settings-card-looks` on People & teams.
- **The search box in the header** (`search/doors.ts`) gates a Settings door by its entry's `seenBy` as well as its tab.
- **Dev doors** (`devDoors.ts`): a row for *Card and footer looks*, owner or dev. It had none while it was a dev's card.

## Deploy

The client calls nothing the migration creates, so the push can follow the merge in any order. Between the two the owner sees the card and reads the looks and the sizes; his save is refused, the switch goes back and the card says he has no permission to save. Once `supabase db push` has run his save passes.

## Seen on a screen

The owner's role opens `/settings?tab=settings-people#settings-card-looks` on the card, with the three looks and the three sizes drawn in his own facts; a switch saves; *card and footer* in Settings search offers both rows and the pick lands on the card. The assistant opens the same tab with no such card and is offered *Your card and footer* alone. Not walked on prod: the owner's real save waits on the push.
