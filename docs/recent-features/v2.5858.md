# v2.5858 — The Collect desk, PR 7 of 7: Friday, did the week move (2026-10-25)

Punch list #177, PR G, on [v2.5857](v2.5857.md). The Friday half of the loop: Monday's recorded plan asked for money from a few payers; Friday says what landed and what Monday carries.

## The kernel (`src/lib/bridge/collect/collectScorecard.ts`)

`collectScorecard({ call, sheet, touches, payments, notices, todayYmd })`, pure. The payers to score are today's sheet plus any payer the plan named who paid off and left it (`plannedMoveParts` reads the plan's *payer: move* titles). Per payer: the payments since the plan's day, filed under the payer; **landed after a call** is a payment within `attributionDays` (7, the owner's decision 2) after a recorded touch on that payer since the plan, the rest landed on its own pace; **dated** is a `promised` touch since the plan or money landed; **disputed** is a `dispute` touch. The denominator is the plan's calls: a statement alone is never a call. The notices are counts from the Lien desk since the plan. The verdict words and *Monday carries* (the open call, the promise to watch, the broken promise, the owner's decision) pass the plain-words rule.

## The reads (`loadCollectScorecard.ts`)

The payments since the plan (`jobs_ledger_payments`), each filed under the payer its job bills (the GC when the job's bills go to the GC, else the customer, read with the payment's job), so a payer that paid off still scores; the pile's jobs' desk items drafted, signed or mailed since, and affidavit filings since.

## The card

In the desk, above the rows, once this week's plan is recorded on Collect: *Since the plan, recorded Oct 26* with three readings (landed of the ask with the after-a-call share; calls that ended with a date, with the no-date and on-the-owner counts; the notices signed and mailed) and *Monday carries*. The outcome itself is still recorded below the dial, in *Last week's call*, where Friday has always asked.
