# v2.6539 — The job window is wider on a computer, with the job's name first and the tabs in a strip under it (2026-11-05)

Micah, 2026-11-05, with a screenshot of the window's tab row and its ? card: "in the job modal, I do not like how the header looks, perhaps we should expand the modal?" The window had been 560px wide at most on every screen since v2.1675 (the Bill tab was drawn to it in v2.4293), and on a desktop it rendered at 520: the seven tabs, the ?, "esc to close" and the ✕ shared the first row at 11px, the title wrapped under them with the prior numbers and the kind chip trailing, and the trade pill and icons made a third row before any content. No migration; nothing to deploy.

## What changed

- `src/components/jobs/JobWindowModal.tsx`: `maxWidth` 760 (the phone stylesheet still makes it full-bleed under 640px). At desktop (`!phone`) a title row comes first (`job-window-head`): the number chip (HCP, else C#), the name, the status chip (`JOB_STEPPER_LABELS`, *Collections* while `collections_at`), then a quiet line with the development, the customer, `priorNumbersBare` and `JobKindChip` at size sm, with the `LegendButton` and the ✕ at the right; the ✕'s tip reads *Close · esc* and the "esc to close" span is gone. The tabs follow as a full-width strip (`tabStripStyle`: underline on the one that stands, like the Jobs page's own), scrolling sideways rather than wrapping. The dialog is named by the title row (`aria-labelledby`). On a phone the older order stays: the pill tabs first with the ? and the ✕ in their row, the pane's own title under them.
- `src/components/jobs/DetailJobModal.tsx`: `paneTitleHidden` keeps the pane's `<h2>` out while the window draws its own, and the trade pill then reads the trade alone (`PLUM`, not `629 PLUM`), since the number sits in the title chip; the action row, the map band and every icon handler stay where they were. `formatJobDetailModalTitleParts` is exported for the window.
- The guide *move between a job's Job, Edit, Bill, and Costs tabs* describes the title row, the quiet line and the strip, and the action band's words no longer claim the job name on a computer.
