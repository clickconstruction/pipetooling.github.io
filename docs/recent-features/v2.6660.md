# v2.6660 — The Legal desk asks a sub on the sheets for a signed statement too (punch list #212, residual F) (2026-11-07)

The second of the three residuals left on the card (`to-dos/signed-hours-statement/README.md`): the Evidence tab's **Ask <name>…** listed only who clocked on the job (`LegalEvidenceWorker` came from the approved sessions), so a sub — who never punches — had no press there even after v2.6636 put them in the window's Who list by their sheets.

## What changed

- **The packet** (`src/lib/legal/legalPacket.ts`): `LegalPacketInput.sheetSubs` — one row per sheet a sub's roster row is assigned to, by their login (`{ jobId, userId, name, sheetId }`) — and `LegalEvidenceWorker` gains `kind` (`clock` | `sheet`) and `sheets`. Each job's workers are who clocked, by sessions, then the subs on its sheets, by sheets; a login that also clocked stays a clock worker, as the window's Who list has it. A job with a sheet and no punches is not a gap job: a sheet is a record. `legalAskWorkerTitle` says what the press opens on: *2 sheets here, read as the sheets’ lines*.
- **The desk's read** (`useLegalPacketData.ts`): one more source, *subs on the sheets* — `people_labor_jobs` on the account's jobs with their assignees' roster rows (`kind = 'sub'`, a login attached); a sub with no login is left off, since a statement is saved on the login. A read that fails names itself with the other sources and the band asks who clocked alone.
- **The band** (`LegalEvidenceStatementsBand.tsx`): **Ask Dale Whitfield… on the sheets** per sub, the same `ask(jobId, userId)` — the window (`initialUserId`) picks them from its list and reads the sheet kind; the empty words read *Nobody has an approved session or a sheet here yet, so there is no one to ask.*
- **Tests**: `legalPacket.test.ts` (the subs after the clock workers, a clocked sub staying clock, the titles), `LegalEvidenceStatementsBand.render.test.tsx` (the press, its words, its title, the call).
- **Docs**: the guide *review a collections account before it goes to your attorney* → Signed hours statements on the matter.

## Decisions

- **Asked by their sheets, not by their punches.** The band could have waited for a sub to clock; the company's record of a sub's work is the sheet, so that is what the press names and what the paper reads.
- **The window decides the kind.** The band passes the login alone, as it always did; the window's Who list (v2.6636) already knows a sub by their sheets.
