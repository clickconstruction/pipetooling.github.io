# v2.7462 — Supply houses: move a payment to the bill the house put it on, and put a removed payment back (2026-11-16)

PR 4 of the supply house payments train. The owner, 2026-11-16: "we will also often be told by a supply house that they have applied our money to one bill instead of another, and we'll need the ability to move payments we thought we made to apply them to different bills."

## What changes

- **Move…** on each bill of a payment's window, and **Put on a bill…** beside what the payment holds on account. The step asks how much (it opens on everything the payment has there), which bill of the same house it goes to (open bills oldest first, then *On account, no bill*), and why (*The house applied it differently*, *I picked the wrong bill*, *Something else*).
- **What changes** reads both bills before and after: what is paid and what is open on each, and whether one reads unpaid again or paid. The payment's day, amount and method stay as they are.
- **The press names the move**: *Move $1,000.00 to S4398120*. It is dead with its reason until the amount fits what the payment has there and what the other bill has open.
- **A trace on both bills.** The bill the money left reads *$1,000.00 moved to S4398120 · Dana · the house applied it differently*; the bill it reached reads *$1,000.00 moved here from S4393015 · Dana*.
- **What happened to it** in the payment's window lists each move, removal and putting back, with **Take it back** on a move that still stands.
- **Remove this payment…** asks why, in the window that says what it does. A removed payment waits in **Removed in the last 30 days** under the house's bills, with **Put it back**. Putting back is refused, naming the bill, when a bill it paid was paid another way since.
- After a payment is recorded, moved, removed or put back, the house's bills are read again where they stand. The card no longer folds to *Loading…* and an open payment window stays open.

## How it is held together

Migration `20261117010000_supply_house_payment_moves.sql` (its note is in `docs/migrations/`): the insert-only `supply_house_payment_events` and four functions, `move_supply_house_payment`, `undo_supply_house_payment_move`, `remove_supply_house_payment` and `restore_supply_house_payment`. The triggers of the ground check every line a move changes, so a move can never put more on a bill than it is for, cross houses or place more than the payment sent.

## Code

- `src/lib/materials/payments/supplyPaymentMove.ts`: where an amount can go, the plan and its What changes lines, a bill's trace, a payment's history, the payments removed lately.
- `SupplyPaymentWindow` gains the Move step (one window, two steps, so no window opens over another), the history and the reason on removal. `SupplyHouseBillsCard` gains the trace lines and the removed strip. `supplyPaymentsIo.ts` reads the events and calls the four functions.

## Tests

The bed `supabase/tests/supply_house_payments/30_moves.sql`: a move and its undo, each refusal, to and from on account, a press payment moved whole, removal with a reason, putting back, putting back refused when a bill was paid since or after 30 days, and the office-only gate under row-level security. Six deliberate breaks of the migration were each caught. Kernel and render tests for the plan, the trace, the window's Move step and the card.

## Order

**Push right after the merge**, then the types. Until the push, Move, Take it back, Remove and Put it back each say the function is not there yet; reading and recording are unaffected.
