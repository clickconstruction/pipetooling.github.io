# v2.7113 — The Bridge: each job with no percent says its hours and who worked them (punch list #334, PR 10) (2026-11-12)

PR 10 of the Bridge train (card #334, `to-dos/bridge-pulse/`), on PR 9 (v2.7112). Client only. No migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

The owner, of the list *Set a percent on the jobs without one*: "let the user know how many man hours have been spent on the job and by who and if they click on it a modal should open showing the details of it."

## What changed

- **A kernel for a job's hours** (`src/lib/bridge/jobHours.ts`, new, pure): `jobHoursByJob` turns closed clock sessions into each job's approved hours, the hours still waiting on approval kept apart, the people with the most hours first, and a row for each person on each day, newest first. `jobHoursLine` and `jobHoursCell` are the words on the job's line. `jobHoursLead` is the window's lead.
- **The read** (`jobHoursIo.ts`, new): the approved `clock_sessions` that `loadEarnedJobs` sums for a job's lifetime hours, plus the ones waiting on approval, never added to the total. Rejected and revoked sessions are left out, as there. One read for the jobs with no percent.
- **The line** (`callDive.ts`, `BridgeMoveTable.tsx`): the move's columns read Job, Hours, Contract, Worked by. The Hours cell is a press, underlined, that opens the window. A percent set on this visit says so where the people were.
- **The window** (`BridgeJobHours.tsx`, new): each person with hours, days and last day, then day by day, twelve then **Show all**. Its foot has **Open the job ›**, **Close** and **Set the percent**, which closes it and opens the app's percent window on that job.

## Not in this PR

- The same hours on the Build window's lists. They read their own.

## Tests

- `jobHours.test.ts` (6, new): the sums, waiting hours kept apart, the people and the days in order, the words, and no line of *0 hours* over real time.
- `BridgeJobHours.render.test.tsx` (3, new): the window's people and days, Show all, and Set the percent handing on.
- `callDive.test.ts` (12) and `BridgeMoveTable.render.test.tsx` (8): the Hours cell as the press, and the Worked by cell.
