# v2.5419 — Messages: a tech on the job can read the customer's thread, behind the scenes (2026-10-13)

Residential build, R5's SQL (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/RESIDENTIAL_BUILD_PLAN.md` on spike/gc-mode, R5 and its data rows). One migration, `20261013190000_messages_tech_door.sql`, with no new table. It comes with the client copy of its team and a bed step. Its record is [`docs/migrations/20261013190000_messages_tech_door.md`](../migrations/20261013190000_messages_tech_door.md). Pushed by the lead after the merge, in stamp order after R4's `20261013170000` and R0's `20261013180000`.

- **The tech's door.** `messages_tech_team()` names primary and helpers. `messages_tech_can_read(contact)` opens a customer's, a lead's or a sub's thread to a tech who is dispatched to or clocked on one of its Pipeline jobs this week or last, Monday to Sunday in Chicago. Those jobs are the ones `contact_jobs` ties to it and its customer's own. The SELECT policies `contacts_tech_read` and `messages_tech_read` read through it, and never show a line marked `office_only`. A tech writes nothing; Job Mode's text goes through `quo-send` as the service role. Billing is left to Job Mode's kernel, since no column on `messages` says it.
- **`messages.office_only`** marks a team note only the office reads. It defaults to false and is held to team lines. R5's screens add the tick.
- **`contacts.has_until`** is the end of the tech's hold on a reply (`hasRule.ts`). It is set only while someone has the reply, and `contacts_has_how_known` is unchanged.
- **`app_settings`** gains `messages_office_wait_minutes` (15) and `messages_office_hours_v1` (Monday to Friday, 7:30 to 5, the tech until 5, no emergency number). Each is changed by the owner or a dev (`is_master_or_dev()`), as the Quo seats are.
- **`notification_history.message_id`**, with its index, lets `messages-notify` read a push back by its line (`notifyRule.ts`, v2.5415).
- **The client.** `MESSAGES_TECH_TEAM` and `canMessageCustomersFromJobMode` live in `src/lib/gc/access.ts`. `access.test.ts` holds them to the function.
- **The bed.** `supabase/tests/messages_spine/30_tech_door.sql`, with 27 checks, runs after the spine's scenario, and the migration runs twice. It covers who reads (a primary dispatched today, a helper clocked last week) and who doesn't (one off the job, a revoked clock-in, a superintendent), with the office reading everything. It also covers the settings' writers, the two checks, a push's line, and anon's grants. It runs in CI, since Docker does not run on the office Mac.
- **Docs**: `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` → Messages, cut 1 gains the tech's door. `doors.ts` lists GC tables only, so nothing joins it.
