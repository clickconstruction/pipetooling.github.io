# v2.7285 — The job window on a phone: the Costs tab as rows (2026-11-14)

Punch list #355, PR G ([`to-dos/job-window-phone.md`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone.md), pass 3; the mock-up [`to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html`](../../to-dos/job-window-phone-tabs.html)). Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no kernel that is copied to the edge functions changed.

## Before

Measured at 375 px, Costs ran 4.1 screens: the four numbers read well, then the cost-against-value chart drew its axes at 10 px and its legend wrapped to three lines, *Where the money went* was a five-column table whose Share and door columns squeezed the source names to two lines, and the dev's *Every cost row on this job* panel drew every stream of the job open, a screen of 11 px rows a dev on a phone never reads.

## What changed

- **`JobCostsTabCharts.tsx`**: on a phone (`useNarrowViewport640`) the chart sits behind a 44 px fold, **Show the chart** with *cost against value earned, day by day* under it; open, it reads **Hide the chart** and draws the chart below. A desk keeps the chart open as before.
- **`JobFormPartsCostSection.tsx`**: on a phone *Where the money went* is rows (`data-money-rows="phone"`), not a table: the source and its sub-line at the left, the hours and the cost at the right in one tabular figure, the share as a 5 px bar under the row, and the door's words (*show below ›*, *shown below ▾*, *Open on Jobs →*) on a third line. A row that opens something is a 44 px `button`; the others are plain rows. The desk's table is unchanged.
- **`JobWindowCostMovePanel.tsx`**: on a phone the dev's panel is a 44 px fold, **Move cost rows…** with *every cost row by stream, tick and move · devs* under it, and nothing of the panel renders until it is pressed; so the Costs tab ends where the parts accordions end.
- `JobCostsTab.phone.render.test.tsx`: at 375 px the money table is rows with a bar and a door, a door row opens its accordion, and the dev panel is the fold until pressed.

## Is this the best we can do?

The four numbers and the baseline strip were already right at phone width, so they are untouched. The chart folds rather than redraws because a 343 px chart cannot carry two series, two axes and a legend and still read; the fold's sub-line says what is behind it so nobody wonders. The rows keep every figure the table held (hours, cost, share, door) and drop only the percent digit, which the bar carries. What is not done: the parts accordions' own tables (supply house invoices, card charges) still scroll sideways inside their card on a phone, a later pass; and the chart, once opened, is the same chart as the desk's.

## Verify

At 375 px open a job as an owner, press Costs: the four numbers first, then **Show the chart**; press it and the chart draws. *Where the money went* is rows, each with a bar; tap *Parts from tally · show below ›* and the accordion opens. As a dev, **Move cost rows…** is the last card; press it and the panel opens. At a desk the chart is open, the table has five columns and the dev panel is as it was.
