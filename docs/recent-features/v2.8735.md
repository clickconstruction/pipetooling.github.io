# v2.8735 — Lien release: only a leader, a controller or a dev releases before a check clears (punch list #437, the mend to piece 1) (2026-11-27)

The mend [`to-dos/held-check/README.md`](../../to-dos/held-check/README.md) names in its `next` line. Call 4, *Who may release before the bank clears it?*, was answered on 2026-11-25 in the user's own words: *"A leader, a controller, or a dev"*. Client only, no migration. It owes nothing on prod.

## Why

Piece 1 (v2.8525) made the Release of record tab hold its print and its save while the paying check had not cleared, and let a typed reason under **Release it anyway…** lift the hold. Anyone who could open the tab could type that reason. The answer holds it to three roles.

## What landed

- **One list of who may act before a check clears** (`EARLY_ON_CHECK_ROLES` and `canActBeforeCheckClears` in `checkCleared.ts`): dev, master_technician (a leader) and controller. The controller is named by itself, and the assistant is not on it. `canConfirmFunds` (Payoffs' **The bank confirmed the funds ›**, v2.8632) now reads the same list, since the owner's second answer that day was *the same three* for every early press on a held check.
- **The gate knows who is asking** (`releaseMoneyGate` in `lienReleaseOfRecord.ts` takes `mayReleaseEarly` and answers `anyway`). On a check that has not cleared, a reason lifts the hold for one of the three and for no one else. That holds while the bank is being asked and when its record could not be read too.
- **The tab** (`LienFilingTabs.tsx`) offers **Release it anyway…** on a check to the three alone. Anyone else reads *A leader, a controller or a dev can release it before the check clears.* in the press's place, with no reason box, and the three buttons stay dead. The amber line still says the day the check clears.
- **A reason typed while money was open does not carry over.** If the bills then read paid by a check that has not cleared, the reason is not taken for the check unless the person is one of the three.
- **Money still open is unchanged.** *Release it anyway…* on a lien settled for less is still whoever opens the tab's, with its reason. Call 4 asked about the bank, and nobody has asked about that one.
- Guide: *release a filed lien after it is paid* → Release a lien before its check clears.

## The server

**The screen alone holds this.** A release is an insert on `job_lien_filings` under that table's office policy, which takes the office (`is_office_staff`: dev, master_technician, assistant, controller) and a job's own master, as the repo's migrations have it (`job_lien_filings_insert_office`, last written in `20260906200000_one_company_policy_sweep.sql`). The live database's policies were not read for this PR. The policy reads no bank row, and the reason is a key in the row's `fields`. So nothing on the server refuses an assistant's early release sent another way, and the same is true of **The bank confirmed the funds**, a `fields` update on the affidavit row. Nothing was built on the server here. Whether the database should hold either is a question for the database lane.

## What it does not do

- Payoffs and the check pane are untouched. Payoffs offers no release press while a check waits, and the pane does not record a check the bank has not cleared, for anyone.
- No release already saved is changed or reread.

## Proved, and what is not

- `lienReleaseOfRecord.test.ts` (2 new): a reason lifts nothing for someone outside the three, in every state of the bank's read, and money still open is as it was.
- `checkCleared.test.ts` (1 new): the three roles, and nine others refused. `lienPayoffCard.test.ts` still names the same three for the funds press.
- `LienFilingTabs.release.render.test.tsx` (4 in place of 1): an assistant gets no press and reads who can; a leader, a controller and a dev each type a reason, and the release saves it as `releasedBeforeClearedReason`.
- **Nothing was written on prod and no live site was opened.** Every job, check and name in the tests is made up.
- Not seen: the tab on a real lien whose check is at the bank.
