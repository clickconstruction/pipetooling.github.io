# v2.8728 — The Team queue says when the office texted a card holder again (punch list #299) (2026-11-27)

The last small piece of [`to-dos/ticket-text/README.md`](../../to-dos/ticket-text/README.md): the card's *Left* list after Text again (v2.8697) named the Team queue line's *texted again*. Client only. It owes nothing on prod.

## Why

Since v2.8697 the office can press **Text again** on a charge texted twice with no receipt. The row then leaves Needs you for twelve hours. The charge's line on the Team queue still read *Reminded Tue 6:14 PM. No receipt yet.*, so a second person in the office could not tell a third text had gone.

## What landed

- **The line says it** (`src/lib/tickets/receiptAgainLine.ts`, `receiptLineWithAgain`): *Reminded Tue 6:14 PM. The office texted again Wed 9:02 AM. No receipt yet.* Two presses read *The office texted again twice, last Thu 9:40 PM.* The words are the Needs you row's own. The time is on the company's clock, the time alone for today, as the line's other times read.
- **Only a waiting line gains the sentence.** A charge whose receipt came, a closed request and a charge that could not be texted keep the line they had.
- **The read asks for two more columns** (`listReceiptRequestsByCharge`): `again_at` and `again_count`, from v2.8697's migration, which is pushed. A read refused with them is asked once more without them, so a database that cannot hand them back still draws every line it drew before.
- **The receipt chip's title** on Banking's ledger, User Sort and Job Summary's card-charge drill-down says the same, since it reads the same line (`receiptState.ts`).
- The chip on the Team queue is unchanged: it still says when the first text went.
- Guide: *sort the team's card purchases from the office* → Text the holder again, and the line about the chip.

## Why the sentence is not in the shared kernel

`receiptLineWords` lives in `supabase/functions/_shared/receiptText.ts`, which the `receipt-request` function imports. A change there would put that function behind its source and owe a deploy for words the function never says. The sentence sits beside the kernel instead, in the app alone. `git diff origin/main --stat -- supabase/` is empty.

## Proved, and what is not

- `receiptAgainLine.test.ts` (7): no sentence before a press, one press today and on another day, more presses, the line with the sentence, the lines that keep their own, the plain-words rule.
- `receiptTextsIo.test.ts` (1 new, 1 amended): the two columns asked for, and the second ask without them when the first is refused.
- `receiptState.test.ts`: the chip's title. `TallyTeamQueue.render.test.tsx` (1 new): the line on the queue.
- **Nothing was written on prod, nothing was sent and no live site was opened.** Every row in the tests is made up.
- Not seen: a real line. No charge on prod has been texted, since the receipt texts switch has never been on. The first real line follows the owner's first press of the switch and the office's first Text again.
- The line is plain words, as the line it joins is. It is not redrawn with values in place.
