# v2.6814 — The lien papers you print claim the same amount the Lien desk shows (2026-11-09)

Follow-up to [v2.6809](v2.6809.md), which made the Lien window fill the § 53.054 affidavit by the desk's own recipe and listed two differences it left. This closes both. Client only.

## 1. The sworn amount (paragraphs 8 and 10)

**What differed.** The desk's affidavit swore what the job's sent bills still owe (the RPC's `open_balance`) less a claim set by hand (`job_lien_claim_corrections`). The window's Mechanic's lien tab swore the job's price less its payments and never read the correction. So did the window's § 53.056 notice, its recorded filing amounts and its notice by hand.

**Which figure is right was already decided.** [v2.4969](v2.4969.md) records the owner's rule of 2026-10-08, *"I can't lien work I have not done yet"*: the lien money is what the sent bills still owe, and it names the affidavit's sworn unpaid amount among the readers that moved. [v2.4970](v2.4970.md) moved the readers that take the job from the client through `lienBilledOpen`, "the money every lien reader counts". The window's tab was on neither list. It was a missed reader, not a second opinion, so nothing here was put to the owner again.

**Can the window reach the desk's figure for one job?** Yes. The desk's `open_balance` is `lien_billed_open()` in SQL; `lienBilledOpen` is its client twin, held to it by the SQL bed (`npm run test:pg:lien-claim`). The window already reads the full job with its bills and payments.

**What changed**

- **`lienWindowClaim(job, correction)`** (`src/lib/jobs/lienWindowAffidavit.ts`, pure): what the sent bills still owe, the corrected claim over it (`correctedClaim`, the desk's own arithmetic), the part of the job not billed yet and the count of sent bills. **`lienWindowClaimNotes`** words the lines under the amount: a claim set by hand with who, when and why (red when it claims more than the bills owe), the part not billed yet, the desk's own sentence when nothing is billed, and a red line when the correction could not be read.
- **`readLienClaimCorrection(jobId)`** (`lienClaimCorrectionIo.ts`): one job's correction, throwing on a failed read. `LienInstrumentsModal` reads it on each open and holds the filing tabs behind *Reading the claim on the job…* until it lands, so a sworn amount is never drawn and then changed. A failed read does not hide: the papers carry the bills' figure and the tab says to check the job on the Lien desk before printing.
- **`LienFilingTabs`** takes `claimCorrection` and `claimCorrectionUnread` and reads one claim everywhere: the notice's claim amount and its *Of which, by month* split (as the desk's notice prints it), the affidavit, the amount stored with a recorded notice and a recorded filing (the filing stores the figure the paper swears, to the cent), the phone sheet's headline and the notice by hand. The window's job row takes its money (status, price, payments, bills, payment rows) from the full read once it lands.
- **A third draw had drifted the same way.** A timeline stop's window on the desk (`affidavitStopPages`, `lienStopPaperPages.ts`) never passed `openBalance`, so its page swore price less payments beside a pane that swore the bills. The desk's three draws (the Affidavits pane, the paper preview behind a Do now row, the stop's window) now call one recipe, **`buildLienDeskAffidavitFields`** (`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskAffidavitFields.ts`, pure).

**What a reader will see change.** On a job billed in stages the window claims the sent bills, with an amber line naming what is not billed yet. On a job with nothing billed the window's claim is $0.00, as the desk's already was, with the desk's sentence to bill the work first. `contractAmount` and `paidAmount` in paragraph 8 stay the job's facts, as v2.4969 left them.

## 2. A job contracted with the owner (paragraph 7)

**What differed.** With no GC on the job the desk's draft printed a blank for the original contractor and listed it as a blank to fill (*Pick the GC*); the window named our own company, without an address.

**Which reading the docs support.** The window's. The guides say it in so many words: *No GC means we contracted with the owner* and *We are the original contractor*. The statute agrees: an original contractor is a person contracting with an owner directly or through the owner's agent (§ 53.001(7)), and the affidavit gives the original contractor's name and last known address (§ 53.054(a)(5)); both read on texas.public.law on 2026-11-09. Picking a GC to fill the blank would have turned the job into a sub's job that owes monthly notices.

**What changed**

- **`lienOriginalContractor({ isSub, gcName, gcAddress, issuer })`** (`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeDraft.ts`, pure), called inside `buildLienAffidavitFieldsForJob`, so every draw gets the one rule whatever it was handed. Under a GC: the GC's row, and a GC with no name on file stays a blank to fill. With no GC: our own company at its own address, the claimant the paper already names in paragraph 1.
- **The desk's paper** names our company and address on such a job, and `LienDesk`'s `paperFactsFor` hands `lienPaperGaps` that name for an affidavit, so the blank and its *Pick the GC* button are gone there. A notice row always has a GC and is unchanged.
- **The window's paper** gains the address after the name.

The statutory wording of the affidavit is untouched (`buildLienAffidavitBlocks`); only the values that fill it changed.
