# v2.7584 — Tasks: an assistant can give a task the way the New task window does (2026-11-17)

The owner, 2026-11-17: "Do you have dev-mcp verbs to assign tasks and set locations and attach photos? if not help me add them". None did. This is the first of three PRs on `dev-mcp` (server 0.95.0, 115 write verbs): **`plan_task`** → **`apply_task`**, and the reads **`find_task`** and **`get_task`**. The place verb and the photo verb follow.

## One set of rules

The New task window's Send lived inside `ChecklistAddModal.tsx`: its five refusals, the `checklist_items` row, the people's rows and the days to make were written there and nowhere else. A second copy in the edge function would drift.

- **`src/lib/tasks/newTaskSend.ts`** (new) holds them: `newTaskSend(draft, me, clock)` answers the window's refusal in the window's words, or the row, the schedule and the days. `nextDisplayOrders` and `newTaskAssigneeRows` are the people's rows.
- **The window** calls it. Its insert is `send.row`, its days `send.instanceDates`. No behaviour changes: the 38 render tests of the window pass untouched.
- **`utils/checklistOrder.ts`** takes its max-plus-one from the same kernel.
- **The seat** reads the kernel through the kernel copy (`scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`, five new entries: the send, the Done when… lines, the photo switch's default, the spot photo's name, the roster's predicate).

## plan_task → apply_task

- A task is who it is for, the words, today or a day or a repeat, a due day, the Done when… lines, the photo switch, links, a reminder, who is told when it is done, and a place as a named point.
- **Who it may be for** is who the window offers: active, human, with a login. Anyone else is refused in words.
- **The plan** writes nothing. It shows the sentence the task reads as, the row, the days, the lines, the place with a map link, and who is told. The place's stamp is left out of the plan, so no clock is in the hash.
- **The apply** writes the window's rows in the window's order, as the dev under RLS: the task, the people, the day rows with their people, the lines. Each step's outcome is named. If the people's rows fail the task is taken back.
- A task already given with the same words for the same day to one of the people is refused unless the call says `allow_duplicate`.
- **It tells nobody.** The window sends no push when a task is given, so the seat sends none. A reminder goes at its own time.
- No RPC and no migration.

## find_task and get_task

Reads, as whoever asks, under RLS. `find_task` lists tasks by their words, their person, or both. `get_task` reads one whole: the task as written, its lines, its place, whether it carries a photo of the spot, its newest days, their thread and their photos.
