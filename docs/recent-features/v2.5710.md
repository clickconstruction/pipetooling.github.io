## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Fewer presses on a phone**: dial from the line above the box, then write and save in the same place the text would go; before, a note plus a hand-written "they said Thursday" nowhere the app could read.
2. **Plain words**: every sentence of `CALL_WORDS` and the aside runs through `plainWordsFailures`; the guide section passes the sweep.
3. **Do what is ready**: the call saves on its own; Our move's write is a second press inside the same save, and its refusal keeps the call and says so in the toast.
4. **Reuse**: the day picker, the after-this radios and the two blanks are the note's and the send window's; the write is Follow up's io and guard; the chip's facts come from the read Follow up already makes.
5. **Cut**: no duration (nobody would type it). **Not carried here**: Waiting on them holding a row until the day they gave — `waitingOnThem.ts` and its io belong to the #139 stack until it merges, so that hold lands with the page PRs (PR 4), reading the same `said` promises. The Messages map has no list of Follow up's presses to extend; `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` carries it.
