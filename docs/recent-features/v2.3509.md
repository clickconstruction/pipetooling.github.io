# v2.3509 — What customers see: the paper, built from the sample by the app's own builders (2026-09-16)

PRs 3 and 5 of the train in `to-dos/what-customers-see-journeys/README.md`, folded into one because they share one mechanism. Client only; no migration, no edge function.

## What shipped

- **A `paper` render kind** in [`customerJourneys.ts`](../../src/lib/customerJourneys.ts): five steps that were *Next release* cards now render — **Bill by email**, **Hazmat notice**, **Demand letter**, **Lien release** (homeowner) and **Notice to the owner of record** (GC). The **Terms page** renders as the live public page (`/estimate/terms`; it reads Settings, so the sample is the real page).
- **[`src/lib/journeys/paperSamples.ts`](../../src/lib/journeys/paperSamples.ts)** (+6 tests): for each paper, the builder's input over the sample fixture (Sam Sample's job 1042, a $1,850 water heater; Sample Contracting on Cedar Bend for the owner notice, with a new **Sample Owner LLC**), an **HTML preview** from the same builder the real send uses — `buildPhysicalInvoiceEmailBodies`, `buildHazmatFeeNoticeHtml`, `buildDemandLetterPrintHtml`, `filingDocPrintHtml(buildLienNoticeBlocks(…))`, the lien waiver's paragraphs — and a lazy **`pdf()`** through `buildPhysicalInvoicePdfBlob`, `buildHazmatFeeNoticePdfBlob`, `buildDemandLetterPdfBlob`, `filingDocPdfBlob`, `buildLienWaiverPdfBlob`. jsPDF and pdf-lib load only when the button is pressed.
- **The tab** frames the preview as `srcDoc` (thumb and expanded), shows the email subject where the paper travels as an email, and adds **Open the PDF ↗** — the test-report card's door: build the blob, open it in a new tab. A builder that throws leaves the card readable with a one-line note rather than breaking the tab.
- The count line gains *N open as the PDF*.

**Not changed:** the sample is fixture-only; nothing reads the database; the demand letter's statement and exhibits are omitted (the letter alone is the surface), and the § 53.056 notice renders the form without the cover letter, which is the run's own paper.

## Verified

Typecheck, lint, build; paper (6), journeys, registry, help and release-note tests. Live: each of the five cards opens to its preview at phone and desktop width; the bill's subject reads *Invoice 1042*; Open the PDF builds and opens each document.
