# v2.8384 — Messages: say what a day they named is, in one press (punch list #421, PR 3 of 5) (2026-11-23)

Punch list #421 (`to-dos/messages-heard-day.md`), the screen, on the owner's calls of 2026-11-23 (*build it*, *straight to the choice*). It calls `write_down_heard` from PR 2 (v2.8383, `20261123185942`), so it waits on that push. No migration, no edge function, nothing in the kernels copy.

## What changed

- **The choice under a line** (`useFollowUpThreadSlots`' `underLine`, over `messages/heardChoice.ts`): under a customer's, a trade partner's or an architect's own text or voicemail that names a day still ahead, *⟪Wed Nov 25⟫ is a promise of*, a press for each of their kinds (`promiseChoices`), and *just talk* with a dashed edge. A voicemail adds **Another day**, which opens Follow up's own `DayPicker` under the line, and the day picked stands in for the day read.
- **One press writes it** (`writeDownHeard` on the thread io, `write_down_heard`): a double kind writes its first (`defaultWrites`: a start, insurance). The toast says what and by when (*Written down. Their quote by Wed Nov 25.*), the window reads the thread again, and the move chip reads *Their move · until Wed Nov 25 · quote* once our word is the newest. A refusal says the database's own words.
- **A customer's *A payment*** opens #129's sheet through the same road as **Write down their word** (`wordPress`, `wordArea`, split out of the slot), on the day shown, the picked day when there is one. A customer's line whose day is written as a payment, or called just talk, keeps the old look: *They said Fri Nov 27.* and **Write down their word**, which changes or voids the payment.
- **A line already said** (`heard` on the thread read: every day row on their lines, any state and day, plans questions left out, a soft read) draws *Written down as their W-9 by Wed Nov 25.* in place of the choice, its values in place. Just talk draws nothing.
- **An io without the write** draws the old look, so a stand-in or an older io never offers a press it cannot make.
- **Tests**: `heardChoice.test.ts` (the toast for each kind, the default of a double kind, the access sentence); `followUpThreadIo.test.ts` (the call with a kind, with a double kind's choice, as just talk, and a refusal's words); `useFollowUpThreadSlots.render.test.tsx` (a trade partner's presses and hover, a press that writes and reads again, a start and just talk, an architect's voicemail with Another day, a line already said and one called just talk, an offered day, a refusal, a customer's A payment opening the sheet on the day, and a customer's just talk keeping Write down their word).
- **Docs**: the guides *reply to a person from their thread* (a new section, *Say what a day they named is*) and *see who is waiting on us and what we owe them*; `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md`'s slots; the Glossary; the card.
