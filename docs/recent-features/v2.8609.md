# v2.8609 — The bids of the day: the app notes the bid you are at work on (2026-11-25)

Punch list #445, `to-dos/clock-out-bids-of-the-day/`, the second PR of the build. Client only. No migration, no edge function. It calls `bump_user_bid_activity`, which `20261125223610_bid_day_ground.sql` made (v2.8605, pushed before this PR was opened).

**The hole it closes.** The bid history ledger says which bids a person changed. Nothing said which bid a person had open and read with nothing changed. On four of one estimator's clocked days the Bids page was in front for hours and no bid changed.

**What it does.** `useBidActivityBeat` (`src/hooks/`), mounted in `Layout.tsx` beside the app activity heartbeat:

- **The bid in front** is read from the address, `/bids?tab=pricing&bidId=…` (`bidInFrontFromLocation`, `src/lib/bids/bidInFront.ts`). No screen reports anything.
- **At work** is a press, a key or a scroll in the last five minutes. The app activity heartbeat counts a visible tab whoever is at it, which is why its total can pass the clocked day. This one stops.
- Every 60 seconds one beat goes out with the seconds that counted, at most 60. Leaving the bid, hiding the tab or closing the page sends the part of a minute. A glance under five seconds sends nothing.
- Nothing is sent while View as is on, and the function writes nothing for training mode or a twin.

**What it costs.** One small request a minute for a person with a bid open and at work on it. About a hundred small rows a person a day.

**Who reads it.** The person, the owner, a dev and the Activity tab's viewers. The owner said "Yes, push it" to that rule on 2026-11-25, and `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` holds the answer.

**Not yet.** The Bid Board has no bid in its address, so time on the board names no bid. The list in the Clock out sheet is the next PR.

**Tests.** `bidInFront.test.ts` (10) and `useBidActivityBeat.render.test.tsx` (7): a minute for a minute, the idle stop and the start again, the part of a minute on a change of bid, a glance, a hidden tab, nobody signed in and View as.
