# v2.5739 — Messages: the walk, in one command (2026-10-22)

Punch list #142 (the walk kit) PR 8, the last of the train: the driver, the report and the docs. `npm run walk` runs the Messages loop on prod end to end, on the company's own inbox and number, prints what landed where with times, and cleans up (`docs/WALK_KIT.md`).

- **The driver** (`scripts/walk/run.ts`, vite-node): reads the three `VITE_` values from the main checkout's `.env.local` and prints none of them; `walk setup`, the tester's sign-in through `dev-login` and `verifyOtp` in memory, the estimate email and the estimate text through the app's own doors with the tester's JWT, `walk open` until the email arrives and the 👀 line lands, `walk reply-email` and the email line in, `walk text` and the text line in with the estimate text's ✓ delivered, STOP and START on the kit's number, an optional answer from the thread (`--answer`), the office's view through the screens' own kernels (`followUpQueue`, `waitingOnThem`, `wentOutRows`, `stripTiles` twice as the kit's difference), the stray check, the report, and `walk cleanup` in a `finally`. A step that holds does not stop the ones that do not need it. The flags: `--keep`, `--owner-presses` (BUILD_PLAN § 4), `--fallback-phone`, `--answer`, `--cleanup-only`.
- **The pure half** (`src/lib/walk/walkRun.ts`): the flags, the fourteen steps and their limits, `waitUntil` (every 15 seconds, ending on its limit with what it saw), the estimate text's words from the sheet's line, what each wait looks for on the thread (`openedLine`, `inboundLine`, `deliveredText`, `stoppedForTeam`), `stripDifference`, `strayRows` over `ownKeys`, the report's rows and lines, and `secretsOut`, which replaces every secret and every accept link by its last four characters before anything is printed or written.
- **Tests**: `walkRun.test.ts` (16) for the kernel; `walkDriver.run.test.ts` (6) runs `runWalk` against a fake io: the sequence of calls, a wait that ends on its limit and the steps it holds, `--keep` and `--cleanup-only`, `--answer`, `--fallback-phone`, a refused setup, and nothing printed carrying a secret.
- **Docs**: `docs/WALK_KIT.md` (new), its row in `docs/README.md`, `MESSAGES_ARCHITECTURE.md` § Testing it, `to-dos/messages-desk/BUILD_PLAN.md` § 0.5 and § 3 (Route B is the fallback now), the Glossary's *Walk kit*. The reports go to `tmp/walk/` (ignored).
- **The first run** is the parent session's, after the `walk` function and the doors are deployed and the two records that carry bids@ are cleared (`WALK_KIT.md` § Before the first walk).
