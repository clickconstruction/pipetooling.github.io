# v2.8494 — Takeoffs: the lines keep their roomy look after a window is dragged narrow and back (2026-11-24)

A fault found on the live look at v2.8480 and v2.8483, the same day. No migration, no edge function.

## What was wrong

`TakeoffLinesBox` (v2.8476) measures itself and draws the line table roomy, snug or scrolling. A width cannot know the rows, so after each draw the box also checks whether the table overflowed anyway, and steps one tighter for that width.

It wrote that against its last *measured* width. A window resize can redraw the page before the box has measured itself again: the layout is then the new size and the measured width the old one. Narrowing a 1,400 px window to 900 drew the table once in a 386 px box while the box still believed it was 884, so two overflows were written against 884. When the window came back to 1,400 the box found that record and drew `scroll`, though the table fit, 884 in 884.

Seen on the live site on a ZZ test bid, read only: after 1,400 to 900 to 1,400 the box read `scroll` at 1,400. What a person saw was the tight look in a wide window: the side padding drawn in and no Actions heading. Every press still worked.

## Measured on the harness

The harness of v2.8476, made to redraw on a window resize as the live page does.

| The window goes | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 1,400 at first | roomy, 884 in 884 | roomy |
| to 900 | snug, 720 in 720 | snug |
| back to 1,400 | **scroll**, 884 in 884, Actions heading 0 px | roomy, heading 16 px |
| to 760 and back | **scroll** | roomy |
| to 1,100 and back | **scroll** | roomy |
| to 390 and back | **scroll** | roomy |

Without the redraw on resize the harness never showed it, which is why v2.8476's own resize walk did not. The reach test of v2.8476 reads the same after this change: 164 of 164 presses in the Sheet and 41 of 41 in One at a time at 1,400, 900, 760 and 390 px.

## What changed

- The box judges an overflow only while its measured width is the width it has (`getBoundingClientRect` against the measured width, both rounded).
- It keeps no record from one width to the next: at a new width a record made at another is dropped.
- The strip of v2.8480 said *Costed 0 of 0* in amber on an empty bid while the rail said it in green. `railStripFacts` now uses the rail's rule: no fixture without lines reads green.

## What it leaves

- A box that stepped tighter for real at some width stays tighter there until its width changes, even if rows are then removed.

## Tests

`TakeoffNarrowFit.render.test.tsx` gains a smoke that redraws the box at a width it has not measured, with the table overflowing, and then brings it back: it stays roomy. The smoke fails on v2.8476's box (*expected 'scroll' to be 'roomy'*). `takeoffRailFold.test.ts` holds the empty bid's green.
