# v2.3267 — Robot price matrix, PR 2 of 5: the door — Price it with the robot (2026-09-10)

Client only; the table it writes (`bid_price_matrix_requests`) shipped in v2.3263's migration `20260911023538` — **push that before this deploys**, or the door renders disabled with a plain notice until it lands. Plan: [`docs/PRICE_MATRIX_PLAN.md`](../PRICE_MATRIX_PLAN.md); canvas boards 1, 2, 7 and 8.

## Why

Wendi's ask was a button. This PR is the button, and everything it needs to be honest before any robot exists: what gets queued, what the estimator sees while she waits, and where a dev pulls the prompt to run it. Nothing here reads a PDF or writes a quote — that is PR 3 (the pricing twin and its verbs).

## What shipped

- **The door.** Bids → Pricing → the Supply house prices ▾ menu → **Price it with the robot** ([`PricingShareMenu`](../../src/components/bids/PricingShareMenu.tsx), a third child under the fixtures item, cost-side roles only). Disabled with a title when the bid has no counted rows or the table is not in the schema yet.
- **The sheet** ([`PriceWithRobotModal`](../../src/components/bids/PriceWithRobotModal.tsx)). Two faces. **Queue:** every `quote_url` the estimator pasted on the bid's Price-requests table, ticked and resolvable to a house name; houses asked with nothing in the folder listed as *skipped — the robot never invents a quote*; the four plain sentences of what it will and won't do; **Queue it for the robot** writes one `bid_price_matrix_requests` row — the counted fixture rows as `scope`, the ticked links as `sources`, `status = 'queued'`, `requested_by` — and nothing else. **Status:** who asked and when, what it is reading, the result summary once ready, **Take it back** only while still `queued` (flips to `cancelled` with a status guard), **Review the matrix** once ready.
- **The chip** ([`derivePricingChip`](../../src/lib/rfq/priceMatrixRequest.ts)). The one Price-requests chip folds the robot in: an open request wins — *Robot pricing · queued* / *reading N quotes* (blue), *blocked* (amber), *Matrix ready · 23 picks, 4 to settle* (green) — and once the estimator opens the ready matrix the request is stamped `reviewed_at` and the RFQ chip is back. Tapping a blue or amber chip opens the status face; tapping green opens the compare.
- **The dev's view.** Robots → Queue gains a **Price matrices** section (violet, between Requested and Ready): every open request across bids with its status, who asked, age, link count and row count, **Copy pricer prompt** ([`buildPricerRequestPrompt`](../../src/lib/rfq/priceMatrixRequest.ts) — the pricing kickoff pinned to one request) and Open bid. Robots → Console's *Bids to run* card gains a **Price matrices · n** chip. The Console's third "Pricing robot" column with the full kickoff arrives with the twin in PR 3.
- **Kernel** `priceMatrixRequest.ts` (10 tests): scope snapshot, readable-vs-waiting sources, the active request, take-back eligibility, result summary, chip derivation, age words, the prompt. Hook [`usePriceMatrixRequests`](../../src/hooks/usePriceMatrixRequests.ts) reads the untyped table (types regenerate after the push) and reports `supported = false` when PostgREST does not know it yet.
- **Render smokes** for the sheet: queue face writes the row with scope + sources; the not-yet-switched-on notice disables Queue it; status face Take it back writes `cancelled`, ready face offers the matrix.
- **Help:** [`get-supply-house-prices-on-a-bid.md`](../../src/content/help/get-supply-house-prices-on-a-bid.md) gains **Let the robot price it** (four steps, the reading rules, the SpaceX example).
