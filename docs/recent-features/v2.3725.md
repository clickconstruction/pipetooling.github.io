# v2.3725 — Payments view: bands by week paid or by person (2026-09-22)

PR 2 of `to-dos/pay-run-payments-view` (#23) — the last item; the folder retires with this PR (the mock-up lives in git history at the deleting commit). Builds on [v2.3717](v2.3717.md) (the method) and [v2.3577](v2.3577.md) (the view). Client only, no migration.

## What shipped

**Flat · By week paid · By person**, beside the window chips (the mock-up's placement), remembered per device with the window and the sort.

- **By week paid** — one band per **company week** the money went out: Sunday-start, America/Chicago, the same week the ledger's pay periods use, so a band reads `9/13–19 w38` exactly like a period and lines up with the Period column. The mock-up drew Monday weeks (*Week of 9/14*); asked whether that was the best we could do, it was not — the office thinks in the pay week. Each band carries the week's count, sum and how many people.
- **By person** — one band per person (name matched case- and space-blind, shown as the rows spell it), with their count, sum and the number of weeks they were paid in.
- Inside a band the rows keep the table's sort. The bands follow that sort's direction only when it is theirs: weeks newest first unless *Paid on* is ascending; people A → Z unless *Person* is descending. Any other sort (say Amount) orders within the band and leaves the bands in their natural order.
- The band row is the ledger's (v2.3318): tinted, the label in small caps with the week number in monospace, the numbers muted after it. The total row is still over what is showing.

## Kernel — [`payRunPaymentBands.ts`](../../src/lib/people/payRunPaymentBands.ts)

`buildPayRunPaymentBands(rows, mode, sort, dates)` takes the rows already filtered and sorted and the three date readers the view owns (`dayOf` in the app time zone, `weekStartOf` = `companyWeekStartSundayContaining`, `addDays`), so the grouping is pure and tested without a time zone; `payRunPaymentBandLine` words the numbers. Five tests over the mock-up's eight rows: the three weeks and their sums, the Paid-on-ascending flip, an Amount sort kept inside the band, the person bands and their weeks, the name key.

## View — [`PayRunPaymentsView.tsx`](../../src/components/people/PayRunPaymentsView.tsx)

The row render lifted into `renderRow`; `renderBand` draws the ledger's band cell over eight columns; the body is the flat list or `bands.flatMap([band, ...rows])`. `mode` joins the per-device prefs (fail-soft, validated on read).
