# v2.6026 — Settings: push me only at these hours (2026-10-28)

Punch list #141 PR 2 of 5 (`to-dos/quiet-hours/README.md` → The plan → PR 2, decision 2 D; the owner's yes of 2026-10-21). The second of the owner's two asks: *long term, I would like for users to be able to set when they receive notifications and when they don't*. Each person now says, in the Push Notifications card they already have under Settings → Your account, the days and hours their phone is pushed, in the Office hours card's own shape said the right way round. The block saves and reads back; **nothing holds a push until PR 3**, and the block's last line says so. One migration, no edge function.

- **The row** (migration `20261028200000_user_push_hours.sql`, [`docs/migrations/20261028200000_user_push_hours.md`](../migrations/20261028200000_user_push_hours.md)): `user_push_hours`, one a person: `days` (0 Sunday to 6 Saturday, at least one), `opens` and `closes` (`"HH:MM"` Chicago, to after from), `updated_at`. No row is any time. The person reads and writes their own row; the owner and a dev read every row (PR 4's coverage line); the service role reads for the senders (PR 3 and PR 5). The three fence appliers run at the end.
- **The kernel** ([`pushHours.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/pushHours.ts), pure): `parsePushHours` reads a row as `hasRule.ts` reads hours, the switch off, and reads no row or a row it cannot read as any time, so a bad row never holds a push; `pushedNow` and `backAt` are `officeOpen` and `nextOpening`, the kernel the office's hours use and already on the edge; `pushHoursLine` (*Pushed Mon to Fri, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM.* or *Pushed any time.*); the draft (`pushDraftFrom`, `samePushDraft`, `pushDraftProblem` with the block's words over the office card's rule, `pushHoursOf`, `pushRowOf`); `PUSH_HOURS_DEFAULT`, every day 6 AM to 8 PM, so weekends off is two presses.
- **The read and the writes** ([`pushHoursIo.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/pushHoursIo.ts)): the own row read fail-soft (no row, or no table yet, reads as any time), a save that upserts, and *Any time* that deletes. The client is untyped on purpose: the table joins `types/database.ts` with the regenerate after the push, as `tell_customer_prefs` did (v2.5597).
- **The block** ([`PushHoursBlock.tsx`](../../src/components/settings/PushHoursBlock.tsx), `#settings-push-hours`), at the foot of the Push Notifications card for everyone who signs in: two rows in My way's shape, **Push me any time** and **Push me only at these hours**, a press saving at once (any time removes the row; the hours save the kept hours, else the default window); under the second the Office hours card's day toggles and **From** and **To**, the read-back line, the line that says what the hours do, **Save my hours** lit only when the draft changed and is good, and **Put them back**. The block is self-contained (`useAuth` for the person, `io` for a fake), so the tab's props do not grow.
- **The door**: *Push me* in the settings search and the go-anywhere box (`settingsSearch.ts`, anchor `settings-push-hours`).
- **Tests**: `pushHours.test.ts` (the row read back and refused, each day on and off, the ends of the window, the next opening across the DST change, the words plain, the draft), `pushHoursIo.test.ts` (the read, the fail-soft read, the upsert on the person's key with padded times, the delete, a refused write), `PushHoursBlock.render.test.tsx` (the hours picked and read back, a day press and the save, no row and the default saved at once, any time, To before From and Put them back).

## Deploy

Merge; CI deploys the client, which reads the missing table as any time. Then `supabase db push` from the main checkout (dry run first) and regenerate the types (`npm run gen-types:linked`, a chore PR). No function to deploy: PR 3 is the first reader on the edge.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Reuse**: the day toggles, the two times, the problem rule and the read-back words are the Office hours card's; the check is `officeOpen`, already mirrored; the kernel adds a reader for one row and the draft.
2. **Fewer presses, nowhere else to look**: the same card the person uses to turn pushes on and send a test; one press on *Only at these hours* saves a window at once, and weekends off is two more.
3. **Said the right way round**: the hours you are pushed, so a day off reads as off and the window never crosses midnight; a night shift is a later ask (*Not carried* on the card).
4. **Honest**: the last line says nothing holds a push until the next update, and the row reads as any time wherever it cannot be read.

## Verify

As any signed-in person on Settings → Your account → Push Notifications: pick **Push me only at these hours**, see the days and times appear and the line read *Pushed Every day, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM.*; press Sat and Sun off, see *Pushed Mon to Fri, …* and **Save my hours** light, press it, read the row back (`read_rows user_push_hours`, your `user_id`). Set To before From and see *To must come after From.* with Save dead. Press **Push me any time** and the row is gone. Type *push me* in the go-anywhere box and the door opens the card.
