# v2.4515 — Overhead: open a Man hours number to see who made it up (2026-10-04)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. PR 3 of 3 on the Man hours card (v2.4513 the table, v2.4514 the picture). A total nobody can open is hard to believe, and the two soft spots in a period (hours waiting for approval, hours on no job) had no way out of the card.

## What changed

- **One period is always picked.** It starts as the newest finished period, the one the headline reads. A click on a table row or on a bar (either chart) picks another; Enter and Space work on both. The picked row and column are marked. Changing the zoom starts the pick over.
- **Who made it up**, [`ManHoursWho.tsx`](../../src/components/people/ManHoursWho.tsx), under the table: one row per person with Field, Office, Bids, Not on a job and Total, most hours first, with *N h waiting* beside a person whose time is not approved yet. The rows come from the same entries as the period, so they add up to its row ([`manHoursWho.ts`](../../src/lib/manHours/manHoursWho.ts), tested against the period). Time on no job is shown on the person it belongs to, which the mock-up's single *Not on a job* line did not do.
- **Three doors** under the list, each shown only when it applies:
  - *Show these days in the table below* (Week zoom): the tab's own `showOverheadWeekOf` moves the day table to that week, and the page scrolls to its toolbar (`#overhead-day-table`).
  - *Approve waiting hours · N h*: `/people?tab=hours&approvals=1`, the existing door into the Hours approvals queue.
  - *Match hours to a job · N h*: `/people?tab=hours&match=1`, a new flag on People that opens Match sessions on arrival and strips itself, the same shape as `approvals=1`. Gated like the button it stands in for (`canAccessHours`).
- **Names.** The session read now embeds `users(name)` so the list has names without a second request.

## Tests

`manHoursWho.test.ts` (4): names off the sessions, the rows and their order, the sums against the period's own row, an empty period. `ManHoursCard.render.test.tsx` (+2): the default list, a clicked row, the two links and their targets, a bar click, and *Show these days* calling back with the week's Sunday. `ManHoursChart.render.test.tsx` (+1): a click and Enter pick a column and the picked one is pressed. Seen in the dev preview on prod data: the week of Sep 27 lists 11 people summing to its 348 hours; the day table moved to Sep 27 – Oct 3; `?tab=hours&match=1` opened Match sessions and left `?tab=hours`.
