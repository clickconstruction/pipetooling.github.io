# v2.8654 — The bid window on a phone: the bid's name gets the top line (2026-11-26)

Punch list #426, finding 262 of the simulator thread's sitting 6 (2026-11-26), briefed by the fixing thread as `426-bid-window-head-squeezes-the-name-on-a-phone.md`. A simulator's look, never a real phone's. The window is older than the card; the card's walk met it. Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Read.** At 402 wide, the bid window opened from a My Bids row. Its head held the name beside **Copy for text**, **Edit bid** and the ✕. The name got about a quarter of the width: a three-word invented name wrapped to four lines and broke inside a word. In `src/components/bids/BidPreviewModal.tsx` the head was one flex row, and the presses' block was `flexShrink: 0` with `flexWrap: 'wrap'`, so on a narrow head the presses kept their width and the name's block took what was left.

**The mend.** On a phone (`useNarrowViewport640`, the window's own signal) the head stacks. It is a grid of two columns: the name's block with the ✕ at its right on the first line, and **Copy for text** and **Edit bid** on the line under, across the whole width. The rule is `bidWindowHeadStacks` in `src/lib/bids/bidWindowPhonePress.ts`. At a desk the head is the one row it was. The three presses are the same elements with the sizes v2.8496 gave them; they are named once (`headCopyPress`, `headEditPress`, `headClosePress`) so each layout places them. In the pane, which has no ✕, the name's block takes the whole first line.

The guide *keep up with your bids on the dashboard* says where the name and the presses sit on a phone.
