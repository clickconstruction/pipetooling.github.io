# v2.6461 — Charges by crew, the live pass: Who counts as what in one list, and the charges screens say why a day prices at nothing (punch list #254) (2026-11-03)

The live pass after PRs 1 and 3 and #251's PR 2 reached the app found the Charges column blank on every job in the company: no one had a standing yet (every person read *Not charged*), so a crew-day priced at nothing, and nothing on the screen said why or where to fix it. The only place to set a standing was each person's desk, one at a time.

## What changed

- **Who counts as what** (`src/components/settings/WhoCountsAsWhatList.tsx`, at the foot of `RatesChargedByCrewSettingsBlock`): every person (not a sample, a digital twin or a walk tester) with their *Charged as*, people who left listed apart under their own heading since their past days still price by it, a select that writes `users.charge_standing` at once, the count of people who count as something, the reason a refused write names, and read-only for anyone but a dev (the desk's own gate). A link to `/settings?tab=settings-jobs#who-counts-as-what` opens the card on the list, and the Settings search finds it by *who counts as what* or *charged as*. The list joins `walkTesterSweep.test.ts`'s files that hide the walk tester.
- **The kernel** (`src/lib/charges/crewDays.ts`): the crew-day totals list `uncharged`, the people out on days that priced at nothing because nobody out counted as anything, most such days first; `unchargedWords` and `UNCHARGED_FIX_WORDS` word them for the office. A job's charges carry the list; the ledger's note leads with it in place of the paper's neutral line. A customer's paper keeps *Nobody out that day is charged to a customer.*
- **The screens**: `UnchargedCallout` in the Charges window and the Statement of charges window (a fresh one, not a saved snapshot) names who counts as nobody and how many days price at nothing, with a link to each desk and to the list; the Job Summary's Charges cell shows the ledger's note when the row prices at nothing; the statement window's count of the span reads `crewSpanWords` (*40 crew-days in this span, none priced yet, from 1,312.5 person-hours*, or *N not priced yet*), since a day nobody counts for has no crew-hours and *0 crew-hours* beside the person-hours read as a fault.
- **The premium waits** (`premiumWaitsWords` in `premium.ts`, `ledgerSummaryCell` in `ledgerCharges.ts`): while any day prices at nothing, the Charges window's *Against the revenue* tile reads *Not yet* with the count, the statement window and the office paper say the premium waits, a saved statement keeps `premium_usd` null, and the Job Summary's Premium cell is a dash with the reason in its title. On the live pass, charges short by every day of labor showed a six-figure premium that was not there.
- **A People record reaches its login** (`loginLookup`; the Jobs page reads `people.account_user_id` beside the standings): the team labor names people by their People record, and the summary matched standings by name alone, so anyone whose record and login are spelled differently counted as nobody even with a standing set (two people on the live pass). It also read the page's list of active logins only, so a person who left never reached their standing (two more on the live pass). The lookups now read every login, through the link first, and the note names a linked person as their login, the name Who counts as what shows. Someone on the crew rows with no login is named apart (`loginlessWords`: *Ana has no login for a Charged as. Link the People record to a login on People.*), since Who counts as what lists logins.
- **The hours statement window**: on the charged basis, the same `UnchargedCallout` names who counts as nobody on the person's unpriced days (`HoursStatementChargeFacts.uncharged`, `unchargedDays`), in place of the advice to change the job's rule or the rate card; the paper keeps its neutral line.
- **Dead saves**: *Save the rates* and *Save the markup* are dead until a value changes, as the counties card's is, and say so under the press (*Every rate is saved as shown.* · *The markup is saved as shown.*). The saved-standing toast reads *<name> is charged as a master.* (or *is not charged to a customer*) on the desk and in the list, not *Charged as not charged*.
- **Made-up test data**: the charges kernels' tests, the Charges window's smoke, the hours statement's charged cases and the charged paper's print, three fragments and the card now use made-up names, invoices and amounts, as the repo's other smokes do.
- **Tests**: the kernel's uncharged list and words and the span's words, the premium's wait, the ledger's note, its login link and its summary cell, the office paper's waiting premium, the Charges window's callout, links and waiting tile, the hours window's callout, the rate card's dead save and the list (filtering, a pick, a refused pick, a reader).

## Decisions

- **Name people only for the days that price at nothing because of them.** A person with no standing out beside someone charged changes nothing about that day, so they are not named.
- **The premium waits rather than shows part of itself.** A premium against charges short by whole days is wrong, not rough, so every surface holds it back and says why.
- **Office screens only.** Who counts as what never reaches a customer's copy.
- **The desk's gate.** A dev sets standings here and on the desk; the users update policy would admit more roles, and the docs now say what the screens do.

## Test

Dev server, `/dev-login?as=1`: Settings → Jobs & billing → Rates charged by crew lists the people with *Not charged* and a count; a pick saves. A job's Charges window names whoever counts as nobody, with a link to their desk; once they count as something, the callout leaves and the days price.
