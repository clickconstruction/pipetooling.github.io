# v2.5511 — Follow-ups: an Idle lens, a Next line and a door on every card (2026-10-13)

PR 2 of the idle-jobs train (v2.5510 put the idle rule in the kernel): the deck shows what the kernel knows. No migration, no edge function.

- **`JobsFollowupModal.tsx`**: `initialLens` (`'idle' | null`) and a `whyFilter` beside the stage filter; an *Idle 21+ (n)* chip in the rail (red when on, `data-testid="followups-lens-idle"`) narrows the deck to the idle cards whatever their stage, and the empty state says *No open job has sat 21 days with no crew on site*. The card's clock chip (`followup-clock-chip`) reads *idle 46 days* in red on an idle card, *quiet 3 days* in amber otherwise. Under *Why it's here* a *Next* line (`followup-next-line`) carries the kernel's `need`; its door matches the need: *Open the Bill tab ↗* through the job-window bridge (`initialTab: 'bill'`, the Job window as the fallback) for a bill, *Schedule it ↗* to `scheduleDispatchWeekUrl(jobId, this week)` for a visit; a nudge or a collections account has no door of its own, the embedded Pipeline row's buttons serve. The List view wears a small *idle* chip on idle rows.
- **`stagesDeepLinks.ts` / `useStagesDeepLinkParams.ts` / `JobsStagesTab.tsx`**: `?followups=idle` parses to `followups: true, followupsLens: 'idle'`; the door takes the lens; the tab holds `followupLens`, hands it to the deck as `initialLens`, and the Follow-ups button opens the whole queue as before.
- **Tests**: `JobsFollowupModal.render.test.tsx` (new, 2: an idle card deals first with its chip and Next line, the lens narrows the count, `initialLens` starts it on); `stagesDeepLinks.test.ts` gains the lens case.
- **Guide** *follow up on quiet jobs* names the chip, the Next line and the two doors; the Glossary gains a *Follow-ups deck* entry.
