# v2.7466 — Dashboard: a short view for a leader, with only what waits on him (2026-11-16)

The owner's ask of 2026-11-16: one leader has been given too much on his Dashboard, will not look at any of it, uses the app only on his phone and hands even small updates to his assistants. Punch list #365 holds the assessment, the three directions and the mock-up. The owner picked direction B, the short Dashboard. This is its first PR. One additive migration, no edge function.

## What a leader on Short sees

The page's cards are replaced by one column (`DashboardShortView.tsx`, an early return in `Dashboard.tsx` after Job Mode's):

- **A greeting and a count**: *Morning, Marcus* and *3 things wait on you.*
- **Waiting on you**: the Needs you lines only he can clear, worst first, each with one press that says the act. They are the five the database reserves for a leader (`lien-notice-approve`, `lien-notice-sign`, `lien-notice-batch`, `lien-waivers-to-sign`, `line-budgets-review`) and a job note that tagged him (`tagged-notes`). The lien notices are v2.7435's card (`LienLeaderSignCardView`), so *Open 891 to sign* lands on the same notice. **Rate your crew** is a row while his round is open. A trial helper's verdict still sits on top when one is owed.
- **His day**: My Schedule as the full page draws it.
- **One line**: *The office is handling 23 other things.* It counts the lines someone else presses.
- **One door, Everything else**: it opens the full Dashboard for the visit. On a phone the sections are folded to headlines (the phone-office folds, now also given to a person on Short). **Back to the short Dashboard** sits on top of the full page. When some of his own lines are behind the door, the door says how many (*2 more of yours are in there.*).
- **Required training** still shows under the door.

## It never says nothing waits too early

`shortDashboardRead` holds the list back as *Reading what waits on you…* until the Lien desk's read is in. If that read fails the page says *The Lien desk could not be read, so this list may be short* with **Open the Lien desk**, shows what did arrive, and draws no count. *Nothing waiting on you* is drawn only on a read that is in. `useNeedsYouInputs` returns `reading: { lienDesk, lienWaivers }` for this.

## The rule

`src/lib/dashboard/shortDashboard.ts` sorts the Needs you items three ways on top of `needsYouActor`: **waiting** (the six keys above), **alsoYours** (his by the actor rule, but not his alone) and **office** (another actor's). The same read as the full page's card feeds it, and each press goes through `useNeedsYouPress`, so a press here opens what the card's press opens.

## The setting

`users.dashboard_view` (`short`, `full`, or NULL for full) through `set_dashboard_view(p_user_id, p_view)`. The control (`DashboardViewPicker`) is in Settings → Dashboard & alerts for the person, and on the People desk's new **Dashboard view** section for the office, so a leader who will not open Settings still gets the short page. The leader, a dev and the assistant press. The controller and a training account read. Only a leader's or a dev's account takes a view. The last value read is kept on the device, so the page does not draw Full and then swap.

Nobody's Dashboard changes with this PR: every account reads as Full until someone presses Short.
