# v2.7198 — The lien clock itself knows a late notice (2026-11-13)

Punch list #335, row 4, on v2.7187. A sub job whose § 53.056 notice windows all closed unsent can still be perfected while its affidavit can be filed: a late notice goes, and the affidavit follows it (v2.4708, the owner's reading of 2026-10-06). The Lien window and the Lien desk read it that way. The clock every billed row hangs on, `buildLienPayRunway`, answered `closed` the day the notice window shut. v2.7047 taught the billed row to read past that, and v2.7184 the phone chip. The Lien desk's By date tab (named Deadlines until v2.7207) and the Share message still said *lien gone*. Client only.

## What changed

- **`lienPayRunway.ts`**: `LienRunwayState` gains `late_notice`. The last month's notice window closed, no month's notice was sent or is in the mail, and the lien's own day is ahead. Lines *notice window closed Sep 15 · lien by Oct 15* / *send the late notice · 17 d*, chip *late notice · 17 d*, amber, red inside a week, sorted with the notices owed by the days to the lien's day. Its track carries the lien flag and a live pay dot, no notice flag. `closed` with `closedBy: 'notice'` is now only a notice window closed with the affidavit's day passed too. When an earlier month's notice did go, that notice carries the affidavit and the clock reads on to `file_first`, `room` or `no_pay`. `lienRunwayWantsTheChip` takes the new state.
- **`lienLineStep.ts`, `billedDatesLedger.ts`**: the read past `closed` is gone. `lienLateNoticeOpen` is `state === 'late_notice'`, and the ledger's *Lien gone* row is `closed` alone. The row's and the chip's words do not change.
- **`lienCalendarBuckets.ts`**: `lienNextDate` answers `{ ymd: lienByYmd, what: 'late_notice' }`, so `lienBucketOf` puts the job in the month of the affidavit's day. A bucket carries `lateNotices` and says *1 late notice* on its bar. The bar's *Draft the N* door still lists only notices owed inside their own window. `lienNextDateCounts` counts a late notice with the notices on its day. `lienPhoneLine` reads *send the late notice by Oct 15*.
- **`lienCalendar.ts`**: a GC row's sub line says *N late notices*, and `totals.lateNotices` counts them. Marks need no rule of their own: the job draws its lien flag on the affidavit's day and no *gone* mark.
- **`lienDeskShare.ts`** needed no change: `pastWindow` counts `closed`, which no longer holds these jobs.
- **`LienDeskCalendarTab`** needed no change: it greys a row on `closed`.
- **Tests**: `lienPayRunway.test.ts` (the state, its words and marks, red inside a week, gone past the affidavit's day, an earlier month sent), `lienCalendarBuckets.test.ts` and `lienCalendar.test.ts` (their *gone* fixtures moved a month earlier so they are gone for good, and a late-notice job added to each), `lienLineStep.test.ts`, `billedDatesLedger.test.ts`, `lienDeskShare.test.ts`. `lienOneReading.test.ts` holds the By date view with the row, the chip and the header on the late notice, and on the lien gone after it.
- **Guides** *see what is due on the lien calendar* and *Texas lien rules the app follows*. Glossary; `LIEN_DESK_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Not done, and why

- **The Dashboard's closed-window card** (*N lien windows closed with nothing recorded … the lien right on that work is gone*, `lien-window-missed` in `dashboardNeedsYou.ts`) still says gone while a late notice can go. It does not read this clock. It reads the Lien desk's summary (`lienDeskMissedSummary` over the notice queue in `lienDesk.ts`), which knows a month's window closed and not whether the job's affidavit can still be filed. Telling the two apart means handing that summary the affidavit windows (`summarizeLienDeskForNeedsYou`'s callers in `useLienDeskData` and the Dashboard's inputs), splitting its lines, and new words on the card and on the Pipeline's money card (`lienDeskMoneyCard.ts`, *the earliest closed Sep 15*). That is its own PR, listed on the card. The desk's Do now list already reads such a job as *Late notice to draft*.
- **The Share message** does not name late notices as their own line. Its payload is read by a deployed function, so a new field waits for a deploy.
- **The By date bar's *Draft the N*** does not include a late notice: the desk drafts it from its missed pile, a different door.
