# v2.7388 — People → Subs: Balance due opens into its sheets (PR 2 of the sub labor train) (2026-11-15)

Client only, no migration. Punch list #346, on the kernels of v2.7383. Taunya walked this page in the design spike on 2026-11-15 and said to ship the train. The page now reads her rule: a sheet that names one teammate and no sub is owed to that teammate.

## What landed

- **The row that opens.** `PeopleSubsTab.tsx`: a sub's Balance due is a press. It opens `SubOwedBlock.tsx` in a row under theirs (a sheet on a phone): the sheets by job, each with its lines, payments and back-charges, an Open and a Paid view, and `owedCheck`'s line that the sheets add up to the figure in the column. `?owed=<people id>` opens a row from a link.
- **The data.** `useSubsOwed.ts` reads what the Pay view reads (the roster with each login's role, the live work orders, the sheets' jobs and the state of their bills) and hands `subOwedBreakdown` each sheet with its parties and its *Pay when*. The page loads its sheets through `useSubLaborLedger`, the Pay view's own hook, so the two pages cannot disagree on a balance.
- **The rule on this page.** A teammate's own sheet is on their row with a *Team · sub labor* chip, and a teammate with no row on this roster is listed under *Teammates paid as sub labor*. The line under the band says how much is open on such sheets and that it is owed to the teammate.
- **Acts in place.** *Pay…* and *Back-charge…* on an open row open `SubLaborPaymentModals`, the Pay view's own windows. *By date ›* opens the person's statement on the Ledger. `Jobs.tsx` reads `?subSearch=` so a door from this page lands on the Pay view already narrowed.
- **Reach.** `PeopleSubsReach.tsx`: four tiles over the roster (owed, ready to pay now, on nobody's row, adds up). An *Owed* chip, a search by sub or job, a sort (*Most owed*, *Name*, *Ready to pay*, *Last worked*) and *Export CSV*, one row per open sheet of the subs shown. On a phone each sub is a card, with the six-column table one press away.
- **The band.** Its head counts the sheets that need one sub and their open money, once. *Whose is it…* (`WhoseSheetWindow.tsx`) takes the place of *Assign…*: it shows what a choice adds to each Balance due and that the parts are the sheet's open money, before the page's own save runs. It offers no way to split a sheet between two subs, since a sheet names only the one person it pays.
- Guide `review-your-subs` rewritten for all of it. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` gains *Sub sheet: whose it is*.

## Tests

`PeopleSubsTab.render.test.tsx`: three tests rewritten for the band's new head, *Whose is it…* and the teammate rule, and the several-names test now also presses Balance due and reads the check. 5 pass.

## Not in this change

The Pay view and the Ledger still read a sheet with no sub the old way until the next change lands, so for that short while this page counts a teammate's own sheet and they do not. The papers' presses and the clean-up of old sheets that name teammates beside a sub come in their own changes.
