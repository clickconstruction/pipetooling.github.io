# v2.5852 — Legal desk: the modal split into files (2026-10-25)

The last of four refinements from the owner's walk of the Legal desk (design spike, 2026-10-25). `LegalDeskModal.tsx` had grown to 1,612 lines, and every PR in the Burd train touched it, which is why the merge queue kept bouncing the stack. A mechanical split, no behavior change:

- `LegalDeskModal.tsx` keeps the props, the header, the rail, the sheets (attorney-ready, ask a dev, pull back, close, in house) and the preview; about 1,000 lines.
- `LegalDeskPacketTab.tsx`: `PacketTab`, which draws whichever tab is open, with the act types the desk hands it (`Curation`, `OfficeActs`, `DocumentsPanel`, `PlanActs`).
- `LegalDeskFirmPanels.tsx`: `SettlementFloorEditor`, `OffersPanel`, `SettlementAnswer`, `DeskVoidableText`, `DeskUndo`.
- `legalDeskShared.tsx`: the tabs and their labels, the pills, `Door`, `SectionTitle`, `Table` with its date cell, the styles, `coverageText`, `FIX_LABEL`, the `Sheet` type, `daysAgo`.

Each file imports only what it uses (the split was checked by the typecheck's unused-import rule). The render smokes, the window checks and the theme check read as before. `docs/PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` names the four files.

## Verify

Open the desk on any account and walk the six tabs, the sheets and the With-the-firm panels: everything reads and acts as it did on v2.5851.
