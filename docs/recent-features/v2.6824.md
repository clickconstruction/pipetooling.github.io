# v2.6824 — A supply house paper counts as a purchase only while its bill is on file (2026-11-09)

Punch list #298, a fix found on PR 1b's live walk. `supply_papers.invoice_id` is `ON DELETE SET NULL`, so a bill deleted from a house's list left its paper with no bill, and `supply_purchase_lines` still counted the paper's lines as purchases.

- **Migration `20261109230000_supply_purchase_lines_need_a_bill.sql`** (`docs/migrations/20261109230000_supply_purchase_lines_need_a_bill.md`): the view adds `p.invoice_id IS NOT NULL`; same columns, same order, `CREATE OR REPLACE`. A paper whose bill is gone keeps its rows and drops out of every count until a bill owns it again — kept rather than cascaded, since the lines are the record of what the paper printed and a bill deleted by mistake is recorded again.
- **`countsAsPurchase`** in `src/lib/materials/lines/paperChecks.ts`: the view's rule in TypeScript (an invoice or credit, with its bill, whose check is `adds_up` or `accepted`) for the sweep and the screens, tested in `paperChecks.test.ts`.
