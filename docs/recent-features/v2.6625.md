# v2.6625 — Lien desk: the four gates fold to one line on a notice that already went (2026-11-07)

Punch list #248, face two (PR 2 of two). The owner picked rule A on 2026-11-07: on a Sent row with nothing due the gates fold to one line with no verdict; a Sent row with months due keeps the four gates on the next notice (the card's rule C for that case).

## What was wrong

A Sent row's pane drew **The four gates** with a verdict as if a notice were about to go. On job 890 on 2026-11-02 (every window closed, nothing left to notice) the verdict read *Can't go out yet · 3 blockers* and sent Taunya to **Set the GC ›** on a job with a GC. v2.6376 and v2.6380 fixed the GC and the money and v2.6384 gave gate 4 *No month open*, but the verdict still said the notice could not go out, about a notice that went Oct 14.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskGates.ts`** — `lienSentGatesFold({ sentOn, gates, noticeMonths, monthShort })` → `{ sentOn, head, line, rows }`: *Sent Oct 14 · Khan Umar · RMC- Dudley Mason · Residential · Bexar · Jul + Aug*. Each gate's value in plain words with no tone (a missing owner reads *owner not on file*, no GC *no GC on the job*, an unknown kind *kind unknown*, no month *no month named*), and the same four as rows for *Details ∨*. Pure, unit-tested.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienDeskGates.tsx`** — `LienGatesSentFold` (`data-lien-gates-sent`): the line in the gates' box with *Details ∨* / *Fold the details ∧*; open, the four rows in the gates' own row style. No verdict row, no tones.
- **`src/components/jobs/LienDeskModal.tsx`** — `gatesFold` when the selected row is on the Sent pile, has no month due and its item is the sent record. The pane body draws the fold instead of `LienDeskGates`; the sticky strip draws a quiet *Sent Oct 14* chip (`data-lien-strip-sent`) instead of the verdict and the blocker chips; the pane's gates head reads *Sent Oct 14 · as the notice went* (`lienPaneSections` takes `gates: { sentOn }`); the stop window's *before* list is empty on such a row.
- **Guide** — [send lien notices from the Lien desk](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md), under *The four gates*: what the folded line reads and why there is no verdict.
- **Tests** — `lienDeskGates.test.ts`, `lienPaneSections.test.ts`, `LienDeskGates.render.test.tsx`, `LienDeskModal.render.test.tsx` (the Sep 2 packet with every month noticed: no gates box, the folded line, the head, Details).
