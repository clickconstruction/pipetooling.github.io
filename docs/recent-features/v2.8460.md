# v2.8460 — Document builder: more of the Sub NDA's words can be typed on (2026-11-24)

Punch list #416, part 3, the first of two PRs for *The rest of the Sub NDA's words as fields*. Client only: no migration, no edge function, and no change to what the database holds.

## The split

#404's step 7 made the disclaimer, a level's meaning, the breach lines and the site lines fields. The review of 2026-11-20 named what was left: section names, fact labels, some of the flow's own words, and the title, the job, the company and the party.

- **This PR** is every piece of words the paper already holds and has a path for, which the flow drew as plain text. Nothing new is kept. Only the wiring changes.
- **The next PR** is the title, the job, the company and the party. The flow never draws those four, so they need paths of their own, a page in the list of what changed, and a place to type them.

## What is a field now

| Words | Where they are typed | Path |
|---|---|---|
| A topic's section name, *Section 5: Photos and video* | On the topic's page, beside *The core clause* | `topic.<key>.section` |
| The name of a fact above the basis slider, *Secrecy lasts* | On *The basis*, above the slider | `topic.<key>.fact`, or the topic's label when it has no fact name |
| The name of the fact that counts what the site must have | The same row | `words.siteFact` |
| What the basis page says while the whole paper is Off | Under the levels, while the slider is on Off | `words.offSays` |
| The last step's press, on and Off | Just above the press, on *Review* | `words.confirmPress`, `words.nothingPress` |
| The line under that press, on and Off | In place | `words.beforeConfirm`, `words.offNote` |

- `flowFields.ts` (pure) says which path the words on show are typed at. A fact reads the topic's fact name or, with none, its label, so the kernel names the one that is on show and the typing always lands.
- **A press cannot be typed in**, so its words are a field above it, as the box's words are on the first step. The press reads what is typed.
- **Once the last press is pressed** the line under it is a count of who was asked. The flow works that out, so it is no field.
- **The Off line belongs to the basis page.** `pathPage` filed `words.offSays` under *Review*, where it is never read. It is now one of the basis page's words (`BASIS_WORDS`, `paperEdit.ts`), so a change to it lists *The basis* and **Back to revision N** on that row puts it back.
- **A GC's view is as it was.** With no `edit` the flow draws the words alone. A look-only link and a host's own press (a job's settings page) draw no field.

## Left as it is, and why

- **The levels' names** and the line beside each. #404 records them as settled by the owner and not fields.
- **A fact's value**, such as *3 years*. It is the level's own blank, which is already a field on the topic's page.
- **Words the flow builds**: *What puts a person in breach*, *Also applies*, *New at Elevated*, the step's names, *Next: …*. They are not in the paper, so making one a field means a new key in every stored draft. Nobody has asked for that.
- **A topic's section name inside a sentence**, *Section 5 is left out of the paper*, shown when the topic is Off. The name is typed on any level of that topic.
- **A fact's name is listed as the topic's name page.** Typed on *The basis*, a topic's fact name lists as *the topic, Its name*, since that is the page that holds it. **Open it** there shows the topic, not the basis.

- `DocumentBuilderFlow.fields.render.test.tsx`: each field for the person who edits, with the path each typing is told at. The press reading what is typed. No field for a GC's view, a look-only link or a host's press.
