# v2.5678 — Pricing: the scoreboard strip carries its cents (2026-10-20)

Wendi's ask (2026-10-20): "i want the revenue number to include cents as well on the pricing
page." Punch list #133 (`to-dos/pricing-strip-cents/`), gated on the owner since it amends his
v2.2208 whole-dollars call; the go came the same day.

The Workbench strip's Revenue and Profit were the only money on the Pricing tab that rounded —
the cards, the rows, the sent-vs-today line and the strip's own tooltips all carry cents — and
`Math.round` rounds half up, so a book total ending ≥ .50 showed a dollar no card, row or
letter carries. For an estimator revising a sent bid the cents are the checksum against the
GC's letter.

In [`BidsPricingTab.tsx`](../../src/components/bids/BidsPricingTab.tsx):

- **Revenue** → `<AmountSmallCents>` (v2.2246's pattern: full-size dollars, cents at 70%,
  copy still yields the full figure), **Profit** → `<SignedAmountSmallCents>` (true minus on
  a loss). `stat`'s value widens `string` → `React.ReactNode`.
- **The alternates line** (v2.4194) — Base, each *+ alternate*, *With every/the alternate* —
  the same; rounded it could fail its own footing ($62,450 + $9,212 reading $71,663). The
  `whole()` helper is gone.
- **Tooltips trimmed** to what they said before v2.2208 (`our cost $X …`); the leading exact
  figure existed only to carry the cents the strip dropped. The crew-rate note and the
  alternates' margins stay.
- Margin, Multiple, row prices, the solver's whole-dollar solves, the brush and every other
  scoreboard in the app are untouched. No guide edit — the guides' "whole dollars" sentences
  are about solved row prices.
