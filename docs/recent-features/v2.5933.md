Punch list #181, PR 4 of 4 (`to-dos/go-anywhere/README.md`), on the owner's "build it all" of 2026-10-26. Until now the estimator, the primary and the superintendent had no magnifier at all, and a helper's box found jobs and nothing else.

## What shipped

- **`doorsOnlyHeaderSearchEligible`** (`src/lib/fieldJobLookup.ts`): the estimator, the primary and the superintendent, outside Farm Mode. `headerSearchEligibleForRole` is now every signed-in role outside Farm Mode, and `fieldJobLookup.test.ts` holds each role in exactly one of the three modes.
- **The doors-only box** (`HeaderGlobalSearch.tsx`, `doorsOnly`): the same box with no data read. The service-type load and the data search skip it, so no `search_jobs_ledger`, bid, estimate or customer query runs for the role; the doors are the role's own (`doorsFor`), so the Bid Board's tabs show by `bidsTabOpenFor`, Jobs' tabs by the router's gates, Settings by `getZonedSettingsGroups`, guides by `guideIsRelevantForRole`. A number alone finds nothing, and the no-matches line says the box looks in pages, settings and guides.
- **A field role's box** (`fieldLookup`): the job lookup and its read-only card as before, with the role's guides under the jobs and no page, Settings card or to-do. The field's day is Clock In on top, only my work and my pay (J24-F8), so its few pages stay where its own nav puts them.
- **Words per mode** (`GO_ANYWHERE_WORDS.modes` in `doors.ts`): the button's name and tip, the placeholder and the no-matches line for the office, the field and the doors-only box. The office's button keeps its name, *Search jobs, bids, estimates, customers*; its tip now names pages, settings and guides.
- **Guides**: `global-search` opens to the three roles with *If your box shows pages and guides only*; `find-a-job-address-from-the-field` gains *Find a guide from the same box*.
- **Docs**: `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (who gets which box, in the index's note), `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (*Go anywhere*, one home), the Header line of `docs/twins/APP_DIRECTORY.md`, and `to-dos/go-anywhere/README.md` moved to *Close out* with where it stands.
