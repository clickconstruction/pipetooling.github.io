# v2.7667 — Supply houses: how a house's jobs and PO codes are read now has its own tests (2026-11-17)

The third cut of `src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`, step 3 of the *SupplyHousesTab extraction order* in `docs/MATERIALS_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`. A pure move: no behaviour changes.

**What moved.**

- `groupAllocationsByInvoice` (`src/lib/materials/supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts`): a house's allocation rows by invoice, each share a number. The tab wrote this three times: in the house's detail loader, in the aging cell window's effect and in the move window's effect. The loader kept a `Map` and the two effects a plain object; all three read the object now.
- `jobsLedgerNames` and `jobsLedgerNamesWithAddress` (`src/lib/materials/supplyHouseBillRows.ts`): the rows of `get_jobs_ledger_by_ids` keyed by job, every missing word an empty string. Also written three times, the third with the address for the invoice form's job cards.
- `poLedgerEntriesFromRows` (`supplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts`, beside `poLedgerCodes`): the house's PO Generator rows as the invoice form's PO check reads them. A row with no job or no number reads as a dash, the person is their name and then their email, and a later row for the same code wins.
- `formatOrdinal` is gone. The tab already imported `ordinalDay`, the same function with tests, and its one call reads that.

**Size.** `SupplyHousesTab.tsx` went from 3,625 lines to 3,575.

**Proof.** 9 new unit tests: 6 in `supplyHouseInvoiceForm.test.ts` (59 in all) and 3 in `supplyHouseBillRows.test.ts` (23 in all). They cover a share that arrives as text, an invoice with no rows, a ledger row with nothing on it, the Click number and email fallbacks, two rows for one code, and the jobs map with and without the address.

**Docs.** The map marks step 3, its Stage-A rows and its coverage row as shipped.
