# v2.6941 — Cards and footers: the facts and the photo's home (punch list #313, PR 1) (2026-11-11)

Punch list #313 (`to-dos/cards-and-footers/README.md`). The owner asked for a tool that makes a business card and an email footer from a person's facts and photo, and on 2026-11-11 said to build it. This is PR 1 of the plan: the ground, with no screen.

- **Migration `20261111200000_person_card_facts.sql`**: three nullable columns on `users` (`card_name`, `photo_path`, `photo_crop`), two named CHECKs (`users_card_name_short`, 80 characters; `users_photo_path_own_folder`, the path sits under the person's own id), the private bucket `person-photos` (2 MB; JPEG, PNG, WebP; `<users.id>/<file>`) and four policies on `storage.objects`. Behind `SET lock_timeout = '3s'`. No table, so no fence call (`docs/migrations/20261111200000_person_card_facts.md`).
- **Who may write**: the person on their own row and folder, and the office (dev, master_technician, assistant, controller) on anyone's. The row's write needs no new policy; the two that carry a name, a phone and a note already cover it. The bucket's write policies refuse a training account and a digital twin themselves, since `storage.objects` has no fence for either.
- **Tried first** on a local Postgres 15 with stand-ins: applied twice, fourteen probes as five accounts, all as designed.
- **Push owed**: `supabase db push` from a clean `main` after the merge, in any order against the Pages deploy. Nothing in the client reads the columns yet. The types follow in a `chore(types)` PR.
- **`docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`**: one bullet for the columns and the bucket.

No code changed. The release note is an `infra` note for devs.
