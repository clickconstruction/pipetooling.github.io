## What was wrong

A computer's row was one line of six columns. At about 950 px wide the job's name and the paper's state were both cut off (*863 · Service Visit …*, *Affidavit approved · re…*), ten rows read the same words, no row carried dollars, and the four dots said the stage a second way beside words that said it a third. An affidavit held by its property facts read *the property record is not complete* whatever the gate, with *Fix the property*; the owner's ask of 2026-11-14 began at a row that hid an unanswered homestead question behind those words. No affidavit row said where its notice stood.

The phone, the find and By date read the same `sub`.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienBoard.ts`: `lienBoardRowStage(board, row)`, the name of the bin a row's paper stands in; a run row stands in the notices' mail bin; a reminder, a served copy, a returned envelope and retainage have none.
- `src/lib/jobs/lienRowWords.ts` (new): `lienRowLine` (the stage, then the detail, never the stage twice; a row with no bin keeps its `sub`), `lienAffidavitNoticeTag` (*No notice needed · contracted with the owner*, *Notice sent Sep 22 and Oct 14*, the count and the last past three, *No notice on record* as the warning), `lienHeadDay` (the one day every dated row under a head shares) and `lienDayLeftWords`.
- `LienDeskNextUp.tsx`: a computer's row is `data-layout="two-line"`: the chip, the job, what it owes (`data-lien-next-up-amount`) and the press in a column 172 px wide, where longer words wrap; under the job (`data-lien-next-up-line`) its own day when the head does not carry it, the bin's name as the door to the step card (`data-lien-next-up-stage`), the detail and the tags. A row with no bin keeps `LienStepMark`. The heads carry the day (`data-lien-next-up-head-day`); *On track* carries `trackPress`. A phone's row is untouched.
- `LienDesk.tsx`: `rowStage`, `rowNotice` (the affidavit entry's notice gate and `lienAffidavitOwnerSends` over the job's filings); `actOnNextUp` opens the property record at the chip's focus for a held affidavit's press, over To do; `trackPress` opens By paper's affidavits on the Ready pile, where the batch print and its options stand; `phoneAmountFor` feeds the computer's row.
- Guides: the Board guide gains *What a row says*; the lien notices guide's lines on the waiting rows and the dots are brought up to date.

## What "is this the best we can do" changed

The mock-up dropped the four dots. On the desk they are also the door to the card that writes a paper's ladder out, so dropping them would have taken that card off every computer. The bin's name is now that door, and a row with no bin (retainage, a reminder, a served copy) keeps its dots, so no row loses its card.

The held affidavit's press first kept opening the Affidavits pane, where the gate's own door sits one more press away. It now opens the property record on the question itself, as the job's chip on the Board does, and To do stays behind it.

*Print N affidavits ›* could have printed from To do. The batch print files a copy of every paper and has options that live on the affidavits list (the help sheet, the notice copies), so the press is a door to that list at Ready to file, not a second print button with the options out of sight.

A row's press column is a fixed width so the dollars line up down the list; the first cut let each press size its own column and the amounts wandered.
