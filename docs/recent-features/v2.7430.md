# v2.7430 — Lien desk: a closed month is one line, and this notice's total sits at the foot of its column (2026-11-15)

Taunya, on Monthly notices with a job's **Months on this job** grid in view (2026-11-15): windows that are closed should not be two lines, they should be one compact line, and the total at the top right of the table (the *This notice* head with the claim and the month count) should be at the bottom. She was shown a mock-up with two placements and picked A: the column keeps its name on top, the total goes to the foot. The mock-up was drawn wider than her pane (about 700 px), where the loss words would not fit on the line, so she was asked and chose to have them said once under the table instead of cut on every row. Client only: no migration, no edge function.

## What changed

- **A closed month is one line.** [`LienDeskMonths.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskMonths.tsx): a row whose window is `closed` (missed, skipped, or named after the window) draws its cells with the tighter `tdOne` padding. The month cell carries `lienMonthOne`, which sets the month and its hours side by side. The window cell of a missed or after-the-window month is a `.lienMonthClosed` flex row: the closed chip, *noted by …* or the *not noted* chip, and **Note it as missed** when the month is not noted. An open month and a month that is not a work month yet keep their two lines.
- **The loss words, once.** *lien right gone · the money still rides*, or *a late notice can still go · the affidavit by …*, depends only on the job's affidavit day, so it was the same sentence on every missed month. It is one line under the grid now (`data-lien-months-missed`): a closed chip, the missed months by name, then the words. A missed month is a closed window with no paper naming it and no skip; a job with none draws no line.
- **What gives way.** A row's trailing words (*named after the window — information*, and the Windows view's *it was open … → …*) are `.lienMonthClosedTail`: one clamped line that ends in an ellipsis, with the whole sentence in its `title`. The chips and the press are `flex: none`, and the row is `flex-wrap: wrap`, so a press that does not fit drops under the chips instead of leaving the card. A skip's reason is its own words, so it is never cut and wraps where it must.
- **Why a clamp and not `nowrap`.** A `nowrap` sentence counts its whole width as the column's minimum, and with a paper's column in the grid that pushed the table out of the card at 730 px. A one-line `-webkit-line-clamp` counts only its longest word, so the column can shrink and the table stays inside.
- **A phone.** Under 560 px of card (`@container lien-months`), the month stacks over its hours again and a row's trailing words wrap whole on a line of their own. Nothing is cut where there is no pointer to read a tip with.
- **The total at the foot.** The *This notice* head is the letter and the name alone. A `tfoot` row (`data-lien-grid-foot`) closes the column: its blue cell (`data-lien-grid-total`) holds the claim, the count of ticked months and **Preview ›**, with a rounded blue edge under it that mirrors the tab on top. The cell to its left reads *This notice names Aug, Sep*. With nothing ticked and a month still open to tick, it says *Tick a month to put it on this notice.*
- **The Windows view.** A closed month no longer draws its full red bar; a bar that is always full said nothing the closed chip does not. *it was open … → …* stays as the row's trailing words.
- **CSS.** [`src/index.css`](../../src/index.css): `.lienMonthOne`, `.lienMonthClosed`, `.lienMonthClosedTail` beside the other `.lienMonths` rules.
- **Help.** [`send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md`](../../src/content/help/send-lien-notices-from-the-lien-desk.md): the grid's bullets say a closed month takes one line and where the total is, and the missed-month paragraph points at the line under the grid.
- **Tests.** `LienDesk.render.test.tsx`: the grid smoke pins the head as the name alone, the foot's words and total, and which rows are the one-line kind; the missed-month smoke pins the press inside the row, the loss words off the row and the line under the grid word for word; a skip draws no such line.

## Not changed

- The claim box under the grid still says the same figure. The grid's kernel (`lienMonthGrid.ts`), the ticks, the paper columns and their heads are untouched.
