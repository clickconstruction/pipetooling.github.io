# v2.8337 — Dashboard: My Bids reads a bid's state as the Bid Board does (2026-11-23)

Step 1 of punch list #426, *My Bids and Recent Reports on the Dashboard: one swipe to put a row away*. The owner's go of 2026-11-23, *as recommended*. No new look in this step: the card's read and its rule for a bid's state. No migration, no edge function.

## What was there

`DashboardMyBidsSection` kept its own rule for a bid's state and its own read, and both differed from the Bid Board's.

- **The state.** The row read the sent date first: no sent date was *Unsent*, whatever the answer. The board reads the answer first (`getSubmissionSectionKey`). A bid marked Won with no sent date sat in the card's unsent list and said *Unsent*.
- **The words.** A sent bid with no answer said *Pending*. The board's heading is **Not yet won or lost**.
- **What the board had put away.** The read did not look at `working_board_archived_at` or `adopted_into_bid_id`. A bid taken off the board's unsent pile with **Archive from board**, and a bid adopted into another bid's package, both stayed on the card.
- **The limit.** The read sorted by due date, oldest first, and took 50. A person with more than 50 bids lost the newest due dates and every bid with no due date, and kept the oldest.

## What changed

- **One rule for the state.** `myBidState` in `src/lib/dashboardMyBids.ts` reads `getSubmissionSectionKey`, the board's rule. `MY_BID_STATE_WORDS` holds the board's words for the four states the card reads.
- **One order for each pile.** `groupMyBids` sorts Unsent by due date with no due date last (`compareBidsForBidBoardDueDate`) and the rest by sent date, newest first (`compareBidsForBidBoardPendingRecency`). Both are the board's own.
- **Off the board, off the card.** The read filters adopted bids and unsent bids archived from the board (`MY_BIDS_ON_THE_BOARD_FILTER`). `isMyBidOffTheBoard` holds the same rule for a row that arrives anyway.
- **The limit cuts the other end.** `MY_BIDS_READ_ORDER`: the bids with no answer first, then Won, then Started or Complete, each newest first. A cut now falls on the oldest decided bids. `myBidsReadIsCut` says when the read came back full, for the card to say so in step 2.
- The read also takes `created_at`, which step 2 needs for the age of a bid with no due date.

## What a person sees

- A won bid with no sent date is behind **Sent bids** and reads *Won*.
- A sent bid with no answer reads *Not yet won or lost*.
- A bid archived from the board is gone from the card.
- The unsent list is in the same order as before. The sent list runs newest sent first.
