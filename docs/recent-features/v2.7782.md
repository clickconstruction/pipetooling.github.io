# v2.7782 — View as on a phone: the Exit button on its own line above the header (2026-11-18)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. View as is dev-only, so only a dev ever sees this.

The owner asked on 2026-11-18, with a 375 px screenshot taken while viewing as the Sample leader: *"when impersonating a user on mobile I would like to see the exit impersonation button above the header instead of in line with the header making it two broken lines"*.

## What was wrong

On a phone the **Exit (…)** button was the last press of the header's icon row (`Layout.tsx`, capped at `8.5rem` with an ellipsis). Measured at 375 × 812 on the Sample leader's Dashboard, before the change: the button read *Exit (Sample l…*, its right edge was at 395 px, and the page was 395 px wide, so it scrolled sideways by 20 px.

## What shipped

- **`ViewAsExitStrip`** (`src/components/layout/ViewAsExitStrip.tsx`): one amber line holding the button, full width, with its whole name. `Layout` mounts it just before `.appNavChrome` while a dev is viewing as someone at a phone's width. A long name wraps inside the line (`white-space: normal`, `overflow-wrap: anywhere`); it never leaves the screen.
- **`impersonationExitPlacement(impersonating, phoneWidth)`** (`src/lib/impersonationUiLabels.ts`): `'strip'`, `'header'` or `null`. `Layout` passes `viewportNarrow` (640 px and under), not `isMobile`. A desk header that collapsed to the phone's shape for want of room (a leader's at 1280 px does) still has room for the button, so it keeps it in the row as before.
- **The header under the strip** draws no Exit button on a phone, so its rows hold what they hold for that person signed in directly. It keeps its amber tint, so the strip and the header read as one warning block. The button keeps `IMPERSONATION_CHROME_BUTTON_STYLE`, its `title`, its aria-label (now the constant `IMPERSONATION_EXIT_ARIA_LABEL`) and `handleBackToMyAccount`.
- **The status bar's padding** (`src/index.css`): the strip takes `env(safe-area-inset-top)` on itself and sets `--app-nav-safe-top: 0px` on the chrome that follows it. `.appNav`, `.appHealthStrip` and the search bar that covers the header (`HeaderGlobalSearch.tsx`) now read `var(--app-nav-safe-top, env(safe-area-inset-top, 0px))`, so none of them pads for the notch a second time. With no strip the variable is unset and all three behave as before. `--app-top-chrome`, which full-screen windows read, is untouched.
- The strip sits outside `.appNavChrome` on purpose: the search bar covers that box edge to edge (`inset: 0`), and it must not cover the way back.

## Tests

- `impersonationUiLabels.test.ts`: three cases for `impersonationExitPlacement`.
- `Layout.viewAsExit.render.test.tsx` (4 cases, the first smoke that renders `Layout` itself): on a phone the one Exit button is outside every `nav`, inside `.appViewAsStrip`, the strip is the sibling just before `.appNavChrome`, the label is *Exit (Sample leader)* and carries no width cap or ellipsis; at a desk it is inside `nav.appNav` and there is no strip; neither is drawn while nobody is being viewed as; one press clears `impersonation_original` and lands on the stash's `returnTo`.
- Broken on purpose: with the placement forced to `'header'` (the old layout) the phone case and the kernel's strip case fail, and both pass again with the line restored.

## Walked live

Local dev server, 375 × 812, signed in as the dev, Settings → View as… → Sample leader. Nothing was saved while viewing as the sample.

| Check | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Button's words | *Exit (Sample l…* | *Exit (Sample leader)* |
| Button's right edge | 395 px | 367 px |
| Page width | 395 px (scrolls sideways) | 375 px |
| Where the button is | inside `nav.appNav` | inside `.appViewAsStrip`, above the header |

Also checked after the change: 320 px wide (button whole, page 320 px); a made-up 57-character label wraps to two lines and a 78-character one with no spaces to three, both inside the screen; dark theme (strip, header and button all on the dark amber token); the header search bar covers the header's box only and the Exit button stays on top and pressable; a full-screen window (New report) covers the whole screen from the top, as before; at 1280 px the button is in the header row and there is no strip; one press on Exit returned to the dev account on `/settings`, the page left.

## Not checked

- Safari on a real iPhone, and the Home Screen app there: the notch padding (`env(safe-area-inset-top)`) reads 0 in desktop Chrome, so the new `--app-nav-safe-top` hand-off was reasoned from the CSS, not seen.
- The Sample leader's header still wraps onto two rows at 375 px, with or without View as: its icons alone are wider than the screen. That is the header's own width, the subject of a separate change in flight (*App header on a phone: never two lines*).
