# v2.7937 — Documents: send a paper to sign from the Agreements tab (2026-11-19)

Punch list #376, the second train, PR 2 of three: send a paper from the tab. It is People → Contracts' quick send on the Documents page. No migration and no edge function change.

## What is new

- **Send a paper**, beside the tab's find box (`src/components/documents/AgreementsSendPaper.tsx`). The Search tab does not carry it.
- **Step 1, the paper.** A window lists the Contract Book's papers, one entry per name, with a find box. The list is `pickablePapers`, the rule a job's *Add a paper* uses, so the customer's contracts and the flows' own papers stay out. A paper with nothing to sign names why (*No words yet. Write it in the Contract Book first.*) and is not a press.
- **Step 2, the person.** People's own `ContractQuickSendPicker`: who has not had the paper, who already has it out, and how many have signed.
- **Step 3, the email.** The shared Send for signature window (v2.7932), on the pick's plan. A person who already has the paper out gets the same copy again, and the window says so.
- **The new line shows on the list.** The list reads again once the email goes.

## One quick send

- `quickSendPickPlan` and `quickSendNoContentWords` are new in `src/lib/contractsQuickSend.ts`. They hold what `PeopleContractsTab` decided inline when a person was picked: the copy to reuse, and whether Send will reuse, fill or make a row. People now calls them, so both pages decide a pick by one rule.
- Nothing is written until **Send email**. The row is filled or made by `materializeQuickSendRow` right before the send, as on People. Closing any step leaves nothing behind.
- `readPersonSendFactsByName` reads the person's address for a paper that is not written yet. `readPersonPaperSendFacts` (v2.7932) now reads the paper and calls it.

## The reads

`readAgreementsSendBook` (`src/lib/documents/agreementsSendIo.ts`) reads three things on the press, together: the Book through `supabaseJobPapersIo.readBook`, every person's papers with the columns a quick send needs, and the roster through `fetchRosterPeople`. A trade company's papers are left out, as on People. Each table's own row security answers.

Who can be picked is `sendRosterNames`: each living person once, by name. A twin, a sample account, a tester and an archived person are not offered.

## Choices worth knowing

- **The press is blue and sits beside the box.** It is the tab's one act that makes something, so it reads as the main press. The owner can ask for it elsewhere.
- **Job contracts are not sent from here.** A job's contract is written against its job, in its Contract window. The paper window says so in one line.
- **The roster is the view's, not the People page's.** The People page builds its names from its own two lists. The tab has neither, so it reads `roster_people`. A person both pages show is named the same on each.
