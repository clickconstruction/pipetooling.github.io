# v2.3424 — Job accounts at the counter, PR 2: the strip and the errand (2026-09-14)

Migration `20260914220000_list_job_account_strip.sql` (fragment in `docs/migrations/`); no edge function. Second of the five-PR train (PR 1: v2.3423 the record and the roster). Client cut on top of PR 1 and rebased.

## Why

The tech at the counter is the one who meets the missing account. PR 1 gave the office a record; this PR puts it on every job card the field opens and gives them one tap to ask, and gives the office an errand card with the rep's number and Mark opened on it.

## What shipped

- **RPC `list_job_account_strip(p_job_ids)`** — per readable job, one row per house that expects a job account (status NULL = none yet) plus every account the job has elsewhere, with the house's job-accounts rep (name, phone). SECURITY DEFINER with the table's own gate, because subs and helpers cannot read `supply_houses`. Hook [`useJobAccountStrips`](../../src/hooks/useJobAccountStrips.ts) (chunked, refetch on focus, RPC error = no strip).
- **The strip** [`JobAccountsStrip`](../../src/components/jobs/JobAccountsStrip.tsx): chips — teal ✓ open, purple *requested*, dashed *none yet*, grey *not needed* — on Dashboard → My Schedule (under the address line), Dashboard → Assigned Jobs, Dispatch Mode → Schedule (compact), and the job window's Job tab under the customer panel. The window's storefront icon reads teal when any account is open, not only when a packet was sent.
  - **✓ sheet**: reference (or "the house keys it on the address"), opened date + how, the rep with a `tel:` link, the note; the amber line *Tell the counter: {company} · job account for {address} · ref …* (company from the physical-invoice issuer setting), **Copy for the counter**, **Call {rep}**.
  - **Ask sheet** (from *none yet* / *requested*): the expecting houses with no account as pick chips (the tapped one preselected), *I'm at the counter now*, a note, **Send to Dispatch**; houses already requested read "Dispatch is on it · asked {date}"; the rep's number is offered for calling yourself.
- **The errand** [`openJobAccountDispatchRequest.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/openJobAccountDispatchRequest.ts): upserts a `requested` row per house (RLS: any job reader, as themselves; `ignoreDuplicates` so an office row is never overwritten), then one `dispatch_requests` row with `pending_action = 'open_job_account'`, `pending_payload = {supply_houses:[{id,name,repName,repPhone}], from_counter, note}` and a title that names the houses and says *asked from the counter, buying now*; a second ask on a job with an open card adds its houses to that card instead of a second one. Push to the group via `notify-dispatch-request`.
- **The inbox card** ([`DispatchInboxSection`](../../src/components/DispatchInboxSection.tsx)): *At the counter now* in amber, the tech's note, then one line per house — rep + phone, **Call**, teal **Mark opened…**, **Send the packet** (the existing share modal), **Not needed**. Mark opened / Not needed open PR 1's [`MarkJobAccountOpenedModal`](../../src/components/materials/MarkJobAccountOpenedModal.tsx) with the payload's rep; on save the request closes with the note *Ferguson job account open · ref …* through the new `closeDispatchRequestWithNote` on [`useDispatchInbox`](../../src/hooks/useDispatchInbox.ts), which also fires the requester's push (`notify-dispatch-request`, closed mode) — so the tech at the counter gets *Dispatch answered: … Ferguson job account open · ref JA-4114*.
- **Kernel** [`jobAccountStrip.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobAccountStrip.ts) (+6 tests): grouping and ordering, the chip words and summary, `counterSentence`, `telHref`, the payload build/parse, the request title, the close note.
- Types: `list_job_account_strip` hand-added to `Functions` (regenerate after the push).
