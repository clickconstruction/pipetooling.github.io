# v2.3764 — Where everyone is: Travel times to the office (2026-09-23)

The owner's ask, the same day v2.3756 went live: *"put next to each person the distance to the office, and the travel time to the office when a user opens the map … maybe add it as a button where after the map is open, someone can click Compute Travel Times."* Built as the button. One edge-function change (redeploy `driving-distance`), no migration.

## What shipped

- **The edge function answers the drive time** — [`driving-distance`](../../supabase/functions/driving-distance/index.ts) asks the Routes API for `routes.duration` beside `routes.distanceMeters` and returns `{ ok: true, meters, seconds }`; `seconds` is left out (never guessed) when Google gives no duration. The bid form's Distance to Office reads `meters` as before. **Deploy after merge:** `supabase functions deploy driving-distance` (`npm run check:edge-drift` names it until then). Docs: [`EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md`](../EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md#driving-distance).
- **The kernel** — [`src/lib/clockedInMapTravel.ts`](../../src/lib/clockedInMapTravel.ts) (pure, 9 tests): `computeTravelForStops` routes each placed stop to the office (the stop is the origin, the office the destination — the drive *to* the office), a few at a time, through an injected invoker; a stop the router refuses (no key, Routes off, a role the function forbids, a network miss) gets `estimateStopTravel` — `jobTravelEstimate.ts`'s straight line × 1.3 at 35 mph — marked `estimate`. Routed answers are memoized for the page by address key + office position; estimates are not, so the next press asks again. `formatTravelLine` reads `20 mi · 32 min to the office` / `≈ 26 mi · ≈ 40 min to the office`; `travelSummaryLine` counts routed vs estimated for the footer.
- **The button** — `Travel times` in the modal header (desktop) and on the phone's summary row, shown when the office anchor exists and at least one stop is placed. *Routing…* while it runs; *Travel times ✓* (disabled) once every placed stop has an answer; live again when a new stop appears. The rail rows, the popup and the phone bar swap their straight-line miles for the travel line; the footer adds *Drive times: 3 routed · 1 estimated (≈ …)*.
- Release note, this fragment, a section in the help guide *see where everyone clocked in is right now*, the architecture-doc row.

## Decisions

The time is *to* the office (the end-of-day question); the numbers are near-symmetric and the words say which. The time belongs to the stop, not the person — everyone at one job shares it; the office row and the Not-on-a-job people have none. Cost stays off the open path: nothing is routed until the button is pressed, and one Routes call per stop is a fraction of a cent. A per-address cache table (so a second day is free) is the natural next PR; the page memo covers the session.
