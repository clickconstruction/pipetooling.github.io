# v2.4054 — Pipeline money cards: the numbers on a chip are never cut off (2026-09-28)

Client only; no migration, no edge function. Items 3 and 5 of the owner's Pipeline look pass.

## What was wrong

`OpportunityChip` (v2.3822) is one `nowrap` string with an ellipsis. At four cards a row, the burn card's second line read "≈ J922 Michael Palmer (Ivan Kopecky) · 56% sp…" — the name is what you already know, the numbers are the point. The lien card's deadline chip ended "… · Michael Holub +6 · 15 noti…" for the same reason: the count sat last.

## What changed

- **`src/components/jobs/PipelineMoneyOpportunities.tsx`** — `OpportunityChip` takes an optional `fact`: the label shrinks and takes the ellipsis, the fact keeps its width. The burn chips pass the name as the label and " · 56% spent at 30% done" as the fact. The lien card draws the deadline's GCs as a second, quiet chip (`pipeline-lien-who-chip`) that opens the same pile.
- **`src/lib/jobs/lienDeskMoneyCard.ts`** — the deadline label leads with the count and the day ("15 notices by Oct 15 · in 17 days", "closed Sep 15", "today"); the GCs move to a new `who` field.

## Tests

`lienDeskMoneyCard.test.ts` — the three deadline shapes carry `who` (null, one GC, one GC on a closed window). `PipelineOverview.render.test.tsx` — the burn chips' text is unchanged end to end; the who chip renders only when there are GCs and opens the pile.
