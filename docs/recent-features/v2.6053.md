# v2.6053 — The Development page reads for estimators, with no money on it (2026-10-29)

Punch list #208 PR 6, the last planned piece of the Development page train (PR 2 v2.6040 to PR 5 v2.6048). The page read `jobs_ledger`, its stage moves, its crews, its bills and payments directly, and an estimator has no SELECT on any of them (the office set does: dev, the leaders, the assistants, the controller, a primary on their own jobs), so an estimator who opened `/developments/<id>` saw the site's bids and nothing else: no jobs, no pieces paired, no stage moves in History. The owner's plan named the fix: a definer read for the GC office team that carries no money column, and a page that draws no dollar for that reader.

## What changed

- **Migration `20261029170000_list_development_overview.sql`**: `list_development_overview(p_development_id uuid)`, SECURITY DEFINER, STABLE, for `gc_office_team()` (dev, master_technician, assistant, controller, estimator); anyone else is refused with its reason. One jsonb: the development's name, its bids and jobs (number, name, status, stage, plans state, the people on them), the jobs' stage moves (newest 500) and crews. No money column at all: never a bid's value, a job's revenue or payments, a bill, a payment or a collections note. REVOKE from PUBLIC and anon, GRANT to authenticated.
- **The door** (`src/lib/developments/readDoor.ts`, unit tests beside it): `developmentDoorFor(role)` sends the roles with a `jobs_ledger` read (the Job window's own list, `isStaffFullJobLedgerDetailRole`) through the office door and everyone else through the overview; `parseDevelopmentOverview` turns the read into the page's `MemberBid` and `MemberJob` rows with every money column null; `countKeysFor` names the tiles each door draws; the two sentences the no-money page says pass the plain-words rule.
- **The fetch** (`fetchDevelopmentPage(developmentId, includeAddress, door)`): the office path is unchanged. The overview door reads the pieces, moves and crews through the RPC, searches the address over bids alone with the money columns left out of the select, never reads a bill, a payment, a job note or the pay-speed RPC, and keeps every read the role already passes (the requests of both lanes, the bids' entries, changes and notes, the customers and their contact log, the people, the History entries).
- **The page**: waits for the role before its first read; three tiles instead of five; the Money look is one head line, *The office reads the money on this site.*, and the strip's chip reads *the office reads it*; no price under a bid, no figure under a job, the Done fold's count alone (`DevelopmentPieces` takes `moneyShown`; `countCards` takes `reader`); the Pipeline and GC Review links are not drawn, the Jobs card's foot reads *See them in Pieces ›* and scrolls there; *Pair bids with jobs* is the office's press, since pairing writes `jobs_ledger`; a job's number shows a note instead of opening the Job window, which reads `jobs_ledger` itself. Ask a question, keep a History line, Mark won, put a bid in or back on the board all stay, since they write what the estimator may write.
- Docs: `docs/migrations/20261029170000_list_development_overview.md`, the Development page row and the Developments section of `docs/ACCESS_CONTROL.md`, a *What an estimator sees* section in the guide *see everything on a development in one place*.

## Deploy

Merge, then `supabase db push` from a checkout at `main` (dry run first). Until the push the estimator's read fails with *function list_development_overview does not exist* on the page's retry panel, which names the reason; the office is unaffected either way, so client first is fine. Then the types chore (`src/types/database.ts`, the dev-mcp catalog) and `supabase functions deploy dev-mcp`.
