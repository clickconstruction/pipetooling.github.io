# v2.6029 — Messages: a push outside a person's hours waits for them, and the office hears at once when the holder is off (2026-10-28)

Punch list #141 PR 3 of 5 (`to-dos/quiet-hours/README.md` → The plan → PR 3, decisions 2, 3 and 4; the owner's yes of 2026-10-21). PR 2 (v2.6026) let each person set the days and hours their phone is pushed; nothing held a push. Now the reply push honours them: `messages-notify` holds a push for whoever is off, writes the row it would have sent as `held`, and when the person who has the reply is off the office hears at once with who is off and until when, so no customer waits on a sleeping phone. One migration, one kernel joining the copy, one function to redeploy. Follows PR 2 (#6383), which merged first.

- **The kernel** ([`pushHours.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/pushHours.ts), now mirrored, `scripts/edge-kernels.mjs`): `pushDecision(push, hoursByUser, now)` splits one push's people into those pushed now and those held, each with when they are back (`backAt`); a person with no row is pushed, and a person named twice is decided once. `holderOffWords` is the office's push when the holder is off: *Dana wrote to Luis, who is off until 6 AM.*, the time alone within the day and *Mon 6 AM* past it (`backWords`, Chicago, the house's clock words). `PUSH_HOURS_WORDS.hold` drops *From the next update*: the hold is real. The Office hours card's words (`officeHoursCard.ts`) join the copy with it.
- **The rule** ([`notifyRule.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/notifyRule.ts)): `pushesFor` reads each person's hours (`NotifySettings.pushHours`). When whoever has the reply is off at the line's time, the office hears at once as `message_reply_off`, the new template, with the hand-on words then the customer's; the holder's own push stays in the list for the planner to hold, since the holder still has the conversation on Messages and the office takes it with Who has it. A push to the office that is the Customers seat alone, the seat off at the push's time, widens to the office roles who are on then (`NotifySettings.officeRoleUserIds`); with none on it stays with the seat, held. `PushSentRow.channel` takes `held`, and `pushHeldRows` writes the held people's rows as the push would have gone.
- **The planner** ([`notifyPlan.ts`](../../src/lib/messages/notifyPlan.ts)): each due push is split at `now` with `pushDecision`; `NotifyPlan.send` goes to the people pushed now and `NotifyPlan.held` lists the people off, each with the push and when they are back. Every member off: nothing sent, a held row each. A held row counts as sent for its line and template, so the line is never pushed to that person later: no catch-up push, the card's decision 5.
- **The function** ([`_shared/messagesReplyPush.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/messagesReplyPush.ts), `messages-notify`): reads every row of `user_push_hours` once per run with the service role, fail-soft (a table not there yet, or a row that does not read, is any time), passes them to the planner, sends `plan.send`, and writes `plan.held` as one insert of `notification_history` rows with `channel = 'held'`; a refused insert is logged. The answer gains `held`, the people held.
- **The migration** (`20261028210000_notification_history_held.sql`, [`docs/migrations/20261028210000_notification_history_held.md`](../migrations/20261028210000_notification_history_held.md)): `notification_history_channel_check` widened to take `held`. No new table, so no fences.

## Deploy

After the merge, from the main checkout, in this order:

1. `supabase db push` (dry run first): PR 2's `20261028200000_user_push_hours.sql` and this PR's `20261028210000_notification_history_held.sql`, both still unapplied.
2. `supabase functions deploy messages-notify`: the function reads `user_push_hours` and writes `channel = 'held'`, so both migrations go first. Deployed before the table exists it reads nobody's hours and holds nobody; deployed before the check is widened it logs each refused held row and sends nothing to the held person.
3. Regenerate the types (`npm run gen-types:linked`, a chore PR), which PR 2's deploy asks for too.

The client is deployed by CI on the merge; its only change is the Push me block's line.

## Is this the best we can do?

1. **Do what is ready**: a holder who is off cannot answer, so the office no longer waits fifteen minutes for nothing. It hears now, with who is off and until when, and takes the reply with Who has it.
2. **Reuse**: the hold is `officeOpen` and `nextOpening` through `pushedNow` and `backAt`, the kernel the office's hours use; the words are the house's clock and day words; the row is the `notification_history` row the push would have written, one channel word apart.
3. **Honest**: a held push is recorded and never sent later, so a phone never buzzes nine times at 6 AM, and the next update's Dashboard line reads the rows this one writes.
4. **Fail-soft**: a table not there yet reads as any time, a row that does not read reads as any time, and a refused held row is logged without stopping the run.
