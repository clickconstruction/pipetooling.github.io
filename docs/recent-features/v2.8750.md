# v2.8750 — A red confirm window opens with the focus on Cancel (2026-11-27)

Punch list #443. Finding 339 of the fixing thread's ledger, from the walking thread's look at **Remove from the bench** on 2026-11-27. Client only. No migration, no edge function. Nothing owed after the merge.

**Whose word.** The person's, typed to the managing session on 2026-11-27: "Picks stand". The row as the person read it: *The app's one confirm window opens a red confirm with the focus on the press that acts, so Enter acts. Pick: open it on Cancel. It is one condition, client only, and it changes every red confirm in the app.*

**Read.** `ConfirmDialogContext.tsx` put the focus on the confirm's own press unless the caller passed `cancelIsSafe`. `danger` changed the press's colour and not the focus. So Enter on a red **Remove from the bench** removed.

**The mend.** One condition. A kernel in `src/lib/confirmDialogPress.ts`, `confirmOpensOn(options)`, answers `cancel` for a confirm that is red or asks for Cancel, and `confirm` for the rest. The window reads it where it sets the focus.

- A red confirm opens on Cancel. Enter cancels. The red press still acts when it is pressed.
- A plain confirm opens on the press that acts, as before.
- `cancelIsSafe` still gives Cancel the focus and paints it blue. Red alone paints nothing new: Cancel keeps its grey.
- Escape cancels every confirm, as before. A prompt window's Enter still submits its box, as before.

**How many it changes.** It is the app's one confirm window, so it is every red confirm. The callers were listed by reading the code's tree, not by a text search, and each one's press was read: 148 confirms pass `danger: true`, in 111 files. 9 of them already pass `cancelIsSafe` and opened on Cancel, so 139 change where the focus opens. Every one is an act that takes something away or cannot be taken back: delete, remove, discard, leave with unsaved words, turn a link or a portal off, rotate a link, revoke a key, archive, end employment, merge, un-send, reject, take back. None is a run of presses made by Enter.

The older plain `ConfirmDialog` component is a different window and is not changed.

**What a person will notice.** Someone who confirmed a delete with Enter now cancels it with Enter, and reaches the red press with Tab or a click.

**Tests.** `confirmDialogPress.test.ts`: the kernel's cases. `ConfirmDialogContext.render.test.tsx`: a red confirm opens on Cancel and Enter keeps the row, with Cancel still grey; a plain confirm opens on the act and Enter does it; `cancelIsSafe` still gives a blue Cancel the focus, red or not; Escape cancels a red and a plain confirm; a red confirm still acts on its own press. The 79 test files that open a confirm pass unchanged.

**To see it live.** Open any red confirm and press nothing: the focus ring is on **Cancel**. Press Escape. Nothing is removed for the proof.
