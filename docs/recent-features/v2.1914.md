# v2.1914 — Bid Board: due-date colors quiet once a bid is sent (2026-08-20)

Follow-up to v2.1857 (which quieted the chips in the decided sections). The
Pending section ("Not yet won or lost") still showed red/amber urgency on due
dates — false alarms, since those bids were already sent and the wait is on the
GC. Now `bidBoardDueCellParts` takes the bid's `bid_date_sent`: any sent bid
renders the quiet gray chip. Pending keeps its `(+N)` day count as
waiting-time context; decided sections continue to drop the count entirely.
Red/amber are now exclusive to the Unsent section, where a due date still
means a bid has to go out.

- `src/lib/bids/bidBoardDateCells.ts` — `dateSent` param; urgency = normal when sent (+ tests)
- `src/components/bids/BidsBidBoardTab.tsx` — chip caller passes `bid_date_sent`; legend copy updated
