# v2.3616 — Supervision, PR 5: the Team leads list retires — My Team reads the crew you supervised (2026-09-19)

PR 5 of five, the last of the Supervision train (v2.3611 the switch · v2.3612 the Dispatch mark · v2.3613 My crew · v2.3614 Rate my crew). Client only; no migration. The to-do `to-dos/supervision/` is deleted with this PR; what is left of Team leads is the residual `to-dos/team-leads-table-retirement.md`.

## The change

- **Gone**: `TeamLeadsModal` / `TeamLeadsManager` / `useTeamLeaderAssignments` / `teamLeadsGrouping` (and their tests), the **Team leads** button and phone-menu entry on People → Users, `PersonDeskTeamSection` on the Person Desk, the lifecycle checklist's *Team lead* items (`assign_leader` / `remove_leader` kinds, the leader picker in the Start / End employment modal, the `leaders` facts), `canEditTeamLeads` / `canEditLeaderVisibility`, and the guide `manage-team-leads.md`.
- **My Team** ([`useDashboardMyTeamSectionState.ts`](../../src/hooks/useDashboardMyTeamSectionState.ts)): no longer reads `team_leader_assignments`. With `supervisedMembershipEnabled` (dev / master / assistant-like) the roster is the distinct crew of `get_supervised_days_payload(this week)`, every member "full"; otherwise the roster is empty and the section hides. The per-member notify bell is gone (`team_leader_clock_notify_prefs` is no longer read or written). [`DashboardMyTeamSection.tsx`](../../src/components/DashboardMyTeamSection.tsx) takes `canApprove` — dev, assistant-like, or a master in `pay_approved_masters` (read once on the Dashboard) — which gates the pending banner, the header chip and the whole pending-approval block; everyone else sees the roster and the week read-only.
- **Frozen, not dropped**: `team_leader_assignments` and `team_leader_clock_notify_prefs` stay, with the `is_team_lead_for_member` branches in the `clock_sessions` policies and the approve / revoke / restore / NCNS RPCs, `notify-team-lead-clock`, and the report-email team-lead scope — all serving the rows that exist, none gaining new rows. The sweep is one release away (`to-dos/team-leads-table-retirement.md`).
- Guides: `approve-my-teams-hours.md` re-based on approvers and the supervised crew; `supervise-a-crew.md`, `open-a-persons-desk.md`, `crew-pnl.md`, `email-reports-to-people.md`, `say-who-can-run-a-job.md` amended. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (five Team-leads lines), `PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md` (the two tables marked frozen; §8 Dashboard), `PEOPLE_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `EDGE_FUNCTIONS.md` (notify-team-lead-clock frozen), `DASHBOARD_SECTIONS_ARCHITECTURE.md` (§17), `GLOSSARY.md`.
