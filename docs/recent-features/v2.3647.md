# v2.3647 — Contracts: Edit & re-send while unopened (2026-09-20)

PR 6 — the last — of [`to-dos/contract-paper-lane/README.md`](../../to-dos/contract-paper-lane/README.md): *the fix for what actually happened on 2026-09-03. Nobody asked for it; it is the highest-value item in the train.* One small migration; no edge function.

## What happened on Sep 3

The only real attempt at the e-sign flow: a Michael Palmer job sent at revision 1 (opened once), revised and sent again (never opened), revised and sent a third time (never opened), then signed on paper the next day. Each revision was **Void & redo** — a voided row with `void_reason = 'Revised by the office'` and a fresh draft — so one job carries three voided contracts, two of them voided though nobody had ever read them.

## The rule — [`jobContractReopen.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/jobContractReopen.ts)

`reopenBlocker` offers Edit & re-send only on a `sent` row with `first_viewed_at` null and `view_count` 0, sent as a link or as a PDF email. Not once they have opened it (what they read stays on the record — Void & redo), not a page `handed` over on paper (it cannot be edited out of their hands), not a draft, a signed or a voided row.

`reopenUnopenedJobContract` is one **guarded** update — `status 'draft'`, `revision + 1`, `next_reminder_at NULL` — with `.eq('status','sent').is('first_viewed_at', null)`, so a customer who opens the link between the office's read and the write wins: zero rows come back and the window says *They opened it just now*. Nothing else on the row moves: `public_token`, `sent_at`, `send_count` and `sent_channel` stay, so the next **Send** (`send-job-contract` reuses the token) goes out on the link they already hold, and the history reads as one agreement sent twice. While the row is a draft, `get-job-contract` already answers that link with *Nothing has been sent to this link yet* — the customer never sees a half-edited page. The lock on a sent agreement was always the client's, never the database's, so no function changes.

A `reopened` event records who and when (`from_revision`, `to_revision`, `channel`) — [`20260920220000_job_contract_events_reopened.sql`](../migrations/20260920220000_job_contract_events_reopened.md) adds the type; the insert is fail-soft before the push.

## The window — [`JobContractModal.tsx`](../../src/components/jobs/JobContractModal.tsx)

**Edit & re-send** sits in the sent strip beside Void & redo and arms first, like it: the strip then carries `reopenNote` — *They have not opened it. It unlocks here as revision 2; the same link carries it when you send again, and shows nothing until you do.*, or for a PDF email, *They have revision 1 as a PDF in their inbox … file only a signed revision 2* — with **Confirm — unlock to edit** and *Never mind*. After the unlock the window re-reads the row, which is now an ordinary editable draft (autosave, Send, the lot). When they have opened it the button is absent and one line says why Void & redo is the way.

The sweep needs nothing: a reopened row is a draft again, so its job is back in the pile until it is re-sent — which is true.
