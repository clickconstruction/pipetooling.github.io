# v2.5313 — Contracts & terms: Papers on the flows (2026-10-12)

Messages cut 4, wave 2's F3 (helper 6). Settings → Contracts & terms puts every paper the office adds to a step of a flow on the index, the "line on the index" the build plan's *A new paper from the editor* asks for. The New paper window (v2.5307) makes these papers as Contract library documents with `flow_kind` set (`flow_paper_create`, v2.5306). Stacked on the guard (#5484, v2.5312), which keeps them out of the Book's own customer documents on this same tab.

- **A data-driven area.** `ContractCatalogArea` gains `flows`, labeled *Papers on the flows* and last in `CONTRACT_AREA_ORDER`. The catalog writes no entry there. `DATA_DRIVEN_AREAS` names it, and `contractCatalogProblems` refuses a written entry in it and a flow paper outside it.
- **`flowPaperEntries.ts`** makes one entry per paper each time the tab reads the library: the trade partners' first, then the customers' and the architects', each by name. A row without a kind the window makes is skipped.
  - The entry id is `flow-paper-<uuid>`, so each paper has its own anchor, review and compare key.
  - The audience is new: `trade` (*Trade partner*) for the library audience `sub`, `flow_customer` (*Customer or architect*) for `customer`.
- **What each kind reads as** (`ContractTextSource` kind `flow_paper`):
  - An **upload** is the new group `theirs` and the new status *Their paper*, with its own lens chip and a count of *N they give us*. It has no wording of ours. The card says how many days ahead the next one is asked for.
  - A **contract** reads as its live clauses (`contract_clauses`, not retired), numbered, a clause kept to a trade saying so, then the Spanish (an empty Spanish clause reads as the English, as on the sign page). Its version is cut 4c's `revision`. With no clause published it is *Nothing set*, so *Needs a look* catches a contract whose clauses still wait. Its door, `ContractEditDoor` kind `clauses`, opens the paper's card on Contracts and clauses for the office (`canEditClause`) and says where for anyone else.
  - A **notice** reads as its English, then its Spanish, dated the day it was written. Without English it is *Nothing set*.
- **History**: `contractSourceRef` reads a flow paper's versions as the library document's (`contract_book`). The trigger keeps them for the customer audience only.
- **The tab** reads the papers with `fetchFlowPapers.ts`, untyped until the types are regenerated after F1's push. That read never fails the tab: on an error the section stays out. On a trade partner's paper the card's line reads **The trade partner**.
- **Tests**:

  - The catalog test learns the data-driven area: flow papers pass every check beside the written entries, and a written entry there is refused.
  - The index test: the order, and no written entry in a data-driven area.
  - The tab's render test: the section, the Book card kept to the one service agreement, the clauses door. Its mock honors the two `flow_kind` filters. Removing the tab's guard on purpose failed it.
