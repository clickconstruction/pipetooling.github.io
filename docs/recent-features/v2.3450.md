## Why

Mock-up moment 3 and decision 5, both taken as recommended by the owner on 2026-09-14. Bill Customer is the last net before a notice is due: the first bill on a GC job is the moment the office is already looking at the job, so the Send-to block says what the appraisal roll answers and asks for one click — never a gate. The Lien desk's *Needs the owner* pile had only the *Find the owner ›* door (three doors deep); now it shows the roll's answer in place. Two things the roll surfaced on the live pile that a paste would have hidden are now rules, not chips: a **public owner** (J690 — the City of Round Rock) is never drafted, because a mechanic's lien does not attach to public property and the remedy is a claim on the GC's payment bond; and the nightly save (decision 5) exists as a **switch that is off**, so the roll's answer can flow in unlooked-at only once the first sitting has shown it is right — and even then a person confirms before *Record the run*.

## What shipped

- **Bill Customer, one line** ([`BillCustomerOwnerLine`](../../src/components/jobs/BillCustomerOwnerLine.tsx), mounted in `SendRecordInvoiceModal`'s Send-to block): on a job that names a GC (or a builder in the customer row) with no confirmed owner, a green quiet line — *Owner of record for 5498 Cibolo Valley Dr 200: Schertz Station Ltd (Guadalupe Appraisal District 2025) — not yet on the job. **Use** it so the lien notice can be mailed when it is due.* Use calls `confirmOwnerForProperty` (PR 1's shared write). A roll miss reads *No owner of record on file — paste the county's page…* and unfolds the property record's paste box in place. An owner the nightly run saved *from the roll · unconfirmed* offers **Confirm** instead. Kernel: `shouldShowBillCustomerOwnerLine` in [`ownerConfirm.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirm.ts) (+3 tests) — GC or builder-customer, no job override, record missing / ownerless / unconfirmed.
- **The desk reads the roll** ([`LienDeskOwnerPane`](../../src/components/jobs/LienDeskOwnerPane.tsx) under *Before it can go out*): an ownerless item shows *The roll says: <owner> · mail to …*, the reads-as chips, the district · year provenance, the CAD link and **Use**; the *Find the owner ›* door stays as the fallback (and alone on a miss). Use writes the property and links the job; the desk re-reads and the row moves to *To draft*.
- **Public owners are never drafted**: `draftReadiness()` in [`lienDesk.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDesk.ts) (+3 tests) returns `no_gc · no_owner · public_owner · no_months` in pane order; `public_owner` uses PR 1's `ownerKind`. The pane and the footer read *Public property — a mechanic's lien does not attach; the remedy is a claim on the GC's payment bond. Talk to the attorney.*; Send for approval and the spoken-word send are disabled.
- **From the roll · unconfirmed**: `ownerFromRollUnconfirmed(row)` (owner on the record, parcel provenance set, `owner_confirmed_at` NULL — a hand-typed owner never reads this way). The desk pane shows *Owner from the roll (2025) · unconfirmed · confirm on Guadalupe CAD ↗* with **Confirm** (`stampOwnerConfirmed` in [`ownerConfirmWrite.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/ownerConfirmWrite.ts)); drafting is allowed on it, and **Record the run refuses** while any notice in the run has one — `RunNotice.ownerUnconfirmed` + `runNoticeProblems` in [`lienDeskRun.ts`](../../src/lib/jobs/lienDeskRun.ts) (+1 test) mark the recipient red like a missing address.
- **The nightly switch, off**: `app_settings.owner_auto_confirm_from_roll_v1` ('false' by the migration; a key-scoped UPDATE policy for `is_master_or_dev()`), [`ownerAutoConfirmSetting.ts`](../../src/lib/ownerAutoConfirmSetting.ts), and [`OwnerAutoConfirmSettingsBlock`](../../src/components/settings/OwnerAutoConfirmSettingsBlock.tsx) on Settings → Jobs & billing (dev + master): *Save owners from the appraisal roll automatically* with the decision-5 sub-line.
- **[`owner-confirm-nightly`](../../supabase/functions/owner-confirm-nightly/index.ts)** (pg_cron 08:15 UTC nightly): when the switch is on, every `list_jobs_owner_to_confirm()` row with `has_owner` false (the RPC now answers the service role too) is grouped by property, looked up the way `property-lookup` does (geocode cache → Google → Census → the parcel under the pin, via the new shared [`_shared/txParcelIdentify.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/txParcelIdentify.ts)), and written exactly as Use would — the plan kernel moved to [`_shared/ownerConfirmPlan.ts`](../../supabase/functions/_shared/ownerConfirmPlan.ts) and the client re-exports it — with `owner_confirmed_at` NULL and the parcel provenance columns set; a row that already names an owner is never touched; up to 40 properties a night, earliest deadline first; `{ "dry_run": true }` looks up and writes nothing; one summary line per run.
- **One lookup cache** ([`propertyLookupCache.ts`](../../src/lib/customers/propertyLookupCache.ts)): the Fix-ups list, Bill Customer and the desk share one per-property session cache with in-flight de-duplication.
- Guides: *file a lien and never miss its deadlines* → "The app finds the owner; you confirm it" gains the desk, Bill Customer and the switch; *send lien notices from the Lien desk* gains the public-owner rule and the Confirm-before-the-run rule. `ACCESS_CONTROL.md` (the switch: master + dev; the cron runs as the service role), `GLOSSARY.md`: *from the roll · unconfirmed*.
