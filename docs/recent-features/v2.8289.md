# v2.8289 — The new phone layout: Needs you is one line on the Dashboard, and the list opens one thing at a time (2026-11-22)

Behind the **New phone layout** switch (v2.8285). With the switch off, or at a desk, nothing here draws differently. No migration and no edge function. One small component is new: the Lien desk's strip, lifted out of the card so the Dashboard can draw it too.

## What a phone gets

Needs you is one list that was already drawn three ways: the card's **Cards** stack, the card's **Walk the list** deck, and the one-line door *Needs you · 10 · the first line* that opens an Inbox tab. This PR draws nothing new. It chooses which of the three a phone gets.

- **On the Dashboard, the door.** Wherever the page's mode has an Inbox tab, the Dashboard draws the door in place of the card, and the door opens that tab.
- **Where the list is worked, Walk the list.** The card opens on Walk the list for every role with no saved choice. The **Cards** chip is beside it as before.
- **A saved choice still wins.** A person who pressed **Cards** on that phone keeps Cards. A press on either chip is kept as it always was.

The rule is the page's mode, not the role, so it holds for a role that changes mode.

| The page's mode | The Dashboard, switch on | The door opens |
|---|---|---|
| Dispatch Mode | the door | Dispatch Mode's Inbox, `/dispatch-mode/inbox` |
| Job Mode, its own Dashboard | the door, while a line is the viewer's own | Job Mode's Inbox, `/job-mode/inbox` |
| Job Mode, behind **Show full dashboard** | the door | Job Mode's Inbox |
| No mode, or Task Mode | the card, opening on Walk the list | no Inbox tab to lead to |

## Where each thing went

Nothing is removed. Where the door replaces the card on a Dashboard, each piece of the list is one press away in the Inbox tab the door opens, and the Lien desk's strip stays on the Dashboard.

| Was on the Dashboard | Is now |
|---|---|
| The Needs you card, with every line and its press | The Inbox tab, one press on the door |
| The count of the viewer's own lines, the card's amber pill | On the door, and in the card's pill |
| The first line | On the door, and the first card of Walk the list |
| *N for others* beside the pill | In the card's header in the Inbox tab |
| The Lien desk's four dots and **The Board ›** | On the Dashboard, under the door, as a card of its own. It is at the top of the card in the Inbox tab too, as before |
| The folds, *The office is on these* and the rest | In the card: Walk the list reaches them after the viewer's own, and Cards draws them folded |
| **Cards** and **Walk the list** | The two chips at the foot of the card, as before |

| The office's round | On the Dashboard, under the door, where it was. It is under the card in the Inbox tab too, as before |
| The green *done for the week* notice | On the Dashboard, as before |

The office person's phone with Dispatch Mode on had the door already (v2.3883). Its door takes the Lien desk's strip, the office's round and *Waiting on the leader* to the Inbox tab with the list, and that is unchanged, switch off or on. A door that is new under the switch stands in for the list alone (`cardsStay`), so the desk's four numbers and the red *No round* mark a leader reads on his Dashboard today are still on his Dashboard.

## The door's words

The door was built for the assistant, before the list said whose each line is (v2.5843). It counted every line and named the list's first. A leader's list is mostly the office's, so on his phone the door read *Needs you · 33 · 11 filed liens have not been served* over a card whose pill says 8 and whose first card is *Sign 7 lien notices before the run*.

Under the switch the door says what the card it opens says (`needsYouDoorLine`): the count is the viewer's own lines and the first line is the first of them. With none of the viewer's own the door reads *Needs you · 0 · 25 for others*, as the card's header does. On Job Mode's own Dashboard, where the door stands in for no card, a list with none of the viewer's own draws no door.

With the switch off the door reads as it read: every line counted.

## How it is built

- `src/lib/phoneLayout/needsYouPlace.ts` is the rule. `needsYouDashboardDoor({ newPhoneLayout, phoneOffice, mode })` answers the door, with the tab it opens and whether the cards under the list stay, or null for the card. `needsYouJobModeDoor` answers for Job Mode's own Dashboard. `needsYouDoorLine` answers the door's count and first line. With the switch off each answers what the page answered before.
- `needsYouInitialMode` in `src/lib/dashboard/phoneOffice.ts` takes `newPhoneLayout`: with no saved choice, Walk.
- `DashboardNeedsYouCard` reads `useNewPhoneLayout().on` and hands it to that kernel. Its Lien desk strip is now `NeedsYouLienBoardStrip`, with the markup it had.
- `NeedsYouLienBoardStrip` draws the strip in the card and, `standalone`, on the Dashboard under a door that keeps the cards. `needsYouLienStripUnderDoor` in the kernel says when: a door is drawn, the plan keeps the cards, and the page draws its banners. On its own the strip is a card: *Lien desk* and **The Board ›** on a head row, the four dots under it. The click is counted as `#lien-board` in both places.
- `DashboardPinnedQuickRow` takes `needsYouDoorTo`, the tab the door opens, and `needsYouDoorKeepsCards`. The door is no longer held back by `hideBanners`, so Job Mode's own Dashboard can draw it alone. No caller passed both before, so nothing changed with the switch off. The hooks that read the list run there only while the door is asked for.
- `Dashboard.tsx` asks the kernel in two places: the full page and Job Mode's own page.

## What the look changed

Walked at 393 × 852 on live data through View as, the sample accounts.

1. **The door's count and first line.** The first picture of a leader's door read 33 and an office line. The card it opens reads 8 and his own first line. The door now reads the card's count and line.
2. **No door for a list with none of the viewer's own, on Job Mode's own Dashboard.** An amber line that says 0 on a field hand's first screen is noise. Where the door replaces a card it stays, since the card would be there.
3. **The office's round stays on the Dashboard.** The door as built took the two cards under the list to the Inbox tab with it. On a leader's Dashboard that hid the red *No round* pill. A door that is new under the switch now replaces the Needs you card and nothing else.

4. **The Lien desk's strip stays on the Dashboard.** The owner's before and after review found the door took the desk's four numbers to the Inbox tab with the card. A leader reads them on his Dashboard every day, so a door that is new under the switch now keeps the strip under it. Drawn as the card's one wrapping line it stood 109 px with **The Board ›** alone on a third row. As a card with a head row it stands 95 px at 393 and 120 px at 375.
