**What the user does.** *Cover Letter*: pick a bid (standard picker), choose which bid versions / offered prices go in which GC's letter and which with-and-without alternates are offered as add-ons, set content (pills, inclusions / exclusions / terms, schedule of values, payment schedule, materials by stage, bid basis), preview with click-to-edit alternate wording, then Print / Copy-to-Google-Docs / attach the submission link, and **Mark sent** (stamps `bids.bid_date_sent` + `bids.bid_value`, writes `bid_version_sends`). *Submittals*: one road of eight steps — build Rev 1 from the Pricing picks or the takeoff's fixtures, fix each row and its parts, drop vendor PDFs and assign pages to rows, build the stamped package, share the review room, read or enter the GC's answers, ask the submittal robot, start the next draft, and keep the procurement log. *Builder Review*: one card per customer (all trades) — outcome sections, quick contact log, PIA / snooze / promised follow-up, contact persons, call sheets and a call session.

Neither child may own selection.
- **Cover Letter's data engine is split.** The parent owns the pricing engine (`bidVersions`, `priceBookVersions`, `pricingCountRows`, `coverLetterPricingRows`, active version / pricing ids) and the 8 content maps; the tab runs a **second, private engine** (CL-5 bundle pricing, CL-6 version GC map + sends, CL-9 schedule / materials / schedule of values, CL-3 defaults) through 8 of its 11 effects (330, 347, 409, 471, 495, 729, 748, 765; the other three — 246, 267 and 293 — are CL-11's alt-text hydrate, CL-13's live stamp and the quick-add reset). Hook seam before any Stage B: `useCoverLetterBundle` (CL-5 + CL-6) and `usePaymentSchedule` (CL-9). The derived letter (CL-4) is the substrate every card reads — after Stage A it is one call whose result is passed down.
- **Submittals has its own engine** (SU-3): one bid's revisions, items and their parts, room, people, events, messages, tasks, picks and takeoff candidates. Every panel reads and reloads it through the tab's props; no hook (see *The split*).
- **Builder Review has no selection pointer.** It is customer-keyed; its substrate is parent caches (`peopleBids`, `gcPacketsByBid`, customers, contacts, contact persons, `lastContactFromEntries`) plus the parent-owned deep-link highlight, and a private prefs store (`customer_followup_prefs`, BR-4).
- The three tabs share **no state with each other**. Shared kits: the bid-picker pair (`BidPickerSearchRow`, `BidPickerStandardList`) + search predicate (Cover Letter, Submittals), `BidWorkflowTabTitleWithPreview` + `bidPreview` (Cover Letter, Submittals), `CustomerNotesTable`, `BidLostQuickPopover`, the stale kernels and `localStorage['bids_followup_stale_days']` (Builder Review with the Followup "By status" lens).

## Hazards

**Money paths (Cover Letter).**
- Mark sent writes `bids.bid_date_sent`, `bids.bid_value` and `bid_version_sends.value`; the value is `headlineAmount` (CL-4) — custom amount, else (version bundle active) `boardValueForRule` (org rule from `app_settings`) falling back to the letter total, else (no bundle) active pricing. `markSentToday` stamps `bid_value` only for the bid's own GC (`isOwnGc`), only sections with a version and revenue > 0 get send rows, and the roll-up date is the first send (the `sync_bid_date_sent_from_sends` trigger enforces the same rule).
- **Display ≠ stamp:** on an all-alternates same-page packet the studio shows `displayHeadlineAmount` (the leading alternate) while Mark sent passes `headlineAmount` (the base sum — 0 there under the default `base_sum` rule → no `bid_value` write; under `active_star` it is the active pricing's revenue). Keep the split. `bestEffortAmount` is a third number: the headline plus the offered add-ons, handed only to `BidBestEffortCard`.
- `totalsResolved` (J13-F3) gates Print, Copy & open in Google Docs and the header Print only; Mark sent has its own `> 0` guards.
- The Approval PDF's letter page plans its sections, packet and layout through `coverLetterDocument.ts` like this tab (v2.4373), and its wording the same way (v2.4375): the parent's `downloadApprovalPdf` (844–863) passes the bid's own exclusions / terms entries, and the PDF reads the org defaults fresh (`coverLetterOrgDefaultsFrom`) and hands the builders `letterWording`, the closing included. Its custom-amount line (`effectiveRevenue`) is still a copy, and it still turns fixtures-per-plan on whenever there is no plan date, where the tab keeps the two toggles independent. The 8 content maps are session-only (lost on reload). Out of scope for a move.
- Payment-schedule row writes (insert / update / delete / reorder / stage shares) and version / pricing writes (`toggleVersionInclude`, `setScenarioOffered`, `setVersionAlternate`, `reorderVersion`) check **no error and no refused-row count**; only `bids` updates use `bidUpdateRefused`. `togglePaymentScheduleEnabled` seeds default rows when the closure's `paymentScheduleRows` is empty — toggling before the hydrate read (347) lands can seed on top of existing rows.
- The add-on stamp (CL-13) writes `bids.cover_letter_alt_texts` from an effect, on a timer, with no user gesture: a reprice on the Pricing tab reaches the bid row the next time this tab is open on it. It must never run before the bid's wording has loaded (`altTextsLoadedFor`), or it saves the empty wording over the saved Offer ticks.
- Bundle pricing (CL-5) re-prices with zeroed cost inputs — revenue only; do not reuse it for cost.

**RLS / role gates.** `resolveBidsTabRoute` (`lib/bids/bidsTabAccess.ts`) turns a superintendent away from `SUPERINTENDENT_OFFICE_BIDS_TABS` (`pricing`, `cover-letter`, `submittals` and the office Followup lenses) and a primary away from every tab outside `PRIMARY_BIDS_TABS`; the router in `hooks/useBidsDeepLinks.ts` lands them on the Bid board and says so. `bidFlowDoorAllowed` (Bids.tsx 676–678) keeps superintendents off the pricing / cover-letter doors; `builder-review` is the superintendent's Followup landing (1385). Read-only (training) users: `bids` writes surface `BID_UPDATE_NOT_APPLIED_MESSAGE`; every other write on these tabs fails silently or throws generically. Untyped clients: Submittals `db` (123) and Builder Review `(supabase as any)` ×5 — column typos are not type-checked. Submittals storage: private bucket `bid-submittals`, 300 s signed URLs; public room links are bearer tokens (`newRoomToken`, `/submittal` route) — "Copy link" and the strip's copy action hand them out.

**Realtime / events.** No Supabase realtime. Window events: Cover Letter listens to and dispatches `bid-version-sends-changed` (also fired by `BidGcSentPanel`; heard by `BidVersionPicker`, `BidPackageSendsDetails`, the parent's `useBidGcPackets`). DOM ids `cover-letter-generate`, `cover-letter-submission-link`, `cover-letter-mark-sent` (both Mark-sent buttons carry it; one renders at a time) are bid-flow landing targets named in `lib/bids/bidFlow.ts` 135–137 — any JSX move keeps them. `cover-letter-schedule-of-values-pill` / `cover-letter-materials-by-stage-pill` / `cover-letter-bid-basis-pill` are set too.
