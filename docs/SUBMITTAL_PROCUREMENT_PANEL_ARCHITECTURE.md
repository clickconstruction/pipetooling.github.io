## What this surface is

[`SubmittalProcurementPanel`](../src/components/bids/SubmittalProcurementPanel.tsx) is the body of step 8, *Procure*, on Bids → Submittals ([`BidsSubmittalsTab`](../src/components/bids/BidsSubmittalsTab.tsx) mounts it under `data-tour="submittals-procure"`; the parent's seam is [`BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md`](./BIDS_DOCUMENT_TABS_ARCHITECTURE.md) §SU-13). It has two importers: the tab and its render test. The tab hands it the newest revision's lines (`items`, built by `procurementItemsFrom`), the reviewers' names, the letterhead, the room link, the houses, and five doors back (`onCounts`, `onOpenItem`, `onAnswerItem`, `onLinesChanged`, `onEnterApproval`). Everything else — the log's own records, the updates sent, each tag's stage from the takeoff, the job's stage dates — it reads and writes itself.

The log grew in four trains: v2.4083 (the log, its dates editor, Send update and the printed sheet), 2026-10-01/02 (a line per part, the three lenses, the tick bar, the missing facts, the doors into the row's windows), v2.4122 (the sheet as a letter to the GC) and the redraw of 2026-10-05 (v2.4581 the four steps and the Next line · v2.4587 the orders · v2.4592 the calendar · v2.4600 the phone cards; its card was PR #4527, closed unmerged). The 2026-10-05 fact sheet: **1,611 lines**, 31 `useState`, 4 effects, 9 `useMemo`, 4 `useCallback`, 5 `useRef`, 3 custom hooks; 7 components (six small drawing pieces at module scope and the panel); tables `bid_procurement_items` and `bid_procurement_updates` direct, the rest through `procurementLogIo`.

**The rule the redraw followed**: logic lives in pure kernels under `src/lib/submittals/` and the panel draws. The panel still owns 31 state values and every write, so the extraction targets below are drawn pieces (the calendar, an order row, the dates editor), not hooks.

## Per-region dossiers

**A. Module scope.** `Props` 58–88: `bidId`, `bidLabel`, `companyName`, `items` (`ProcurementItemSource[]`, the tab's `procItems`), `reviewerNames`, `currentUser`, `letterhead`, `projectAddress`, `gcName`, `roomUrl`, `busy`, `houses`, and the five doors (`onCounts` with the four step counts, `onOpenItem({ itemId, partKey, house? })`, `onAnswerItem({ itemId, partKey })`, `onLinesChanged`, `onEnterApproval`). The looks are `CSSProperties` objects (`th`, `td`, `itemTd`, `editorField`, `btn`, `btnPrimary`, `link`, `inp`); the calendar's are `CAL_TD` (a third of the table, 300 px least), `CAL_INSET` (12 px clear at each edge), the three saturated literals `CAL_BLUE` / `CAL_RED` / `CAL_AMBER` and the tone maps. The six pieces: `Diamond` 115–117 (filled for order by, open for the GC's answer), `CalendarLines` 119–126 (today in blue down every row, each stage's needed day in grey), `CalendarCell` 128–142 (one row's cell: the lines, then a mark's dotted run, bar, late bar, tick and diamond; `aria-label` = the mark in words), `CalendarHead` 144–159 (each stage named beside its line, the Mondays, *today* in blue; every label sits on the surface so a line cannot strike through it), `Chevron` 161–167 (the fold), `Rails` 189–198 (By tag's tree connectors by column). `readLens` 208–215 remembers the lens per device.

**B. Loader + derived rows.** One effect 311–332 reads four things in parallel for a `bidId` and the items' tags (`tagsKey`): the records, the updates, each tag's stage (`loadTagStagesForBid`: count rows + stage splits), the job's stage dates (`loadStageDatesForBid`: the newest job made from the bid, its Order stages' windows). A second 335–343 fetches the logo and the room's QR ahead so a print stays inside its click. `rootRef` 259–270 measures the box with a `ResizeObserver`: `narrow` = a phone (`useNarrowViewport640`) or a box narrower than `TABLE_MIN_WIDTH` 760, so a side pane gets cards. Then the memos, in order: `rows` 345 (`buildProcurementLog`: every line's status, order-by, expected, float), `gcRows` 347 (what the GC's copies carry: no order-only parts), `blockers` 348, `shown` 350 (a step's lines, else a blocker's, else all), `steps` 351, `sections` 354 (By tag / By house only; To order is `orders`), `changes` 356 (against the last update sent), `orders` 361 (`orderSections` over `shown.rows`, new against an update that has gone), `axis` 363 (`calendarAxis` over every line, so a filter does not move the weeks). Effect 368–372 hands `onCounts` the journey's four numbers plus the step counts. **Hazard**: `rows` is rebuilt on every records write; `renderRow` closes over it, so the whole table re-renders after each save — fine at 47 lines, watch it at 300.

**C. Drafts + the one write queue.** `write` 393–416 is the only path to `bid_procurement_items` for a line: an update when the record exists (found through `recordsRef`, by `partKey` or by tag), else an insert with the next `sort_order`; writes run one at a time through `writeQueue` so the second box filled on a new row updates the record the first made (v2.4239). `dateCell` 465–510 is one date box: a text box as wide as what it holds, the browser's calendar behind it (`openCalendar`, opened by a click into a box not already in use — `clickedInto`), a typed date saved on blur or Enter (`commitTypedDate`: a half-typed date is never written), and a soft line under it (*2 days ago*, *house said*). `commitText` 512–533 saves PO, note, a hand row's label and lead time.

**D. Marking and setting facts.** Two ways to mark ordered: the tick bar's `markTicked` 536–554 (every ticked line, one day, one PO) and, since v2.4587, `markGroup` 557–571 on an order (its ticked parts when any, else the whole group — `rowsToMark`). Two ways to set a house, lead time or stage: the tick bar's `setTickedFacts` 599–614 and, since v2.4581, `setOnBlocker` 617–631 from *Set…* on the missing-facts line; both go through `saveFacts` 574–589 → `setLineFacts` on the rows' parts, then `onLinesChanged` so the tab reads its rows again. These write the submittal rows, not the log.

**E. Filters.** `onlyPicked` (a missing fact's lines) and `stepPicked` (a step's lines) are one filter at a time: `showOnly` 639 clears the step, `pickStep` 646 clears the blocker; effect 634 drops a blocker that names nothing any more. Under a filter every order is open (`isOpen` in L) so the short list shows its lines.

**G. The sheet, the file, the update.** `letter` 682–692 is the printed sheet's fixed part (company, project, GC, prepared by, the room's link and QR). `sendAndRecord` 739–756 inserts the update with `snapshotRows(gcRows)` and `changes`, then prints it (`printAndFile`: a print is a sent copy, `docs/SENT_COPIES.md`) and copies its text for the email. `printLog` 699 prints the log as it stands. The ⋯ menu (I) holds `printLog`, `downloadCsv` 706 (a blob from `procurementLogCsv`, saved from the app's own address), `openInGoogleSheets` 717 (copy the TSV, open `sheets.new`) and the updates list.

**H. `renderRow`.** One function draws a line three ways. The shared pieces come first: the part cell (tag, maker and model, catalog words; a quiet line for *in place of the priced …*, *added by hand*, *in <assembly>*, *order only*, *approved on Rev N*, *counted with*), `tick`, `item` (a hand row's label box, else a button that opens the row's Edit window through `onOpenItem`), `house` (the same door with `house: true`), `stage` and `lead` (a hand row types its own), `statusButton` (`lineStatus`: *Order by 10/06* · *Arrives 10/20, 14 d late* · *On site 09/29*; a tap opens the line's dates), `answerButton` (`answerDoor`: *Enter their answer…* on a line the GC holds, *Change their answer…* on one sent back), `editor` 863–900 (Ordered · PO · Expected · Delivered · Note for the GC · *The GC and the job*). ## Recommended extraction order (value ÷ risk)

1. **The calendar pieces** (A: `Diamond`, `CalendarLines`, `CalendarCell`, `CalendarHead`, ~50 lines) → `ProcurementCalendarCells.tsx` with a render test over `calendarAxis` / `groupMark` on the SpaceX fixtures. No state; the panel's test already draws them.
2. **The orders** (L + N, ~210 lines) → `ProcurementOrdersTable.tsx` and `ProcurementOrderCards.tsx`, props in and callbacks out, the line's drawing handed down as a render prop until 3 lands. Moves half the render out.
3. **The line's editor** (H's `editor` + C's `dateCell`) → `ProcurementLineEditor.tsx`, with `write` passed in. The hardest: `drafts`, `clickedInto` and the write queue travel together.
4. **The steps strip** (I) → `ProcurementSteps.tsx`, presentational.
5. `rowsFromSnapshot` (O) → `procurementLog.ts`.

## Hazards

- **One filter, two states.** `onlyPicked` and `stepPicked` must never both be set; `showOnly` and `pickStep` clear each other. A third filter needs one state with a kind, not a third flag.
- **The calendar's axis reads every line, the orders read `shown.rows`.** Keep it so: a filter that moved the weeks would make the marks jump.
- **`changes` is against `gcRows`, so an order-only part is never "new".** By design; the GC's copies leave those off.
- **A print is a sent copy** (`printAndFile`): every print and update files a row under the bid. A preview that must not file goes through `buildProcurementUpdateHtml` without it.
- **`lens` is per device** (`localStorage`); a test that does not set it inherits the last one. The render test sets it in `beforeEach`.
- **The panel's `narrow` is container width, not only viewport**: a side pane under 760 px gets cards and loses the calendar.
