### Mount/state semantics (preserve in every extraction)

- `Jobs.tsx` (1900) renders `<JobsStagesTab ref={stagesTabRef} active={activeTab === 'stages'} …/>` **unconditionally**. The board is `{active && …}` (3238–4405); the **modal tail (4406–4961) renders regardless**, so Pipeline state survives tab switches.
- **Exception:** the six dialogs mounted inside the section IIFE (4276–4403: Weekly movement, Weekly money, GC Review, Total by Name, Capable, Est-bill-date) sit inside the `active` block — their open flags survive a tab switch, the dialogs unmount.
