## The shared substrate (or lack of one)

**There is still no shared data engine and no shared record pointer on this page.**

1. **Every tab self-loads** (`loadToday`/`loadUpcoming`/`loadCardEvents`, `loadHistory`, `loadOutstanding`, `loadItems`/`loadUsers`). No caches are shared; switching tabs remounts and refetches.
2. The only cross-tab **UI pointer** is `editItemId` → the shared `ChecklistItemEditModal` (Review + Manage). It stays in the parent.
3. Cross-surface **freshness is event-based**: `checklist-item-saved` (dispatched by [`ChecklistAddModal`](../src/components/ChecklistAddModal.tsx) — a **global** modal mounted in [`Layout.tsx`](../src/components/Layout.tsx), drawn as the New task window since v2.7085 (Glossary), opened via [`ChecklistAddModalContext`](../src/contexts/ChecklistAddModalContext.tsx); since v2.5818 its `onSaved` receives the new item's id and since v2.5825 `preset.when` opens the window on today, a date or repeat, so Quickfill's Physical inbox deck links a paper to the task its Task or Comes back press made (`office_inbox_papers.checklist_item_id`) and lists the repeating ones as the tray's routines; since v2.6617 Repeats draws `repeat-ring-hint`, and since v2.7085 **Done when…** and **Photo at the finish** are presses on the window's Add row and the bar's sentence is `newTaskSentence` (`lib/tasks/newTaskWindow.ts`), which takes `lineCount` and `asksForPhoto` for its tail; since v2.7584 its Send runs `newTaskSend` (`lib/tasks/newTaskSend.ts`), the one home of the refusals, the `checklist_items` row, the people's rows and the days, which the dev seat's `plan_task` runs too from the kernel copy, so change a send rule there and nowhere else; since v2.7112 a `preset` may carry `doneWhen` (the lines it opens with) and `asksForPhoto`, and since v2.7117 `sourceTag`, written onto the card as `checklist_items.source_tag` after the insert so the page that made it finds it again (the Bridge's check, Glossary **The Bridge**) — and by `ChecklistItemEditModal`) is heard by Review (`loadOutstandingRef`, 1389–1395), Manage (`loadItemsRef`, 89–95 of its file) and `ChecklistReviewInboxSection`. **New since the last map:** `checklist-instance-completed` (v2.2271) is dispatched by [`completeChecklistInstance`](../src/lib/checklistCompleteInstance.ts), Review's `markComplete` (1471) and `undoComplete` (1533, `detail: null`), and heard only by `ChecklistReviewInboxSection` (the sign-off queue) — Today's `toggleComplete` does **not** dispatch it. Both contracts must survive extraction unchanged.
4. **The completion engine's notifications exist four times; its next occurrence once** (since v2.3836 every path materializes the next `days_after_completion` occurrence through the kernel's `createNextChecklistRepeat`): Today's inline `toggleComplete` → `sendCompletionNotifications` 703–734 + `maybeCreateNextInstance` 736–739 (now a two-line call); Review's inline `markComplete` side effects 1474–1502; the tested kernel `lib/checklistCompleteInstance.ts` (v2.2039 — used by Review's row activity panel `completePanelInstance` 1436 and Manage's activity panel, 382 of its file); and [`DashboardMyInboxCard`](../src/components/dashboard/DashboardMyInboxCard.tsx). **The copies disagree** — see Risk flags.
5. **Module-scope seam (mostly cut):** `roadmapGoalChip` and the role type (`ChecklistRole`, which the page imports as `UserRole`) live in [`checklistPageShared.tsx`](../src/components/checklist/checklistPageShared.tsx) since v2.7724; the chip is called by Today (917, 1155), the Review row (279 of `OutstandingByPersonList.tsx`) and Manage (254 of its file). `RoadmapTaskEmbed` and the day's row (`PersonDayRow`, the page's `ChecklistInstance` alias at 90) live in `lib/tasks/personDay.ts` since v2.7692; `OutstandingInstance` is Review-only and lives with Review's row (22–27 of `OutstandingByPersonList.tsx`), and `ChecklistItem` moved with Manage. Left in the page: `toLocalDateString` 147–152, with 7 call sites (Today 454, 564, 649, 886, 905, 1018, 1147), which the Today move swaps for `localCalendarDayKey`; History's four went with its move (v2.7754). `dueCardChip` 93–106, `vehicleTaskChip` 113–139 and the `TODAY_PART_LABEL` / `TODAY_PART_LIST` styles 144–145 are Today-only; `outstandingAgeChip` (30–65 of `OutstandingByPersonList.tsx`) is Review-only; `tabStyle` 141 is the parent's.

**What this means for extraction:** no seam *hook* is needed before moving tabs — each tab's props are its full contract — and the seam *file* exists (point 5). The absence of shared caches is why this page is low-risk. The `?tab` router + Task-Mode bounce + `editItemId` + the edit modal never move into a child.

### Risk flags

- **Days-after-completion next date (fixed v2.3836).** Today's `maybeCreateNextInstance`, Review's `markComplete` and `DashboardMyInboxCard` each computed the next date as `new Date(ymd)` (UTC midnight) + local `setDate` + local format — in America/Chicago **scheduled + N − 1** (`2026-09-25` + 3 → `2026-09-27`; for N = 1 the next date was the day just completed, so Today's exists-check returned and Review/Inbox hit the UNIQUE pair — the repeat stopped). All three now call the kernel's `createNextChecklistRepeat` (`checklistNextRepeatYmd` = `ymdAddDays`, tested), which also gives Review the exists-check. The base is the **completion day** (`todayYmdInAppTz()`, v2.3842, the owner's call 2026-09-25) — it was `scheduled_date` (Review: the newest missed date), so a task done late got its next date early; `MissedGroup.newestScheduledDate` went with it.
- **Render smokes** reach three of the four tabs through the page: `Checklist.today` 1, `Checklist.todayParts` 11, `Checklist.todayForward` 3, `Checklist.reviewLook` 7, since v2.7724 `Checklist.manage` 3 (Manage's first) and since v2.7732 `Checklist.history` 3 (History's first: the grid, the ledger, edit mode's delete, and the window with one-offs alone); `checklistPageShared.render.test.tsx` 4 holds the chip. There is none for `ChecklistTechTreeTab.tsx`, `useChecklistTechTreeData`, `useTechTreeTaskMutations` or `domFullscreen`.
- Money math on this surface is the **cost lens** only (dev, controller and, since v2.6758, the owner; `canSeeTaskCosts`): `sumEstimateDollars` / `estimateAccuracy` / `estimateAccuracyByPerson` / `formatWholeDollars` / `formatMultiplier` are kernel-tested (`checklistCostEstimate.test.ts`, 13 cases); only the estimate-key pick (`roadmap_group_task_id ?? checklist_item_id`) is inline, twice — the row's `ChecklistCostButton` (318 of `OutstandingByPersonList.tsx`) and the person-card total 1914–1917.
