## Overview

[`src/pages/Materials.tsx`](../src/pages/Materials.tsx) is a **2,039-line** orchestration shell — one component `Materials` (74–2039) whose render is 1444–2038 (595 lines) — down from ~6,935 lines / ~144 `useState` when this map was first written. The 12-PR train (v2.1275–v2.1293) extracted every tab into [`src/components/materials/`](../src/components/materials/); since then four tab bodies were **born extracted** (Held for suppliers v2.2652, the estimator's Directory-only Supply houses v2.3167, On site v2.6851, What we buy v2.7114), the tab strip moved out to `MaterialsGroupStrip` (v2.6851) and **no tab moved back inline**. What the parent still holds: the three seam-hook destructures, 74 `useState` of cross-tab and PO Builder working state, the handlers that write them (R13–R14, ~645 lines), the URL router, three inline modals, and the strip's mount with the three windows its presses open.

[`src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx`](../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx) is **768 lines** (`SupplyHousesTab` 93–768, render 432–767 = 336 lines). It was 3,678 lines on the morning of 2026-11-17 and the largest file on the page; twelve cuts that day (v2.7662 to v2.7682, the *SupplyHousesTab extraction order* below) made it a shell. It calls four engines above its access gate (the data, the aging windows, the payments, the invoice form), keeps the doors between their windows, and draws the Directory pane's fold, the Accounts payable header and total, and the summary table. The windows are files of their own under [`src/components/materials/`](../src/components/materials/): the invoice form (772 lines), the aging cell window (565), the open house's panel (262), the aging heat map (241) and the payment windows' mounts (145). What grew the file to 3,678 is in those files now: the bill reader (v2.6095), the move window (v2.5677), the aging cell window (v2.5673), the house's card on its bills (v2.7088) and the payments train (v2.7450 to v2.7471, which replaced Apply Payment with Record a payment). Churn: 67 commits in 90 days, 12 of them the cuts.

This map is coupling/refactor-oriented and follows [`PAGE_DECOMPOSITION_PLAYBOOK.md`](./PAGE_DECOMPOSITION_PLAYBOOK.md); for feature/workflow behavior see [`PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md`](./PROJECT_DOCUMENTATION.md) §Materials Management and the Materials System section of [`GLOSSARY.md`](./GLOSSARY.md).

### Key structural differences from Bids

1. **No shared record pointer.** Nothing like `?id=` is URL-synced. The URL-driven selections are `?tab=`, `?po=<id>` / `location.state.openPOId` (opens a PO on `purchase-orders`), `?list=movable` (v2.5723, the Needs You line's door: opens the Supply houses tab's worked list once its book is read — read once, then dropped from the URL), `?list=finish` (v2.7469, the other Needs You door: opens the list of supply house payments that still need how they were paid), `?house=<id>` with an optional `&bill=<file>` (v2.6095: opens that house on the Supply houses tab, and its Add invoice window on the thread's file; the tab itself reads `&ticket=` and `&due=` beside it), `?addPart=true`, `?addAssembly=true`, and `location.state.refreshPrices`.
2. **The shared substrate is data, not selection**: the service-type scope (`selectedServiceTypeId`) plus the caches the three seam hooks hold — parts/`allParts`, `partTypes`, `assemblyTypes`, `supplyHouses` (catalog); `materialTemplates`, `templateItems`, stats caches (assemblies); `allPOs`/`draftPOs` (POs). See [Shared infrastructure](#shared-infrastructure).
3. **Two tabs share one selection anyway**: `selectedTemplate` + `templateItems` are read/written by BOTH `assembly-book` and `assemblies-po`, and `editingPO`/`selectedPO` are shared between `assemblies-po` and `purchase-orders`. These two pairs are the real coupling clusters.
4. **Every tab is extracted; `supply-houses` has two bodies by role** — the office gets [`SupplyHousesTab`](../src/components/SupplyHousesTab.tsx) (Directory + Accounts payable), the estimator gets [`MaterialsSupplyHouseDirectoryTab`](../src/components/materials/MaterialsSupplyHouseDirectoryTab.tsx) (54 lines, Directory only), switched by `supplyHousesPaneFor(myRole)`.

### How to read a dossier

Each tab section lists: **parent JSX** (range + prop count), **parent-owned state/handlers** for the tab (stay or move per the playbook), **inside the child** (what already lives in the extracted file), **data**, **external coupling**, and **status**. Only the two covered files carry line numbers; child files are named, not ranged.

- Refresh with `npm run map` on both covered files and bump `mapped_at`; anchor every range by symbol.
- When a region leaves the parent (or SupplyHousesTab), flip its row in the region map and its line in [Recommended extraction order](#recommended-extraction-order-value--risk).

- **Parent JSX:** `<MaterialsPartsBookTab active={…}>` 1524–1557 (32 props), always mounted (returns null when inactive). `PartFormModal` 1560–1571, Part Prices shell 1575–1611 and Price coverage modal 1614–1702 render after it.

### Coupling inside `SupplyHousesTab`

**The order the engines are called in is the wiring.** Each engine reads what the one before it hands back, so the four calls cannot be reordered: the data (117) first, the aging windows (144) next, since they patch the book, then the payments (192), then the invoice form (279), which reads the open house, its payments and the aging windows' refresh. All four sit above the access gate (333).

| Shared state | Owned by | Read by | What a mover must carry |
|---|---|---|---|
| The open house (`selectedSupplyHouseForDetail`, `supplyHouseInvoices`, `supplyHouseJobDetailsMap`, `poGeneratorEntriesForSelectedHouse`, `housePayments`) | the data engine; the payments engine patches the bills and the payments in place through the setters it is handed | the panel, the phone screen, the invoice form and its window, the payment windows, the Look window | every window is a guest of the one open house: it takes the house and its rows and never loads them |
| The book (`supplyHouseSummary`, `agingUnpaidInvoices`) | the data engine; the aging engine patches one unpaid row through `setAgingUnpaidInvoices` | the heat map, the summary, the move strip, both aging windows, the bill candidates, the bank side's gate (`agingBookLoaded`), the Quickfill metric | the worked-list door compares `agingMoveDataBook` with `agingUnpaidInvoices` by identity, so the array must reach the aging engine uncopied (quirk 31) |
| The form's fields | the form engine; the bill reader inside it writes ten of them and guards each with `billTypedRef` | the form's window | the bill reader cannot leave the form's engine |
| Reload counters | `linesRefresh` (tab), `agingCellJobsRefresh` (aging engine), `finishReloadKey` (payments engine), `directoryReloadKey` (tab) | `BillLinesHeld`, the open cell's effect, `SupplyPaymentsToFinish`, `SupplyHouseDirectory` | the form engine is handed `setLinesRefresh` and `setAgingCellJobsRefresh`, and bumps them after a save in the order the tab always did |
| `error` | the tab; every engine is handed `setError` | one line at the top of the tab (434) | a failed save says why behind the form, not in it (quirk 26) |
| The doors between windows | the tab (T10) | the aging cell window and the heat map | a door loads the house through the data engine, then opens the form or Record a payment over the aging window, which stays mounted under it; one `useBodyScrollLock` covers both (308) |

**The two mounts.** Materials passes eleven props: the house list and its reload, the role, the trade for Create PO, the PO door, and three one-shot doors each with its handled callback (`autoOpenHouseId` from Held for suppliers and `?house=`, `openBill` from `?bill=`, `openList` from `?list=`). Quickfill's [`SupplyHousesSection`](../src/components/quickfill/SupplyHousesSection.tsx) passes nothing: the data engine loads its own houses, the tab takes the role from `useAuth`, loads the first trade, navigates to `/materials` for a PO, and reports the *60+ past due* metric and its Next line. Both mounts are conditional, so every engine's state resets on a tab switch. Two doors are read from `window.location.search` once, at mount: `?ticket=` by the form engine and `?due=` by the data engine.

**Why engines and not self-contained windows.** The aging cell window and the form are on screen only while open, and things outside them read their state: the scroll lock, the move strip, the doors, each other. State in a component that unmounts would be lost. So each window's state lives in an engine the tab calls for as long as the tab is mounted, and the window only draws.

## Preserve-quirks list (odd but load-bearing — do not "fix" during a move)

1. **Load All mode defaults OFF** and is persisted per user in localStorage (`materials_loadAllMode_${uid}`, in `useMaterialsCatalog`) — the v2.46 Supabase disk-IO optimization. `fetchPricesForParts` batching (500-ID `.in()` chunks, each chunk paged past the 1,000-row cap, + client re-sort) and `loadAllTemplateItemsForStats` scoping to the selected service type are the same wave. Keep the batch sizes and the default.
2. **`PARTS_PAGE_SIZE = 50` + window-scroll infinite scroll** with a `loadingPartsRef` re-entry guard and a 200px bottom threshold, gated off in Load All mode (in `useMaterialsCatalog`). Virtualization was deliberately skipped.
3. **Price-count sort is a special path**: only with `sortByPriceCountAsc` on AND no part-type/manufacturer filter does `loadParts` call RPC `get_parts_ordered_by_price_count` and slice pages client-side; with filters the flag is silently ignored.
4. **`supply_houses` SELECT has a legacy fallback**: on error it retries without `monthly_payment_day` and stubs `null` — in `useMaterialsCatalog` and three times in the data engine (`useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts` 106–115, 127–135, 163–166). `assembly_types` is queried `as any`.
5. **`partIdsWithNoPrice` is computed from the paginated `parts` cache** (670), so "unpriced parts" badges/stats know only the pages loaded so far (accurate in Load All mode). Keep this data source.
6. **Tax-percent inconsistency** (in `MaterialsPurchaseOrdersTab`): `viewedPOTaxPercent` defaults to `'8.25'`; the `selectedPO` footer uses `parseFloat(...) || 0`, the all-POs rows `parseFloat(...) || 8.25`. Preserve both.
7. **The assembly filter states + `filterAssemblyTypeDropdownRef` are rendered by two tabs** (Assembly Book and PO Builder, both always mounted). Legal because each renders its body only when `active`; filters carry over between the two tabs by design.
8. **Add-only PO notes**: `addNotesToFinalizedPO` re-verifies `status === 'finalized' && notes === null`, then UPDATEs with `.eq('status','finalized').is('notes', null)` as a race guard.
9. **Price confirmation writes history**: `confirmPOItemPrice` inserts a zero-delta `material_part_price_history` row ("Price confirmed via PO: …") and tolerates its failure. `updatePartPriceInBook` (1255–1274) treats price `0` as **delete the price row**.
10. **`duplicatePOAsDraft` copies items sequentially**, does NOT copy `price_confirmed_at/by`, deletes the partial PO on any item failure, and lands on `assemblies-po` with the copy as `editingPO`.
11. **Draft `printPO` fires one price query per item** (`Promise.all` of `fetchPricesForPart`) for the "All prices" column; finalized print is single-pass.
12. **Optimistic multi-target writes**: `updatePOItemSupplyHouse` (1296–1357) patches up to four slices (`selectedPO`, `editingPO`, `draftPOs`, `allPOs`) and reverts by server reload on error. `handleNavigateToPOFromSupplyHouses` and the `?po=` router seed `draftPOs`/`allPOs` before `loadPurchaseOrders` settles.
13. **Double-`requestAnimationFrame` scroll** to `selectedPODetailRef` after PO deep-links (lets the tab switch paint first).
14. **`addItemToTemplate`/`handleAddItemFromModal` merge quantities** for a part already in the assembly and block adding an assembly to itself (direct self-reference only — deeper cycles are handled by `calculateAssemblyCost`'s `visited` set). `deleteTemplate` (832–850) deletes the items, then the template — not atomic.
15. **"Go to Projects to Add"** uses `window.location.href = '/projects'` (full reload).
16. **Closed (v2.3168).** The Price coverage modal lost its second copy of supply-house CRUD; house editing is `useSupplyHouseEditor` behind both Supply houses bodies.
17. **`SupplyHousesTab` has two mount contracts.** Materials passes data, callbacks and three one-shot doors; Quickfill passes nothing, so the data engine self-loads houses (`loadSupplyHousesInternal`, `useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts` 106–115), and the tab reads the role from `useAuth`, loads the first service type for Create PO (273–275), and falls back to `navigate('/materials', { state: { openPOId } })` (335–341). Every prop stays optional.
18. **Hooks run above the access gate** (the four engines, and `useReportQuickfillSectionMetric` 324–331, before `return null` at 333) so the Quickfill metric reports; it no-ops outside the Quickfill provider.
19. **The invoice sign comes from the document kind** (`signedAmountForSave`), never the typed amount; `on_job_account` is **sent only when it changed** (the merge-to-db-push window), and so is a credit's pair since v2.5035 (`creditsInvoicePatch`: `credits_invoice_id`, picked in **Credits invoice…** (`CreditPairPick`) from `creditableInvoices`, read on both rows by `creditPairingLine`, and held to the same house by the `supply_house_invoices_credit_pair_guard` trigger) and forced off unless exactly one job is allocated; `paidAtPayload` sends `paid_at` only when the typed day differs — the DB trigger stamps `now()` on the flip to paid. Since v2.6088 these rules run inside `supplyInvoicePayload` ([`supplyInvoiceWrite.ts`](../src/lib/materials/supplyInvoiceWrite.ts)); the tab only hands it the draft.
20. **Allocations save delete-then-insert** (in `saveSupplyInvoice` since v2.6088), not atomic; rows with pct ≤ 0 are dropped; an edited invoice with no allocations deletes all. A failed allocation write after the row saved comes back as `allocationProblem`: the form engine sets `error` and still closes the form (`useSupplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts` 590).
21. **A payment never writes `link`** — `link` is the invoice scan. Since v2.7455 a payment's receipt link is `supply_house_payments.receipt_link`, written by **Record a payment** (kernels [`lib/materials/payments/`](../src/lib/materials/payments/): `supplyPayment.ts` reads, `supplyPaymentWindow.ts` plans, `supplyPaymentsIo.ts` loads and records; since then `supplyPaymentMove.ts` for a move and a removal, v2.7462, `supplyInterest.ts`, v2.7467, `supplyPaymentsFinish.ts` + `supplyPaymentsFinishIo.ts`, v2.7469, and `supplyPaymentBank.ts` + `supplyPaymentBankIo.ts`, v2.7471); the row's **Receipt ›** reads the bill's own `payment_link` first (a by-hand Mark paid's, and every bill paid before payments were rows) and the payment's link after it.
22. ~~**`loadSupplyHouseDetail` reads the whole `supply_house_invoice_job_allocations` table** with no filter and no paging, `loadSupplyHouseSummary` reads every `supply_house_invoices` row the same way, and PO items load one query per PO with nothing reading them.~~ Closed v2.7682: the book is paged, the jobs read is scoped to the open house's bills and paged, and the PO items read is gone (all in `useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts`). A house's own bills and purchase orders are still read with no range, each scoped to the one house.
23. **The job-account flag defaults itself** (the form engine's effect, `useSupplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts` 165–201): a new invoice on exactly one job whose account at this house is `open` turns the flag on, unless the user touched the box (`invoiceOnJobAccountTouchedRef`); editing an invoice never defaults. A fill clears the touched mark again unless the person answered the flag (415).
24. **Remembered toggles are per device**: localStorage keys `supplyHouses.accountsPayable.{directory-open, show-paid-invoices, job-accounts-open, show-last-payment, mark-job-account-invoices, summary-sort}`, every access in try/catch.
25. **Summary rows sort twice**: `summarizeSupplyHouseBalances` sorts by outstanding, then the `sortedSupplyHouseSummary` memo (220) applies the remembered pick.
26. **A failed save says why behind the form.** `error` is drawn once, at the top of the tab (434); the invoice form covers it. A move keeps that; putting the reason in the form is a fix with its own PR.
27. **Opening a house empties its payments and its paid-this-visit set first** (`loadSupplyHouseDetail`, `useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts` 160–161). `refreshHouseMoney` and `toggleInvoicePaid` (`useSupplyHousePayments.ts` 80, 97) patch the rows in place on purpose, so the card keeps its place and an open payment window stays open.
28. **Two doors are read from the URL by the engines themselves, once, at mount:** `pendingTicketRef` (`?ticket=`, `useSupplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts` 107) and `pendingDueRef` (`?due=`, `useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts` 95). `?house=`, `?bill=` and `?list=` arrive as props from Materials. The mount is conditional, so a tab switch reads them again.
29. **A pending bill is picked one tick after the form opens:** `setTimeout(…, 0)` after `openAddInvoice()` (`useSupplyHouseInvoiceForm.ts` 248, 268), so the pick runs once the form's state has landed.
30. **A link that names a house scrolls twice** (`useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts` 237, then 240 after 1.2 s, only when the target drifted): the rows under the card settle after it reads.
31. **The worked-list door waits on array identity:** `agingMoveDataBook !== agingUnpaidInvoices` (`useSupplyHouseAgingWindows.ts` 224). The move effect stores the very array it read for. The data engine hands the aging engine the book uncopied; a seam that copies it breaks the door.
32. **An unpaid row in the book carries two amounts** (`openSupplyBook`, called at `useSupplyHouseAccountsPayable.ts` 147): `amount` is what is left on the bill and `billed` is the bill's own amount. The aging window's *left of* note (`AgingCellWindow.tsx`) reads both.
33. **One scroll lock covers the form and the aging window** (`useBodyScrollLock(invoiceFormOpen || agingCellModal != null)`, 308), and the window stays mounted under the form a door opened. It re-reads its jobs when the form saves (`agingCellJobsRefresh`).
34. ~~**Every handler under the gate is a hoisted function declaration**, and effects above the gate call them even for a role the gate turns away.~~ Retired by the cuts: the handlers the effects called live in engines now, which have no gate. What is left under the tab's gate (the doors, Create PO) is called from the render alone.
35. **The four engines are called in one order** (the data, the aging windows, the payments, the invoice form: 117, 144, 192, 279). Each takes what the one before hands back, and their effects run in that order. See [Coupling inside `SupplyHousesTab`](#coupling-inside-supplyhousestab).
36. **A window's state is the engine's, never the window's.** The aging cell window and the invoice form unmount when they close, and the scroll lock, the move strip and the doors read their state from outside. A piece that keeps state in the component loses it and blinds them.
