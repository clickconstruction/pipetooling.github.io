# 20261110090000_permit_skim.sql (2026-11-10, v2.6835)

Permits: builders with a mark, the hand-off, the put-back, the count and the nudge (punch list #302, PR 6 to 8; `to-dos/permit-skim/`). One migration for the rest of the first build, so prod is pushed once.

## What it does

- **`permits.builder_key`** (text, null until filled) and the partial index `permits_city_builder_key_idx`: the company (else the person) on the contact line, keyed as `research_name_key` keys a name, so every permit of one builder finds its siblings. `permit-pull` fills it on every row it upserts and, in batches, on the rows the table already held, through **`permit_set_builder_keys(jsonb)`** (`[{id, key}]`; the service role only).
- **`builders`** (key, name, kind, customer_id, updated_by, updated_at, created_at): what the office says about a name. A row is made the first time someone says something; a name with no row is still a builder. `kind` is one of gc, owner, developer, architect, plumber, files_for_others, person. Researchers read; a researcher inserts and updates in their own name (`updated_by = auth.uid()`).
- **`builder_marks`** (builder_key, mark, reason, marked_by, marked_at): good, avoid, or none to clear, append-only; the newest per key is the mark. A good and an avoid carry a reason (`builder_marks_reason_check`). Researchers read; a researcher inserts in their own name.
- **`permit_verdicts`**: `reason_code` (the one-tap reason as a code), `for_user_id` (the person a keep or a pursue is handed to), `pursue_for` (this_job, next_jobs, both), and a fifth verdict, `back`, which puts a permit back on the skim.
- **`research_permits`**, same shape, replaced in place: a permit whose newest verdict is `back` reads as unanswered.
- **`research_permits_v2(city, since, residential)`**: `research_permits` with the builder beside each row (key, name, kind, mark with its reason and who, the builder's commercial building permits in a year and how many have no plumbing permit) and the rest of the newest verdict (reason code, who it is for, what a pursue is for).
- **`research_builders(city)`**: every name that pulled a commercial building permit in the city in the last year, with its permits, how many are open, the last one, the plumber named most on its jobs, our bids and wins with the name, its kind and its mark.
- **`permit_funnel(city, since)`** (jsonb): permits that arrived, first answers given and by whom, where the touched permits stand now, the pass reasons, what the pursues are for, and how many pursued permits became a bid and a win. Days are read in Central time.
- **`permit_skim_wait(city, floor)`**: how many permits wait on a skim and the day the oldest arrived from the feed, as one row, for the Needs you line.

Every new function is closed to `anon` by name and granted to `authenticated` (`permit_set_builder_keys` to `service_role` alone). The four fences close the file.
