# 20261123185942_heard_day_write.sql (2026-11-23, v2.8383)

Punch list #421, PR 2 of 5: a heard day said in one press, and a payment mark's job. The owner's calls of 2026-11-23: *build it*, and *straight to the choice*. Additive and idempotent. It ships alone, ahead of the client that calls it.

## Why

When a person names a day still ahead in their own text or voicemail, Messages will draw one press under the line: *Wed Nov 25 is a promise of*, then the kinds by who they are, then *just talk*. `write_down` takes only an offered `message_promises` row, and nothing a signed-in client can call makes one. The table grants a client no insert. So the press needs its own write.

The card's small extra rides along. A customer has one thread, so a payment mark speaks for every job of theirs. A customer with two jobs promised saw each job's **They promised** row name the other's day.

## What it does

**`write_down_heard(p_message_id uuid, p_kind text, p_day date, p_writes text DEFAULT NULL)`**, `SECURITY DEFINER`, returns nothing.

- **The guard** is Follow up's own (`follow_up_press('write down a promise')`): a sign-in, no training account, no digital twin, the office team.
- **The line** must be their own text or voicemail (*A day is written on their own text or voicemail.*), from a customer, a trade partner or an architect (*Only a customer, a trade partner or an architect makes a promise here.*). A line that is not there, and no day, are refused in words.
- **The kinds** are the ones the screen offers each person (`promiseChoices`): a customer's `decision` or `access`, a trade partner's `quote`, `start` or `papers`, an architect's `answer` or `cert`. Any other is *That is not a promise a … makes.* A `payment` is refused here, *A payment is written on the job. Press A payment to open its sheet.*, so a payment has one road.
- **A double kind** writes its first unless `p_writes` says the other: a start or delivery writes `start`, insurance or a W-9 writes `insurance`, as `write_down` does.
- **The row** is made when the line has none. An offered day and a day called just talk are written over. The same kind again takes the new day. Another kind over a written day is refused: *Someone has said what that day is already.*
- **A null kind** is just talk, kept with its day.

**`message_promises.job_id uuid`**, nullable, no foreign key: the Pipeline job a payment mark is for. Adding a foreign key would lock `jobs_ledger`, the busiest table, while crews use the app. The writer checks the job is there instead.

**`write_down_payment(p_message_id uuid, p_day date DEFAULT NULL, p_job_id uuid DEFAULT NULL)`** replaces the two-argument function, dropped first since a third argument is a new signature. It writes `job_id` with the mark, refuses a job that is not there (*That job is not there.*), and clears the job when the mark comes off. Called the old way it writes a mark with no job, as before.

`message_promises` is altered, not created, so the four `apply_*` fence calls are not needed. Both functions write as their definer, and the guard refuses a training account and a twin first.

## The live client meanwhile

Nothing calls `write_down_heard` until PR 3 (v2.8384). The live client calls `write_down_payment` with two named arguments, which the new function answers through its default.
