# 20261013120000_mail_pieces.sql (2026-10-13, v2.5379 — the Docsmit train PR 2)

The company's one certified-mail table (`docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, *The shape → Data*). The Lien desk's run is its first user: a § 53.056 notice's envelopes go out through Docsmit as US-Letter PDFs (PR 3's functions), and the USPS number, the acceptance scan and the delivery come back to this row. The kernels that read and fill it are `src/lib/mail/` (v2.5375).

## What it does

1. **`mail_pieces`**, one row per envelope = one provider message. `kind` (check-constrained: `lien_notice_53_056`, `lien_retainage_53_057`, `lien_affidavit_copy`, `demand_31_04`, `lien_release`, `collections_demand`, `hazmat_notice`, `hr_letter`, `other`) says what it is and where its facts land when they come back; `source_ids uuid[]` the rows behind it; `job_id` the job when there is one (`ON DELETE SET NULL`); `provider` (`docsmit` today); `recipient` in the sender's words; `short_code` the QR code on the owner's copy, never sent to the provider; the address as sent after CASS (`name`, `organization`, `address1`, `address2`, `city`, a two-letter `state`, a ZIP or ZIP+4 `postal_code`); `send_type`, `envelope` (`#10` | `Flat`), `sheets`; `provider_message_id` and `client_msg_id` (`<kind>:<piece id>`); `price`; `status` (`prepared → sent → mailed → in_transit → delivered | notice_left | returned | failed | unknown`; `unknown` is the needs-a-person bucket); `usps_tracking`; `mailed_at` (when USPS accepted it: the day a notice is given); `delivered_at`, `signed_by`; `delivery_status` and `delivery_as_of` as the provider last said; `events jsonb` append-only; `opened_at`; `pdf_bucket` (default `lien-release-documents`) + `pdf_path`, `certification_path`; `problem`; `created_by`, `created_at`, `updated_at`. A sent piece must carry a provider id; a delivered one a date.
2. **Indexes**: by job; the open statuses by `updated_at` (the poll's list); a GIN on `source_ids`; unique `client_msg_id`, unique `(provider, provider_message_id)`, unique `short_code`, all partial on non-null.
3. **`mail_pieces_touch()`** trigger: `updated_at` on every write, `created_by` from `auth.uid()` when a person made the row.
4. **RLS**: select, insert and update for the office as a lien filing has it (`is_office_staff()`, a dev, an assistant, or the master on the piece's job). Nobody signed in deletes a piece; the service role (the provider functions) has all.
5. The three house fences at the end: read-only training mode (writes and statements) and the digital-twin write fence.

No new bucket: the mailed PDF and the Certification PDF go under `mail/<piece id>/` in the private `lien-release-documents` bucket, written by the service role.

Idempotent: `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`, `CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS`, `CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION`, `DROP … IF EXISTS` before each trigger and policy. Apply with `supabase db push` after the PR merges (list pending first). `src/types/database.ts` is regenerated in a follow-up chore once the table exists in prod.
