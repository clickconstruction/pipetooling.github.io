# 20261012140000_gc_bill_day_walk.sql

GC mode, Messages cut 9 (v2.5265): Bill day's walk and its two records. It is wave 2's SQL for cluster 7 (GC Reviews helper 7, directed by the lead session GC Review). The kernels that read it shipped as v2.5248 (`src/lib/gc/billDay/`). The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md`, cut 9, on branch `spike/gc-mode`.

**Why:** Bill day reads each trade's draw against the deadline. The superintendent walks the claims of the draws waiting on us and taps what she sees, and the office needs her taps to say why it sends a draw back. A trade that sends nothing this period has to be recorded with who said it, never typed over a draw. The cut-off is one setting for every job (decision 7, the lean). The walk goes in the order the lines are on site.

## What it does

1. **`gc_sow_lines.where_on_site`**: where a line is on site (Site, Yard, Level 1). Null reads as the site. A blank is refused (`gc_sow_lines_where_said`). The walk orders by `WALK_PLACES` in `walk.ts`, then any other place.
2. **`gc_superintendent_checks`**: one row per tap on a line of a draw waiting on us. Each row holds the percent in place she sees (`we_see_pct`) or whether the stored material is on site (`stored_on_site`), never both (`gc_superintendent_checks_one_reading`). It also holds a photo's Drive link (`photo_url`), a note, who (`checked_by`) and when (`checked_at`, `seq`).
   - A composite foreign key to `gc_draw_lines (draw_id, sow_line_id)` means only a line the draw claims is checked, and the checks go with the draw.
   - Each tap stays as it went: `authenticated` has no UPDATE or DELETE on the table. The newest tap on a line is the one that counts (`LineCheck` and `newestChecks` in `walk.ts`).
3. **`gc_bill_day_nothing`**: a trade sends nothing for a bill day. It records who said it in words (`said_by`), who of ours wrote it down, and when, one row per statement of work and bill day (`gc_bill_day_nothing_once`). *Put it back* deletes the row.
4. **`app_settings.gc_bill_day_cutoff_days`** = 6, inserted once. The money team may update this one key (policy `money_team_update_gc_bill_day_cutoff`, the `gc_card_bill_on_v1` pattern). Dev already manages every row.
5. **`gc_record_line_check(p_draw_id, p_line, p_we_see, p_on_site, p_photo_url, p_note)`**: a tap, by the kernels' line id, as every draws press takes it.
   - A percent under the claim becomes the line's `we_see`, the way the Draws window reads a doubted line. A tap at or over the claim clears `we_see`, so a line reads doubted only while the newest tap doubts it. A stored-material tap leaves `we_see` as it was.
   - It refuses, in words: signed out, training mode, a twin, anyone but a dev, a draw not waiting on us, a line the draw does not claim, neither reading or both, a percent outside 0 to 100, and on site on a line with nothing stored.
6. **`gc_say_nothing_this_period(p_sow_id, p_period_to, p_said_by)`**: saying it again for the same bill day replaces who said it. It refuses a statement of work not signed, no bill day, and no one named.
7. **`gc_set_bill_day_cutoff(p_days)`**: 2 to 20 days, for the money team. The walk's last day is two days before bill day, so the cut-off needs at least two days to leave a checking day.

All three functions run with the caller's rights, `authenticated` only. Both tables are dev only while Bill day is built, as every Building table is, with a `FOR SELECT` policy for `gc_money_team()` (Owner Billing's O9 rule). `src/lib/gc/doors.ts` lists both: the checks open at Building's door, the nothing rows at its money door. It ends with the three fences.

**Changed from the lead's sketch, and why:**
- The tables and functions carry `gc_`, like every GC table, so `doors.test.ts` holds them to a door.
- `photo_id` is `photo_url`: main keeps photos as Drive links, never copies (decision 6; `gc_daily_logs.photos_url`, `gc_punch_items.photo_url`), and has no photo table to point at.
- `by` and `at` are `checked_by` / `checked_at` and `recorded_by` / `said_at`, so no column is an SQL keyword.

## The lock note

`ALTER TABLE gc_sow_lines ADD COLUMN` and its constraint take a brief lock on `gc_sow_lines`, which the draws and the statement of work read. The new policy on `app_settings` takes a brief lock there too, a table many reads touch. `SET lock_timeout = '3s';` heads the file, so a busy moment fails the push fast rather than queueing reads behind it. The fences create only what is missing.

## Verify after the push

Read only, in `BEGIN READ ONLY; … ROLLBACK;`:
1. **The tables and the column.** `gc_superintendent_checks` and `gc_bill_day_nothing` exist and are empty. `gc_sow_lines.where_on_site` exists, null on every row.
2. **The policies.** `pg_policies` on each table shows the dev policy, the money team's read and the six fence policies. Each table carries the `read_only_block_stmt` trigger.
3. **The setting.** `SELECT value_num FROM public.app_settings WHERE key = 'gc_bill_day_cutoff_days'` gives 6. The policy `money_team_update_gc_bill_day_cutoff` names the key and `gc_money_team()`.
4. **The functions.** `gc_record_line_check`, `gc_say_nothing_this_period` and `gc_set_bill_day_cutoff` are `prosecdef = false`, executable by `authenticated` and not by `anon`.
