# 20261118060000_trip_bill_line.sql (2026-11-18, v2.7756)

Put a trip on a bill: the ground. Punch list #391, PR 2. No screen names any of it yet. The client that follows adds **Put on a bill** to an open Extra trip.

A trip marked Extra is work past the job's price, and nothing took it to a bill. The owner picked a line on the job over a bill of its own. Every dollar asked of a customer stays a line, and the Bill tab's own *Bill the N picked* already bills one line by itself.

## What it adds

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| `jobs_ledger_fixtures.trip_id` | The trip a line came from. Nullable, `REFERENCES trips(id) ON DELETE SET NULL`. Null on every line that is not a trip's. |
| `jobs_ledger_fixtures_one_line_a_trip` | A unique index on `trip_id` where it is set. One trip, one line. It is also the index the foreign key reads when a trip is deleted. |
| `trip_put_on_job_line(p_trip_id, p_job_id, p_amount, p_name, p_words, p_dry_run)` | The one function that writes the line. SECURITY DEFINER, shaped like `apply_job_discount`. |

No table is created, so no fence call is owed.

## The mark lives on the line

- Delete the line and the trip holds nothing. It reads not billed again by itself, and the press works again.
- Delete the trip and the line stays with its name, its words and its price. Only the mark goes.
- The Job window's save writes a line under its own id with the columns it knows. `trip_id` is not one of them, so a rename or a new price leaves the mark.
- A combine moves a job's lines by `job_id` alone (`migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete`), so a moved line keeps its trip.
- Changing a trip keeps its row (`trip_save` updates it), so the line keeps its mark.

## What the function does

1. Refuses nobody signed in, an archived caller, a training account and a digital twin, in that order. A definer stands behind the tables' fences, so it holds them itself, as `trip_phone` does.
2. Takes the four roles that may add a discount to a job: dev, master_technician, assistant and controller. A primary reads every trip and may not press. A crew member on the trip may not either.
3. Asks that the caller reads the trip (`trips_office()` or `trip_is_mine`) and the job (`can_read_job_activity`, as `apply_job_discount` asks).
4. Locks the trip row, then the job row. Two presses on one trip wait for each other on the trip.
5. Answers a trip that already holds a line with that line, its job and `duplicate: true`. Nothing is written. This comes before the billing check, so a trip moved off Extra after its line was made is still answered with its line.
6. Refuses a trip that is not Extra, in words.
7. Takes a job that is one of the trip's jobs, the job that holds the trip, or a job of the trip's site. Any other job is refused.
8. Refuses the GC desk's billing job (`gc_projects.billing_job_id`). That job's total is the contract and the interest billed (`gc_owner_billing_revenue`), not its lines, and `apply_job_discount` has no answer for it.
9. Holds the amount above nothing and at or under $1,000,000, rounded to cents. Cuts the name to 200 characters, as `apply_job_discount` does, and the words to 2,000. A blank name is refused.
10. On a dry run, stops here and answers the total before and after. Nothing is written.
11. Inserts one `work` line with `count` 1 and `trip_id` set, at the highest position plus one. No discount's basis names that position and no line moves. The line is an *Any time* stage, the column's default, so the Bill tab can pick it.
12. Writes the job's total again from its named lines and `job_rider_fees`, the sum `apply_job_discount` writes. It is written whole, never raised by a delta.
13. Adds the job to the trip's jobs when it was not one.
14. Logs one `job_activity_events` row, `trip_line_added`, marked financial. The line table's own trigger also logs *Specific work added*, as for any line.

The reply: `ok`, `duplicate`, `dry_run`, `fixture_id`, `job_id`, `revenue_before` and `revenue_after`.

It bills nothing. A trip still changes no clock row, bill or cost. A bill takes the line later, from the Job window.

## Left as `apply_job_discount` leaves it

A job that reads Paid and gains a line still reads Paid until the Job window is closed on it, which moves it back to Billed. `apply_job_discount` has the same edge.

## The locks

`jobs_ledger_fixtures` is read by every Job window and written by every billing save. The file starts with `SET lock_timeout = '3s'`, so the push fails fast and is tried again in a quieter moment instead of queueing the crews.

- The column is nullable with no default. That is a catalog change and rewrites no row.
- The foreign key is added `NOT VALID` and validated after, as `20261117113000` did on `message_attachments`. Every row's value is null today, so the check finds nothing to look up.
- The unique index is partial, and no row is in it yet.

- a trip with no job named put on one of the site's jobs by a master, the job joining the trip's jobs, and a long name cut to 200;
- a job that holds its own trip, by a dev;
- a trip moved off Extra that still answers with its line;
- a line renamed and repriced by an upsert under its own id, and a line moved to another job, each keeping its trip;
- a deleted line that frees the trip, with the next press writing the total whole from the lines and the line past the highest position;
- a deleted trip that leaves the line with its words.
