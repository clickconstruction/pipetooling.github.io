# 20261119180000_trip_join.sql (2026-11-19, v2.7960)

Join a trip with the next.

This file is the ground. It ships alone, ahead of the press that calls it, so no deploy waits on the push. Until the press ships, nothing on screen changes: no trip is joined, so the two restated functions answer as before.

A trip is one going out to a site and coming back. Two trips can turn out to be one: the crew pressed End the trip by mistake and started again, or one going out was typed twice. It is also how a split is taken back. Until now the fix was to delete the second trip and paint its days onto the first with Change.

## What it adds

Three functions, and two restated. No table, column or index.

### `trip_join(p_trip_id uuid, p_next_trip_id uuid, p_dry_run boolean DEFAULT false) RETURNS jsonb`, `SECURITY INVOKER`

- The trip keeps its id, its number, its words and its `via`. Where its purpose, who asked or its outcome is empty, it takes the next trip's. Who asked is taken whole or not at all.
- Everyone on either trip is on the joined trip, told apart by name as `trip_save` tells them apart. Each keeps every day from both. Where both give one person the same day, this trip's kind stays, and the reply names those days (`shared_days`).
- Its jobs are both trips' jobs.
- Billing: an empty one takes the other's.
- A line only the next trip holds is tied to the joined trip.
- `ended_at` is the next trip's.
- The next trip is deleted. Its number is not given to another trip.

It writes through `trip_save` inside the one call, so the save, the line's tie and the delete land together or not at all. The line's tie is moved first, while the second trip is still the next one: the joined trip's days may sort it somewhere else.

### `trip_next_id(p_trip_id uuid) RETURNS uuid`, `STABLE`, `SECURITY INVOKER`

The trip that went out next on the same home: the same development, or the same job when the trip has no development. The order is the Trips look's own (`sortTrips`): first day, then last day, then id. Null for the last trip.

### `trip_join_line(p_trip_id uuid, p_next_trip_id uuid, p_move boolean DEFAULT false) RETURNS text`, `SECURITY DEFINER`

What the two trips hold of a job's lines: `none`, `stays` (this trip holds the line), `moves` (only the next trip holds one) or `both`. With `p_move`, a line only the next trip holds is tied to this trip. Nothing else of the line changes: no price, no bill, no total.

It is a definer because `jobs_ledger_fixtures`' own policies are by role and by job, narrower than the trips office. An invoker that could not read a line would see none, and the delete of the next trip would clear the tie without a word.

### Restated, from prod's live bodies

Both were read from prod on 2026-11-19 through the Management API and matched the repo byte for byte before the change.

- `trips_archive_on_delete()` (was `20261118203000`): the capture also keeps `joined_into`, `{ trip_id, trip_number }`, when `trip_join` is what deletes the trip. `trip_join` names the joined trip in a setting for its own delete alone (`trips.joined_into`, set for the transaction and cleared after the delete). The capture takes it only when that trip is there and is not the trip going.
- `trip_deleted_list(uuid)` (was `20261118100000`): each item carries `joined_into`, null for a trip somebody deleted.

## Who

`trips_office()`, checked first so the refusal reads in the join's own words. After that the trips tables' own policies decide, since `trip_join` is an invoker: a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller are refused where `trip_save` refuses them.

`trip_join_line` holds its fences itself, as `trip_put_on_job_line` does: signed in, not archived, not a twin, the trips office, and not a training account when the move is asked for. It acts only for a trip and its next trip on the same home, so it moves no tie between any other pair. A primary, who cannot put a trip on a bill, can so carry a tie from one trip to the next by joining them.

`EXECUTE` on all three is granted to `authenticated` and `service_role`, and revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`. The capture stays a trigger function nobody calls.

## Put back, and what is not guarded

The trip that went waits in `deleted_records_archive` for 90 days, as every deleted trip does. Put back as things stood would return its days beside the joined trip, every one of them twice. The mark lets the fold say *Joined into Trip 7* and draw no Put back. A join is taken back with Split in two.

`trip_save` itself does not refuse a joined trip made again under its own id. A client loaded before this ships does not read the mark, and could offer Put back for one until it reloads. The days would then stand on two trips, and deleting the trip that came back fixes it. The dev's `restore_deleted_records` still works on the bundle: `jsonb_populate_record` passes over a key the table does not have.

## The reply

Dry run: `{ dry_run: true, would: 'join', trip_id, trip_number, next_trip_id, next_trip_number, first, second, joined, shared_days, line, billing, ended }`. `first` and `second` are each trip as it stands (`trip_number`, `people`, `first_day`, `last_day`), and `joined` is `trip_save`'s own dry-run reply for the one trip they become. Nothing is written, and no row is locked.

For real: `{ dry_run: false, did: 'join', trip_id, trip_number, gone_trip_id, gone_trip_number, joined, shared_days, line, billing, ended }`.

## Locks

No DDL beyond the `CREATE OR REPLACE`s. The file starts with `SET lock_timeout = '3s'`. A join locks its two trip rows, in id order, so two presses on one pair wait for each other.

## Verified on prod

On the ZZ Test development, as the dev, on the local dev server against prod with the app's own presses.

- A trip filed through the dev seat (Trip 9, Nov 2 to 8, two people) was split at Nov 6 with **Split in two**, which made Trip 10.
- Trip 10 was then changed through the seat so one person had Nov 5 on both trips, on the road there and at the site on Trip 9, and so it said how it went.
- With Trip 10 set to Extra, the window named the two billings and its press was dead. Set back to Ours, the press was live.
- **Join into one trip** left one trip: Trip 9, Nov 2 to 8, both people, every day as first filed, Nov 5 still at the site, and how it went taken from Trip 10.
- Deleted trips read *Trip 10 · Joined into Trip 9 on Nov 19* with no **Put back**.
- `read_trips` read the one trip back.

Trip 9 was then deleted with the app's own press.

A second walk, on the deployed site after v2.7963 went live, took the three rules the first left to the bed.

- Trip 11 (Nov 2 to 7, Extra, one job) was filed through the seat and split at Nov 5. The split ended Trip 11 and left Trip 12 open.
- Trip 12 was given a second job through the seat, and **Put on a bill** put it on JP1113 as a $100 line.
- The window read *Trip 12 is on a bill. Its line moves to the joined trip.*
- After the join the line's `trip_id` named Trip 11, with its price and its job untouched and no bill on it. The job's window showed **Open Trip 11** under the line.
- The joined trip held both jobs.
- Its ended stamp was Trip 12's: empty, where Trip 11's own had been set.

The line kept its name, *Trip 12, 1 person, Nov 5 to 7*. A join moves the tie and changes no word on the line, so the office changes the name by hand when the joined trip's number or days should show on the bill.

Not walked live, and held by the bed alone: a join by a primary.
