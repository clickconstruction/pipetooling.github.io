# 20261110071633_in_kind_arrangements.sql (2026-11-10, v2.6827)

Punch list #301, PR 1 of four: one place for work paid in kind. The ground under the screens; no client reads it until PR 2. The card and the mock-up are [`to-dos/in-kind-arrangements.md`](../../to-dos/in-kind-arrangements.md).

- **`in_kind_arrangements`**, one row a deal: the customer (`customer_id`, with `partner_name` kept from the customer's name when the deal was made), `what_given`, a `note` of the terms, and for a deal with a schedule the usual credit a month (`monthly_amount`), the whole monthly bill it comes off (`monthly_due`), the name that bill goes out under at the bank (`bank_payee`) and its first month (`starts_on`, a first of the month). A monthly amount and a first month come together or not at all. `ended_at` closes the deal.
- **`jobs_ledger.in_kind_arrangement_id`**: the jobs a deal pays, on the job's own row, so every reader that already holds the job can tell its open bill is scheduled in kind. Nullable, no default, ON DELETE SET NULL, a partial index.
- **`in_kind_lines`**, one row a thing given: `given_on`, `amount`, `memo`, `cost_job_id` (the job it counts on as material; null is overhead) and `for_month` (the month a scheduled line settles; one live line a month). An amount of zero is allowed only with a month: no credit that month. `removed_at` takes a line back and keeps the row.
- **`in_kind_line_payments`**: the In kind rows of `jobs_ledger_payments` a line wrote. ON DELETE CASCADE from both sides, so a payment removed from its bill by hand drops out of its line.
- **Gates**: the office (`is_office_staff()`) reads all three. The office inserts a deal in its own name, updates it, and deletes it only while no live line stands on it. Nobody writes a line or its payments directly; the table grants are taken from `authenticated` as well.
- **Triggers**: `in_kind_arrangements_guard` keeps `created_at`, `created_by` and `updated_at`, fills `partner_name`, and stamps `ended_at` / `ended_by` itself. `in_kind_arrangements_end_releases_jobs` clears `in_kind_arrangement_id` on the deal's jobs when it ends.
- **`in_kind_arrangement_set_jobs(uuid, uuid[])`**: the whole list of the deal's jobs each time. Refuses a job of another customer (the job's customer or its GC must be the deal's) and a job another deal already pays.
- **`in_kind_line_add(arrangement, given_on, amount, memo, cost_job_id, for_month, dry_run)`**: places a line on the deal's billed jobs, the oldest bill first, as In kind payments through `mark_invoice_paid` (a job billed with no bill row: `mark_job_paid`), all in one transaction; a refusal from either raises and nothing is kept. A payment's note is the memo, or the memo with its part (*Rent credit, May 2026: $860.00 of the $3,000.00 given*) when the line spans bills. A bill on Stripe is passed over and returned in `held`, since Stripe is told by the bill's own window. What finds no open bill comes back as `unapplied` and stays on the line. The deal's row is the lock, so two adds take turns. With `p_dry_run` nothing is written.
- **`in_kind_line_remove(uuid)`**: each payment the line wrote is removed through `remove_jobs_ledger_payment_and_reconcile`, so its bill and job reopen; the line stays with `removed_at`.
- **`in_kind_line_set_cost(uuid, uuid)`**: a job id counts the line on that job; null counts it as overhead.
- Every function is SECURITY DEFINER, office only, answers `{ok, …}` or `{error}`, and is granted to `authenticated` and `service_role` by name.
- The four fences close the file. Additive and idempotent; the push may come at any time after the merge.
- **Bed**: `npm run test:pg:in-kind` (`supabase/tests/in_kind/`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`), 52 checks on the whole schema.
- **Not here**: a combine of two jobs carries no line across (`migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` does not name the tables; a deleted job's lines fall back to overhead and its link is cleared), and pay speed still counts an In kind payment (PR 2).
