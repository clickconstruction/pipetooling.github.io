# 20260822062831_bid_tab_capture (v2.2081)

Four nullable columns on `bids` for structured bid-tab capture — the numbers a
GC reads off the tab, kept comparable so losses can teach ("when we lose on
price, we lose by X% over the low"):

- `bid_tab_low numeric` — lowest bid on the tab
- `bid_tab_high numeric` — highest bid on the tab
- `bid_tab_rank_from_low integer` — our position counting from the lowest bid
  (1 = we were the low bid; ranks count from the bottom because that's how
  tabs get read on the phone)
- `bid_tab_bidder_count integer` — how many bids were on the tab (optional)

All nullable: capture is optional and partial tabs are worth keeping. No new
RLS (existing `bids` policies cover the columns); no CREATE TABLE, so no
read-only-block reapplication needed. Vocabulary, parsing, validation, and the
derived %-over-low insight live in `src/lib/bidTabCapture.ts`; capture UI is
the Waiting to hear "Bid tab received" flow and the Why we lost lens card.
