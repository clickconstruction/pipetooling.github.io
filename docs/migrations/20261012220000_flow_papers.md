# 20261012220000_flow_papers.sql (2026-10-12, v2.5306)

Messages, cut 4, the New paper window (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md` §4, "A new paper from the editor"; the prototype's v1.177; cluster 6's F). A paper the flow editor adds is a Contract library document. Its id is the flow's paper (`doc:<uuid>`, `src/lib/flows/facts.ts`'s doc family, v2.5305), and its kind says which facts finish the step. Nothing here is a new table.

## What it does

1. **Four columns on `contract_template_documents`**:
   - `flow_kind`: `upload`, `contract` or `notice`, null on every library document that is not a flow paper (`contract_template_documents_flow_kind_known`).
   - `name_es`: the Spanish name the trade partner's and the customer's pages show.
   - `book_body_es`: a notice's Spanish words. The English stays in `book_body_html`.
   - `renew_ask_days`: for an upload that runs out, the days before it does that the ask for the next one goes. Null means 30, the renewal ask's `INSURANCE_ASK_DAYS`. It's held from 1 to 365 (`contract_template_documents_renew_ask_days_sane`).
2. **One packet per audience**, seeded once by name in `contract_templates`:
   - "Flow papers · trade partners", for audience `sub`;
   - "Flow papers · customers", for audience `customer`, the architect's papers with them.
3. **`flow_paper_create(p_flow, p_kind, p_name, p_name_es, p_renew_ask_days, p_body_en, p_body_es)`**, a definer, returns the new document's id. It's the office team's (`gc_office_team()`). The library's own insert policy leaves out controllers and estimators. It refuses the signed out, a digital twin and training mode, a flow or a kind it doesn't know, an empty name, a notice with no words, and days on anything but an upload. It files the paper in its audience's packet after the last one there, tagged `flow-paper` and dated `app_today()`. A contract's clauses are cluster 9's: the window writes them through `edit_clause` once this returns the id, so anyone's but a controller's or a dev's wait for one of them to approve.
