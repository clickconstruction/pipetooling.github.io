# 20260917140000 — people_labor_job_payment_events + move / remove / restore (v2.3562, sub payments: move or remove with a trace, PR 1)

One small ledger of what happened to a sub sheet payment: `kind` moved · removed · restored, `payment_id`, `from_job_id` / `to_job_id` (both `people_labor_jobs`, cascade), a snapshot of the payment (`amount`, `memo`, `payment_date`, `hidden_from_sub`, `sequence_order`), `reason`, `actor_user_id` / `actor_name`, and `restored_event_id` on a removal once it is undone. Indexes on `(from_job_id, created_at DESC)` and `(to_job_id, created_at DESC)`. RLS: `is_office_staff()` selects, inserts and updates.

Three `SECURITY INVOKER` functions (so the caller's RLS gates every write, and the read-only statement trigger still stops training-mode users): `move_labor_job_payment(p_payment_id, p_to_job_id, p_reason)` re-points the live row (same id, amount, memo, date, visibility; last slot on the destination) and writes one `moved` event both sheets read; `remove_labor_job_payment(p_payment_id, p_reason)` snapshots into a `removed` event and deletes the row, so every existing reader of `people_labor_job_payments` stays correct with no filter; `restore_labor_job_payment(p_event_id)` puts the snapshot back as a new row within 30 days and links the two events. Granted to `authenticated`, revoked from `PUBLIC` / `anon`.

New table → ends with `apply_read_only_write_blocks()`, `apply_read_only_stmt_blocks()` and `apply_digital_twin_write_blocks()`. Idempotent. Apply with `bash scripts/db-push.sh` from a checkout at `main`; the client ships fail-soft (the events read is ignored until the table exists, and the row buttons appear regardless — a Move before the push fails with a plain error toast).
