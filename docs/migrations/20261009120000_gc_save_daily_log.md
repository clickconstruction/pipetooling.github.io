# 20261009120000_gc_save_daily_log.sql (2026-10-08, v2.4957)

GC mode, the real build, the Building lane's U3a-i: the daily log's press (`to-dos/gc-mode/BUILDING_REAL_BUILD.md` → *Writing it*, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). One function, no table: the tables are `20261008030000_gc_building_records`.

- **`gc_save_daily_log(log jsonb)`** returns the log's id. It writes a day's log with its crews and what held work up, and replaces that day's when saved again: the log by `(project_id, log_date)`, its crews and delays deleted and written again, in one transaction. It takes the prototype's `saveDailyLog` shape: `projectId`, `date`, `today` (the writer's company day), `sky`, `high`, `low`, `weatherStop`, `crews` (`packageId`, `workers`), `done`, `delays` (`packageId` or null, `reason`, `note`) and `visitors`.
- **It refuses**, in words:
  - a training account and a digital twin (`is_read_only()`, `is_digital_twin()`; the tables' blocks refuse their writes as well);
  - a job not being built: `stage <> 'building'` or no `started_on`;
  - a day after the writer's own day, or before the day work started;
  - a writer's day more than one day off the server's, so a stale page cannot write tomorrow's log;
  - a trade on the log that is not one of this job's.
  - A sky or a reason the tables do not know is refused by their own checks.
- A crew with nobody on site is left off, as the prototype does.
- **Not yet:** the prototype's tighter rule, our own crew or a trade with a signed statement of work (`logTrades`), is the window's until the Board's B6 makes statements of work real. U3b adds it here by `CREATE OR REPLACE`, with the start promises a crew on site keeps (`gc_keep_promises`).

`SECURITY INVOKER`: the tables' dev policies decide who may. `EXECUTE` is revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`, granted to `authenticated`.

Apply order: any time after `20261008030000`. It is `CREATE OR REPLACE`, idempotent, and locks no table.

**Before the push**, the SQL bed (`scripts/pgtest-gc-building.sh`, `npm run test:pg:gc-building`; GitHub's runners run it from `.github/workflows/sql-beds.yml` on this PR, since no session here reaches Docker) applies every migration to the Supabase Postgres image and plays `supabase/tests/gc_building/20_daily_log.sql` in one transaction that rolls back. It saves, replaces and catches up a day as a dev, and refuses each case above in its words. A trainee and a twin are refused by name. An estimator reads the job but RLS refuses the write. It ends `gc_building PASSED`.
