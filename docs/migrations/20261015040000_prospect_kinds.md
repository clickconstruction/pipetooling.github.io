# 20261015040000_prospect_kinds.sql (2026-10-15, v2.5559)

Prospects: three kinds of company to find (punch list #110). A prospect is now a **customer** (a GC or an owner who could send us plans for our trades; every row before today), a **GC lead** (an owner, developer, architect or owner's rep with a job we could build as the GC) or a **sub** (a company that does a trade we buy out). One deck serves all three. Everything here is additive; nothing changes for a customer prospect or for the Hiring board.

Shipped with v2.5559 (the Jobs to GC and Subs tabs). Apply order: this migration right after the client, in the same sitting — the new client reads `prospects.kind` on every load and the deck is empty until the column exists; the old client never reads it.

## What it does

1. **`prospects.kind`** text, not null, default `customer`, `prospects_kind_known` (customer · gc_lead · sub). An index on it (`prospects_kind_idx`): every deck load filters by it.
2. **Each kind's facts** on `prospects`: `source` (where we found them, free words); for a sub `trades text[]` (default empty), `max_miles` (`prospects_max_miles_positive`), `lang` (`prospects_lang_known`: en · es, as `gc_companies.lang`); for a GC lead `company_role` (`prospects_company_role_known`: owner · developer · architect · ownersRep · gc) and `customer_id` (a lead who is already a customer links to that record).
3. **What it became**: `converted_customer_id` and `converted_gc_company_id`, both `ON DELETE SET NULL`. Until today the customer a prospect became was named only in the converted comment's text; `markProspectConverted` writes the column from here on, and `markProspectOnBench` the other.
4. **`prospect_jobs`**: a GC lead's jobs, one row each (name, address, sq_ft, size_note, plans_link, bid_due, status open · asked · lost · dropped, won_by, note), cascading with the prospect, `updated_at` kept by `update_updated_at_column()`. RLS mirrors `prospects`: `user_has_prospects_staff_access()` on select, insert, update and delete; anon revoked; authenticated granted; the service role everything. The three fence appliers close the migration, so training mode and the twins' fence cover the table. The client reads and writes it from PR 3 of the train; this migration only makes the table so one push serves the train.

## Why the shape

- The kind on the prospect, not a table per kind, because the deck's machine (the queue and its orders, the 30-minute calling lock, the five outcomes, warmth, callbacks, timers, copy templates, the week report) is the asset and reads one table.
- A job as a row, not a sentence in the notes, because an owner has several over the years and the bid day that orders the queue is the job's.
- Real links on conversion, because the comment's text was the only tie and could not be joined.

## Verify

Read-only on prod after the push: `select kind, count(*) from prospects group by 1` reads every row as `customer`; `\d prospect_jobs` shows the four policies and the trigger; a sub added on Prospects → Subs lands under its trade on GC projects → Trade partners with vetting `new` and the prospect's `converted_gc_company_id` set.
