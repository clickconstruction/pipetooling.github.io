# 20261024030000_trade_levels.sql (2026-10-24, v2.5812)

Hiring, the owner's scale for the trades (the 2026-10-23 design spike; the kernel is `src/lib/hiring/tradeScale.ts`, the io `qualificationsIo.ts`). One scale for everyone we may hire or use, a candidate on the Team hiring board or a sub on the roster, read per trade: 0 never done it, 1 some experience and no tools, 2 says they can run a job alone and no tools, 3 the same with tools.

- **`trade_levels`** (`team_prospect_id` → `team_prospects` or `person_id` → `people`, never both, on delete cascade; `trade` in plumbing, electrical, hvac; `level` 0 to 3; `noted_by` defaulting to the signed-in user; `noted_at`): one row a trade, each trade once per record (`trade_levels_once`, `UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT`), partial indexes by each id.
- **`team_prospects`** and **`people`** gain `location` (the words), `location_lat` and `location_lng` (the map's pin), `license` (the words, `''` for none) and `license_asked` (so `''` reads as none rather than unknown).

RLS: `user_has_prospects_staff_access()` on every verb, as the hiring board's roles' policies; the three fence appliers run (read-only users, the statement trigger, digital twins).

Additive. Shipped with v2.5812 (`feat/flows-candidate-sub-qualifications`); `supabase db push` after the merge. Until it lands the client's qualifications read fails soft (the step stays unmarked) and a save refuses with its words. The types regenerate in their own PR after the push.
