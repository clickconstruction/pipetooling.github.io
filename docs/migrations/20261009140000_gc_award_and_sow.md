## What it does

1. **The contract step.** `gc_projects` loses `general_conditions`, `contingency_pct` and `fee_pct`.
   - B5-a (`20261008130000`) copied them to `gc_project_money`, which only the money team reads.
   - Since B5-c (v2.4930) the client reads and writes only the new table. No function, view or policy names the old columns (checked on prod, below).
2. **Award** on `gc_trade_packages`:
   - `awarded_invite_id`, a composite FK to an ask on this trade (`(awarded_invite_id, id)` → `gc_invites(id, package_id)`) with `ON DELETE SET NULL (awarded_invite_id)`, as carry has it. A project's or a trade's delete takes the award with it. The awarded ask alone is still kept: the award's day check and the statement of work's own key refuse its delete;
   - `awarded_by` (a user) and `awarded_on` (`public.app_today()`), with a check that an award always has its day;
   - `gc_invites` gains `UNIQUE (id, company_id)` for the statement of work's FK.
3. **The statement of work, standing alone** (call 6). `step_commitments` is not touched.
   - `gc_sows`: one per trade. It holds the ask awarded (an ask of that company and on that trade, by two composite FKs), the status (draft, sent, signed, cancelled), the price, retainage, the plan set, their own schedule of values, what they will not do, the sent, signed and accepted days, and the ESIGN signer fields.
   - `gc_sow_lines`: one per scope item or change order (exactly one of the two), each scope item once.
   - Both are dev only while the Board is built, with the training-mode and twin blocks, and `src/lib/gc/doors.ts` lists them as `dev('Board', …)`, opened by award's door (call W); `doors.test.ts` holds the list to the migrations.
4. **`esign_consents.record_type`** takes `'gc_sow'` (call E). The CHECK is rebuilt with the six types it had, as `20261006040000` did.
5. **`gc_exclusion_key(text)`** is `exclusions.ts`' `fold()`: lower case, `&` as *and*, anything else not a letter or digit as one space, trimmed.
6. **`gc_leveled_total(invite)`** (SECURITY INVOKER) is `leveledTotal`: the newest quote, plus the ask's plug on each scope line the quote does not say yes to, its taken alternates, and its cover on each exclusion whose key is not one of the trade's Known exclusions. Null when no quote is in.
7. **`gc_award(invite, estimator)`** (SECURITY INVOKER), one transaction:
   - refuses anyone but a dev first, in words (call W, the owner's: dev while built). Door 2 opened the trades to the office, so this is said before any write rather than left to `gc_sows`' policy;
   - refuses our own trade, a lost project, a trade already awarded, and an ask with no quote, in words;
   - refuses in `canAward`'s words a company new to us (with or without its form), one declined, or an award above the company's approved limit, measured against the leveled total;
   - writes the award;
   - drafts the statement of work as `sowFromBid` does: the price, retainage 10, the newest plan set, their schedule of values, what they will not do (with who does a Known one), and one line per scope item, the price split in hundreds with the rest on the last line;
   - returns the statement of work's id.

Every date is `public.app_today()`, never `CURRENT_DATE`.

## Rollback

Not expected.
- To undo the award and the statement of work: drop `gc_award`, `gc_leveled_total`, `gc_exclusion_key`, `gc_sow_lines` and `gc_sows`; the three award columns with their two constraints; `gc_invites_id_company`; and rebuild the consent CHECK without `'gc_sow'`.
- The contract step cannot be undone with the values: re-adding the columns would give zeros, and `gc_project_money` holds the real ones.
