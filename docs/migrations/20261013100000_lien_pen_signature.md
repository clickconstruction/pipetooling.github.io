# 20261013100000_lien_pen_signature.sql (2026-10-12, v2.5366 — the pen train PR 1)

Lien desk signing, signed with a pen (`docs/DOCSMIT_LIEN_RUN_PLAN.md`, section *Signed with a pen*). A packet prints, the leader signs the papers with a pen, the office mails them, and Record the mailing held the envelopes as *Unsigned* with no way to say he had already signed the paper. Every packet printed before the signing rule (20261010020000) sits in the same spot. This adds the third way a notice is signed: on the paper as printed, written down by the office. A signature is the record, not the mark (`src/lib/jobs/lienNoticeSignature.ts`): the row names who signed, when, who wrote it down and how they know, and the frame's record ID already on the paper ties the page to the row.

## What it does

1. **`job_lien_desk_items`** gains three nullable columns: `signed_recorded_by` (the signed-in office user who wrote it down, `users.id`), `signed_witness_how` (`watched` = they watched him sign, `told` = he told them, `page` = the signed page is in hand; its photo, when there is one, goes in the existing `signer_signature_storage_path`), and `signed_leader_confirmed_at` (when the leader pressed *Yes, I signed these* on his phone; nothing waits on it).
2. **`signer_signature_mode`**'s check widens from `type` | `draw` to `type` | `draw` | `pen`. The constraint is dropped and re-added under the same name; `signed_witness_how` gets its own check.
3. **The guard** `job_lien_desk_items_guard()` is re-created with the pen rule and is otherwise as 20261010020000 left it. A newly signed row still names who signed, how and the printed name, and the signer is still a master or dev. When the mode is `pen`: `signed_witness_how` is required, `signed_recorded_by` defaults to `auth.uid()` and must equal it when set, and `signed_on_device_of` is cleared. The electronic rule (his own sign-in, or his drawing on this screen) applies only to the other two modes. A newly set `signed_leader_confirmed_at` is refused unless the mode is `pen` and the writer is the signer himself.

Additive and idempotent: `ADD COLUMN IF NOT EXISTS`, `DROP CONSTRAINT IF EXISTS` + `ADD`, a guarded `ADD CONSTRAINT`, `CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION`. No new table, so no read-only or digital-twin fences to re-apply. Apply with `supabase db push` after the PR merges; `src/types/database.ts` is regenerated in a follow-up chore once the columns exist in prod (the kernel types the ten columns itself until then).
