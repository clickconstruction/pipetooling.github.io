# 20261117040000_sub_papers.sql (2026-11-16, v2.7474)

The register of sub papers (punch list #350, step 2 of the six under the card's *Where it stands*: the numbers' database half, alone). No screen calls it yet, so its merge holds no deploy.

- **`sub_papers`**: every Work statement, Payment record and Statement of account that was made. `kind`, `number`, `revision`, the person, the sheet (`labor_job_id`), the sending or the lone payment, the period, `snapshot` (the paper as issued, the model the print page and the PDF both draw), `fingerprint` (a short text of its figures, to tell a change from a reprint), `comes_to`, `still_open`, who issued it and when, `replaced_by` and the void pair. A numbered copy is one of a kind: unique on `(kind, number, revision)` where the number is not blank.
- **`sub_paper_job_counters`**: the last n given out for `WS-<job>-<n>`, per job number. No policy and no grant. Only the mint touches it.
- **`sub_paper_acks`**: what a sub said back about a paper from their portal (`received`, `confirmed`, `something_wrong`), with the revision they saw. Written only with the service key, by the portal's function in a later step.
- **`sub_paper_sa_number_seq`**: the company-wide sequence behind `SA-<n>`. No login may take a number from it.
- **`mint_sub_paper(p_kind, p_person_id, p_snapshot, p_fingerprint, p_labor_job_id, p_sending_id, p_payment_id, p_period_from, p_period_to, p_comes_to, p_still_open)`**, `SECURITY DEFINER`, returns `(id, number, revision, reused)`.
  - *Work statement*: `WS-<job>-<n>`, minted the first time the sheet's paper is made. The same fingerprint again hands back the row it has. A different one is the next revision under the same number, and the copy it replaces points at it.
  - *Payment record*: one per sending, numbered `PR-<the sending's number>`. A payment recorded before sendings has no number, and its record is filed with a blank one.
  - *Statement of account*: always a new paper, `SA-<n>`.
- **`void_sub_paper(p_paper_id, p_reason)`**, `SECURITY DEFINER`: the paper keeps its number and reads void. The reason is required.

**Never reused.** A deleted sheet's row stays with its number (`labor_job_id` goes NULL). A voided paper keeps its number, and the sheet's next paper takes a new one.

**Who.** Both functions refuse a caller who is not office staff (`is_office_staff()`: dev, master technician, assistant, controller), a training account, a digital twin and an archived account, each with 42501 and its own words. A definer function runs past the row fences, so it holds them itself. Execute is revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon` and granted to `authenticated`. The office reads `sub_papers` and `sub_paper_acks` by policy. Every other privilege on the three tables is revoked from `anon` and `authenticated`, so a row cannot be typed, changed or removed through the API.

**What changed from the spike's file** (`20261115060000_sub_papers.sql` on the snapshot branch): the three refusals above, a transaction lock per sheet and per sending inside the mint (two people pressing at the same moment on a sheet with no paper yet would each have taken a number), the grants revoked, and execute revoked from `anon` as well as `PUBLIC`.

Starts with `SET lock_timeout = '3s';`. Three new tables, so it ends with the four fence calls. Additive and idempotent: `IF NOT EXISTS`, `CREATE OR REPLACE`, policies dropped and made again.
