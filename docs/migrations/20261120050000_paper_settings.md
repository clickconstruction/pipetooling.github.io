# 20261120050000_paper_settings.sql (2026-11-19, v2.8014)

The Contract Book takes settings, the ground (punch list #389 step 1, `to-dos/sub-nda-builder/README.md`). A Book entry may carry a settings list, a clause may be tied to one choice of one setting, and the database puts the paper together for a job's picks. No screen rides with it. No clause wording is written.

## What it makes

No table, so no fence call. Every statement is safe to run twice.

- **Six columns on `contract_template_documents`.** `settings_def` (null on every ordinary paper) and the read mark: `read_revision`, `read_by_name`, `read_on`, `read_marked_by`, `read_marked_at`. Two checks hold the list to an object and the mark to whole or absent.
- **Two columns on `contract_clauses` and on `contract_clause_proposals`**: `slot` and `when_opts`. A check holds `when_opts` to an object that is not empty, and a `slot` to a key that comes with a `when_opts`.
- **A guard on `contract_template_documents`**, `contract_paper_settings_guard`. Nobody signed in writes the settings list, the read mark, the revision or `published_at` by hand. The table's own policies let the pay team write the row, and the revision had no guard before this.
- **`set_paper_settings(p_paper_id, p_def)`** and **`mark_paper_read(p_paper_id, p_name, p_on)`**, a controller's or a dev's, as a clause is approved. Each refuses the signed out, an archived account, a digital twin and a training account in words.
- **`edit_clause` with two more names**, `p_slot` and `p_when_opts`. The seven-name function is dropped here, because a new parameter is a new function. `contract_clause_apply` keeps its signature and carries the two columns.
- **`contract_paper_clauses` with two more names**, `p_settings` and `p_fields`. The three-name function is dropped here.
- **A trigger on `person_contract_documents`**, `contract_copy_refuses_bare_settings`. A copy of a paper built from settings is refused, and so is signing a copy onto one, for every caller.
- **Four helpers** nobody signed in may call: `contract_paper_settings_def_problem`, `contract_paper_picks_problem`, `contract_paper_fields` and `contract_clause_when_problem`.

## Rules it settles

- **A settings list is data, not wording.** `{settings: [{key, label, choices: [{key, label, says, out, fills}]}], fields: [{key, label}], levels: [{key, label, alignedWith, picks}]}`. A choice's place in its list is its strictness, the loosest first. `out` marks a choice that leaves the topic out, as *Off* does.
- **A clause is kept when `when_opts IS NULL OR when_opts <@` the job's picks.** Null is a clause in every paper.
- **A slot is a place with several wordings.** Clauses of one paper that share a `slot` are wordings of one place. Exactly one is in the paper for a job's picks, and it reads where the place's first wording is. A clause with a `when_opts` and no `slot` is an add-on: left out for every other choice, with no gap to answer for.
- **What stops a paper going, in words:** no settings at all, a setting with no pick of its own, a place with no wording for the pick or with two, and a blank with no value. The database raises them (`P0001`). The kernel says the same words in the preview.
- **A blank is `{key}` in a clause's words**, for a key on the list's `fields`. A picked choice fills it (`fills`, and `fillsEs` in Spanish), and what was typed for the job goes on top. Only keys on the list are filled, so braces in an ordinary paper's clause are left as written.
- **A read belongs to a revision.** `mark_paper_read` writes the paper's revision with the attorney's name and the day. A later publish moves the revision on, so the paper is unread again with no write. A new settings list is a new revision too.
- **A list cannot drop what a live clause is tied to.** `set_paper_settings` refuses, and says to change that clause first.
- **A paper built from settings is never signed bare.** A caller that passes no settings is refused by the assembly, and a copy is refused when it is made and when it is signed. The stop is in the database because `accept-contract` signs a copy's body when its clause read fails. Step 3 of the card puts a job's settings on the gate's own row and loosens the copy's refusal for that row alone.
- **A paper takes its first settings list before it goes to anyone.** `set_paper_settings` refuses while a copy of the paper is unsent or sent.

## Safe under what is deployed

- The edge functions deployed today call `contract_paper_clauses` with three names. They get the defaults, so an ordinary paper reads as before.
- The app on Pages today calls `edit_clause` with seven names. It gets the defaults: a clause in every paper, in a place of its own.
- `contract_clause_freeze` calls the assembly with three values and is not changed.
- No Book entry has a settings list until someone sets one, so nothing changes for any paper on the day this is pushed.
