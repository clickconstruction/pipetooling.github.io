# 20260924040000_team_prospect_role_shares.sql (2026-09-24, v2.3798)

Hiring → the helper try-out loop, PR 4 (`to-dos/helper-tryout-loop`): the column share. One new table, two helpers, and the hiring policies rewritten so a share is enforced by RLS, not only hidden by the client (the trimmed tab is PR 6).

- **`team_prospect_role_shares`** — `role_id` → `team_prospect_roles` (cascade), `user_id` → `users` (cascade), `shared_by` → `users` (set null), `created_at`; **primary key `(role_id, user_id)`**. RLS: SELECT — a full holder (`user_has_team_prospects_access()`), a dev, or one's own rows; INSERT — a full holder, `shared_by = auth.uid()`; DELETE — a full holder; **no UPDATE** (a share is made or removed). Ends with `apply_read_only_write_blocks()`, `apply_read_only_stmt_blocks()` and `apply_digital_twin_write_blocks()`.
- **`user_hiring_shared_role_ids()`** → `SETOF uuid` — the columns shared with the caller, **empty without prospects staff access**, so a share written to a helper or customer account grants nothing. `SECURITY DEFINER`; the hiring policies and the tally join it beside `user_has_team_prospects_access()`.
- **`user_can_work_team_prospect(p_prospect_id)`** → `boolean` — a dev, a full holder, or a share on the card's column. `create-user`'s `trial_prospect_id` door calls it in place of `user_has_team_prospects_access()`; the trial-verdicts SELECT policy reads it.
- **`team_prospect_roles` SELECT** — full holder, a dev (the column names only — the Active accounts line names the columns an account holds; the cards stay behind the switch), or the column is shared with the caller. INSERT / UPDATE / DELETE (add, rename, delete a column) stay the board's.
- **`team_prospects`** — SELECT: full holder, or the card is in a shared column **and `status IN ('active', 'calling', 'trial')`** — a hired or passed card is never returned to a share. UPDATE (USING and WITH CHECK): full holder, or shared column and `status IN ('active', 'calling')` before and after — so a share may edit, rank, *Talked today*, Advance and send back to Screen, and the database refuses Hire, Passed, a move to another column, and *Keep trying* (a trial card). INSERT: the existing rule (`is_office_staff()`, or the board under the owner rules) plus a share — a card by the caller, `status = 'active'`, in a shared column, under the same owner rules. DELETE stays the board's.
- **`team_prospect_trial_verdicts` SELECT** — `user_can_work_team_prospect(prospect_id)` or the leader's own rows (was: full holder, dev, or own rows).
- **`team_prospect_trial_tally()`** — same body as v2.3715; the cards gate gains `tp.role_id IN (SELECT user_hiring_shared_role_ids())`, so a share reads the tally on its own column's Try-out cards.
- **Unchanged on purpose:** `end_team_prospect_trial()` (Hire / Pass are the office's — the to-do's decision 5), `users_guard_privileged_columns` (`create-user` runs as the service role), `team_prospect_reviews` and the onboarding tables (already readable by every prospects-staff account).
