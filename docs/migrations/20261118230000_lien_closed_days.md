# 20261118230000_lien_closed_days.sql (2026-11-18, v2.7817)

A lien day that passed with nothing filed stays listed until someone answers it. It ships with the desk's read of it in the same PR ([v2.7817](../recent-features/v2.7817.md)).

`list_lien_affidavit_windows` stops listing a job seven days after its last day to file (`d.deadline >= app_today() - 7`). So a lien day nobody answered left the Lien desk by itself a week later, and the Board's closed bin, *Lien day passed · not filed*, emptied with no one having said what became of the money. The notices' reader has kept an unrecorded closed month since v2.3680. The affidavits' reader had no such rule.

No table changes. Three functions.

| Function | What it is |
|---|---|
| `lien_affidavit_days()` | New. The body of `list_lien_affidavit_windows` as [`20261113200000`](./20261113200000_lien_months_from_bill.md) left it, with its two date filters and its `ORDER BY` taken off and nothing else changed: one row per billed, unpaid, collectible job that has a last day to file, whatever that day. The month rules and the role gate live here once. |
| `list_lien_affidavit_windows(p_within_days integer DEFAULT 30)` | Replaced. The same arguments, the same sixteen columns, the same rows in the same order: now those two date filters and that `ORDER BY` over `lien_affidavit_days()`. |
| `list_lien_closed_days()` | New. The rows of `lien_affidavit_days()` whose day is more than seven days past (`deadline < app_today() - 7`), with no filed affidavit (`NOT filed`), on a job still on the lien path (`jobs_ledger.lien_path_answer IS NULL`). The same sixteen columns, ordered by day, then job. |

- **The two lists never share a job.** The windows reader keeps a day down to seven days past. The closed days start on the eighth.
- **How a job leaves the closed days.** A filing is recorded (`job_lien_filings`, kind `affidavit`, a filed day, not voided). Its last day of work is corrected, so its day moves ahead and the windows reader lists it. It is taken off the lien path (`set_job_lien_path_answer`). It is marked Uncollectible. It is paid. The last two are the shared reader's own rules (`uncollectible_at IS NULL`, money still owed). A paper that is not a filing changes nothing: a drafted, approved or held affidavit, a notice, an affidavit record with no filed day, a voided filing.
- **No cut-off date.** Every job whose lien day passed more than a week ago with no answer is listed from the day of the push, however old the day. They were counted read-only on prod before this was written, and the owner was told the number before the PR was armed.

## Who may call what

- All three are `LANGUAGE sql STABLE SECURITY DEFINER SET search_path = public`, as the windows reader was.
- **The gate** is the windows reader's, word for word, and now stands in `lien_affidavit_days()` alone: dev, assistant, master_technician and the service role get rows. Anyone else gets none.
- `list_lien_affidavit_windows` keeps its grants (`authenticated`, `service_role`). `CREATE OR REPLACE` keeps them, and the file restates them so it stands alone.
- `list_lien_closed_days`: `EXECUTE` to `authenticated` and `service_role`, revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`.
- `lien_affidavit_days`: `EXECUTE` revoked from `PUBLIC`, `anon`, `authenticated` and `service_role`. Only its owner may call it. The two readers run as that same owner (`postgres`, read off prod's `pg_proc` on 2026-11-18), so they reach it and PostgREST does not offer it to anyone.

## What does not change

- Every reader of `list_lien_affidavit_windows` keeps its seven days: the Lien desk, the lien book, Materials, the law firm's portal, the dev seat's verbs. None of them was edited.
- The two older beds, `lien-hand-day` and `lien-bill-months`, apply their own migration last, so they still pin the reader as those files left it.
- Nothing files, voids or deletes a paper. Nothing is written at all.
- The body that moved is the body prod runs: on 2026-11-18 prod's `pg_proc.prosrc` for `list_lien_affidavit_windows` equalled the body in `20261113200000_lien_months_from_bill.sql` byte for byte (a read-only read), and the moved body diffs clean against that file but for the two date filters and the `ORDER BY`.

## Order

Merge and push in one sitting. The desk calls `list_lien_closed_days`, which only this migration creates, so `npm run check:calls-ahead` labels the PR *push right after the merge* and the Pages deploy holds until the push. The desk's read also answers no rows while the function is absent, so a client that did land first reads as it did before. On the day of the push every listed job appears in the closed bin at once.

## Bed

`npm run test:pg:lien-closed-days` (`supabase/tests/lien_closed_days/`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): the `lien_hand_day` bed's stand-in tables, the two days a bill carries, the job's lien path answer, the two real deadline functions and the readers as `20261113200000` left them. The windows reader of that file is then kept under the name `list_lien_affidavit_windows_before`, this migration is applied twice, and 30 assertions run in one transaction that rolls back.

- **The same rows as before.** On three steps (seven days past, eight days past, after every answer) and as the service role, the new body and the old one answer the same rows, every column, in the same order, for the asks 0, 30, 180, 400, a negative one and none.
- **The eighth day.** A job seven days past its day is the windows reader's and not a closed day. The next day it has left the one and joined the other. No job is on both.
- **The row.** Each closed day's row equals, column for column, the row the old reader gave the job while it still listed it (the day set back to five days past).
- **Each answer.** Filed, off the lien path (and put back), Uncollectible, paid, the last day of work corrected: each drops its job, and what is left is what nobody answered.
- **The gate and the grants.** An assistant and a master read; a technician and a signed-out caller get nothing from either reader; `has_function_privilege` for the three functions; and under `SET LOCAL ROLE authenticated` both readers answer while a direct call to the shared reader is refused.

No Postgres or Docker on the machine that wrote it, so the bed ran on GitHub before the PR was opened, alone on a throwaway branch (`ci-test/lien-closed-days`, since deleted).
