# 20261118203000_trip_line_tie.sql (2026-11-18, v2.7860)

A trip put back is tied to its line again.

A trip's line on a job carries the trip's id (`jobs_ledger_fixtures.trip_id`, `20261118060000_trip_bill_line.sql`), and deleting the trip clears it. The Undo of a delete and **Put back** (`20261118100000_trips_deleted.sql`) both make the trip again under its own id. Neither tied the line back. The trip then read *Extra · not billed yet* with its old line still on the job, and **Put on a bill** would add a second line for the same work.

## What it changes

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| `trips_archive_on_delete()` | The trip's capture into `deleted_records_archive`. It takes the place of the archive's generic capture on `trips` under the same trigger name. The row it writes is the row as it was, with `held_line_id` when a job's line carries the trip. |
| `trips_settle_archive()` | As before, a trip made again marks its archive rows restored. First it reads `held_line_id` from the delete nothing has put back and ties that line to the trip. |

No table, column or index is added, and `jobs_ledger_fixtures` is not altered.

## When the line is tied

All of these must hold. Otherwise nothing is tied, and the trip comes back holding no line.

- The trip held a line when it was deleted.
- The line is still there. A line removed since is not made again.
- The line is tied to no trip.
- The trip made again holds no line.

While the trip is gone the line is an ordinary line, as before. It can be repriced, billed or removed. A line billed in the meantime comes back tied, and the trip's chip then reads that bill.

## What it covers

The Undo of a delete, **Put back**, the dev seat's `apply_trip` on a deleted trip's id and the dev's own `restore_deleted_records` all insert the trip again, so all four tie the line.

## What stays as it was

- Deleting the trip still clears the mark and leaves the line, with its name, its words and its price.
- A capture never blocks a delete and a tie never blocks a save. Each is guarded and logs a warning.
- Every archived row is still restorable by the dev's `restore_deleted_records`: `jsonb_populate_record` passes over a key the table does not have.
- `trip_deleted_list` does not say whether a deleted trip held a line.

## Locks

Two `CREATE TRIGGER` statements on `trips`, a small table. The file starts with `SET lock_timeout = '3s'`. It is idempotent.

## Tests

`supabase/tests/trips/70_line_tie.sql`, the sixth scenario of the trips bed (`npm run test:pg:trips`):

- the trip's capture is its own, under the archive's own trigger name;
- a deleted trip leaves its line untied, and its capture keeps the line's id;
- a dry run of the put back ties nothing;
- a put back ties the line, and a second press is answered as a duplicate and adds no line;
- a line removed while the trip was gone is not made again, and the press is free;
- a line another trip took meanwhile is left with that trip;
- the dev's restore ties the line too;
- a trip that held no line keeps no id.
