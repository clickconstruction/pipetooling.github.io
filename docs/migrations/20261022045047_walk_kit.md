# 20261022045047 — the walk kit's rows: walk_setup and walk_cleanup (v2.5725)

**What it does**: two `SECURITY DEFINER` functions the service role alone may call (`REVOKE … FROM PUBLIC, anon, authenticated; GRANT … TO service_role`), for punch list #142's walk kit (`to-dos/walk-kit/`, PR 4), plus two small helpers of theirs (`walk_setting_json`, a setting's JSON or null; `walk_contact_record`, the record a contact is, in words).

- **`walk_setup(p_tester uuid, p_app_from text, p_phone text DEFAULT NULL, p_shared_phone_ok boolean DEFAULT false) RETURNS jsonb`** makes the kit once and answers it. It reads Settings' boxes row (`app_settings` `messages_boxes_v1`): the kit's email is the bids box's address, its phone is `p_phone`, else the bids box's number. In one transaction it makes a `customers` row (*ZZ Walk Messages Desk*, residential, `contact_info` with the email and phone, `master_user_id` from `company_owner_user_id()`, the SQL mirror of the client's `resolveCompanyOwnerUserId`, else the maker), its contact (kind `customer`, no name, as `contact_for_customer` makes one) with the two channels, a draft estimate (*ZZ walk estimate (delete me)*, one line of $1.00, `customer_email` the kit's, `created_by` the maker, valid 14 days), and writes `walk_kit_v1` (`customerId`, `contactId`, `emailChannelId`, `phoneChannelId`, `estimateId`, `email`, `phone`, `testerId`, `sharedPhone`, `madeAt`), answering it with `made: true`. A second call while the kit's customer exists answers the stored kit with `made: false`.
- It **refuses, in words and writing nothing**: the bids box with no address or number; the two boxes on one number, or the kit's phone on the team box's number (the kit would text itself); a maker who is not the walk tester or a dev; and any contact carrying the kit's email, the kit's phone (unless `p_shared_phone_ok`, the owner's-cell fallback of BUILD_PLAN § 4), the team box's address, the team box's number or the app's sender (`p_app_from`), naming each carrier's contact id, kind and record. The rule: only the kit may carry an address or number of our own, and nothing may carry the side that sends to it (README decision 4).
- **The maker's check reads `users.is_walk_tester` only when the column exists** (PR 2's migration may land before or after this one): through `information_schema.columns` and dynamic SQL. Before the column, the tester is what PR 2's decision 5 makes it, a training-mode assistant (`role = 'assistant'`, `read_only`, not a sample, not a twin). A dev account not in training mode passes too, for the walk with the owner's two presses, where the owner is the estimate's maker.
- **`walk_cleanup() RETURNS jsonb`** deletes the kit's rows and nothing else, answering the counts per table (`{ cleaned: true, counts, kit }`), or `{ nothing: true, words: 'nothing to clean' }` with no kit row. Children first, the contact before the customer (`contacts_keep_thread` on `customers` would otherwise keep it as an unknown carrying bids@ and the bids number): `message_attachments`, `message_promises`, `message_highlights`, `contact_owed`, `call_tasks`, `nudge_offers`, `nudge_sent`, `nudge_holds`, `quick_reply_uses`, `contact_step_skips`, `contact_jobs`, the `messages`, `contact_channels`, the contact, `estimate_customer_events`, `sent_documents` (the kit's customer, or an `estimates` source that is the kit's), the estimate, the customer, the setting.
- It **refuses and deletes nothing** when another contact carries the kit's email or phone (unless the kit recorded `sharedPhone`); when a line on the kit's thread sits on a channel that is not one of the kit's two (a real person folded in through Who is this?); when a file was kept on the thread (`bucket_path`; the kit sends none); when another contact or estimate is on the kit's customer, or another thread's line points at the kit's estimate; and when any foreign key from a table not on the list above points at the kit's customer, contact, estimate, channels or lines with a row, read live from `pg_constraint`, so a table added later is refused, not cascaded. A job on the kit's customer is one of those: delete it in the app (Delete job), then clean up again.
- **Kept**: the tester account, `email_send_log`, the mail in Gmail and the texts in Quo.

**Tables**: none made or changed. **RLS**: none (the functions are definers; nobody signed in may run them). **Fences**: none (no table).

**Bed**: `npm run test:pg:walk-kit` (`scripts/pgtest-walk-kit.sh`, `supabase/tests/walk_kit/`): the whole schema on the Supabase image (CI, `scripts/sql-beds.txt`), or on a local Postgres 15 with `PGTEST_PGBIN` and the bed's `00_local_extras.sql` standing in for what the image has. Setup twice makes one kit; every refusal above; cleanup deletes only the kit's rows while a real customer beside it keeps every row; the fallback phone and a dev as the maker.

**Apply**: `supabase db push` from a clean copy of `main` after this merges (the parent session of punch list #142 pushes it). Nothing deployed reads the functions until the `walk` edge function (PR 7), so the order against PR 2's push does not matter.

**Check**: `select proname from pg_proc where proname in ('walk_setup', 'walk_cleanup')` answers both; `select has_function_privilege('authenticated', 'public.walk_cleanup()', 'execute')` is false.
