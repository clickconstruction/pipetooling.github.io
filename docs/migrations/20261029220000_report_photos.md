# 20261029220000_report_photos.sql (2026-10-29, Phone first: the camera-first report, punch list #189 PR 5b)

The ground under the camera-first field report (`to-dos/phone-first/`, section 6 of the mock-up as drawn, the shrink the owner asked for on 2026-10-26): a tech's Leave report opens the phone's camera, the phone shrinks the shot and sends it here, Save ties it to the report, and the `photo-save` sweep files it in the job's Drive photo folder.

- **`report_photos`** — one row per shot: the job, the report once saved (`report_id`, null while the form is open; a row still null is a form that was not saved, and the client discards it), the stage it shows (`fixture_id` → `jobs_ledger_fixtures`, `stage_name` for the name), the caption the file is named by, who took it, when, the sizes before and after the shrink (`original_bytes`, `bytes`, `width`, `height`), where it waits (`storage_path`), and where it landed (`drive_file_id`, `filed_at`) or why it has not (`fail_why`, which the report card reads as *not in Drive yet*).
- **The gate**: `can_report_stage_progress(job_id)` (v2.3192's door: office roles, or a field user the job is visible to) for reads and inserts; a row is changed only by its taker, and discarded by its taker only while no report holds it.
- **The bucket** `report-photos`: private, 2 MB a file (nothing full size can slip through), JPEG, PNG and WebP; objects are `<job_id>/<photo_id>.jpg`, so the same gate reads the job from the path; the taker may delete their own unsaved object, the sweep deletes with the service role once Drive has the file.
- **The setting** `app_settings.report_photo_size` = `medium` on day one (`small` 1200 px · `medium` 1600 · `large` 2400, the long edge the phone shrinks to), set by a master or a dev on Settings → Jobs & billing → Photos (a key-scoped UPDATE policy, the `gc_card_bill_on_v1` pattern).
- **The cron** `photo-save` is re-scheduled (unschedule, then schedule, as `20261029160000` did) to also call while a saved report's photo waits.
- The three house calls at the end: training-mode write and statement blocks, and the digital-twin fence (no `bid_id`, so a twin writes nothing here).
