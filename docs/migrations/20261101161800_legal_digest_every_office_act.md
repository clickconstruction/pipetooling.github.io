# 20261101161800_legal_digest_every_office_act.sql (2026-11-01, v2.6283)

Punch list #233, PR 4: the firm's weekly digest carries every office act (the owner's call 3).

- `legal_notification_queue`'s trigger CHECK becomes `legal_notification_queue_trigger_check_v3`: the seven of v2 plus `step`, `plan`, `event`, `offer`, `statement`, `paper`, `released` (the v2 constraint is found by definition and dropped; the new one is added `NOT VALID`, then validated).
- `legal_entries_notify_answer()` (same name, so the existing `AFTER INSERT` trigger runs it) now also queues the office's `step` (payload `body`, `stage`; a step whose body only echoes a release or a pull-back is left to the `referred` / `pulled` rows), `plan` (the body with *· planned for Mon D*), `event`, `offer` (the body prefixed *made by the office* / *received by the office*, with `amount`) and `statement` (its title). These five queue **only while the firm has the matter**: `released_at` set (a pull-back clears it, so a pulled-back or in-house matter stays home even though it keeps `firm_id`), and `closed_at` null or equal to `now()`, so the closing step `legal_close_matter` writes in the close's own transaction reaches the firm and nothing after it does. One held back under `held_overrides` (`<kind>:<id>` = true) at insert is not queued. The four kinds that email now (answer, ask, note, payment applied) keep their old gate, a firm on the matter.
- Every row the function writes carries `entryId`, so the dispatcher can read the entry when the digest is built and drop one the office voided, or held back after it was queued (holding is a press after the save).
- New `legal_matter_documents_notify()` with two triggers on `legal_matter_documents`: `AFTER INSERT` queues `paper` (the title, `documentId`) unless the row is held or voided; `AFTER UPDATE OF held_reason` queues `released` when a held paper's reason is cleared to blank. Both only while the firm has the matter and it is open.

The dispatcher holds every new trigger to the digest (`legalTriggerSendsNow`), reads each line's source when the digest is built (`legalDigestShows`), and from v2.6283 each digest reads back to that person's own last digest (or their confirmation, at most eight days), so two people on different weekdays no longer split the week. No columns change.

**Tested on a throwaway Postgres 15** (2026-11-01): the v2 migration `20261006170000` then this one twice over the queue's first inline CHECK; only `legal_notification_queue_trigger_check_v3` remains. A scenario across a live, a pulled-back, an in-house, an earlier-closed, a closing and a firm-less matter queued exactly the 14 rows it should: the office's five acts and two papers on the live matter, the closing step, the four emailing kinds under their old gate; a held plan, the release echo, the firm's own step, every act on the pulled-back, in-house and earlier-closed matters, a voided paper and a paper on the just-closed matter stayed home. An answer still quotes the firm's question, a malformed `askId` still never fails the insert, and an unknown trigger is refused.
