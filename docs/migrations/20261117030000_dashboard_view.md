# 20261117030000_dashboard_view.sql (2026-11-16, the Dashboard view: Short or Full per person, punch list #365)

The short Dashboard (v2.7466): a leader can open onto a list of what waits on him alone. Two pieces, both additive.

- **`users.dashboard_view`** (text, nullable, CHECK in `short`, `full`): the Dashboard the person opens onto. NULL reads as full, so no account changes until someone presses. A person reads their own row and the office reads the People desk's, as they read `users.week_signal`. No new policy.
- **`set_dashboard_view(p_user_id, p_view)`** (SECURITY DEFINER, `search_path` public): the press from Settings (the person's own) or the People desk (the office's). The leader and the office (`is_master_or_dev()` or `is_assistant()`). A training account or a twin is refused in words (`{ ok: false, error }`). `p_view` must be `short` or `full`. Only a `master_technician` or `dev` account takes a view, since the short page is drawn for those roles alone. EXECUTE for `authenticated`.

No table is made, so no fence call.

Apply order: the client in the same PR reads the column and calls the function. Push right after the merge. Until the push the read fails quietly and every account draws the full Dashboard.
