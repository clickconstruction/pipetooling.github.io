# 20261110195527_bid_photos.sql (2026-11-10, Bid photos, punch list #306 PR 1)

The ground under photos on a bid (punch list #306, v2.6854): the Bid Board's photos icon, always in its place in the Links column, and the window behind it where photos are added and managed in the app while Google Drive keeps them. One table and one bucket. No screen reads or writes either yet, and `bids` itself is not altered.

- **`bid_photos`** — a job photo's shape (`20261102171500_job_photo_notes.sql`) keyed on the bid: `name` (what the window shows, and the Drive file's name once filed), who (`by_user_id`, `by_name`) and when (`taken_at`), the sizes, `storage_path` while the shot is in the bucket, `drive_file_id` and `filed_at` once the sweep has filed it, `fail_why` while it cannot, and `added_at`. The desk or the phone shrinks the shot and puts it in the bucket with a row; the press that adds the photos writes `added_at`; the `photo-save` sweep (PR 2) files only rows with `added_at` into the bid's project folder under Team only › Photos, the place a picture saved from a text onto a bid already lands, and deletes the bucket copy. A row with no `added_at` is a window closed without the press; the client discards it.
- **A picture already in that Drive folder** (dragged in through Drive, or saved from a text) is adopted as a row by the service role, with no `storage_path`. A unique index on `drive_file_id` makes one Drive file one row.
- **Removing is a stamp, never a delete**: `removed_at` and `removed_by`. The Drive file goes to Drive's trash (PR 4) and the row stays, so an Undo can bring it back.
- **The gate is the bid's own.** Every policy asks whether the caller can read the bid with a sub-select on `bids`, which runs under `bids`' own policies: the six roles that read a bid, a primary only on the bids they estimate, manage or made (`primary_scope_bids`), and nobody archived. `can_access_bid_for_pricing` is not used, since it lets any primary through. **Select** by whoever reads the bid; **insert** by the same people as themselves; **update** (the name, the removal stamp) by the adder or the office (`is_office_staff`: dev, master, assistant, controller); **delete** by the adder only while `added_at` is null.
- **The bucket `bid-photos`** — private, 2 MB a file, JPEG, PNG and WebP, objects `<bid_id>/<photo_id>.jpg`. Insert and select read the bid from the path's first folder through the same sub-select. A path whose first folder is not a uuid fails the pattern before any cast, so it is refused as a row-level violation and never as an error. The adder deletes their own object; the sweep deletes with the service role.
- The four fence calls close it, since a table is made. The twin fence finds the `bid_id` column, so a twin writes only on a bid it made or estimates.

**Who may remove is the owner's call 6 on the card.** The update policy is drawn to the card's pick. A different answer is one policy, changed with PR 4.

**The bed**: `npm run test:pg:bid-photos` (`scripts/pgtest-bid-photos.sh`, `supabase/tests/bid_photos/20_scenario.sql`) applies every migration, then this one a second time, and walks the gate as a dev, an assistant, an estimator, a primary on the bid, a primary off it, a subcontractor, a superintendent, a training account and a twin, on the table and on the bucket.
