# 20261016020000_on_my_way.sql (2026-10-16, v2.5573)

On my way and I'm here (punch list #112, `to-dos/on-my-way/README.md`): Job Mode's big button clocks the tech in on the next job as on the way and texts the customer the office's line; while on the way the button reads I'm here and stamps the arrival. The client reads the new columns fail-soft, so it may deploy before the push; push after the feature PR merges and before the types regenerate.

## What it does

1. **`clock_sessions`** gains `travel_started_at`, `arrived_at` (timestamptz) and the arrival's point, `arrived_lat`, `arrived_lng`, `arrived_location_source`, beside the clock-in's. A visit stays one row; the drive is `arrived_at − travel_started_at`. No policy changes: the tech's own-row insert and update already cover them, and the read-only and twin fences still hold.
2. **`quick_replies.phone_press`** (`on_my_way` | `im_here`): which live team line each big button sends, one line a press among live lines (`quick_replies_phone_press_one`, a partial unique index on `removed_at IS NULL`).
3. **`set_quick_reply_press(p_id uuid, p_press text)`**: the office points a press at a team line for customers on the phone, or clears it with null. The press leaves any other live line first, with a `phone` version on each line that changed; returns how many. Refuses in the quick replies' own words (`quick_reply_caller`): signed out, a training account, a twin, off the office team; and an unknown press, a line that is not there, a removed line, a line off the phone. SECURITY DEFINER; EXECUTE to `authenticated`, revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`.
4. **The seeds, once**: the first live phone line that uses `{minutes}` becomes the On my way line, else one is added (*{first}, this is {me} with Click Plumbing. I am on my way, about {minutes} minutes out.*, named *On my way*); an I'm here line is added when none holds that press (*… I am here at {address}.*, named *I’m here*). Each new line takes the next place on the phone with its `added` and `phone` versions. A second run changes nothing.

## Why the shape

- Two instants on the session, not a travel session of its own: two rows a visit would break every hours, approval and payroll reader.
- A column for the press, not a match on the words: the office rewords either line in the editor and the press stays; the editor's **Big button on Job Mode** picker shows it.
- No function for the job's point: `address_geocodes` has been open to every signed-in user since v2.3131, so the phone reads it as the map does.

## Verify

Read-only on prod after the push: `select phone_name, phone_press, phone_order from quick_replies where phone_press is not null` reads two live lines; `select count(*) from clock_sessions where travel_started_at is not null` reads 0 until the first press. The SQL bed `npm run test:pg:on-my-way` (CI) walks the seeds, the press moving between lines, each refusal, a helper's read of a geocoded point, and a helper's own stamps.
