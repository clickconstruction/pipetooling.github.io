# 20261008140000_gc_trade_writes.sql (2026-10-08, v2.4924)

GC mode, the real build, the trade partner portal's P2a (`to-dos/gc-mode/PORTAL_REAL_BUILD.md` → *The PRs, in order*; plan `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/portal-p2a.md`, on branch `spike/gc-mode`). It adds the first things a trade does in its portal, as SQL: twelve `gc_trade_<verb>` functions on the company record (B1's tables) and on `gc_plan_questions`. Nothing calls them yet. P2b's `submit-gc-trade-portal` will, once it has turned a link into its company.

- **Who calls them**: only the service role. Each function is revoked from `PUBLIC`, `anon` and `authenticated` and granted to `service_role`. They are `SECURITY INVOKER`, so they run as the service role that calls them.
- **The company check**: every verb takes the link's company first and acts only on that company's rows. The ask verbs start with `gc_trade_ask(company, invite)`, which locks the ask and refuses three things:
  - another company's ask (`notYours`);
  - a bid we lost (`projectLost`);
  - an ask the company passed on (`youPassed`).
  The people verbs refuse another company's person. A question needs an ask on that trade that the company did not pass on.
- **Refusals** are keys the page says in the company's language (decision 11): `RAISE EXCEPTION '<key>' USING ERRCODE = 'P0001'`, with the plain words in `DETAIL`. The plan doc lists all twenty.
- **The verbs**:
  - Got it: `portal_opened_on`, kept once.
  - Language.
  - People on its emails: add, take off, who gets what. Every kind of email keeps someone, and what only a person got goes back to the main contact when they are taken off.
  - Open the plans: `seen_rev`, and invited reads opened.
  - A quote day: a portal line on the ask.
  - Send a quote: a new `gc_quotes` row, append only, priced on the set it opened, every scope line yes or no, its schedule of values adding up.
  - Our number stands on the newest set.
  - Answer the lines the office could not read.
  - Pass, before a quote is in.
  - Ask about the plans, under `gc_record_question`'s closing day.
- **Unchanged**: the office's own numbers on an ask (`plugs`, `exclusion_covers`, `taken_alternates`) are never touched. Dates are `public.app_today()`, the office's day.
- **No table, column or policy.** Idempotent: every function is `CREATE OR REPLACE`, and the grants run again.

Apply order: after `20261008020000_gc_company_record` (B1), whose tables these write. It runs well alongside `20261008120000_gc_company_record_app_today`, in either order.
