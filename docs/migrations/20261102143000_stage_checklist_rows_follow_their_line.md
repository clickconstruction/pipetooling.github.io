# 20261102143000_stage_checklist_rows_follow_their_line.sql (2026-11-02, a stage's checklist follows its line through a split or a combine, punch list #237, v2.6350)

A split (`split_job_ledger_fixtures_to_new_job`) and a combine (`migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete`) both move a stage line by writing `jobs_ledger_fixtures.job_id`, and neither knew the checklist rows that name the line: `stage_checklist_answers` since #164, the job items on `stage_checklist_templates` since PR 1, `stage_checklist_skips` since PR 1b. The rows kept the old job. Every read filters on `job_id`, so after a split the new job's sheet read none of them. A combine then deleted the source job, and its cascade deleted them.

- **`jobs_ledger_fixtures_carry_checklist`** (`AFTER UPDATE OF job_id`, `WHEN` the job really changed, over `jobs_ledger_fixture_carries_checklist()`, `SECURITY DEFINER`): the rows on that line in the three tables take the new job, whatever door moved it. Only `job_id` changes: the pick, the stamp, the photo and `updated_at` read as they did. A retired item and a skip put back go too, since they are the record. Definer, since whoever moves a line holds no write on these columns: an adder's update is the retire, and `authenticated` may update only a skip's two put-back columns.
- **`stage_checklist_answers_on_stage_job`** (`BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE OF job_id, fixture_id`, over `stage_checklist_answer_on_stage_job()`, `SECURITY DEFINER`, since field roles cannot read the lines): an answer's job is always its line's job. A sheet opened before a split or a combine still writes the job it was opened on, and would strand a new row on the old job or fail on the deleted one. The row's job is read from the line before the policy's `WITH CHECK` runs, so the gate asks about the line's real job, and a field user who names their own job no longer answers a stage on another job. Both doors lock the job before they move a line, so the trigger takes the `FOR KEY SHARE` the foreign key would take anyway, then reads the line again. A press and a move take turns, and the press holds nothing while it waits, so there is no deadlock. Items and skips keep their refusal (*That stage is not on this job.*), the right answer for someone authoring.
- **`migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete`** restated: `20261031020000_job_prior_numbers.sql`'s body with one more line, `report_photos` moves with `reports`. A saved report moved to the target while its photo rows stayed on the source, so the source's delete took them, and `report_photos_mirror_checklist_answers_delete` then cleared the photo off any answer one of them answered. A waiting shot's row went the same way, leaving its file in the bucket. A split leaves the report and its photo on the old job, as the Separate tab says of every report. The answer moves and keeps naming the photo, so the mirror still reaches it.
- **The repair**: rows an earlier split stranded rejoin their line, one idempotent `UPDATE` per table. Prod held no answer, question or report photo on 2026-11-02 (read before this file was written), so this should find nothing. A row a combine already deleted is gone.

A bucket path keeps the old job's folder, `<job_id>/<id>.jpg`. The sweeps read the row's path, and the job they file under is now the right one. Until the sweep files a waiting shot, the sheet may draw no thumbnail for it, since the bucket's gate reads the job from the path and the sheet works out a report photo's path from the job it is on. The answer and the shot are both safe.

What this does not cover: deleting a job outright, or moving its costs to a bid, still takes its stage lines and their checklists, with no line left to carry them to. Each save of the job form's billing slice deleted and re-inserted the job's lines with new ids (`writeBillingSlice`), and `ON DELETE CASCADE` on `fixture_id` took every checklist row on the job with them, until v2.6420 made the save keep a line's id (`docs/recent-features/v2.6420.md`).

Additive and idempotent. No table is created, so there is no fence call. `CREATE OR REPLACE TRIGGER` takes `SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE`, which leaves readers alone and holds writers for a moment; the stage lines are locked first, the order every delete of a line takes before its cascade, and `lock_timeout` makes a busy moment fail fast. The two trigger functions are revoked from `PUBLIC`, `anon` and `authenticated`. The generated types do not change: neither trigger function is callable, and the combine's signature stands.

Apply order: right after the merge. The client changes nothing but words, so this is harmless on either side of the Pages deploy, but it restates a function the client calls, so `check:calls-ahead` labels the PR *push right after the merge* and the deploy holds until the push. `20261102084500` (#6798) comes first, since the trigger writes `stage_checklist_skips`.

The bed: `scripts/pgtest-stage-checklist-items.sh` now seeds stranded rows after its first scenario (`supabase/tests/stage_checklist_items/30_stranded_rows.sql`), runs this file a second time to repair them, and plays `40_follow_the_line.sql`: a stage answered, checked and skipped, then a split, a hand move, two combines and late presses from a sheet opened before each, as the people who would press them. That scenario does not stop at its first miss.
