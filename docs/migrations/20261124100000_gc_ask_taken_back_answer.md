# 20261124100000_gc_ask_taken_back_answer.sql (2026-11-24, v2.8490)

The answer for an ask we took back after the company heard about it: `gc_invites.declined_why` takes `'withdrawn'`, `gc_take_ask_back` closes such an ask instead of refusing it, and the trade's own lock `gc_trade_ask` says so in its own key.

- **Why**: `gc_take_ask_back` (`20261124050000`, v2.8440) deletes an ask the company never heard about and refused every other one, pointing at **Will not do it** and **Cannot do it**. Those two are the company's words. An ask made on the wrong company that was already emailed, opened or called on could only be closed by writing down that the company would not do it, which is false, and their record then counted a pass they never made. The owner's word of 2026-11-24: build the withdrawn answer for an ask they heard about.
- **A third answer, on the column that holds the other two**: the CHECK `gc_invites_declined_why_known` now allows `'wont'`, `'cant'` and `'withdrawn'`. The ask's `status` is `'declined'` as for the other two closes, so everything that reads an ask as out reads it right with no change: the quotes wanted, who is emailed a new plan set, `gc_remove_bench_company`. The kernels change the words only (`askTakenBack` in `src/lib/gc/decline.ts`).
- **`gc_take_ask_back(p_invite_id uuid)`**, restated. The gate and the order are as they were. It refuses in words, as *‹company› stays asked on ‹trade›, since …*:
  - the trade is awarded to this ask (*Take the award back first, in Compare quotes.*);
  - a statement of work names it;
  - a quote is in;
  - the company already passed.
  An ask already taken back is refused as *The ask to ‹company› on ‹trade› is already taken back.*
- **Then one of two things**, by what the company heard:
  - nothing (status `invited`, `seen_rev` null, no email keyed by the ask in `gc_trade_messages`, no contact on it but the office's own notes): the ask is deleted, with its row in `gc_asks_taken_back` written first, exactly as before;
  - they opened it in their portal, we emailed them about it, or a contact with them is logged on it: the ask is kept and closed, `status = 'declined'`, `declined_why = 'withdrawn'`, `decline_reason` null, `decline_note` empty, `declined_on` the company's day. Nothing is deleted on that path, and nothing is sent.
- **The trace on the kept path**: the ask says what and when. Who is one line on the company's own call log (`gc_company_contacts` with no ask, `how` *note*), which the company's Activity shows, as `gc_take_award_back` writes its undo: *Ask taken back on ‹trade›, ‹job›. Asked ‹day› by ‹name›. They had opened it in their portal.* (or *We had emailed them about it.*, or *A contact with them was logged on it.*). `gc_asks_taken_back` stays the ledger of the asks that were deleted.
- **`gc_trade_ask(p_company_id, p_invite_id)`**, restated whole with one branch added: an ask we took back raises `tookBack`, before the `youPassed` it raised for every closed ask. The portal says `tookBack` in our words. Every portal verb on an ask starts with this lock, so none of them writes on an ask we took back.
- **Rights**: unchanged. `gc_take_ask_back` is `SECURITY DEFINER` behind `gc_office_team()`, with a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller refused first. `gc_trade_ask` is the service role's.
- **No table is made**, so there is no fence call. The CHECK is dropped and added in one statement and both functions are `CREATE OR REPLACE`, so a second push changes nothing. `gc_invites` is small: the CHECK validates at once, under `lock_timeout = '3s'`.
- **Order**: the client calls no new name, so the merge may deploy first. Until the push, the press on an ask the company heard about gets the old refusal in words (*Close an ask they heard about with Will not do it or Cannot do it.*), which is drawn beside the press. After the push, deploy `submit-gc-trade-portal` and every importer of the kernel copy that `npm run check:edge-drift` names.
- **Bed**: `supabase/tests/gc_take_ask_back/20_scenario.sql` (`bash scripts/pgtest-gc-take-ask-back.sh`, already in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`) plays every role's gate, each refusal, each of the four ways of having heard closing the ask with its line on the call log, the ledger gaining no row, a second take-back refused, the trade's lock telling the two closes apart, and the CHECK refusing an answer it does not know.
