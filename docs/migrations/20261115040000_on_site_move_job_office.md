# 20261115040000 — on_site_move_job: the office's door to the whole-job move (v2.7305)

Materials on site (punch list #209): the office's press for moving everything a job holds on site to another job. Since v2.6808 a whole job's on-site records change job through `on_site_carry_job`, called by the whole-job combine and by the dev seat's `dev_on_site_move_job` ([`20261109160000`](./20261109160000_on_site_move_job.md); the four tables of 2026-11-13 since [`20261114070000`](./20261114070000_on_site_carry_job_renames_ties_prices_runs.md)). The map left the office with no press on purpose until it asked a second time; on 2026-11-14 the owner asked, looking at the Materials desk's site row for Gun Dog, where a paid job from July (JP224, 91 boxes) and the working job (JP1091, 2 boxes) hold one site's material. Three functions, `CREATE OR REPLACE`; no table, column, index, policy or trigger is made, so no fence call is owed and nothing waits on a table lock.

1. **`on_site_move_job_run(p jsonb, p_dry_run boolean, p_door text)` → jsonb**: the move's body, `dev_on_site_move_job`'s since v2.7265 with its gate lifted out — the parse of `{ from_job, to_job }`, every refusal (two ids that are not ids, the same job twice, a job that does not exist, a source with nothing in any of the nine counts), `on_site_carry_job`, the add-up check on every table, the words, the note on both jobs in the caller's name, and the dry run that does the move, reads both jobs and unwinds. `p_door` names the calling door in every refusal (`on_site_move_job: JP224 has nothing on site to move …`), and the one sentence that named the seat's `read_on_site` reads *Read both jobs' boxes before you move* through the office's door. `SECURITY DEFINER`, since the carry is nobody's to call; `EXECUTE` revoked from `PUBLIC`, `anon` and `authenticated`, so the body is reached through its two doors alone.
2. **`dev_on_site_move_job(p jsonb, p_dry_run boolean DEFAULT true)` → jsonb**: the seat's door, unchanged in what it admits — `auth.uid()` + `is_dev()`, a training account and a digital twin refused in words — now one `RETURN public.on_site_move_job_run(p, p_dry_run, 'dev_on_site_move_job')`. The reply's shape is as it was; the seat's `plan_materials_move` → `apply_materials_move` and `supabase/functions/dev-mcp` need no change and no deploy.
3. **`on_site_move_job(p jsonb, p_dry_run boolean DEFAULT true)` → jsonb**: the office's door. Who: a `users.role` of `dev`, `master_technician`, `assistant` or `controller`, the roles `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` lets combine two jobs ([`20261103054522`](./20261103054522_combine_moves_every_row.md)), who can open both jobs as the combine reads them (`master_user_id = auth.uid()`, `is_dev()`, `is_office_or_estimator()` or `assistants_share_master`); a signed-out caller, every other role, a training account (`is_read_only()`), a digital twin (`is_digital_twin()`) and an archived caller (`caller_is_archived()`) are refused in words, each before any job is read. A malformed id is left to the body's refusal, and a job that does not exist to the body's *not found*. `SECURITY DEFINER`; `EXECUTE` revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`, granted to `authenticated`. The plan is the seat's: `{ ok, dry_run, from, to, by, words, moved, places_merged, unsorted_folded, labels_renamed, photos, ties, on_hand, before, after, notes, next }`, `by` the caller's own name.

The client (`src/lib/materials/onSiteMoveJobIo.ts`, `components/materials/OnSiteMoveJobModal.tsx`) calls `on_site_move_job` twice: `p_dry_run = true` on **Read the plan**, drawn as the confirm window, and `p_dry_run = false` on the counted button. The window's roles are the same four (`canMoveOnSite`, over `REFILE_ASK_ROLES`); the database decides.

## Order

Merge, then **`supabase db push` right after the merge**: the client calls `on_site_move_job` by name, which only this file creates, so until the push the press's **Read the plan** is answered *function does not exist* (`check:calls-ahead` labels the PR and holds the Pages deploy until the push). The seat's door changes body and not shape, so a move through the seat before the push runs the old body and after it the new. No edge function to deploy. `src/types/database.ts` and the seat's catalog gain `on_site_move_job` and `on_site_move_job_run` at the next `gen-types:linked` run; the io casts the client until then.

## Locks

`CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION` only, so nothing waits on a table lock; a move in flight finishes on the old body. `SET lock_timeout = '3s'` is the file's first line all the same.
