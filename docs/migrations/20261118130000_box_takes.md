# 20261118130000_box_takes.sql (2026-11-18, v2.7820)

A take ties its ledger rows and its photos together. Take from a box, step 2 of 4, on the owner's ask of 2026-11-18: *when someone goes to take materials out of a box they take a photo of how they found the box, they mark the materials they're taking, and they take a photo of the box after*. This is the database half. No screen reads or writes the columns yet.

Until now a box photo was a photo of a box and nothing more. `job_box_photos` held no word for before or after, and nothing tied a photo to the ledger rows of the parts that left.

## The columns

| Table | Column | Type | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `job_box_photos` | `kind` | `text`, nullable, `CHECK` in (`before`, `after`) | `before`: the box as found. `after`: the box after the parts left. NULL: a photo of a box outside a take, which is every photo taken before this file. |
| `job_box_photos` | `take_id` | `uuid`, nullable | The take this photo belongs to. |
| `job_material_movements` | `take_id` | `uuid`, nullable | The take this ledger row belongs to. |

A take is not a row of its own. It is an id minted on the phone when a person presses **Take from G12**, written on every row that take makes. What the rows say together needs no state column: a before photo, rows and an after photo under one id are a whole take; rows with one photo missing are a take with a photo skipped; a before photo and nothing else is a take that was started and left.

Two partial indexes, `job_box_photos_take_idx` and `job_material_movements_take_idx`, cover only the rows inside a take.

## How they are written

By the phone, the way every other on-site row is written: an `INSERT` under the tables' own policies, which ask `on_site_can_touch_job(job_id)`. No function, no grant, no change to a policy or a trigger. The ledger takes no `UPDATE` from anyone, so a row's `take_id` is fixed when it lands. `on_site_guard_box_photo` (v2.7240) does not pin `kind` or `take_id` on a later update by the photo's own taker, and nothing on a screen changes either.

## Order

Additive and idempotent: nullable columns with no default are a catalog change alone, and no row is rewritten. No `CREATE TABLE`, so no fence calls. The push can come any time after the merge. **The client that follows must not merge before the push**: it names `take_id` in the read of every ledger row and every box photo, and PostgREST refuses a select that names a column it does not have.

**Applied** to prod on 2026-11-18 by `bash scripts/db-push.sh` from a clean checkout of `main` at the merge of #8765: the dry run listed this file alone, and `npm run check:migration-drift` then read 1,371 local files against 1,371 remote versions, fully applied. Read back as a signed-in dev over REST: `take_id` on `job_material_movements` and `kind`, `take_id` on `job_box_photos` answer 200, and a column that does not exist answers 400 (42703).
