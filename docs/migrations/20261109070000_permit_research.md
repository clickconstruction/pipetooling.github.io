# 20261109070000_permit_research.sql (2026-11-09, v2.6782)

Research: the permits a city issues, on a ledger (punch list #292, PR 1; `to-dos/permit-research/`).

## What it does

1. **`user_can_research_permits()`** (SQL, STABLE, SECURITY DEFINER): `is_office_staff()` or an estimator. The people who read the ledger and call on it.
2. **`permits`**: city, source (`sa-issued`, `sa-applied`, or `file`), permit number and type, kind (`building` | `plumbing` | `other`), stage (`applied` | `issued`), residential, work type, project name, address and its pairing key, zip, point, submitted and issued days, declared value, area, contact, the raw record, first-seen and pulled stamps. Unique on (city, source, number, type), since a commercial job's trade permits share the building permit's number. Read by researchers; `file` rows insertable by researchers; the feeds write as the service role.
3. **`permit_verdicts`**: permit, verdict (`look` | `approach` | `not_for_us` | `taken`), a non-empty reason, a look-again day, the GC lead an Approach made, who and when. Append-only; the newest per permit is the row's verdict. Researchers read and insert their own.
4. **`permit_pulls`**: one row per feed per run with its since-day, rows seen, rows kept and any error. Researchers read.
5. **`research_permits(p_city, p_since, p_residential)`** (SQL, STABLE, SECURITY INVOKER): the ledger's rows — a city's building permits filed since the day, each with the plumber a plumbing permit on the same number or address names (filed within 30 days before the building permit's submission or later), and the newest verdict with the decider's name.
6. **pg_cron `permit-pull`** at `30 11 * * *` UTC, POSTing the function with the vault's `PROJECT_URL` and `CRON_SECRET`, the shape of `court-precinct-nightly`.

Three CREATE TABLEs, so the four fence appliers close the file.
