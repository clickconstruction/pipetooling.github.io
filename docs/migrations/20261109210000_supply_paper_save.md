# 20261109210000_supply_paper_save.sql (2026-11-09, v2.6819)

Supply house bill lines, the office's two presses (punch list #298 PR 1b). [`20261109170000`](./20261109170000_supply_paper_lines.md) made `supply_papers` and `supply_paper_lines` with SELECT for the office and every write the service role's, for the sweep that reads the bills on file. Two writes belong to a person at the Add invoice window and the bill's row, and this file gives them a door. Additive and idempotent; no table changes.

- **`supply_paper_save(p_invoice_id uuid, p_paper jsonb, p_lines jsonb) → uuid`** — the paper and its lines for a bill, written whole: at Save of a bill filled from its PDF, and again when a person fixes or adds a line in the Look window (then `read_by` is `person` and `read_id` null). One paper per bill through this door: a second call keeps the paper's id, rewrites its money, state and note, clears any acceptance and replaces the lines. **The state is the function's, not the client's**: it sums the counted lines (every line but a `component`) and holds them to the subtotal, the subtotal with tax and freight to the total, and the total to the bill row's amount, each within a cent, the same rules as `checkPaper`; any gap writes `off` with the gap's words as the note (`supply_paper_gap_words`). The client's `check_state` can only make an `adds_up` paper `off` (each line's own arithmetic and the code-in-text check run there), never the other way. `p_paper` carries `kind` (invoice or credit; a bill's row holds nothing else), `number`, `dated_on`, `order_number`, `po_text`, `file_path`, `drive_file_id`, the four `*_cents`, `read_by`, `read_id`, `read_at`, `check_state` and `check_note`. Each element of `p_lines` carries the line's columns; a kind or pricing unit it does not know falls to `part` / `each`; text is cut to the columns' widths.
- **`supply_paper_accept(p_paper_id uuid, p_note text) → void`** — *Count it as read anyway*: a paper whose state is `off` becomes `accepted`, with `accepted_by = auth.uid()`, `accepted_at` and the gap kept in `check_note`; the note must say something. A paper that already adds up or was accepted is refused in words.
- **`supply_paper_gap_words(integer) → text`** — *$12.40 short of* / *$12.40 over*, the words `checkPaper` uses, for the note.
- **Gates**, said in each body because a definer passes the policies: signed in; `is_office_staff()`; not `is_read_only()`; not `is_digital_twin()`; not `caller_is_archived()`. The invoice must exist. `REVOKE` from `PUBLIC, anon`; `GRANT EXECUTE` to `authenticated, service_role`.
