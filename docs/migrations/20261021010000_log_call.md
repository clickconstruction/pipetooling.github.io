# 20261021010000_log_call.sql (2026-10-21, v2.5710)

Punch list #140 PR 7 (Messages, worked like a desk: *Log a call*), the train's one migration. Two changes, both additive and idempotent:

- `message_promises.kind` gains **`said`** (the CHECK `message_promises_kind_known` is dropped and re-added with it): the day a person gave on a call, written as a promise on the call's own line with no kind of its own. `runningPromise` on the client reads it like any written promise, so the thread's chip reads *Their move · until Tue Oct 27 · their word* and Waiting on them can hold the row until the day (that hold lands with the page PRs, since `waitingOnThem.ts` belongs to the #139 stack until it merges).
- **`log_call(p_contact_id, p_said, p_day, p_day_words, p_job_kind, p_job_id)`**, `SECURITY DEFINER`, returns the line's id. A signed-in client cannot write a call line (`messages_team_is_note_or_event`; the restrictive policy `messages_client_writes_own_team_lines` admits notes and events only), and a team note would leave the chip on *Our move* and never hold Waiting on them. The function: Follow up's guard `follow_up_press('log a call')` (a sign-in, no training account, no digital twin, the office team), *Write what they said first.*, *A day is a date or the words typed, not both.*, the day's words at most 80 letters, *That conversation is not there.*, `contact_job_check` on the job; then our line in `messages` (`channel 'call'`, `direction 'out'`, the caller as `who_user_id` and `who_text`, the body *Called Priya. They said: …* with the first name from `contact_display_name`, a title skipped and a bare number kept whole, the job on its column), which the trigger `messages_move_state` reads as a reply (state done, whoever had it lets go); the promise row when a day was given (`kind 'said'`, `state 'written'`, the date or the words, `decided_by` the caller); and the person's open `call_tasks` closed with `done_how 'call'`, `done_by` the caller and the line as `done_message_id`, as `call_tasks_close_by_call` closes them for a Quo call. `EXECUTE` revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`, granted to `authenticated` and `service_role`.

No table is created, so the three `apply_*` fence calls are not needed; the fences on `messages`, `message_promises` and `call_tasks` are untouched (the function writes as its definer, and the guard refuses a training account and a twin first).
