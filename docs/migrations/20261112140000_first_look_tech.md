# 20261112140000_first_look_tech.sql (2026-11-12, v2.7006)

A tech reads one look before a job exists (punch list #319 PR 7). No new table, so no fence call.

## What it does

1. **On `first_looks`**: `asked_tech_ids uuid[] NOT NULL DEFAULT '{}'` (the people the look was asked of), `asked_task_id`, `asked_tech_at`, and the tech's read: `tech_read` (`can_price` or `needs_visit`), `tech_note` (1,000 characters at most), `tech_read_at`, `tech_read_by`, `tech_read_name`. A constant default is a catalog change and rewrites nothing.
2. **`ask_tech_first_look(p_look_id uuid, p_item_id uuid) → jsonb`**: reads the task's people from `checklist_item_assignees` and writes them on the look. A task with nobody on it is refused. SECURITY DEFINER.
3. **`answer_first_look(p_look_id uuid, p_read text, p_note text) → jsonb`**: keeps the read on the look and, when the look is a person's, writes one team note on their thread (`messages`: `channel 'note'`, `direction 'team'`, in the caller's name, as `add_note` writes one). SECURITY DEFINER.
4. **Three SELECT policies** named `first_looks_select_asked` and `first_look_files_select_asked`: a person in `asked_tech_ids` reads that look, its files, and its objects in the bucket `first-look-files`.

## Who may do what

- **`ask_tech_first_look`**: the office team. A training account and a digital twin are refused in words.
- **`answer_first_look`**: a person the look was asked of, or the office team. Anyone else is refused in words, and so are a training account and a twin.
- **Read**: beside the office team and the job's team, a person the look was asked of, for that one look. Nothing else of the customer's thread opens to them.
- `anon` has no EXECUTE on either function.

## Release order

Merge, then `bash scripts/db-push.sh` right after. The client calls both functions, which only this migration creates. Until the push, the task still goes and the card says the look was not opened to the tech.

## Verify

Read-only on prod after the push: `select proname from pg_proc where proname in ('ask_tech_first_look','answer_first_look')` reads two rows; `select policyname from pg_policies where policyname like 'first_look%select_asked' order by 1` reads three rows; `select has_function_privilege('anon', 'public.answer_first_look(uuid, text, text)', 'execute')` reads false.
