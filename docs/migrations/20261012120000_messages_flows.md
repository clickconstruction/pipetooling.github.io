# 20261012120000_messages_flows.sql (2026-10-12, v2.5267)

Messages, cut 4, the flow suite (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md` §3 and §4 on branch `spike/gc-mode`; the
kernels are `src/lib/flows/facts.ts`, `state.ts` and `seed.ts`, v2.5254 and v2.5260). It holds the flows the office edits and
what the office does to one person's place on them. State is never stored: where a person stands is a read over the
stamps (`readFlow`), so no column says which step anyone is on. Comes after the spine (`20261012010000_messages_spine.sql`),
whose `contacts`, `messages` and job columns it points at.

## What it does

1. **`flow_fact_ids()`, `flow_paper_ids()`**, immutable: the facts and the papers in `facts.ts`. A step's `facts` and `papers`
   must be among them (`flow_steps_facts_known`, `flow_steps_papers_known`). A new fact replaces the function, and
   `src/lib/flows/flowsSql.test.ts` holds both lists to the code.
2. **`flows`**: one per kind (customer, trade, arch, unknown), with its name.
3. **`flow_steps`**: a flow's steps, the columns of `FlowStep`:
   - `step_key` (stable, unique per flow);
   - `lane` (once, per_job, any_time) and `position`;
   - `name`, `name_es`, `phase` (setup, pricing, building) and `who` (us, them, both);
   - `seat_user_id`;
   - `facts`, `join_word` (and, or) and `papers`;
   - `loop_words` with `loop_ends` (rounds, next), both or neither;
   - `fork`, `aside` and `skip_one_job`;
   - `later_en`, `later_es`;
   - the nudge: `nudge_days`, `nudge_how` (text, call, email) and `nudge_send` (ask, app) all together, and `nudge_day_fact`
     (promised, pay_by, questions_close) only with them;
   - `usual`, `owed_words`, and `updated_at` (by trigger).
4. **`flow_step_history`**: every edit with `what`, `before` and `after` (the step as jsonb), who and when; append only.
   **`flow_removed_steps`**: a removed step whole, with Put it back (`put_back_at`, who by trigger). **`flow_fact_wishes`**:
   Ask for a fact that is missing.
5. **One person's place**, each on a contact (cascade) and a step key, and on at most one job the spine's way
   (`bid_id`, `project_id`, `jobs_ledger_id`; none on the Once lane). A step's team note is a line of the thread:
   `messages.step_id` (text, no foreign key, so a removed step keeps its notes; only on a team line), with
   `messages_client_writes_own_team_lines`, a restrictive policy by which a signed-in user inserts a team line (a note
   or an event) as themselves and no other line, written identically in cut 2's migration so whichever runs last
   leaves the same policy, and `INSERT` on the note's columns granted:
   - **`contact_step_skips`**: a skip with its reason; one standing per person, step and job (`NULLS NOT DISTINCT`);
     undo stamps `undone_at`, and the trigger stamps who.
   - **`nudge_holds`**: Hold until a day; one standing per person, step and job; let go stamps `released_at` and who.
   - **`nudge_sent`**: what a person or the app sent (`sent_by`), how, for which round, with the message it went as;
     the app's has no person (`nudge_sent_app_has_no_person`); append only.
6. **`flows_stamp_lifted_by()`**: the trigger that stamps who undid a skip, let go of a hold or put a step back, from
   `auth.uid()`; the column is its argument.
   **`contacts_fold_flows(p_from, p_into)`**: the spine's `contacts_fold` runs every `contacts_fold_<something>(uuid,
   uuid)` before the folded contact goes (the lead's rule; `contacts_fold` itself is never redefined). This one moves
   the notes, skips, holds and sends onto the contact that stays; a skip or a hold both had keeps the survivor's, the
   other lifted. `SECURITY INVOKER`, with `UPDATE (contact_id)` on the four tables granted for it.
7. **The four facts main did not keep**:
   - `gc_trade_portal_links.first_opened_at` (timestamptz);
   - `gc_invites.lost_on` (date);
   - `gc_projects.won_on` (date);
   - `gc_owner_pay_apps.paid_on` (date).
   Nothing writes them yet; their writers come with cut 4's screens. The warranty year stays computed from
   `gc_owner_acceptances.accepted_on`. The day a customer asked stays `gc_projects.created_at` or the first plan set.
8. **`flows_seed()`**: the four flows as `SEED_FLOWS` draws them (prototype v1.178), only for a kind that has no flow yet.
   A second run adds nothing and never brings back a step the office removed. The seats are left for the office.
   Revoked from every client role; the migration runs it once.
9. **The presses**, `SECURITY INVOKER` so training mode and a twin change nothing, each taking the job as
   `(p_job_kind, p_job_id)` through the spine's `contact_job_check` and the step through `flow_step_check` (on the
   person's flow, else `stepUnknown`): `flow_add_note` (`noteEmpty`), `flow_skip_step` (`whyEmpty`, `alreadySkipped`),
   `flow_unskip_step`, `nudge_hold_until` (after today, else `dayPassed`; a standing hold is let go first),
   `nudge_let_go`, `nudge_record_sent` (`howUnknown`), `contact_set_off` (`whyEmpty`), `contact_put_back_on`; an undo of
   nothing is `notFound`.
10. **`flow_rows(p_contact)`**: one person's rows as `src/lib/flows/load.ts`'s `FlowRows`, for the office team only
   (`notAllowed`). `SECURITY DEFINER`, since the facts sit behind the Portal's and the money's doors: it returns days and
   marks, never an amount, a price or a token, and a trade partner's page as `previewPath` (`/t/<token>`). Their jobs
   are the ones the office tied them to and the ones their kind's facts put them on.
11. **Who reaches it**: the office team (`gc_office_team()`), one policy a table, with the read-only and twin fences.
   The client inserts and updates only the columns a press sets: who and when are the table's. The flows themselves
   are the seed's. `contacts.off_since`, `off_why` and `off_by` (the spine's) become updatable by the team: Off the flow.

## Safety

`SET lock_timeout = '3s'`. Every statement is idempotent (`IF NOT EXISTS`, `CREATE OR REPLACE`, drop-and-create for
triggers and policies, the seed's new-kind rule). The bed re-applies the file and expects no change. The four
`ADD COLUMN`s are nullable with no default, so each is a catalog change under a brief lock.

## Verify on prod (read only, after `supabase db push`)

```sql
SELECT f.kind, count(s.id) FROM public.flows f LEFT JOIN public.flow_steps s ON s.flow_id = f.id GROUP BY f.kind ORDER BY f.kind;
-- arch 5, customer 11, trade 9, unknown 1
SELECT array_length(public.flow_fact_ids(), 1), array_length(public.flow_paper_ids(), 1);  -- 31, 17

  AND (table_name, column_name) IN (('gc_trade_portal_links','first_opened_at'),('gc_invites','lost_on'),('gc_projects','won_on'),('gc_owner_pay_apps','paid_on'));

```

## Bed

`scripts/pgtest-messages-flows.sh` (`npm run test:pg:messages-flows`), `supabase/tests/messages_flows/20_scenario.sql`:
- the four flows read back step by step;
- a second seed adds nothing and brings back no removed step;
- the CHECKs refuse an unknown fact or paper, a loop that does not say when it ends, half a nudge and an unknown lane;
- only the office reads and writes;
- a fold of two contacts moves a note, a hold, the skips and a send onto the survivor;
- each press writes as the caller with its refusals, and a client writes no line but a team note of its own;
- `flow_rows` gives a trade partner's first visit, job and preview path with no token or amount, and nothing outside the office;
- history, removals, wishes, skips, holds, notes and sends carry their person;
- training mode and a twin change nothing, and every table carries its policy and fences;
- the four columns are there.
