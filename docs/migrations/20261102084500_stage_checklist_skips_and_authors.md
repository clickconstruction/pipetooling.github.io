# 20261102084500_stage_checklist_skips_and_authors.sql (2026-11-02, what the walks added to stage checklists, punch list #237 PR 1b)

What the four walks settled for the stage checklists (`to-dos/stage-checklist-document/`, v2.6347), on top of `20261102050000_stage_checklist_job_items.sql`. The rules are `src/lib/jobs/stageChecklist.ts` and `stageChecklistItems.ts`.

- **Three columns on `stage_checklist_templates`**: `plan_sheet` (40 characters) and `why` (80), what the add box takes beside the words, and `promoted_from` → the job item a company row was added to the company's list from, so PR 4's source line can say which job a list came from.
- **`stage_checklist_may_author(job)`** (`SECURITY DEFINER`, `STABLE`, EXECUTE `authenticated`): who authors on a job, in one place. The office (`is_master_or_dev() OR is_assistant()`) anywhere; a superintendent on their own job (`superintendent_report_job_anchor_allowed`, the assigned project or the job's team); an estimator on a job they priced (`jobs_ledger.bid_id` to `bids.estimator_id`); never a digital twin. The client asks it for a superintendent or an estimator, so they meet no press on a job that is not theirs.
- **The add gate** is re-created: the office as before, or `job_id IS NOT NULL AND created_by = auth.uid() AND stage_checklist_may_author(job_id)`. **The update gate** is re-created with the adder's branch held to `retired_at IS NULL` in its `USING`, and the trigger `stage_checklist_templates_adder_only_retires` over `stage_checklist_item_adder_only_retires()` (`SECURITY INVOKER`) refuses an adder's update that changes anything but `retired_at`, `retired_by` and `updated_at`. A policy cannot compare a row before and after: PR 1's gate let the adder of a retired item rewrite it, and let a retire rewrite the words in the same statement, under the answer the item keeps. The office is exempt, and `reorder_stage_checklist_items` runs as the table's owner (`current_user` is not `authenticated`). The office's branches of the policies are unchanged.
- **`stage_checklist_skips`**, the one new table: a company question left off one job's stage, with `why` (3 to 200 characters after a trim), `skipped_by`, `skipped_by_name` and `skipped_at`. A live row (`put_back_at` null) hides the question on that stage alone. Put it back writes `put_back_at` and `put_back_by` and the row stays. `stage_checklist_skips_one_live_idx` keeps one live row per stage and question. The trigger `stage_checklist_skips_names_a_company_question` (`SECURITY DEFINER`, since field roles cannot read the stage lines) holds the stage to the job and the question to a live company row. **Policies:** read by `can_report_stage_progress(job_id)`; insert by an author in their own name; update only from live to put back, by an author in their own name, with `authenticated` granted `UPDATE` on `put_back_at` and `put_back_by` alone, so the reason and the stamp stand as written; delete dev only.
- **`reorder_stage_checklist_items(stage, ids)`** (`SECURITY DEFINER`, EXECUTE `authenticated`): a stage's own checks in the order given, in one transaction, returning how many rows moved. Definer, since an adder's own update is the retire alone and a superintendent moves a check the office added. It asks `stage_checklist_may_author`, which refuses a twin, and the read-only statement trigger fires inside it. Ids the list does not name keep their order after the ones it does, and ids from another stage or job match nothing, so a list read a moment ago can never move another job's check or lose a new one.
- **`promote_stage_checklist_item(item)`** (`SECURITY INVOKER`, EXECUTE `authenticated`, the office alone, never a twin): one job item onto the company's list, in one transaction. The job's row retires first, which locks it, so a second press finds it gone. A new company row takes the words and the photo ask, last in the kind's order, with `promoted_from`. **The job's answer moves to the new row**, or the stage this job already answered would read not ready. **The plan sheet and the why stay on the job's row**: they were written for this job's plans, and the company's row asks every job. A company list that already asks the same words (ignoring case and the spaces around them) refuses the promote with *The company's list already asks that.*, and nothing is written. Invoker, so the office's own policies and both fences hold.
- Additive and idempotent. One table is created, so the file ends with the three fence calls, which lock only that table. The new foreign keys take a short `SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE` on `jobs_ledger`, `jobs_ledger_fixtures`, `stage_checklist_templates` and `users`, the two re-created policies a short `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` on a table of a few rows; `lock_timeout` makes a busy moment fail fast. No `VALIDATE` and nothing rewritten: the two inline checks on the new columns scan a table of a few rows.

Apply order: push right after the merge and before the Pages deploy finishes. The client reads `plan_sheet` and `why` in every question read, `stage_checklist_skips` in the sheet's, the read-out's and the board's, and calls the three functions. `check:calls-ahead` sees the table and the functions and labels the PR *push right after the merge*, and the deploy holds until the push; it cannot see the two columns. **The stack's order matters:** `20261102050000` (#6797) is pushed first, since this file's policies and function name its columns. After the push, `npm run gen-types:linked` as its own `chore(types)` PR.
