## What it does

- **`gc_office_team()` names its roles**: dev, master_technician, assistant, controller and estimator. That is the same answer door 1's `is_office_or_estimator()` gave, now written out the way `gc_money_team()` is. The client's copy is `GC_OFFICE_TEAM` (`src/lib/gc/access.ts`); `access.test.ts` reads this migration and fails when the two lists differ. A role added to the office later joins GC mode only when it is named here.
- **Ten tables open to the GC office team**, each with one `<table>_team` policy for every verb, `(SELECT gc_office_team())`:
  - B1's eight: `gc_companies`, `gc_company_people`, `gc_company_vetting_forms`, `gc_invites`, `gc_quotes`, `gc_company_contacts`, `gc_trade_promises` and `gc_trade_promise_moves`;
  - B5-a's two, kept dev only "until the Board's door": `gc_bid_tabs` and `gc_bid_tab_views`. B5-d's board loader reads them; left dev only, every tab would read *not shared yet* for the office.
- **The functions on them** are `SECURITY INVOKER`, so the policies are their gate: `gc_add_company`, `gc_vet_company`, `gc_invite_companies`, `gc_office_decline`, `gc_record_promise`, `gc_keep_promise` and `gc_keep_promises`. `gc_record_promise` and `gc_keep_promises` are granted to `service_role` too. Only the Portal's functions use it, after they check the trade's link.

**Stays as it was:**

- the trade portal links and messages (`gc_trade_portal_links`, `gc_trade_messages`) stay dev only until the trade wave;
- our number (`gc_project_money`) keeps `gc_money_team()`;
- `anon` has no grant on any of the ten (B1, B5-a);
- the append-only three (`gc_company_contacts`, `gc_quotes`, `gc_trade_promise_moves`) keep no UPDATE or DELETE privilege, which no policy can open.

**Every GC table's door** is listed in `src/lib/gc/doors.ts`. `doors.test.ts` reads the migrations and fails CI when a GC table is missing from the list, when its policy lets in someone other than the list says, or when a dev-only table does not say what opens it.

What the columns show:
- **Reads companies**: one company was there before the run. Each role's own *Added by* company is deleted before the next role.
- **`gc_vet_company`** for a role the policy shuts out says *No company with that id.*, because the function cannot see the row. For a twin it says the same, because the fence hides the row from its update.
- **`gc_quotes` update** is refused for everyone by the privilege, not a policy: a quote is never changed, and a new quote is a new row.
- **Reads portal links** and **Reads gc_project_money** are the two boundaries that stay: the portal is a dev's, and our number is the money team's.
