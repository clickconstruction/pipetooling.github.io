# 20261104200000_add_worker.sql (2026-11-04, v2.6514)

PR 2 of punch list #260: adding a person makes a users row. Since `20261104190000` a users row need not have a login behind it; until this PR every "add a person" in the app inserted a People record alone, with `account_user_id` NULL — a person who could hold no clock session and no Charged as.

- `add_worker(p_name, p_kind, p_email, p_phone, p_notes, p_start_date) → { user_id, person_id }`, `SECURITY DEFINER`, `search_path` pinned to `public`, EXECUTE to `authenticated` (revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`). Gate: a signed-in caller `is_office_staff()` admits — dev, master, assistant, controller — the roles the People insert policy admits; refused with `42501` and plain words otherwise. A blank name or a kind outside the roster kinds (`dev` included) is refused with `22023`; an address another users row holds with `23505`, naming who holds it.
- Writes, in one transaction: the users row (`id gen_random_uuid()`, `name`, `email` lower-cased or NULL, `phone`, `role` from the kind — `sub → subcontractor`, `helper → helpers`, the rest their own name — `login_attached_at` NULL, every flag its default, `needs_supervision` true), then the People record (`master_user_id` = the caller, as the forms wrote it; `kind`, `name`, `email`, `phone`, `notes`, `start_date`, `account_user_id` = the new users row). The phone-from-account trigger (`20261015030000`) runs as for any linked record.
- Why a definer: the users insert policy admits a dev alone, and the point is that a master or an assistant adding a sub from a job form makes the whole person. A caller in training mode is stopped by the read-only statement trigger on the inserts as today.

Idempotent (`CREATE OR REPLACE`); no table is created, so no fence calls.
