# 20261106140000_away_notes.sql (2026-11-06, the Away note, punch list #256 PR 8, v2.6587)

While a teammate is away, the office leaves them one screen (`to-dos/bring-back-a-teammate/`, the Away note): a note in the office's words, a countdown to the day back, where the office is and the office line. They sign in as always and the app draws that screen and nothing else, behind the seal of `20261106120000_archived_seal` (the pre-request gate refuses every request from an archived caller except `/rpc/my_away_note`, the fence on every table reads nothing for them, and the edge guard turns them away). This migration is the note's table and the one RPC the gate lets through.

- **`away_notes`** — one row per account (`user_id`, the primary key, cascades with the users row): `words` (600 characters at most), `countdown_ymd` (null for no countdown), `show_where`, `show_call`, `alert_on_day` (a Needs you item on the day, PR 9; a check holds it to a note with a day), `created_by`, `created_at`, `updated_at` (the shared `update_updated_at_column` trigger).
- **Reads** for the people who may archive (`is_pay_approved_master() OR is_controller()`, the gate `archive-user` admits). **No write policy for anyone**: the `away-note` edge function writes as the service role, since a note on an archived login also lifts the sign-in ban and taking it down puts the ban back. `anon` has nothing; `authenticated` has SELECT only.
- **`my_away_note()`** — `SECURITY DEFINER`, `STABLE`, `search_path` pinned; for the signed-in archived caller with a note, everything the sealed screen draws: `first_name`, `words`, `countdown_ymd`, `show_where`, `show_call`, `left_ymd` (the roster row's end date, else the day the account was archived in the company's calendar), `updated_ymd`, `office_address` (Settings → Office address as stored, JSON with address, lat and lng) and `office_phone` (the dispatch phone, `field_dispatch_phone_v1`). Null for an active account, an archived account with no note, or no caller. Executable by `authenticated` and `service_role`.
- Idempotent (`IF NOT EXISTS`, `CREATE OR REPLACE`, the policy and trigger dropped and made again). One table is created, so the file ends with the four fence calls; `apply_archived_read_blocks()` fences `away_notes` too, which is right: the person reads theirs through the RPC, never the table.

Apply order: **push right after the merge** (`check:calls-ahead` labels the PR): the client reads `away_notes`, calls `my_away_note()` and invokes `away-note`, which writes the table. Deploy `away-note`, `archive-user` and `restore-user` right after the push. Then `npm run gen-types:linked` as its own `chore(types)` PR.

Tested by `npm run test:pg:away-notes` (`scripts/pgtest-away-notes.sh`, `supabase/tests/away_notes/20_scenario.sql`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): the migration applied twice; the fence on the table; the dev and the controller read the row and a helper reads none; nobody but the service role writes it; Ava, archived with a note, gets her note from `my_away_note()` with her first name, the day she left from her roster row, and the office settings; Ben, active, gets null; Ava with no note gets null; the gate still passes `/rpc/my_away_note` and refuses her everywhere else.
