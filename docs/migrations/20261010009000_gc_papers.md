# 20261010009000_gc_papers.sql (2026-10-09, v2.5041)

GC mode, the Board's B6-b-i: a trade partner company's papers. The plan is `to-dos/gc-mode/mockups/board-b6b.md` on branch `spike/gc-mode`, approved 2026-10-09 (call 5 with the reader audit's three additions and the six display guards; call S, the company branch, with the person path pinned first). Its SQL is the mock-up's block, byte for byte.

It is idempotent. It alters `person_contract_documents`, a table People and Contracts use all day, so **its push is the evening batch after 23:00 UTC**, alone or with other files that touch nothing of it; the CHECK is added `NOT VALID` and validated in its own statement.

## What it does

1. **`person_contract_documents.company_id`** (uuid, nullable, `REFERENCES gc_companies(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT`, with a partial index): the trade partner company a paper is for. A company with papers cannot be deleted: its signed papers are kept.
2. **`person_contract_documents_company_paper`**, both ways: a company's paper has no person and the name `'gc-company:' || company_id`; that name never stands without its company. The audit found the plan's one-way CHECK let `create_pending_contract_versions_after_book_save`'s copies (which carry `person_name` only) through with no company.
3. **`contract_docs_set_person_id`**: a company's paper keeps no person, and a well-formed `gc-company:<uuid>` name with no company takes it back (a Book-save copy stays keyed). A person's paper is resolved as before. The trigger now fires on `UPDATE OF person_name, company_id` (`CREATE OR REPLACE TRIGGER`).
4. **`gc_paper_sends`** (`PaperSend`): every send of a paper to a company, with `sent_on`, `due_on` (PaperSend's `on` and `by`; `on` is reserved), `note`, `first`, `draws int[]` and `sent_by`. A statement of work and a waiver are one trade's (`gc_paper_sends_job_paper`). It cascades with its company, project and trade, as `gc_trade_promises` does. Dev only, with the three blocks.
5. **`gc_send_paper(p jsonb)`**: a send and its promise in one transaction, the prototype's `sendPaper` (a waiver's promise is `closeout`). Its refusals are in words; a dev only.
6. **`gc_company_paper(company, book entry)`**: a company's copy of one Contract Book entry, as `materializePacket` makes a person's, and no assignment row, so the Book's cascade never sees a company. A W-9's form and `doc_type` come through the form trigger. A dev only.
7. **`gc_company_paper_kept`**, a trigger (`AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OF status`, `WHEN (NEW.company_id IS NOT NULL AND NEW.status = 'signed')`): a company's paper, signed or filed, keeps its promise through `gc_keep_promises` in the same transaction (msa, w9 for a W-9, insurance for a certificate). Its WHEN lets only a company's paper through, so `accept-contract`, the person path, is untouched; an update of a paper already signed keeps nothing.
8. **`gc_record_company_coi(company, expires_on, url)`**: the office files a company's insurance certificate (`COI (filed)`, signed today, with its expiry and its https link), as the sub form files a sub's; the trigger keeps the insurance promise. A dev only, refusals in words.

Every date is `public.app_today()`.

- No `users.name` and no `people.name` starts with `gc-company:`. Expected 0 and 0.

**The SQL bed** `supabase/tests/gc_papers/20_scenario.sql` (`npm run test:pg:gc-papers`; GitHub's `SQL beds`, job `gc-papers`). It applies every migration and this one twice, then:
- refuses an estimator in words, and reads them no send;
- makes the company's master agreement under its stored name with no person, the Book's entry and a second call the same row; the company's W-9 is a `w9` from its form;
- records a send and its promise; a reminder moves the promise and keeps its first day; a waiver's send names its draws and its promise is `closeout`;
- refuses no company, an unknown paper, a statement of work with no trade and no day, in words;
- takes a lost company back from its name, finds a person's person as before, keeps no person on a company's paper, and refuses a malformed stored name, a company under a person's name or another company's, and moving a company's paper to a person's name;
- finds `gc_paper_sends`' dev policy and its blocks, and the trigger firing on `company_id`;
- keeps the master agreement's and the W-9's promises when they are signed and the insurance promise when the certificate is filed, keeps nothing when a person's paper is signed or a signed paper is updated again, and refuses `gc_record_company_coi` in words;
- deletes a project with its sends while the company's own stay, and refuses deleting a company with papers.
