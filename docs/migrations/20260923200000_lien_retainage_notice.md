# 20260923200000_lien_retainage_notice.sql (2026-09-23, v2.3753)

Punch list #33 PR 1: the § 53.057 retainage notice. Four sections, all idempotent:

1. **`jobs_ledger`** — `lien_contract_ended_on date`, `lien_contract_ended_how text` (`complete | terminated | abandoned`, a pair CHECK keeps the two NULL or set together), `lien_contract_ended_set_by uuid → users`, `lien_contract_ended_set_at timestamptz`, `lien_retainage_held numeric(12,2) ≥ 0` (NULL = not recorded), `lien_payment_bond text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'unknown'` (`yes | no | unknown`). Constraints are added inside a `DO` block guarded by `pg_constraint`, since `ADD CONSTRAINT` has no `IF NOT EXISTS`.
2. **The third kind** — `job_lien_desk_items_kind_check` and `job_lien_filings_kind_check` are dropped and re-added with `retainage_53_057`. Both tables are small; the validation scan is instant under the 3 s lock timeout.
3. **`lien_retainage_deadline(date)`** — 30 days after the day our contract ended, rolled off a weekend (§ 53.057(b), § 53.003); NULL in, NULL out. Mirrors `retainageDeadlineFor` in `src/lib/jobs/lienDeadlines.ts`.
4. **`list_lien_retainage_windows(integer)`** — the Retainage tab's reader (`DROP` + `CREATE`, `SECURITY DEFINER`, office roles only): every sub job with `lien_retainage_held > 0`, kept when its clock has not started, or its deadline is within `p_within_days` ahead or a week behind, or a live `retainage_53_057` desk item exists. Columns: `job_id, retainage_held, contract_ended_on, contract_ended_how, deadline, noticed` (a live § 53.057 filing), `in_claim` (a live § 53.056 filing whose `fields->>'retainageIncluded'` is set), `open_balance, customer_id, gc_customer_id, property_kind, has_owner, payment_bond, desk_item_id, desk_status`.

Deploy order does not matter: the client reads the columns through a tolerant select and the RPC through a `.catch(() => [])`, and the Edit Job row says *available after the next update lands* until the columns exist.
