# 20260904153002_debit_card_role.sql (v2.2750)

Adds `mercury_debit_card_nicknames.card_role text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'person'` with a CHECK on `person | company`.

- `person` — the card belongs to someone; its fuel-tag purchases count as that person's fuel once the card is linked (auto-assign user).
- `company` — a management-tool card (GPS, charging, app subscriptions). Its purchases never count as anyone's fuel and the card never shows on the Wheels "no person on it" list.

Additive and idempotent; no new table, so the read-only policy re-apply calls are not needed. Existing RLS on the nicknames table covers the column. The Debit cards modal keeps a row (nickname may be empty) whenever the role is `company`, so clearing a nickname no longer deletes the role.
