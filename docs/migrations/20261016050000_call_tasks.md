# 20261016050000_call_tasks.sql (2026-10-17, v2.5596)

Calls and the scripts (punch list #115 PR 5, `to-dos/tell-the-customer/README.md`): a call to make becomes a task in Follow up's We owe them, closed by Quo's webhook; the script lives on the flow step. The client reads the new table fail-soft (no calls before the push), so it may deploy first; push after the feature PR merges, redeploy `quo-webhook` (it closes the tasks) and regenerate the types.

## What it does

1. **`call_tasks`**: one row per call to make — the person, the step, the job (`bid_id` / `project_id` / `jobs_ledger_id`), `due_on`, `what` ("about the estimate"), the `script` as filled, who added it, and how it ended (`done_how` `call` with the call's line, `called` with the team note, or `skipped`). One open task a person a step a job (a partial unique index). One SELECT policy for the office team (`gc_office_team()`); `authenticated` holds SELECT alone, `anon` nothing; the service role and the functions write. The three fence appliers run at the end.
2. **`call_task_add(p_contact, p_step, p_job_kind, p_job_id, p_due, p_what, p_script)`**: Add to my calls, through `follow_up_press` (signed out, a training account, a twin and anyone off the office team are refused in words); an empty *what* or no day is refused; a second press for the same person, step and job moves the open task's day and words instead of adding one. A gray line in the thread each time something changes.
3. **`call_task_done(p_id, p_how, p_note)`**: *I called* writes a team note in the thread (the given words, or *Called Dana about the estimate.*) and closes the task with it; *Skip* closes it with a gray line. Refused for a task that is not open.
4. **`call_task_move(p_id, p_due)`**: Tomorrow, or any day from today on; refused for a day gone by.
5. **`call_tasks_close_by_call(p_contact, p_message)`**: the webhook closes every open task to a person when an answered call to or from them lands, with the call's line. EXECUTE to `service_role` alone.
6. **`flow_steps.call_script`, `call_script_es`, `suggest_call`** (default true): the script in both languages and whether the receipt suggests a call; seeded on the residential customer flow's Booked, Estimate sent and Approved where none is written (`src/lib/flows/callScripts.ts` holds the same words). `flow_save` writes the three columns; `follow_up_count` counts a person with an open call.

## Why the shape

- Its own table, not a `contact_owed` row: We owe them keeps one open row a person, and a person may owe us nothing while a call to them is due.
- The webhook closes by person, not by number, so a call from the customer's other number still counts; an unanswered call does not.
- The script on the step, not in the kernel: the office shapes the words in Flows as it does the lines.
