# 20261107200000_superintendent_scores.sql (2026-11-07, v2.6666)

**What it does**: the scoreboard's one read and the owner's one setting (punch list #234 PR 4), on the owner's three calls of 2026-11-07 — the office's sign-off is the certified percent, the bonus is a share of the labor kept (hours saved, in dollars), a stage a sub performs counts on the sub's sheet hours.

- `superintendent_scores(p_from date, p_to date, p_job_ids uuid[] DEFAULT NULL) → jsonb`, `SECURITY DEFINER`. For every project with an award (`job_budgets.awarded_at`) whose superintendent is known (`project_superintendents` through the job's project, or `gc_projects.superintendent_user_id`), one row per superintendent per job: the certified percent (the newest `job_pct_events` change by an office role — dev, master technician, assistant, controller — on or before `p_to`, with its day and who), the award's hours and labor dollars and kind, the team's hours and dollars at `labor_burden_factor_v1` (the `people_crew_jobs` × `people_hours_recorded` × `people_pay_config` read `list_project_board` makes), the subs' sheet hours and dollars (`people_labor_job_items`, hours × rate or the direct amount), labor earned and kept, hours under, the planned finish, and whether the job scores (a bid or typed award with hours and a sign-off; a book award is a guess and does not) and whether its sign-off fell in the period. **What leaves**: the dollars (earned, actual, kept) only to `has_payroll_access()`; a caller's own rows carry `bonus_usd` alone (labor kept × the share, floored at zero); a superintendent gets his own rows and no other's. The office roles get every row.
- `app_settings.superintendent_bonus_v1` — `{"sharePct":10,"period":"month","paidAt":"office_signoff"}`, seeded once; the owner (`is_master_or_dev()`) updates it through a key-scoped policy, as the line budget settings are.

**Tables**: none created, so no fence is owed. **RLS**: one key-scoped UPDATE policy added. The function is `SECURITY DEFINER` on purpose: a superintendent's RLS shows him no `job_budgets` row and no crew row, and the office's shows no wage without payroll access; the function reads them all and lets out only what the caller may see, as `list_project_board` does for the board.

**Why the office's sign-off and not a pay app**: the owner's call. No pay application in the app carries a certified line percent (`job_pay_applications.lines[]` is the office's own figures, never locked; `gc_owner_pay_apps.certified` is a whole-app dollar figure), and `job_pct_events` records who set every percent, so the office's own percent set is the sign-off the rule reads.

**Apply**: `supabase db push` from a clean checkout of `main` after this merges, with PR 2's two migrations if they are not yet pushed (the read joins `job_budgets.planned_finish`). The Scoreboard tab and the superintendent's card call the function and read a refusal as their own words until then.
