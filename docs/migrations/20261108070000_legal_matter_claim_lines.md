# 20261108070000 — legal_matter_claim_lines (v2.6679)

The claim on a legal matter, line by line (punch list #171, `to-dos/legal-desk-claim/`, PR 1 of 3). The desk sized a case by its open bills, and since v2.5849 by the open bills plus the retainage no sent bill carries (v2.6103). A dispute can claim more — materials left on site, damages, interest, attorney fees — and on job 891 those lines lived only inside the demand letters. One `CREATE TABLE`, two RPCs, the four fences.

- **`legal_matter_claim_lines`**: one row per part the office types — `kind` (materials · damages · interest · fees · other), `title` (1–120 characters), `amount` (over zero, rounded to cents), `basis` (up to 300 characters: a contract clause, a statute, how it was counted), `document_id` (an Evidence document of the same matter, `ON DELETE SET NULL`), who added and last changed it, and `removed_at` / `removed_by` for a line taken off (kept, never deleted, like a document). RLS: office `SELECT` (`legal_office_can_read()`); no insert, update or delete policy, so every write goes through the RPCs. The two parts that fill in on their own — the open bills from AR and the retainage held from the job — are read by the packet kernel, never stored here.
- **`legal_claim_line_save(p_payer_key, p_customer_id, p_payer_name, p_job_ids, p_line_id, p_kind, p_title, p_amount, p_basis, p_document_id)`**: office add (`p_line_id` null) or edit. The ensure-the-matter upsert is `legal_answer_gap`'s (v2.5920): the matter is made when the account has none, jobs only added, refused once closed; the document must be a live document of the same matter. Returns `{ ok, matter_id, line_id }`.
- **`legal_claim_line_remove(p_line_id)`**: sets `removed_at`; refused when already gone.
- The fences: `apply_read_only_write_blocks`, `apply_read_only_stmt_blocks`, `apply_digital_twin_write_blocks`, `apply_archived_read_blocks`.
