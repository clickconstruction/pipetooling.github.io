# 20261106040000_stage_checklist_signature.sql (2026-11-05, needs a signature on a stage checklist answer, punch list #237 PR 2, v2.6563)

The signature on a checklist answer (`to-dos/stage-checklist-document/`, *The signature is the app's own*), on top of `20261102084500_stage_checklist_skips_and_authors.sql`. The rules are `src/lib/jobs/stageChecklist.ts` (the hash and whether a signature stands) and `stageChecklistSignature.ts` (the sheet's words and rules); the record follows `lienNoticeSignature.ts` (v2.5077): the record is the signature, not the mark.

- **`needs_signature` on `stage_checklist_templates`**: the author's toggle beside `asks_photo`. Default false, so every question written so far asks for none.
- **Six signature columns on `stage_checklist_answers`**: `signed_at`, `signed_by` (→ `users`), `signer_printed_name`, `signer_signature_mode` (`type` · `draw`), `signer_signature_storage_path` (a drawn mark's PNG in the `stage-checklist-photos` bucket under `<job_id>/sig/<answer_id>-<ms>.png`, read by the bucket's own job-folder policy; the `photo-save` sweep reads `photo_storage_path` alone, so it never files or deletes a mark) and `signed_answer_hash` (the question's words, the pick and the note at signing, `checklistAnswerHash`; the client reads the signature only while the hash still matches).
- **Four void columns**: `voided_at`, `voided_by`, `voided_by_name`, `void_why` (3 to 200 characters). *Take it back* voids the row; the next press writes a new live row. **`UNIQUE (fixture_id, question_id)` is dropped** for the partial unique index `stage_checklist_answers_live_idx` on the live rows (`voided_at IS NULL`), so a stage and question keep one live answer and any number of voided ones; `stage_checklist_answers_voided_idx` reads the voided ones back.
- **The lock, `stage_checklist_answers_signed_row_locks`** (`SECURITY INVOKER`, before insert or update): a signature is whole (a pick, the signer's name, a mode, a drawn mode's PNG, the hash) and the signer's own (`signed_by = auth.uid()`); once signed, the pick, the note, the stamp and the signature never change (*That answer is signed and locked. Take it back writes a new answer with a reason; the signed one stays.*); a void is the voider's own (`voided_by = auth.uid()`) with a reason, and a voided row's answer, stamp, signature and void never change after it. The photo columns stay the sweep's and the report-photo mirror's to write, on a signed row and on a voided one, and the row's `job_id` still follows its line (`20261102143000`). The service role and the office's own policies are not exempt: a signed answer is changed by no one.
- **`promote_stage_checklist_item`** re-created as `20261104170000` left it, copying `needs_signature` to the company's row with the words and the photo ask. The answers that move to the company's row keep their signatures: the copied words are the signed words, so the hash still matches.
- Additive and idempotent. No table is created, so no fence call. The new columns are nullable or defaulted, so no rewrite; the constraint drop and the index create take a short `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` on a table of a few rows, and `lock_timeout` makes a busy moment fail fast.
