# 20261117140000_sticker_printed.sql (2026-11-17, v2.7564)

A sticker remembers when its strips last came out of the printer. Punch list #373 ([`to-dos/sticker-print-record.md`](../../to-dos/sticker-print-record.md)), and the owner's words of 2026-11-16 on Print future stickers: *add Recording that a run's strips came out. It needs a new column*. This is the database half. No screen reads or writes the column yet.

The app reserves a run of stickers at the press (`mint_sticker_run`), before any strip leaves the printer, and kept no record that one did. `sticker_runs.printed_at` is `NOT NULL DEFAULT now()`: the moment the run was made. A printer that fails, a stop, a closed window or a cancelled print dialog all leave stickers with no strip, and every screen still read *Printed Nov 16 by Dana*. The twin of the mark on a box's own label, [`20261117110000_box_label_printed.sql`](./20261117110000_box_label_printed.md).

## The column, on `box_stickers`

| Column | Type | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `printed_at` | `timestamptz`, nullable, no default | NULL: the app has not seen this sticker's strips go out. A time: when they last did, the last copy of its strip leaving the P-touch or the print window closing on it. A person saying the strips did not print sets it back to NULL. |

## How it is written

No client wrote `box_stickers` before this: it had a SELECT policy and no other, and a sticker moves only through a `box_sticker_events` row whose definer trigger updates it. The mark is the one column a client changes, the way `job_id` is the one column of a run.

1. **The grant.** Table-level `UPDATE` is revoked from `anon` and `authenticated` and granted back to `authenticated` on `printed_at` alone. The new policy therefore opens this column and no other. A `DO` block fails the migration if the revoke or the grant did not take. The events trigger and the mint trigger are `SECURITY DEFINER`, and a foreign key's `SET NULL` runs as the table's owner, so none is touched by the revoke.
2. **The policy**, `box_stickers_update`: the SELECT policy's own predicate in `USING` and `WITH CHECK`. A sticker on a box follows its box's job and a reserved one its run's job, both through `on_site_can_touch_job`, and an orphaned run's stickers are the office's. Whoever reads a sticker may write its mark.
3. **The stamp**, `box_stickers_stamp_printed`, `BEFORE UPDATE OF printed_at`: from a direct caller (`current_user` is `authenticated` or `anon`, the on-site guards' own test) a time that is not null becomes `now()`, so a client cannot write a day of its own. NULL is let through. The service role and a definer pass untouched.

The read-only blocks, the twin fence and the archived seal already stand on `box_stickers`, so a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller leave no mark. A training account's update is refused in words by the statement trigger, and the screen that follows takes that quietly.

## Looked at and not picked

- **A `printed` kind of `box_sticker_events`.** It would keep who printed and every reprint, at the cost of rewriting the judge and apply triggers and one row a sticker a print.
- **A `SECURITY DEFINER` function.** `on_site_can_touch_job` is `SECURITY INVOKER` on purpose and would judge the function's owner, not the caller.

## Stickers made before the column

They read NULL whether or not they printed, and nothing can tell the two apart. The client that follows counts a NULL as *not printed yet* only for a sticker made after the mark began, and says nothing about the older ones until they print.

## The bed

`supabase/tests/sticker_names/40_printed_mark.sql`, run by `scripts/pgtest-sticker-names.sh`, which also applies this file a second time. Through RLS: a new sticker has no mark; the office and a helper on the job's crew write it, on a reserved sticker and on one on a box, and the time kept is the server's; the mark comes back off; `container_id`, `lost_at` and `box_name` refuse an update, alone or beside the mark; a helper on no job changes nothing; a training account changes nothing; a sticker still goes on and off a box through its events; and a caller that is not a direct one keeps its own time.

## Order

Additive and idempotent: a nullable column with no default is a catalog change alone, and no row is rewritten. No `CREATE TABLE`, so no fence calls. The push can come any time after the merge. **The client that follows must not merge before the push**: it names the column in the read of every sticker, and PostgREST refuses a select that names a column it does not have.
