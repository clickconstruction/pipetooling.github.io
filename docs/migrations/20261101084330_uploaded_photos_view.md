# 20261101084330_uploaded_photos_view.sql (2026-11-01, every photo uploaded, one list, punch list #232 PR 1)

The ground under Documents → Photos (punch list #232, shipped as v2.6264, v2.6270 and v2.6272): one view that reads the four photo tables as one shape, newest first, so the office sees every photo uploaded to the app in one list with where each is saved and who took it. The rules are `src/lib/photos/uploadedPhotos.ts` (v2.6264).

- **`uploaded_photos`** — a view, `security_invoker`, four branches in one shape: `source` (`report`, `box`, `checklist`, `message`), the row's own `id`, the job (`job_id`, or `bid_id` / `project_id` for a picture on a line), what it came with (`report_id`, `container_id`, `question_id`, `message_id`, and the line's `contact_id` for the thread's door), `caption` and `stage_name` (a box's container label, a checklist's stage), who (`by_user_id`, `by_name`; a line's `who_text`, with `direction` and `channel`), `taken_at`, the sizes, where it waits (`bucket`, `storage_path`), where it landed (`drive_file_id`, `filed_at`) or why not (`fail_why`, a box's `status`, a line's `left_in_gmail` as `status`), and the job's `job_hcp_number`, `job_click_number`, `job_name`, `customer_name` and a bid's `bid_number`, so the tab searches them on the server.
- **What each branch leaves out.** A report photo with no report (a form closed without saving; the client discards it). A checklist answer that names a report photo (`report_photo_id`, v2.6219): the same shot, listed once under the report. A checklist answer with no photo. A message attachment that is a link or not an image.
- **The gates.** The view runs as the caller, so every table's own policy decides: the stage-progress gate on report and checklist photos, the ledger-or-team gate on box photos, the office team and the estimator on message pictures. The inner join on `jobs_ledger` runs under the ledger's RLS too, so a primary's and a superintendent's list follows their jobs scope, which the report photos' own gate does not. `anon` reads nothing.
- **No table is made**, so the three fence calls are not needed; nothing here writes.
