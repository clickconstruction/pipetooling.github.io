# 20261115030000_bid_difficulty.sql (2026-11-15, v2.7301)

GC difficulty rating, PR 4 of six (punch list #357, [`to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md`](../../to-dos/gc-difficulty-rating/README.md)): the lines a bid was priced with, pinned.

- **`bid_difficulty`** — one row per bid (`bid_id` unique): the `cost_estimate_id` the rows were written on, the `rating_id`, `criteria_version_id` and `price_version_id` the lines came from, the `picks` and the `lines` as written (`[{key, name, points, hours, rate, amount, sentence, words}]`), the `row_ids` of the `cost_estimate_other_rows` the lines live in (one row per line, the dollars split by stage as the bid's field hours are), the lines a person took off (`removed`: `[{key, name, amount, reason, byName, at}]`), the totals and the inputs (`points`, `total_usd`, `field_hours`, `labor_rate`, `own_cost_usd`, `carried_usd`), who priced and when. The lines are Other rows and not a sixth direct-cost kind, so the cost kernel, the pricing history, the budgets and the stamp count them with no change; the pin is how the Workbench and the Labor tab tell them from a typed Other row.
- **The guard** stamps `priced_by`/`priced_by_name`/`priced_at` on insert and whenever the lines change; `updated_at` always.
- **The gates**: everyone signed in reads; `is_bid_pricing_user()` (dev, owner, assistant, controller, estimator) writes — whoever may price a bid.
- **`bids.priced_difficulty_usd`** — of `priced_cost_usd`, the part that is the difficulty lines, so Bid vs actual can hold the hours against the clock.
- **`stamp_bid_priced_margin`** gains `p_difficulty_usd numeric DEFAULT 0`. The six-argument shape is dropped first, so a call with six named arguments is not ambiguous, and the seventh defaults, so the client of the deploy before this one stamps as before. `bidPricedMarginSql.test.ts` pins the new signature.
- The four fence calls.
