# 20261001200000 — `bid_submittal_item_parts` (v2.4319)

**What**: one new table, the parts of a submittal row. Each part is what is bought and what the GC reviews: its catalog label (maker, model, the words after), how many go on one fixture, whether the GC sees it (`on_submittal`; false = order only, the trim), where it came from (`source` takeoff / file / hand, the takeoff line and assembly item, the assembly's name), the house, the lead time, the stage it is needed for, its cut sheet pages, the reviewer's call on it, and `procure_key`, which follows the part from revision to revision for the procurement log. A helper, `submittal_item_bid(item_id)`, returns the row's bid.

**Why**: Wendi, 2026-10-01: "need to show parts not just assemblies in submittals". A fixture priced from a price-book assembly read the assembly's name on the row, the GC's page and the package ("LAV 1 assembly SPACEX"); the parts inside never reached the submittal. Parts are bought separately (a carrier at Rough In, the china at Trim Set), so each carries its own house, lead time and stage. The row's own columns stay the roll-up every older reader uses: `submitted_label` = the parts on the submittal joined with " + ", the house of the first of them, the longest lead time among them.

**Safety**: `SET lock_timeout = '3s'`; `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`, nothing existing is altered. RLS as the rows: `is_pricing_sharer()` and `can_access_bid_for_pricing(bid_id)`; a write also needs `bid_id = submittal_item_bid(item_id)`, so a part can only land on a row of its own bid. `ON DELETE CASCADE` from the row and the bid. Ends with `apply_read_only_write_blocks()`, `apply_read_only_stmt_blocks()` and `apply_digital_twin_write_blocks()`. Dry-run in `BEGIN … ROLLBACK` on 2026-10-01: clean, 8 policies on the table.

**Client**: `src/lib/submittals/itemParts.ts` (the kernel) and `itemPartsIo.ts` (paged reads; a missing table reads as no parts, so a client ahead of the push keeps working). Written when Rev 1 is built from the takeoff, by Split, by New revision (carried with their row), and by the row editor.
