# 20261119010000_trip_split.sql (2026-11-18, v2.7891)

Split a trip in two.

A trip is one going out to a site and coming back. A trip that grew on the phone a day at a time, or one typed from memory, can turn out to be two: the crew went home in the middle and came back. Until now the fix was to change the trip to its first part and type the second part again as a new trip.

## What it adds

One function. No table, column or index.

`trip_split(p_trip_id uuid, p_from_day date, p_dry_run boolean DEFAULT false) RETURNS jsonb`, `SECURITY INVOKER`.

- The trip keeps its id, its number and every day before `p_from_day`.
- A new trip on the same home takes `p_from_day` and every day after it. The home hands it the next number.
- Each person goes where their days are. Somebody with days on both sides is on both trips.
- Both keep what the trip was for, who asked, how it went and the jobs its work goes on. The new trip's `via` is `app`.

It writes through `trip_save` twice inside the one call: the new trip first, then the trip cut down to its first part. Both land or neither does, and every rule the save holds is held here without being written twice.

## Who

`trips_office()`, checked first so the refusal reads in the split's own words. After that the trips tables' own policies decide, since the function is an invoker: a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller are refused where `trip_save` refuses them. `EXECUTE` is granted to `authenticated` and `service_role`, and revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`.

## What it refuses, in words

| Case | Words | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Nobody signed in | *Sign in to split a trip.* | 42501 |
| Not the trips office | *A trip is split by the office or a leader.* | 42501 |
| No trip or no day named | *Name the trip and the day its second part starts.* | 22023 |
| The trip is gone | *That trip is not here any more. Close this and look again.* | 22023 |
| No day before the day | *Pick a later day. No day of this trip is before …* | 22023 |
| No day on or after the day | *Pick an earlier day. No day of this trip is on or after …* | 22023 |

The day need not be one of the trip's days. A day at home in the gap cuts the same way as the day the crew went back.

## Three things the save does not know

1. **A trip on a bill keeps its line.** `jobs_ledger_fixtures.trip_id` names the trip that stays. The new trip then starts with no billing said, so nothing reads *Extra, not billed yet* for work the line may already cover. The reply says so (`on_bill`, `second_billing`).
2. **`trips.ended_at`**, the phone's End the trip. The new trip takes the stamp the trip had: ended if it was, open if it was. So a trip still growing on the phone keeps growing as the second trip. The first trip is ended once its last day is behind today (`app_today()`), so the phone stops offering it.
3. **Nothing else follows.** No clock row, bill or cost changes.

## The reply

Dry run: `{ dry_run: true, would: 'split', trip_id, trip_number, from_day, first, second, on_bill, second_billing }`, where `first` and `second` are `trip_save`'s own dry-run replies for each half. Nothing is written.

For real: `{ dry_run: false, did: 'split', trip_id, trip_number, new_trip_id, new_trip_number, from_day, first, second, on_bill, second_billing }`.

## Tests

- the grant: a signed-in user, not the anon key;
- the dry run says both halves and writes nothing;
- each refusal in its words, a crew lead and a training account refused, and the trip whole after all of them;
- the split: the number kept and the number made, who is on each trip, each day's kind, the words and the jobs on both, and nothing kept as deleted;
- a trip still out, split at a day to come, is not ended;
- an ended trip passes its stamp to both parts;
- a trip on a bill keeps its line, and the new trip says no billing.

## Verified on prod

On the ZZ Test development, as the dev. A trip filed through the dev seat (Trip 7, Nov 9 to 15, one person on all five days with two days at home in the middle, a second person on two) was split with the app's own press, on the local dev server against prod, with every other write refused in the browser.

- The window picked Nov 14, the day after the days at home.
- The trip kept its id and its number, Nov 9 to 11, with both people, and was ended.
- A new trip, Trip 8, took Nov 14 to 15 with the one person who went back, `via` `app`, not ended.
- `read_trips` read both back with their numbers.

Both trips were then deleted with the app's own press. They wait under Deleted trips for 90 days, and the site's next trip is Trip 9.
