# 20261029210000_stage_checklists.sql (2026-10-29, a checklist per stage, punch list #164 PR 2)

The ground under the stage checklists (`to-dos/job-photos-and-stage-checklists/`): the office writes a short list of questions once for each stage kind, Rough In, Top Out and Trim Set, and the crew answers them on the phone for each job's stage. The rules are `src/lib/jobs/stageChecklist.ts` (v2.6072).

- **`stage_checklist_templates`** — one question per row: `stage_kind` (`rough_in`, `top_out`, `trim_set`), `sort_order`, `words` (1 to 200 characters), `asks_photo`. A question the office takes off is `retired_at`, never deleted, so an answer already given keeps its words. A kind with no live question has no checklist, and its stages read as before. Ships empty: the owner types the questions.
- **`stage_checklist_answers`** — one row per job stage (`jobs_ledger_fixtures`) and question, unique on the pair: `pick` (`yes`, `no`, `not_here`, or null for a note or photo left before the pick), `note`, who and when, and the photo's waiting-room path (`photo_storage_path`), its name in Drive (`photo_file_name`), its Drive id and `photo_filed_at` once the `photo-save` sweep files it, and `photo_fail_why` when it could not (tried again every run).
- **The gates.** Everyone signed in reads the questions; the office writes them (`is_master_or_dev() OR is_assistant()`, so the controller too); a dev may delete. A job's answers are read, written and updated by whoever may stamp the job's stages, `can_report_stage_progress(job_id)` (`20260909141454_stage_progress.sql`: the office roles, or a field user on the job's team, clocked in on it, or under the sub rule); the office may delete one.
- **The bucket** `stage-checklist-photos`: private, 2 MB a file, image types only; objects are `<job_id>/<answer_id>.jpg`, so the same gate reads the job from the path.
- **The cron** `photo-save` is re-scheduled (unscheduled first, so one row stays) to call every five minutes while some job's tick is on, a box photo waits, or a checklist photo waits (`photo_filed_at IS NULL AND photo_storage_path IS NOT NULL`), on top of `20261029160000`'s two conditions. Until PR 3's function deploys the sweep runs its two older passes and a checklist photo keeps waiting; nothing is lost.
