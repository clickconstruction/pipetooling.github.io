# 20261012010000_messages_spine.sql (2026-10-12, v2.5258)

Messages, cut 1, the spine (`to-dos/gc-mode/messages/BUILD_PLAN.md` and `BUILD_HELPERS.md`'s cluster 1 on branch
`spike/gc-mode`; the kernels are `src/lib/contacts/kernel.ts` and `src/lib/messages/thread.ts`, v2.5249): one thread
per person. Four tables, the functions the first screens call, the seed, two delete triggers and one step in
`merge_customers`. Nothing writes a message yet: calls and texts arrive with the Quo link (cut 5), email with the bids@
reader (cut 6).

## What it does

1. **`contact_channel_key(kind, value)`**, immutable: a number as ten digits ("+1 (210) 555-0113" is `2105550113`), an
   address in lower case, `''` when it is neither. The same rule as `normalizeCustomerPhone` / `normalizeCustomerEmail`.
2. **`contacts`**, a person the app already has, never a fifth list:
   - exactly one record (`customer_id`, `customer_contact_person_id`, `gc_company_id`, `gc_company_person_id` or
     `person_id`, each `ON DELETE RESTRICT`), or none for a number nobody has named (`kind` `unknown`); the CHECK
     `contacts_one_record` says so;
   - `kind`: customer, trade, arch or unknown; `name`; `goes_by`; `off_since` / `off_why` / `off_by`, off the flow,
     which cut 4 writes (a why or a who only with a since); `created_at`;
   - a person's row is one contact; a company's row (`customer_id`, `gc_company_id`) is its own person (`name` null)
     and anyone else it holds by name, one contact each (unique indexes on the record and `lower(btrim(name))`). That
     is how a second person with no row of her own, Dee at Alamo Concrete known only by a cell, has a thread.
3. **`contact_channels`**: `contact_id` (cascade), `kind` (phone or email), `value` as given, `key` generated from
   `contact_channel_key`, `opted_out_at` (a STOP marks that number, never the person; an address is never stopped),
   `created_at`. Unique per contact and key; indexed by `(kind, key)`. A channel must have a key.
4. **`contact_jobs`**: the jobs the office put a person on (a bid, a GC project or a Pipeline job, exactly one) and
   the star (`is_default`, one per contact). It starts empty: the jobs a person is on by the facts are the read's.
5. **`messages`**: `contact_id` (`RESTRICT`: a contact with lines folds, it does not go), `channel_id` (set null),
   at most one of `bid_id` / `project_id` / `jobs_ledger_id` (set null), `channel` (call, voicemail, missed, text,
   email, note, event, portal), `direction` (in, out, team; team is a note or an event and nothing else),
   `who_user_id` / `who_text`, `at`, `subject`, `body`, `optout_line` (first, silence or held, on a text we sent),
   `back_on` / `back_by` (notes only), `created_at`. Indexed by `(contact_id, at desc)` and `channel_id`. Quo's and
   Gmail's ids, the recording, the transcript, the summary and `duration_seconds` come with cuts 5 and 6.
6. **One policy a table, the office team's** (`gc_office_team()` for every verb). `anon` holds nothing.
   `authenticated` holds `SELECT`, and only what the functions change: `UPDATE` on a contact's kind, record and names
   and `DELETE`; `UPDATE (contact_id)` and `DELETE` on a channel; `INSERT` / `UPDATE (contact_id, is_default)` /
   `DELETE` on a job; `UPDATE` on a line's contact, channel and job. Nobody makes a contact, writes a line or marks a
   STOP by hand; the seed and, from cut 5, the service role do. **Never run this migration again once a later cut's
   is in**: its grants start with a REVOKE on the four tables, which also takes back the column grants a later cut gave
   on them (cut 2's `UPDATE (state, snoozed_until, has_user_id, has_since, has_how)` on contacts). Its bed runs the
   second run right after the first for that reason (v2.5274).
7. **The functions**, `SECURITY INVOKER` so the policies and the house fences apply:
   - `contact_for_channel(kind, value)`: whose number or address it is, by key; a number two people share goes to the
     one it last carried a message for, else the one that had it first; null for nobody.
   - `name_contact(contact, record_table, record_id, kind, name, job_kind, job_id)`: *Who is this?* Refuses a contact
     already named (`namedAlready`), a kind or a table it does not know (`kindNeeded`, `recordNeeded`), a record or a
     job that is not there (`recordNotFound`, `jobNotFound`, `jobUnknown`). Re-points the contact, or folds it into
     the contact that already is that person. A job picked while naming goes on the person, takes the star when they
     have none, and ties this number's untied lines.
   - `tie_message(message, job_kind, job_id)` ties or unties a line; a new job goes on the person's rail.
     `set_default_job(contact, job_kind, job_id)` moves or clears the star.
   - `contacts_fold(from, into)`: the fold. Lines, numbers, addresses and jobs move; a number both have keeps the copy
     that says STOP; the star and goes-by stay the person's, else come over; the name is never changed. A later cut's
     rows on a contact move through its own `contacts_fold_<something>(p_from uuid, p_into uuid) RETURNS void` in
     `public`, owned as the fold is: the fold runs every such function, in name order, before the folded contact goes.
     No cut redefines `contacts_fold`, so the result never depends on the order the cuts merge in.
   - `contact_name_on`, `contact_job_check`: the name rule on a company's row, and a job's check.
8. **Two `BEFORE DELETE` triggers** (`contacts_keep_thread`, `SECURITY DEFINER`): the app deletes a customer contact
   person (Customers, Edit customer, the builder review) and a roster person (Settings → People) outright. Their
   contact moves off the row first, so the delete goes through and the thread stays: a person at a customer onto the
   customer under the person's name (into the contact already there under that name, if any); a roster person to an
   unknown number that keeps the name.
9. **`merge_customers`**, the one existing function this touches: as `20260817221950`, plus one step before the
   victim goes. Each contact on the victim's row folds into the survivor's contact of the same name, or moves to the
   survivor. The people at the victim move to the survivor as before, and their contacts with them.
10. **The seed**, `messages_seed_contacts()` with `messages_seed_customers()` (the database's own; nobody signed in may
    call them), run once at the end: a contact, with its numbers and addresses, for every trade partner's main
    contact, every person a trade partner named (not removed), every person at a customer, an architect or a property
    owner on a GC project, and each of those customers with nobody named at it. A customer that is only ever the
    architect reads as one. A company's own number and address go to its own person. A second run adds nothing.
11. **The three house calls**: `apply_read_only_write_blocks()`, `apply_read_only_stmt_blocks()`,
    `apply_digital_twin_write_blocks()`. `messages` and `contact_jobs` carry `bid_id`, so the twin fence holds a twin to
    lines and ties on its own bids, the house rule; `contacts` and `contact_channels` have none, so a twin writes none.

## Checked before the push

**The SQL bed** (`npm run test:pg:messages-spine`, the new `messages-spine` bed, in CI's SQL beds workflow; no session
on the office Mac reaches Docker): every migration on Supabase Postgres 17, the spine's migration once more right after
its first (changing nothing), then the scenario, rolled back.

- The seed makes the five people of a GC project and its trade partner, with their eight numbers and addresses, and
  nothing the second time. A company with a person at it has no contact of its own.
- A known kind with no record, an unknown number with one, two records, a second contact on a person's row or on a
  company's own person, a STOP on an address, a channel that is not a number or an address, a team line that is not
  a note or an event, an opt-out line on a text from them and a line on two jobs are each refused.
- No sign-in reads nothing; a superintendent reads none; the office reads all, and makes no contact, writes no line
  and marks no STOP by hand.
- Greg's number, as a webhook writes it, finds his other phone, the newest line's; his address finds him in capitals;
  a number nobody has, and words, find nobody.
- An assistant names Dee on the trade partner under her own name, on the clinic with the star, her line tied to it;
  names Greg's other phone in another case, which folds into Greg: one contact, both lines, one number.
- The dev names a number that sent STOP as Elena (her number keeps the STOP, once), a number as a roster person (the
  contact carries no name), and one as the duplicate customer; the seven refusals of `name_contact` each hold.
- An estimator ties Elena's email to the clinic, stars it twice (one star), clears the star and unties the line.
- Training mode and a digital twin tie, name and star nothing.
- Deleting Elena's person row puts her contact on the customer under her name with her whole thread; deleting the
  roster person leaves an unknown number with his name and his line.
- Merging the duplicate customer into the dental office leaves one contact with both lines and both numbers, and no
  duplicate.
- A trade partner with contacts, and a contact with lines, are not deleted.

-- 1. The tables, their policies and grants.
SELECT tablename, policyname, permissive, cmd FROM pg_policies
WHERE tablename IN ('contacts', 'contact_channels', 'contact_jobs', 'messages') ORDER BY 1, 2;
-- expect: per table the three digital_twin_write_fence_* and three read_only_users_cannot_* (RESTRICTIVE), <table>_team (PERMISSIVE, ALL)
SELECT table_name, string_agg(privilege_type, ',' ORDER BY privilege_type) FROM information_schema.role_table_grants
WHERE table_name IN ('contacts', 'contact_channels', 'contact_jobs', 'messages') AND grantee = 'authenticated'
  AND privilege_type IN ('SELECT', 'INSERT', 'UPDATE', 'DELETE', 'TRUNCATE') GROUP BY 1;
-- expect: contacts DELETE,SELECT; contact_channels DELETE,SELECT; contact_jobs DELETE,SELECT; messages SELECT
SELECT count(*) FROM information_schema.role_table_grants
WHERE table_name IN ('contacts', 'contact_channels', 'contact_jobs', 'messages') AND grantee = 'anon';

-- 2. The seed: one contact per person, every one on one record.
SELECT kind, count(*) FROM public.contacts GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;
SELECT count(*) FROM public.contacts
WHERE num_nonnulls(customer_id, customer_contact_person_id, gc_company_id, gc_company_person_id, person_id) <> 1;
-- expect: 0
SELECT kind, count(*) FROM public.contact_channels GROUP BY 1;

-- 3. The triggers and merge_customers' step are there.
