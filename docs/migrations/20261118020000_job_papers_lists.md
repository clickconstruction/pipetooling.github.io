# 20261118020000_job_papers_lists.sql (2026-11-17, v2.7665)

Papers before a job opens: the lists. Step 5 of seven. Step 4 closed a job's rows to the crew and subs who owe it a paper. The functions here run as their definer and name jobs to whoever calls them, so row security never asked them anything. Each now asks the lock itself.

## What it makes

Two new functions and eight restated. No table and no policy.

- **`job_shut_to_user(job, user)`** and **`jobs_shut_to_user(user)`**: the lock's question about a named person. The same rule as `job_shut_to_me`: the person owes a paper on the job and is not a prompt-only role. For callers with no login of their own: the edge functions that read with the service key, and the two schedule functions below. Service role only.
- **`search_jobs_ledger`** (from `20261102170000`): a locked job answers with its number and no name or address. It is found by its number alone: its name, its address and its development no longer match a search.
- **`list_assigned_jobs_for_dashboard`** (from `20260722258000`) and **`list_superintendent_jobs_for_dashboard`** (from `20260908050000`): a locked job keeps its number and loses its name, address, three Drive links, revenue and stage name. The first also loses its customer.
- **`list_jobs_for_tally`** (from `20260619160000`): a locked job keeps its number.
- **`list_reports_with_job_info`** (from `20260927233000`): a report on a locked job is left out. A report is part of the job.
- **`list_job_schedule_blocks_for_schedule_email`** and **`list_schedule_blocks_for_share`** (from `20260927233000`): a block on a job the recipient or viewer is locked out of keeps its number and its time. Its job name reads *Papers to sign first*, and its address and note are blank.

- **`job_papers_roster`** (from `20261117220000`): the office it lists leaves the walk tester out, as it leaves out the sample accounts.

## Rules it settles

- **No returned column changes.** Every `RETURNS TABLE` is as it was, so the grants in force stay and every caller keeps working. The client marks a locked job from its own read of the jobs closed to it.
- **A locked job must not answer to its name.** Blanking the output alone would leave a way round: type the name, get the number of the job that carries it.
- **The role list is written twice.** `job_shut_to_me` asks `is_office_staff()` and `is_primary()` about the caller. `job_shut_to_user` names the same five roles about someone else. The bed holds the two to the same answer for every role.

## What it leaves alone

Other definer functions that return a job's name and were not on the plan's list: `list_ready_to_bill_assigned_jobs_for_dashboard`, `search_jobs_for_reports`, `search_jobs_for_tally_mercury_assign_as_user`, `list_tally_parts_with_po`, `list_reports_for_job_ledger`, `get_jobs_ledger_by_ids`. They were not audited here. They are named so the next pass starts from them.

## Order and proof

The client already copes with a blank name, so the push follows the merge. Nothing changes while no job requires a paper.
