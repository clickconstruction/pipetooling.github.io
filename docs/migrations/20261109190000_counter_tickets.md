# 20261109190000_counter_tickets.sql (2026-11-09, v2.6812)

Punch list #295, PR 1 of four: a photo of the supply house ticket, taken at the counter. The ground under the press; no screen reads it until PR 2. The card and the mock-up are [`to-dos/ticket-photo/`](../../to-dos/ticket-photo/README.md).

- **`counter_tickets`**, one row a ticket: who took it and when (`taken_at`, with `taken_at_from` = `press` · `photo` · `none`, since only a time that says when the paper was in hand may match a swipe), the job (`job_id`, more in `also_job_ids`, or `no_one_job`), the PO code it was bought on (`po_entry_id`), a typed `total` and a `note`, `added_at` (the Save press; the lists and the sweep read only a saved ticket), and how it met its money: `mercury_transaction_id` with `match_kind` (`minute` · `total` made with no press, `asked` on a Yes, `picked` by the office), `declined_transaction_ids` (charges it was said not to be), `paid_other` (cash, own money, another card, other) or `supply_house_invoice_id` (the bill it became). `used_at` is the office's first act on it; `removed_at` hides one taken by mistake. Never deleted: `authenticated` holds no DELETE. One live ticket a charge (`counter_tickets_one_per_charge`, a partial unique index).
- **`counter_ticket_pages`**, one row a page: its place, its kind (a picture or a PDF), where it waits (`storage_path`) and where it landed (`drive_file_id`, `drive_folder_id`, `filed_at`, `fail_why`).
- **Gates**: a ticket is read by its taker, the office (`is_office_staff()`) and the holder of the card its charge is on (`counter_ticket_charge_is_mine(uuid)`, the card id read with `mercury_debit_card_id_from_raw`); `counter_ticket_visible(uuid)` says the same for the pages and the bucket. The taker inserts and updates their own, the office any. The trigger `counter_tickets_guard` holds the rest: a taker ties a ticket only to a charge on a card of their own (another card raises *That charge is on another person's card. The office matches it.*), never changes the job of or removes a ticket the office has used (*The office has already used this ticket. Ask the office to change it.*), and never writes `used_at`, `paid_other` or `supply_house_invoice_id`; the match, the removal and the first use are stamped there, not by the client.
- **A moved ticket**: `counter_tickets_job_change_refiles` clears `filed_at` on the ticket's filed pages when `job_id` changes, and the sweep moves the Drive file to the new job's folder.
- **The bucket `counter-tickets`**: private, 10 MB a file, JPEG, PNG, WebP and PDF, objects named `<uploader>/<ticket_id>/<page_id>.<ext>`. A person writes under their own id; a page is read by its uploader, the office, or whoever may see its ticket.
- **The cron**: `photo-save` is re-scheduled with a ninth clause, a saved, unremoved ticket with a job and a page not in its folder; the eight earlier clauses are written again from `20261106230000`.
- The four fences close the file. Additive and idempotent; no client reads either table yet, so the push may come at any time after the merge. Deploy `photo-save` after the push.
- **Bed**: `npm run test:pg:counter-tickets` (`supabase/tests/counter_tickets/`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`).
- **Not here**: a combine of two jobs sets a ticket's `job_id` to null (the key is ON DELETE SET NULL, and `migrate_job_ledger_costs_and_delete` does not name the table yet), so the office places it again; and the `uploaded_photos` view does not list ticket pages.
