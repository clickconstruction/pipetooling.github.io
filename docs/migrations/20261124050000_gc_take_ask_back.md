# 20261124050000_gc_take_ask_back.sql (2026-11-24, v2.8440)

Taking an ask back: one function, `gc_take_ask_back(p_invite_id uuid)`, the write behind **Take the ask back…** on a trade's asks and on Follow up, and one table, `gc_asks_taken_back`, its trace.

- **Why**: an ask (`gc_invites`) only ever closed with the company's own answer, and `gc_remove_bench_company` (`20261109004500`) keeps any company that was asked. An ask made on the wrong company, or on a test trade (punch list #416's walk 1c), had no way back. The owner's word of 2026-11-24: add it.
- **Deleted, not marked withdrawn**: the function takes back only an ask the company never heard about, and deletes its row with the office's own notes on it (`gc_company_contacts` with `how` *note*, by cascade). They were never told, so reading as never asked is true. A withdrawn state would be a new answer on `gc_invites` for the board, the trade's portal and every function that reads an ask to learn, and an ask the company did hear about already has an honest close in the company's words, **Will not do it** or **Cannot do it**. So those are refused, never deleted.
- **What it refuses**, the first found naming the refusal as *‹company› stays asked on ‹trade›, since …*:
  - the trade is awarded to this ask (*Take the award back first, in Compare quotes.*);
  - a statement of work names it (`gc_sows.invite_id`);
  - a quote is in (`gc_quotes`, or status `bid`);
  - the company already passed (status `declined`);
  - the company opened it in its portal (status past `invited`, or `seen_rev` set);
  - we emailed them about it (`gc_trade_messages` keyed by the ask, the invitation);
  - a contact with them is logged on it (any `gc_company_contacts` line on the ask that is not the office's note: a call, a text, an email, a nudge, their portal).
  The last three end *Close an ask they heard about with Will not do it or Cannot do it.*
- **The bench's Remove is not touched**: once the ask is gone nothing of it is left on the company, so `gc_remove_bench_company` lets go of a company whose only history was that ask, by its own rule. A company we emailed, or whose portal was opened, still stays.
- **The trace, `gc_asks_taken_back`**: the ask is gone and its company may follow, so the undo cannot live on either. One row a take-back: `package_id` (cascade with the trade), `company_id` (`ON DELETE SET NULL`) and `company_name` as it read that day, `asked_on` and `asked_by` from the ask, `taken_back_on`, `taken_back_by` and `by_name`. Append only.
- **Door**: `SECURITY DEFINER`, since `gc_trade_messages` and `gc_sows` read under a dev's policies. Whoever may ask may take an ask back: `gc_office_team()` in the body (*Only the office takes an ask back.*); a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller are refused first in their own words. `REVOKE … FROM PUBLIC, anon`, `GRANT EXECUTE … TO authenticated, service_role`.
- **The table's policies**: one `FOR SELECT` policy for `gc_office_team()`. No write policy, and INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE are revoked from `authenticated`, so the function is the only writer. The file closes with the four fence calls (`apply_read_only_write_blocks`, `apply_read_only_stmt_blocks`, `apply_digital_twin_write_blocks`, `apply_archived_read_blocks`).

Idempotent: `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`, `CREATE INDEX IF NOT EXISTS`, `DROP POLICY IF EXISTS` before its create, `CREATE OR REPLACE`. The one new table is empty, so the fences lock nothing in use.

**The bed**: `supabase/tests/gc_take_ask_back/20_scenario.sql` (`scripts/pgtest-gc-take-ask-back.sh`, in `scripts/sql-beds.txt`): the table's fences, every role's gate, each refusal with its words, the take-back and its ledger row, the office unable to write the ledger by hand, the company asked again, the bench's Remove keeping an emailed company and letting the taken-back one go with its name kept in the ledger, and the award guard still refusing a plain write.
