# 20261118150000_person_watches.sql (2026-11-18, v2.7825)

A watch on what one person does with the material on site. Database only; the Person desk's control and the By person look's chip come in the PR after the push.

- **`person_watches`**: one row a watch: who is watched, why, who to tell (`tell_user_ids`), who set it and when, and who lifted it and why. One live watch a person (a unique index over the rows not lifted). A watch is lifted, never deleted, and a lifted watch is closed. `person_watches_stamp` (BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE) makes the setter and the lifter the caller and keeps the watched person and the setter from changing.
- **`watched_box_opens`**: one row each time a watched person opened a box on a screen: the watch, the person, the box, when. Insert only through the function below.
- **`on_site_note_box_open(p_container_id)`**: the box screens call it for everyone. It writes only when the caller has a live watch, at most once a box each ten minutes, and answers nothing either way, so a caller cannot tell whether they are watched. A refusal by the training-mode or twin fences is swallowed for the same reason. `SECURITY DEFINER`, granted to `authenticated`.
- **Who reads.** Devs alone, by `is_dev()`, on both tables, as the HR file is. Devs insert and update `person_watches`; nobody deletes. The watched person cannot read either table.
- Additive and idempotent. Ends with the four fences.

Apply: `supabase db push` after the merge. No client calls any of it yet.
