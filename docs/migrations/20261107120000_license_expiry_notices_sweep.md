The window (today through today + 30, in the app's calendar day, `app_today()`) and the once-only stamp (`person_licenses.expiry_dispatch_notified_at`) of the baseline's `notify_dispatch_license_expiry_if_needed` live here; it inserts one open `dispatch_requests` row (*License expiring: … · Open [1]*) under the given sender and stamps the license, or answers NULL when nothing is owed. - **`notify_dispatch_license_expiry_if_needed(p_license_id, p_link)`** keeps its signature; its caller gate is a dev, a pay-approved master or an assistant, signed in (the three the pay-visibility overhaul of `20260714200000` left standing), and it files through the row-maker under `auth.uid()`. - **`license_expiry_notices_sweep()`** (`SECURITY DEFINER`, `search_path` public; EXECUTE for `authenticated` and `service_role`): runs with no signed-in user (the cron) or as a dev; anyone else is answered `{ ok: false, reason }`. The sender is `app_settings.portal_requests_from_user_id` when it names a `users` row, else the first unarchived dev, as `submit-portal-request` picks it. It walks every `person_licenses` row inside the window with a null stamp and files each through the row-maker, then POSTs each new row to `/functions/v1/notify-dispatch-request` with the Vault `CRON_SECRET` (`{ dispatch_request_id, mode: 'created' }`; the function's cron door, v2.6630) so the Dispatch group hears the push as it does after a Save. A Vault or `pg_net` that is missing (a local bed) leaves the rows standing and names the miss as `push_error`. Answers `{ ok, filed, dispatch_request_ids, pushed, from_user_id, push_error? }`. A license already past its expiry gets no notice; the Expired section is its place.
- **Cron `license-expiry-notices`** — `12 13 * * *` (13:12 UTC: 7:12 AM Central in winter, 8:12 in summer), `SELECT public.license_expiry_notices_sweep();`, unscheduled first so a second push leaves one row.
