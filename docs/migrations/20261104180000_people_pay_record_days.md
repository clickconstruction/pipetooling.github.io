# 20261104180000_people_pay_record_days.sql (2026-11-04, v2.6501)

A person with no login holds their own days and their own Charged as (punch list #254). A clock session belongs to a login (`clock_sessions.user_id` → `users` → `auth.users`), so a person paid from bank rows and never given a login could hold no day and no standing, and a job they worked priced short by every day they were out. The owner's rule (2026-11-04): "we want to be able to track people even if they have no login".

- **`people.charge_standing`** — the four values of `users.charge_standing` (`master`, `tech`, `helper`, `none`; null reads as none), the check added `NOT VALID`. A People record linked to a login reads the login's standing; an unlinked one reads its own (`standingForPerson` in `src/lib/charges/chargeStandings.ts`).
- **`people_pay_record_days`** — `id`, `person_id → people ON DELETE CASCADE`, `job_ledger_id → jobs_ledger ON DELETE CASCADE`, `work_date`, `hours numeric(6,2)` (more than zero, at most sixteen), `note` (never blank: the bank row, the trip record, the person's word), `origin` (`imported`, the only value), `approved_at` / `approved_by`, `created_at` / `created_by`; one row a person, a job and a day (`people_pay_record_days_one_a_day`), indexed by job and by person. RLS: SELECT for `is_dev() OR has_payroll_access()` — the roles that price a job's charges, as `charge_standings()` and `job_charge_adjustments` are gated; no INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE policy, so the two doors below write as the definer and nothing else does. `REVOKE ALL FROM anon`, `GRANT SELECT TO authenticated`. **Pay never reads these rows**: the people on them are paid from bank rows, not payroll; `approve_person_days` writes no `people_hours` row and runs no crew-job sync.
- **`import_person_days(p_rows jsonb)`** — `SECURITY DEFINER`, EXECUTE to `authenticated`, refused inside unless `is_dev()` (42501). Modeled line for line on `import_clock_sessions`: each row carries `person_id`, `job_ledger_id`, `work_date`, `hours` and a `note`; a row is refused, by position and reason, when the person is not on the People roster (a linked or an unlinked record both count), the job is unknown or billing-only, the hours are zero or more than sixteen, the day is after today in the company's zone, the note is blank, or a field will not read. The rest still go in, unapproved, `created_by` the dev. A rerun skips what is there. Answers `{ inserted, skipped, refused: [{ row, reason }] }`.
- **`approve_person_days(p_ids uuid[])`** — `SECURITY DEFINER`, the hours-approval gate (`is_dev() OR has_payroll_access() OR is_assistant()`, refused otherwise with 42501), stamps `approved_at = now()` and `approved_by` on the rows still waiting and nothing else. Answers `{ approved, skipped }`.
- **`charge_standings()`** (v2.6490) is dropped and made again with `person_id` beside `id`: every login as before, plus every People record with no login as a row with `person_id` and no `id`, its `people.charge_standing`. Archived rows first; among live rows a People record sorts before a login of the same name, so a last-wins map by name still lands on the login. A client built before this change ignores the new column.
- The three fence calls, since a table is created.
