  - A revoked or rejected session does not count. Nor does a salary day the app fills in itself (`origin = 'salary_schedule'`), a quick add (`quick_add_minutes`, office minutes for an off-hours call), a sample account or a digital twin.

  - A dev only while Building is built, never a twin. At most 92 days a call; U8b's io reads in 92-day pages.
