# 20261102050000_stage_checklist_job_items.sql (2026-11-02, stage checklist items on the job, punch list #237 PR 1)

Stage checklists as documents on the job (`to-dos/stage-checklist-document/`, v2.6328): the company's questions in `stage_checklist_templates` (`20261029210000_stage_checklists.sql`, #164) gain a second scope, an item added for one job's stage by the office or the job's superintendent. The rules are `src/lib/jobs/stageChecklist.ts` and `stageChecklistItems.ts`.

- **Four columns on `stage_checklist_templates`**: `job_id` → `jobs_ledger` and `fixture_id` → `jobs_ledger_fixtures` (both cascade; NULL on the company's rows, both set on a job item, held together by the check `stage_checklist_templates_job_scope`), `created_by_name` (the adder's name for the sheet's stamp, *Added by Pat Sample · Nov 1*) and `retired_by` → `users` (who took a job item off; `retired_at` already says when, and the Settings editor retires a company question without naming anyone). A job item taken off is retired, never deleted, so an answer it already has keeps its words. The partial index `stage_checklist_templates_job_live_idx` covers the live items by job and stage.
- **One trigger**, `stage_checklist_templates_stage_on_job` over `stage_checklist_item_stage_on_job()` (SECURITY DEFINER, since field roles cannot read `jobs_ledger_fixtures`): the stage an item names must be on the job it names, or it raises *That stage is not on this job.* Without it an item added on one job could count on another job's stage on the Pipeline.
- **The gates**, the three policies re-created: **select** the company's rows for everyone signed in as before, a job's items by whoever may stamp the job's stages (`can_report_stage_progress(job_id)`); **insert** the office (`is_master_or_dev() OR is_assistant()`, so the controller too) anywhere, or a superintendent on their own job in their own name (`created_by = auth.uid() AND is_superintendent() AND superintendent_report_job_anchor_allowed(job_id)`: the assigned project or the job's team, the reports' own rule); **update** the office, or on a job item its adder to retire it and nothing else (`created_by = auth.uid()`, and the row must come out with `retired_at` set and `retired_by = auth.uid()`, so an update cannot move a live item onto a job that is not the adder's). The dev's delete policy is unchanged. The answers table, its policies, the bucket and the cron are untouched: an item is a question row, so an answer references one table and the strict rule counts it like a company question.
- **The fences stand.** The read-only and twin policies on this table are restrictive and keep their own names (`read_only_users_cannot_*`, `digital_twin_write_fence_*`), so dropping the three gates leaves them in place; the twin's allowance on this table is still none, since it has no `bid_id` or `cost_estimate_id`.
- Additive and idempotent; no table is created, so no fence call. The new foreign keys take a short `SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE` on `jobs_ledger`, `jobs_ledger_fixtures` and `users`; `lock_timeout` makes a busy moment fail fast, and the table being altered holds a few rows, so nothing is rewritten or scanned for long.

Apply order: the client reads the new columns by name (`job_id` in every question read), so push right after the merge and before the Pages deploy finishes; `check:calls-ahead` cannot see a column, so the label's meaning is carried by hand. Until the push the sheet, the strip, the board's mark and the field report's chips read nothing (fail-soft), Documents and the Settings card show the error's words. The old client reads this schema unharmed, and no job item exists until the new client adds one. After the push, `npm run gen-types:linked` as its own `chore(types)` PR.

The gates have their own bed, `scripts/pgtest-stage-checklist-items.sh` over `supabase/tests/stage_checklist_items/20_scenario.sql`: who adds, the stage held to its job, who reads, who takes off and what the adder may write, the answer kept, the company's rows, the training and twin fences, and a second run of this file. Beds run only in CI, so the scenario was desk-checked against this file before its first run; read its `ok:` lines in the job log (42 for this file's gates; 141 since PR 1b's section of the scenario).
