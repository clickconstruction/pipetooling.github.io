# 20261019100000_paid_claims_read.sql (2026-10-19, v2.5650)

The office side of a paid claim (punch list #129 PR 4, `to-dos/estimator-money-word/README.md`): the live claims, read by the two surfaces that act on them. On `20261019080000_paid_claim.sql` (the kind) and `20261019060000_job_people.sql`; push after them, after the PR merges. The client reads fail-soft before the push (no suggestion, no Needs you line), so client first is fine.

## What it does

**`list_paid_claims() → jsonb`** (STABLE, SECURITY DEFINER): every live `paid_claim` row the caller may read — all of them for the promise readers (`can_read_payment_promises()`), their jobs' for the job's people (`is_job_people`) — oldest day first, each with: `id`, `jobId`, `jobNumber` (the HCP number, else the Click number, else ''), `jobName`, `jobStatus`, `customerId` and `customerName` (the job's), `payerCustomerId` (the claim's payer by the bill-to rule), `paidYmd` (the day they say they paid), `method`, `amount`, `saidBy`, `heardById`, `heardByName`, `channel`, `note`, `createdAt`, and **`landedSince`** / **`landedOn`**: the sum and the latest day of the job's payments on or after the day they named (0 and null until one lands). `[]` for nobody's.

Nothing on `jobs_ledger`'s policies changes.

## Why the shape

- What landed is counted on the server from the day they named, so the client's two questions — *is this claim still open?* for the allocation's suggestion, *has nothing landed in seven days?* for the stale line — are one number each, and a claim closes on its own when the deposit is allocated.
- One read for both the office and the job's people, gated inside, as the per-job promise read is: the Dashboard's line is the estimator's, the allocation's suggestion the office's.
