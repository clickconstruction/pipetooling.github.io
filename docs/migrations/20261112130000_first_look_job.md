# 20261112130000_first_look_job.sql (2026-11-12, v2.7005)

The look follows the job (punch list #319 PR 6). No new table, so no fence call.

## What it does

1. **`first_looks.job_id uuid`** to `jobs_ledger(id)`, `ON DELETE SET NULL`, the foreign key added `NOT VALID` and validated in its own statement, and **`first_looks.drive_job_folder_at`**, the stamp the sweep sets once the look's Drive folder sits under the job. A partial index over the rows with a job.
2. **`first_look_follows_estimate_job()`** and the trigger **`estimates_first_look_follows_job`** (`AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OF job_ledger_id, first_look_id`, only `WHEN` both are set): the look takes the estimate's job, once. SECURITY DEFINER. Its one write sits inside an exception block that swallows every error, so an estimate's write never fails because of a look.
3. **`set_first_look_job(p_look_id uuid, p_job_id uuid) → jsonb`**: the same stamp for Book a visit, where there is no estimate. Keeps the first job. SECURITY DEFINER.
4. **Three SELECT policies** named `first_look_files_select_job_team` and `first_looks_select_job_team`: a look with a job, its files, and its objects in the bucket `first-look-files` are read by whoever `can_report_stage_progress(job_id)` lets report on that job.

## Who may do what

- **`set_first_look_job`**: the office team. A training account and a digital twin are refused in words. `anon` has no EXECUTE.
- **Read**: the office team as before. Beside it, for a look with a job only, whoever may report on the job: the office roles, and a field user on the job's team or clocked in on it, which is the gate on the job's own photos. A look with no job is the office team's alone.
- **Write**: nobody from the app, as before.

## Release order

Merge, `bash scripts/db-push.sh`, then `supabase functions deploy photo-save`. Any order is safe. The Job Mode card's read answers no look from a database without `job_id`, and the sweep's move step reads nothing there.

## Verify

Read-only on prod after the push: `select tgname from pg_trigger where tgname = 'estimates_first_look_follows_job'` reads one row; `select policyname from pg_policies where policyname like 'first_look%job_team' order by 1` reads three rows; `select has_function_privilege('anon', 'public.set_first_look_job(uuid, uuid)', 'execute')` reads false.
