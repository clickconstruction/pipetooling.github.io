# 20261109090000_nudge_send_at.sql (2026-11-09, v2.6774)

Send later (punch list #291 PR 4, `to-dos/text-the-bill/README.md`): a text the office hands to a time, not only a morning. The owner: "when it comes to send in the morning I would like for the user to be able to know exactly when that is and to potentially change the time". Deploy `messages-nudge-dispatch` first (it reads the new column and runs the fifteen-minute tick), then push right after the merge: the client's `nudge_offer_make` call carries a ninth argument the old function lacks (`check:calls-ahead` labels the PR), and the client falls back to the morning's eight arguments when the function is not there yet.

## What it does

1. **`nudge_offers.send_at timestamptz`**: the instant an office-made text goes. `send_on` stays the day beside it (the run's morning path and every older reader keep it). A partial index on the open rows with a time, for the fifteen-minute read.
2. **`nudge_offer_make(…, p_send_on, p_about_on, p_send_at default null)`**: the office hands a text to a time; `send_on` is filled from `send_at`'s Central day when the caller leaves it out. The eight-argument function is dropped first, as `20261102200000` did, so PostgREST never sees two matches.
3. **`nudge_offer_retime(p_offer, p_send_at)`**: Today's sends changes the time of an open office-made text; refused outside 8 AM to 9 PM Central (*Texts go between 8 AM and 9 PM. Pick a time inside them.*), for a text already sent or put away, for one the app prepared, and through `quick_reply_caller`'s own refusals. Clears `held_why`, so a row the run held outside the window goes at its new time.
4. **The cron**: `messages-nudge-dispatch` is unscheduled by name and scheduled `*/15 * * * *`. The function prepares the 9 AM Central offers once a morning (it guards with `messages_nudge_last_run_v1`'s day) and on every tick sends the office texts whose `send_at` is due inside the window; one due outside is moved to the next 8 AM with `held_why` *Texts go between 8 AM and 9 PM. It goes at 8 AM.*

No table is made, so no fence call. Both functions are SECURITY DEFINER with EXECUTE to `authenticated`, revoked from `PUBLIC` and `anon`.

## Why the shape

- A timestamp beside the date, not in place of it: every reader of `send_on` (the run's morning path, Today's sends' *Goes in the morning of*, the stale check) keeps working while the new one adds the time.
- The fence lives in two places on purpose: the function refuses an out-of-window retime so the office sees the rule at the press, and the run holds a row it finds outside anyway (a clock change, an older client) rather than texting at night.
- Every quarter hour, not a second daily tick: the owner asked for the exact time, and a text handed to 6:00 PM should go then, not at the next morning.

## Verify

After the push and the deploy: `select send_at, send_on, held_why from nudge_offers where sent_at is null and dismissed_at is null and send_at is not null` reads the office's waiting texts with their times; `select schedule from cron.job where jobname = 'messages-nudge-dispatch'` reads `*/15 * * * *`. On a ZZ Test customer: Send later · Today 6:00 PM from the sheet, the row on Today's sends reads *Goes Today 6:00 PM*, Change the time moves it, and the text lands within the quarter hour (the walk on the card).
