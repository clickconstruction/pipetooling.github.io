# 20261106030000_machines.sql (2026-11-06, v2.6555, Equipment pre-use check, punch list #270 PR 1)

The tables behind the equipment pre-use check, pushed alone before any client reads them. A machine is a `vehicles` row with `kind = 'machine'`; the office picks it a template by kind and size; a crew signs a morning walk and a leaving check on it with its gauges and hour meter; its photos file into its own Drive folder; Dispatch attaches it to a job's day on the schedule. Additive and idempotent; nothing changes for a truck.

- **machine_templates** — a kind of machine at a size (*Small excavator*, *Skid steer*, *Directional drill*) with its gauges in `levels`, the `LevelDef` list the phone draws (`key`, `name`, `look` of tank, glass, stick or dial, `marks`, `lowBelow`, `color`, `where`). Anyone signed in reads; masters, devs and assistants write; devs delete. Nine seeded by fixed id: excavators small, medium and large, skid steer, directional drill, trencher, compactor, trailer, other.
- **machine_check_questions** — a line of the walk: both owners null for every machine, `template_id` for a kind, `vehicle_id` for one machine (`machine_check_questions_one_owner` keeps it to one). `holds_machine` says a No holds it; `asked_on` is `pre`, `post` or `both`. Same policies as the templates, with delete open to masters, devs and assistants. Twenty-one seeded: seven for every machine's walk, three for the leaving check, and the kinds' own.
- **vehicles** — `kind` (`truck` by default, `machine`), `machine_kind`, `unit_label` (E-12), `meter` (`miles` or `hours`), `code` (the label's, unique where set), `template_id`, `levels` (the machine's own gauges when they differ from the template's, else null), the four `rental_*` columns, and `google_drive_link` for the machine's folder. CHECKs by name in a DO block so a second push adds nothing.
- **vehicle_possessions.job_id** — a machine held by a job rather than a person; `vehicle_possessions_one_holder` keeps a row to one of `user_id` and `job_id`.
- **machine_checks** — a `pre` or `post` check: job, day, who, started and signed, verdict (`unfinished`, `safe`, `held`), the answers and gauge readings as JSON, hours, where it was left, Dispatch's run-it-anyway, and the Dispatch request a hold raised. Anyone signed in reads; a row is inserted by its own `by_user_id`; its author, masters, devs and assistants update; devs delete.
- **machine_check_photos** and the bucket **machine-check-photos** — a photo of a check (`shot`: meter, front, side, back, wrong, `gauge:<key>`, `no:<question id>`) with the name it gets in Drive. Objects are `<vehicle_id>/<photo_id>.jpg`, owned by the taker; the sweep deletes with the service role. The partial index serves the sweep.
- **machine_assignments** — a machine attached to a job for a day from the block editor, who brings it, and the morning's answer (`here`, `later`, `not_coming`) with `why`. One row per machine, job and day. Masters, devs and assistants write; the crew on the job may update (the answer), through `can_report_stage_progress`.
- **dispatch_requests.pending_action** — the comment names `machine_held`; a partial index on the open ones.
- **The cron photo-save** — unscheduled and scheduled again with every earlier clause and a sixth: a `machine_check_photos` row with a `storage_path` and no `filed_at`. The sweep's sixth source ships in a later PR; until then the clause wakes it for nothing, and it answers with no rows.
- **app_settings `drive_fleet_root_folder_id`** — a row, no DDL: the folder the machine folders are made under, named *PipeTooling Fleet* in the words; read by the sweep when it ships.

The three fence calls close it, since tables are made.
