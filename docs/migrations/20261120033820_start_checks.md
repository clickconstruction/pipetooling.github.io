# 20261120033820_start_checks.sql (2026-11-19, v2.7997)

Before we start, the tables. Punch list #382, PR 3 of eleven. The rules they hold are the kernel of PR 2 (`src/lib/startChecks/startChecks.ts`, v2.7992). No screen reads or writes them yet: the list on a stage is the PR that follows.

For each stage line the office writes a short list of things that must be true before the crew goes. Nothing here blocks a schedule, a clock-in or a bill.

## The tables

| Table | One row is | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `start_check_templates` | a bullet on the company's list for a stage kind | `stage_kind` (`rough_in`, `top_out`, `trim_set`), `words`, `waits_on` (`them` or `us`), `wants_photo`, `reads`. A stage with no list is offered its kind's list as a starter and copies it. A change here changes no stage that already has its list. |
| `start_check_bullets` | one thing that must be true before a stage line begins | `job_id`, `fixture_id`, `words`, `waits_on`, `wants_photo`, `shown_to_gc` (the eye), `by_day` (the day they say it will be true), `reads` for a bullet of ours (`order_log`, `trip`, `permit`, `hand`), the hand tick, `removed_at`. |
| `start_check_marks` | a yes on a bullet on them | `know` (`word`, `photo`, `saw`), `who_name`, `how`, `said_on`, `source` (`office`, `portal`, `crew`), the photo's columns, and the take-back. One live yes a bullet (`start_check_marks_live_idx`). A yes taken back stays as a row. |
| `start_check_asks` | one bullet in one ask, or a hold note sent | `batch_id` (one press of Ask), `kind` (`ask`, `hold_note`), `how`, `to_name`, `asked_on`, `message_id` when it went out through Messages. |
| `start_check_their_days` | a day they gave for ready | `ready_day`, `said_on`, `who_name`, `how`, `source`. Every one is kept. One typed by mistake is marked `removed_at`. |
| `start_check_decisions` | a Go or a Hold | `kind`, `decided_on`, `open_then` (the words of every bullet open then), `start_then`, `hold_until`, who pressed it, and the take-back. |
| `start_check_arrivals` | what the crew found on arrival | `arrived_on`, `not_as_told` (bullet ids, empty when all were as told), `trip_id`, `dispatch_request_id`. |
| `start_check_settings` | a site, or a job | A site's row: `notice_days`. A job's row: `shows_on_gc_page`, and its own `notice_days` when the job is on no site. |

A bullet of ours is never a row in `start_check_marks`. The app reads it, and a trigger refuses a yes on one in words. A bullet of ours that reads `hand` is ticked on the bullet itself.

A null `notice_days` reads the default: ten days for a far site and two for a near one (the owner's call 2, on its default).

## Who

Three gates name the audiences, each in one place.

- **The desk**, `start_checks_desk(job)`: the office (dev, master_technician, assistant, controller), an estimator or a primary with a claim on the job (`is_job_people`), and a superintendent on their own job (`superintendent_report_job_anchor_allowed`). It reads and writes a job's bullets, yeses, asks and days, and reads its decisions and arrivals.
- **The decision**, `start_checks_may_decide(job)`: the office and the job's superintendent (the owner's call 3, on its default).
- **The crew**, `start_checks_crew(job)`: on the job's team, clocked in on it, or a sub who reads it. It reads the bullets on them that are still on the list and their live yeses. It writes nothing here yet. The look on arrival is PR 10.
- A primary reads no job's list beyond `is_job_people`, and is never crew here.
- A job shut by its papers is shut here too: each gate asks `job_shut_to_me`. The tables carry no `job_papers_fence` of their own, since that short fence is for a table that grants a job's rows with no helper.
- The company's lists are read by everyone signed in and written by the office.
- A site's settings row is read by whoever reads the site, and written by the office. A job's row follows the job's desk.
- The desk writes a yes and a day as `source = 'office'` in its own name. The GC's page writes with the service role, in the release that shows it.
- `anon` holds no grant. A signed-in person holds only the grants that have a policy: no insert or update on a decision or an arrival, no update on an ask, and on a day they gave an update of `removed_at` and `removed_by` alone.
- The four fences are called at the end, so a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller are held as on every table.

## Go and Hold: one door

`start_check_decide(p_fixture_id, p_kind, p_open_then, p_hold_until)` is `SECURITY DEFINER`. It keeps who pressed it from the caller's own row, so the name cannot be typed.

- **Go** keeps the day, what was open and our first day as it stood. A Go on a stage with no days is not blocked.
- **Hold** does the same and moves `job_stage_windows` to the day picked with its length kept. A superintendent may decide and holds no write on a window, which is why the door is a definer.
- It refuses in words: a kind that is neither, a Go that names a day, a hold with no day, a hold on a stage with no days of ours, a list that is not a list, a stage that is gone, and anyone who may not decide.
- `start_check_take_back(p_decision_id)` marks a decision taken back with who and when. The row stays. A second call changes nothing. A hold taken back moves no day.
- Both refuse a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller themselves, since a definer stands behind the tables' fences.

## Our days, for the desk

`start_check_windows(p_job_ids)` reads our window for each stage line of the jobs named: the two days and nothing else. `job_stage_windows` is read by the office, an estimator and a superintendent, and by no primary. A primary with a claim on a job is on its desk, and a list that could not say our first day would say nothing. The read answers only for a job whose desk the caller is on, so it opens no job to a primary beyond `is_job_people`, and it answers nothing for an archived caller.

## A row's job

- `job_id` is kept on every job table so a policy reads it without a join. A trigger sets it from the stage line on every write (`start_check_row_on_stage_job`), and a yes takes its bullet's job (`start_check_mark_on_bullet`).
- A stage line that moves to another job carries its list: `jobs_ledger_fixtures_carry_start_checks`, beside the trigger that already carries the checklist and the window.

## What goes with what

- A stage line deleted takes its bullets, their yeses, its asks, the days they gave, its decisions and its arrivals.
- A job deleted takes its lines' lists and its settings row. Combining two jobs moves the lines first, so their lists go with them. The source job's own settings row is lost with it, and the job that stays keeps its own.
- A site deleted takes its notice.
- The company's lists stand whatever goes.

## The photos

Bucket `start-check-photos`: private, 2 MB a file, images only, as `stage-checklist-photos`. Objects are named `<job_id>/<mark_id>-<ms>.jpg`, and the desk's gate reads the job from the path. Nothing uploads yet.

## The bed

`supabase/tests/start_checks/20_scenario.sql`, run by `scripts/pgtest-start-checks.sh` (`npm run test:pg:start-checks`), which also applies this file a second time. Through RLS, as each role: the fences and the grants; the office writing a list, a yes, an ask and a day, with each refusal in its words; Go, the Go taken back, and a Hold that moves the window; who may not decide; our days read for the desk; who reads what, role by role; the settings; a line carrying its list to another job; and what goes with a line, a job and a site. It holds 102 checks, five of them on the fences.

Before the PR the file and the scenario were run on a local Postgres 15 over stub tables, twice, since Docker was not running on the build machine. That proves the SQL's logic and not its fit to the app's schema. The SQL beds workflow is the proof.

## Order

Additive and idempotent. Eight new tables, so the four fence calls close the file. It adds one trigger to `jobs_ledger_fixtures`, which takes a brief lock under the 3 second timeout. Nothing in the app names these tables until the list on a stage, so the push can come any time after the merge, and **the list on a stage must not reach the live app before the push**.
