# 20261103053000_job_warranty.sql (2026-11-02, the warranty in place of a contract, PR 1)

The owner's call of 2026-11-02: when the office is uncomfortable sending a residential customer a contract, it sends a warranty the homeowner initials and signs, which binds both sides because the customer certifies the work on it. The warranty is a kind of job agreement in the Contract Desk, so the window, the link, the page, the signed copy, the chip and the reminders all serve it. The rules are `src/lib/jobs/jobAgreementKind.ts`, `src/lib/flows/facts.ts`, `seed.ts` and `residentialLoad.ts`.

- **Two columns on `job_contracts`**: `kind` (`contract` | `warranty`, `job_contracts_kind_known`, default `contract` so every row from before reads as it was) and `initials` (a warranty's lines as the customer initialled them, `[{line, initials}]`, `job_contracts_initials_list`; null until they sign and on a contract). The one-live-agreement-per-job index stands across the kinds: a job has a contract or a warranty out, not both.
- **`job_contracts_to_activity()`** is re-created from `20261008080000`'s body with its words by kind: *Warranty sent to …*, *Warranty opened by the customer*, *Warranty signed by …*, *Warranty voided — …*; a contract's words are unchanged. The event types stay `contract_*`, so the feed's tags and buckets read them as before.
- **The residential flow's Warranty step**: `flow_fact_ids()` gains `warranty_signed` after `estimate_approved` and `flow_paper_ids()` gains `warranty` after `estimate`, in `facts.ts`'s order (`flowsSql.test.ts` pins both); `flows_seed()` is redeclared with the step as `seed.ts` draws it, an aside loop (`each warranty`, `rounds`) at the end of the Per job lane, their move, the `warranty` paper, a text reminder after two quiet days; the flow prod already has gets the step (`step_key` `warranty_paper`, since the GC customer flow's year after closeout is `warranty` and a quick reply keeps only the id) once, at `max(position) + 1` of its Per job lane, where the office left the other steps alone. The reminder stays off until the owner turns it on (`messages_nudge_app_sends_v1` is not touched), as every residential step's did.
- Additive and idempotent. No table is created, so no fence call. The two `ADD COLUMN`s take a short `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` on `job_contracts` (a column with a constant default is metadata only); the two inline checks scan a table of a few hundred rows; `lock_timeout` makes a busy moment fail fast.
