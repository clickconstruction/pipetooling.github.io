# 20261019070000_bid_money_strip.sql (2026-10-19, v2.5647)

The money line under a won bid (punch list #129 PR 2, `to-dos/estimator-money-word/README.md`): the job's people read where the job born of a bid stands on money without a `jobs_ledger` row of their own. On `20261019060000_job_people.sql` (`is_job_people`, `list_job_payment_promises_for`); push after it, after the PR merges. The client draws nothing until the function answers, so either order.

## What it does

**`list_bid_money_strip(p_bid_ids uuid[]) → jsonb`** (STABLE, SECURITY DEFINER): for each bid asked, the newest job opened from it (`jobs_ledger.bid_id`, by `created_at`), only when `is_job_people(job)` is true for the caller, as one entry: `bidId`; `job` (id, status, revenue, payments_made, collections_at, uncollectible_at, job_name, hcp_number, click_number, billing_only, customer_id, customer_name, customer_phone, customer_email, created_at); `invoices` (id, job_id, status, amount, sequence_order, billed_at, created_at, stripe_invoice_id); `payments` (invoice_id, job_id, amount, paid_on, sequence_order); `promises` (the job's live promises, `list_job_payment_promises_for`'s shape). A bid with no job, a bid the caller is no person of, and a bid that is not there give no entry; no bids give `[]`.

Nothing on `jobs_ledger`'s policies changes.

## Why the shape

- The rows, not the answer: the open amount is the AR page's bill truth (`computeBillTruth` through `papers.ts`'s `billPapers`), the pay page is `billLink.ts`'s rule, the word is `paymentPromises.ts`'s parse. Worked out in SQL they would drift from the Pipeline's; handed over as rows, the line and the Pipeline cannot disagree.
- The job's customer columns ride along so the customer's door (`JobCustomerSheet`) opens from the line with nothing else to read.
- One function per bid list, as `list_bid_job_account_strip`; the Edit Bid's Job block reads one bid, the lens (PR 5) will read the page's.
