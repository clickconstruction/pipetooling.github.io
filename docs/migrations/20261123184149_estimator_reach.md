# 20261123184149_estimator_reach.sql (2026-11-23, v2.8378)

Where an outside signer lives, the owner's second decision (punch list #403, `to-dos/outside-signer-record/README.md`): how far an estimator's reach goes. This is the database's half. One guard on `contacts`, one helper restated, and two read rules. No table, so no fence call. Every statement is safe to run twice. The screens and the signed form's gate follow in v2.8379.

## The decision it builds

The card's pick: a signed line opens its record from the tab. An estimator reads the form's layout and the signature's image for an outside company's paper. Naming a party who holds papers needs one of the four roles. A training account reads the line and is refused the signed file. On 2026-11-22 the owner answered "Yes, all four" to the card's four decisions, and on 2026-11-23 he said to build this one with a training account reading the line only.

## What was so before

- **An estimator could open a party's papers by naming them.** A party on no list is closed to an estimator until **Add to a list** says what they are (call 4). But Messages' **Who is this?** (`name_contact`) is the whole office team's, the estimator among them, and a paper follows its contact. So an estimator named such a party a customer, a supply house or a trade company and then read that party's papers, a W-9 among them. The ground's bed tested this on purpose.
- **The same could be done with no function.** The `contacts` table is the office team's to write (`contacts_team`, one policy for every command), so one `UPDATE` of a contact's kind and record did what `name_contact` does. The fold could be asked for by itself too (`contacts_fold`, and its helper `contacts_fold_papers`).
- **The signed record did not read whole.** An estimator opened an outside company's signed record since v2.8162 and was refused two of its reads: the form's layout (`contract_form_templates`) and the signature's image (the `contract-signer-signatures` bucket). The window said so in words.

## What it makes

- **`contact_holds_paper(p_contact_id)`**, a new function. Whether a contact is the party of any paper. A definer, since an estimator reads no paper of a party on no list and the guards cannot ask as them. `EXECUTE` is revoked from `PUBLIC`, `anon` and `authenticated`, so it tells nobody signed in which contacts hold a paper.
- **`contacts_paper_holder_named()` and the trigger `paper_holder_named_by_office`** on `contacts`, `BEFORE UPDATE OF` the kind and the nine record columns. Two changes are a naming. A contact on no list (kind *unknown* or *lead*) is given a kind or a record. Or a contact that was not an outside company's becomes one, whatever it was before. When the contact holds a paper, either needs `is_office_staff()`: a dev, a leader, an assistant or the controller. Anyone else signed in is refused with `officeNamesPaperHolder` (`42501`) and the words *They hold a paper, so only the office says who they are.*
- **`contacts_fold_papers(p_from, p_into)`**, restated from the ground with one refusal added. Outside a trigger, a signed-in caller who is not one of the four roles carries no paper off a contact that is not an outside company's. It raises the same words, so `name_contact` is undone whole.
- **The read policy `contract_form_templates_estimator_outside`**, new. An estimator who is not a digital twin and not in training reads a form's layout when an outside company's paper is filled on it (`paper_is_outside_company`, asked of each paper the caller reads).
- **The storage policy `contract_signer_signatures_estimator_outside`**, new, on `storage.objects`. An estimator who is not in training reads a signature image in `contract-signer-signatures` when the folder it is kept under is a paper `estimator_reads_paper` says yes to. A path that is no paper's (`gc-sows/…`, `commitments/…`) is never read by it.

## Rules it settles

- **Every path, one rule.** Named in place by `name_contact`, named by a statement of the caller's own, folded by `name_contact`, folded by `contacts_fold` or by the helper alone: each meets the same refusal. The guard is on the table, so a later function that names a contact meets it too.
- **A contact that holds no paper is named as before.** The office team, an estimator among them, still says who an unknown number is.
- **What is not a naming passes.** A record deleted by a screen moves its contact off the row or back to no list. A merge moves a customer's contact to another customer. A thread's state, a name and a goes-by are not among the trigger's columns. A fold between two outside companies' contacts carries its papers for anyone on the office team, as before. A fold a trigger runs is not asked.
- **No signed-in caller, no question.** A write by the service role, a cron or a migration passes.
- **The read of the row is unchanged.** `person_contract_documents_estimator_outside` is not touched. A training estimator still reads an outside company's row, which carries the answers that are not sensitive and the last four of a tax number. What is closed to them is the layout, the image, and the signed file (the gate, in v2.8379).
- **The image follows the gate's own question.** The storage policy asks `estimator_reads_paper`, the function `open-contract-form-pdf` asks. So the image, the file and the row cannot come apart.
- **The four roles read as before.** The table's own read policy and the bucket's own policy (`contract_signer_signatures_select`, made out of band) are not touched. No write policy changes.
- **A digital twin reads neither.** The layout's policy says so, and `estimator_reads_paper` says no to a twin.

## What it does not do

- It does not stop a naming of a contact that holds a paper by one of the four roles. That is **Add to a list**.
- It does not close the office team's direct write of `contacts` for anything else.
- It changes no screen's code. Once pushed, the signed record an estimator already opens from a company's own page lists its form answers and draws its signature, since both reads now answer. Until v2.8379 an estimator who is refused a naming reads the database's own words.
