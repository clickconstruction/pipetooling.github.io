# 20261102171500_job_photo_notes.sql (2026-11-02, Job photos, punch list #244 PR 1)

The ground under the Job photos window (punch list #244, v2.6359): a photo added to a job with a description, from the Pipeline row's camera icon or the Job Mode card, filed by the same sweep into the same Drive folder as a report's photo and listed on Documents → Photos as a fifth source. The rules are `src/lib/photos/jobPhotoNotes.ts`.

- **`job_photo_notes`** — a report photo's shape (`20261029220000_report_photos.sql`) plus `description` (up to 500 characters), `caption` (what the Drive file is named by: the description's first words, else the stage, else *Job photo*) and `added_at`. The phone shrinks the shot and puts it in the bucket with a row; the press **Add N photos to the job** writes the words, the stage and `added_at`; the `photo-save` sweep (PR 2) files only rows with `added_at` into the job's Customer Pictures folder, writes `drive_file_id` and `filed_at` and deletes the bucket copy. A row with no `added_at` is a window closed without the press; the client discards it.
- **The gate** is the job's own, `can_report_stage_progress(job_id)`: select for anyone who may stamp the job's stages; insert by the taker; update (the words, the stage) by the taker or the office and the estimator (`is_office_or_estimator`); delete by the taker while `added_at` is null. An added photo stays: Drive is its home.
- **The bucket `job-photo-notes`** — private, 2 MB a file, JPEG, PNG and WebP, objects `<job_id>/<photo_id>.jpg`; the same gate on the path's first folder; the taker deletes their own object.
- **`uploaded_photos`** gains a fifth branch, `source = 'job'`: the description as `caption` (so the server-side search reads every word), the stage, who and when, the sizes, the bucket and path, the Drive id. Only added rows. The other four branches are as `20261101084330` wrote them.
- **The cron `photo-save`** is re-scheduled with every clause so far plus `job_photo_notes` with `filed_at IS NULL AND storage_path IS NOT NULL AND added_at IS NOT NULL`.
- The three fence calls close it, since a table is made.
