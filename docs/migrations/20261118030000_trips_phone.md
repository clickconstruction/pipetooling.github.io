# 20261118030000_trips_phone.sql (2026-11-17, v2.7700)

The trip record's phone ground. Punch list #379, PR 7a. The crew knows first that a trip has started, so the phone may start one, join one, mark a day and end one. No screen calls these yet: the phone's sheet is the PR that follows.

The tables' writes stay the office's alone, as [`20261117234500_trips.sql`](./20261117234500_trips.md) set them. The crew gets no write policy. Each act is one checked function that touches the caller's own row, on a trip of a job the caller is working.

## What it adds

| Piece | What it is |
|---|---|
| `trips.ended_at` | A nullable stamp. A trip started on the phone grows a day at a time, so its days cannot say it is over. **End the trip** stamps it. Where a trip stands on the screen is still read from its days. |
| `trip_crew_on_job(job)` | The gate: the caller is on the job's crew or its schedule this week or last (`helper_on_job`), or has a clock row of their own on it today or yesterday. It answers yes or no about the caller. |
| `trip_phone_state(job)` | What the phone needs before it asks: the job's development, whether the site has any trip, the caller's own open trip there with their days, and the open trips crewmates started, by first name and first day. A crew member reads no trip they are not on, so this hands over first names and days and no row. Anyone not working the job gets `{ ok: false }`. |
| `trip_phone(p)` | The four acts, `{ action, job_id, trip_id?, kind?, outcome? }`. |
| `trip_save` | The same function, with `was_updated_at` in its reply. |

## The four acts

- **start**: a trip on the job's development, from the phone, with the caller on it, today painted (At the site when no kind is given), and the job named under *Work goes on*. A caller who already has an open trip on that site gets that trip, with today painted, so starting twice makes one trip.
- **join**: the caller onto an open trip of the job's development, today painted.
- **day**: the caller's own today painted on a trip they are on. *Clock in* paints At the site and *Heading home* paints On the road.
- **end**: the trip is stamped ended. An outcome is kept when the trip has none yet; a second one never replaces the first.

Always the caller's own row, and always the server's own today (`app_today()`): a phone cannot paint another person's day or another date. A trip is open while nobody has ended it and its last painted day is no more than three days back, so a weekend can pass and an abandoned trip is not offered for good.

Refused, in words: a caller not working the job, a job with no development, a trip of another site, a day on a trip the caller is not on, a join or a day on an ended trip, a training account, a digital twin and an archived caller. The functions are definers, so the tables' fences do not stand in front of them; each refusal is the function's own.

The office changes or deletes a phone trip like any other, through `trip_save` and the table's delete.

## The stamp on `trip_save`

The dev seat hashes the dry run's reply. The dry run used to return counts alone, so a trip changed in the app between a plan and its apply was replaced without a refusal. The reply now names `was_updated_at`, the stamp of the trip it would replace (null for a new one). A phone act and a save both move that stamp, so a stale plan no longer matches.

## Not here

- **A job with no development.** The phone refuses to start a trip there. A trip held by a job alone still waits for the combine to carry it.
- **A job combined into another** still drops its line under *Work goes on* on the trips that named it. The combine function is restated in its own migration.
