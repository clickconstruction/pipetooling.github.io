# 20261019110000_my_money_strip.sql (2026-10-19, v2.5651)

Money on my bids (punch list #129 PR 5, `to-dos/estimator-money-word/README.md`): the Dashboard's card reads the signed-in person's jobs with their money, with no bid ids in hand. On `20261019060000_job_people.sql` and `20261019080000_paid_claim.sql`; push after them, after the PR merges. The card reads fail-soft before the push (no card), so client first is fine.

## What it does

**`list_my_money_strip() → jsonb`** (STABLE, SECURITY DEFINER): the signed-in person's jobs — the jobs of the bids they estimated or account-manage (`bids.estimator_id`, `bids.account_manager_id` through `jobs_ledger.bid_id`) and the jobs they account-manage (`jobs_ledger.account_manager_user_id`), each once, under `is_job_people` — newest first, each entry in `list_bid_money_strip`'s shape (`bidId`, `job`, `invoices`, `payments`, `promises`). `[]` for nobody.

Nothing on `jobs_ledger`'s policies changes.

## Why the shape

- The same entry as the two strips before it, so the card draws the Bid Board's own lens (`buildMoneyLens`) over the same kernels and the two say the same words.
- "Mine" is the person's, not the role's: an assistant who account-manages a job reads it here too, and an estimator reads only what they sold or hold. The Bid Board's lens, which has the page's bids in hand, uses `list_bid_money_strip` with *Only my bids* as a client filter instead.
