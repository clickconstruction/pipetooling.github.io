# 20261120010000_paper_party.sql (2026-11-19, v2.7977)

Where an outside signer lives, PR 1 of seven: the ground (punch list #403, `to-dos/outside-signer-record/README.md`). A paper names the contact of the party it is with, and keeps who signs. No screen and no client change ride with it.

## What it makes

No table, so no fence call. Every statement is safe to run twice.

- **Six columns on `person_contract_documents`.** `party_contact_id` (a `contacts` row, `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, with a partial index), `party_name`, `signer_name`, `signer_title`, `signer_email` and `signing_for`. All null on every row that exists today.
- **Two checks.** `person_contract_documents_party_words`: a word is trimmed text of 200 characters at most or null, never empty, and an address holds an `@`. `person_contract_documents_party_paper`: a party's paper is filed under `party:<contact id>`, with no roster person, no company and no partnership, and with its frozen name. That stored name never stands without its party. Both are added `NOT VALID` and validated, as the company check was.
- **The name trigger**, `contract_docs_set_person_id`, restated from `20261010009000_gc_papers.sql`. A party's paper keeps no roster person. A copy that carries only the stored name takes its party back from it, and its frozen name from the party's newest paper, else from the contact. The trigger fires on `party_contact_id` too.
- **`contacts_fold_papers(p_from, p_into)`**, which the spine's `contacts_fold` runs with every other `contacts_fold_<something>`. It moves a party's papers, and their stored name, onto the contact that stays.
- **`paper_party_contact(p_record_table, p_record_id)`**, the door a later PR's Send a paper window goes through. It answers the contact that is one record (`customers`, `customer_contact_persons`, `supply_houses`, `supply_house_contacts`, `gc_companies` or `gc_company_people`) and makes it when the record has none, with the record's own number and address as its channels. A customer's own goes through `contact_find_or_make`.
- **One read policy**, `person_contract_documents_estimator_outside`.
- **The view `agreements_on_file`**, restated with `CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW` from its newest definition, `20261118050000_agreements_job_papers.sql`. Only the person branch changes. The columns are the same and in the same order.
- **One more audience on `esign_consents`**, `outside`. The check is restated from the list the table was made with, which no migration had restated.

## Rules it settles

- **A business and its signer are two things** (the owner's call 2). The party is a record, reached through its contact. The signer is words frozen on the paper, because a rep leaves a supply house and the paper must still say who signed.
- **A party named like a teammate never lands on the teammate.** The stored name is one no person can have, as a trade company's `gc-company:<id>` is, and the check holds it both ways.
- **Who reads an outside party's paper** (calls 4 and 7): the four roles as before, and an estimator. The estimator reads a row of the kind `agreement` that is a trade company's own (`company_id`), or whose party's contact is a customer, a person at one, a supply house, a rep, a trade company or a person at one. The contact is read under the estimator's own `contacts` policy.
- **What the estimator's read leaves closed.** Every paper filed under a person. A party on no list, until someone says what they are. A W-9, a certificate and every other kind that is not an agreement: the Board's call of 2026-10-10 kept those to their states for the office (`gc_company_paper_states`), and this does not undo it. The owner's call 9 of 2026-11-20 then did: since [`20261120080000`](./20261120080000_estimator_reads_outside_papers.md) the estimator reads every kind of an outside company's paper. A digital twin, which reads as an estimator.
- **A fold never loses a paper.** The helper is a definer, since the papers are the four roles' to write and a fold runs as whoever names a number, merges two customers or deletes a record. Outside a trigger it moves nothing for a caller off the office team, a training account, a digital twin or an archived account.
- **The door is the four roles'**, who send a paper. It refuses a caller who is not signed in, an archived account and every other role in words. A training account and a digital twin may find a contact and never make one.
- **The list's four new kinds.** A party's paper is `customer_agreement`, `supply_house_agreement`, `trade_agreement` or `outside_agreement`, by the record its contact points at today. The card's plan named three. The fourth, `outside_agreement`, is the party on no list that the owner's call 6 added, and anyone whose record is none of the three.
- **The line's name** is the record's own name today, else the name frozen on the paper, else the signer. `party:<id>` is never shown or searched.
- **A staff, sub, partner or trade company paper reads as before**, except that every person-branch line's `facts` may now carry `signer_name`, `signer_title`, `signing_for` and `signer_printed_name`. A key with no value is left out.

## Two things it does not do

- **A signed form's PDF.** `open-contract-form-pdf` opens one for a dev, the controller and a pay-approved master. An assistant, who reads the row, is refused today. The card expected the estimator's rule to be copied there, and that would let an estimator open a file an assistant cannot. The gate is unchanged, and the question is the owner's. He answered it on 2026-11-20 (calls 10 and 11): an estimator opens an outside company's signed form, a signed W-9 among them, since v2.8108.
- **A trade company's `company_id` papers stay as they are.** A trade company has a contact, so they can move later.
