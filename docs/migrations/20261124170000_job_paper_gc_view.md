# 20261124170000_job_paper_gc_view.sql (2026-11-24, v2.8533)

The office sets what a GC sees of who has signed a job's paper (punch list #389 step 5, its last part, `to-dos/sub-nda-builder/README.md`). No clause wording is written.

## What it makes

No table and no policy, so no fence call. Every statement is safe to run twice.

- **Three columns on `job_required_papers`**: `gc_view`, `gc_view_set_by` and `gc_view_set_at`. `gc_view` is null, `'count'` or `'names'`, held by the check `job_required_papers_gc_view_check`.
- **`job_required_paper_keeps_gc_view()`** and its trigger, before insert, for a row made in another's place.
- **`job_paper_set_gc_view(p_required_paper_id, p_view)`**, new, for the office.
- **`job_papers_gc_page(p_job_ids)` replaced**, with the same grants. It started from the function as `20261124160000` left it, copied by script.

## The three views

| `p_view` | Kept as | What the GC's page shows |
|---|---|---|
| `count` | `'count'` | How many have signed |
| `businesses`, or empty | null | How many, and each sub business with its day |
| `names` | `'names'` | Those, and every signer by name |

Every row starts null, so a job shows what it showed before this migration.

## Rules it settles

- **It is no part of the paper.** The setter changes the row in place whoever has signed, and nobody signs again. The lock on a signed row watches the settings, the blanks and the two revisions, and none of these columns.
- **A new version keeps it.** A change of settings after a signature makes a new row with `revision_of`. The trigger hands that row the old row's three columns, whatever function made it.
- **The row keeps who set it and when.** Setting the view it already has writes nothing.
- **The database withholds what the view hides.** `businesses` is an empty list under `count`. `signers` is filled only under `names`, and is an empty list otherwise.
- **A signer is someone who has signed.** `signers` holds `{ name, business, signed_on }` for each person on the roster with a signed copy, in the order they signed. `business` is the trade company of a sub, and null for anyone else. A person who has not signed is not on it.
- **Guards**, as `job_paper_set_basis` has them: signed in, not archived, never a twin, the office only, not training. A word that is none of the three is refused, and so is a paper not built from settings.

## Safe under what is deployed

- The app on Pages reads `job_required_papers` without `gc_view` and calls no new function. Nothing it reads changes.
- The deployed `customer-portal` reads `job_papers_gc_page` and ignores the two new keys. A row nobody set answers what it answered before.
- **Push right after the merge.** The settings page of this release reads `gc_view` and calls `job_paper_set_gc_view`. Until the push, that page cannot read a job's settings paper. Only a ZZ Test job can take such a paper today.
