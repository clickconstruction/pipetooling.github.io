    Notes on this screen

    The rule

    • A signature is the record, not the mark. The app already says so for the electronic one. A pen signature is the leader’s own act on the paper as printed; the record names who, when, who wrote it down, and how they know.
    • The paper is already tied to the row. The frame prints a record id in its corner, like L878-4C2E91. A photo of the signed page carries it.
    • On his word, again. This mirrors Approve on his word: the office writes it down, the leader sees it under Signed by pen, as recorded, and can say Not what I signed.
    • Paper path only. Docsmit mails the PDF, so a Docsmit envelope still needs the electronic signature. Later: sign the signature page on paper, scan it, and it rides in the PDF.

    Why it happened

    The packet printed Oct 9. The signing rule shipped the next day. Every packet printed before it is unsigned in the record and signed on the paper. This path records those too.

    Decided Oct 12

    1. The photo is asked for, not required. Needs you nags until it is there.
    2. He told me counts on its own. The affidavit's served line says which of the three it was.
    3. The leader's confirmation is not needed. It strengthens the record when it comes.

    1. One pane for every held notice in the run, as drawn, or one per envelope?
